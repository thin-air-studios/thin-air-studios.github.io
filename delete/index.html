Delete your Short Final data

Short Final by Thin Air Capital

Short Final keeps almost everything on your phone. Our server holds data only for players who post ranked scores to the online leaderboards. If you never posted a ranked score, we hold nothing about you.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Short Final and tap Leaderboards on the home screen.
  2. Open the Your airports tab.
  3. Tap Remove my scores, then tap Remove my scores again to confirm.

The button shows while you are signed in with Google Play Games, the sign-in used to post scores.

Or ask us by email

Email thinaircapital@gmail.com with the subject "Remove my scores" and the display name you used on the boards. We act on an email request within 30 days, and it deletes the same data.

What is deleted

  • Your scores and the replays that check them.
  • Your entries on the boards.
  • The reports you filed about names on the boards.
  • Your player record on our server: your Google Play Games player ID, your display name and your sign-in session.

What is kept

  • Scores already posted to Google Play Games stay with Google. You can manage them in Google Play Games.
  • The stats and settings on your phone stay on your phone. Delete them in the game with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game.
