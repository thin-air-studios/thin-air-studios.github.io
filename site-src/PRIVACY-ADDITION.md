# Privacy policy addition: the website (for the lead to apply in the game repository)

Paste this into the privacy policy under a heading such as "Our website", after the section on the app and the online boards. It describes the website as it is built now. If Google Analytics is not switched on, delete the two Google sentences.

---

**Our website (playshortfinal.com).** The Short Final website uses two measurement tools. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and does not use cookies or store anything on your device. Google Analytics measures page views and a few clicks, such as pressing "Get the game", opening the browser demo, choosing a language, reading most of a guide, leaving the site for another website (we record only that website's address), and downloading a press file. Google Analytics runs only after you press Accept on the banner at the bottom of the page. Until you do, the Google script is not loaded and nothing is sent to Google. Your choice is kept in your browser's local storage, under the name "sf-consent", and you can change it by clearing the site's data in your browser. We do not record your name, email address or account, we do not put your searches on the airport pages into our measurements, and we do not use these measurements to show you advertising. Google's and Cloudflare's own privacy policies apply to what they process: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/. Questions about the website can go to thinaircapital@gmail.com.

---

Notes for the lead: the policy's existing list of service providers should name Cloudflare and Google for the website. Do not add any other processor for the website. The game's own sections of the policy are not changed by this text.
