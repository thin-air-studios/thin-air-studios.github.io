Thanksgiving Rush is a holiday event in Short Final, from 25 to 29 November 2026. The days around Thanksgiving are among the busiest travel days of the year in the United States, and the event plays that busy week at some of the busiest airports in the country, such as Atlanta (ATL), Chicago O'Hare (ORD), Dallas Fort Worth (DFW), Denver (DEN) and Los Angeles (LAX).

## What you play

- Real flights, live from the same feed the game uses every day.
- The event is part of the game from version 3.4. The release notes say what is new in each version.
