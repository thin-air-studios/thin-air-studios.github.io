Short Final™

Real traffic. Any airport.

Coming soon to Google Play

Short Final is an air traffic game played with the real sky. Pick any airport on Earth, and the real flights landing there arrive at the edge of your screen on their real bearing, under their real callsigns.

Draw a path from each one to a runway, keep everyone apart, and land as many as you can, in that airport's real weather and time of day.
