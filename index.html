Short Final is an air traffic game played with the real sky. Pick any airport on Earth, and the real flights landing there arrive at the edge of your screen on their real bearing, under their real flight numbers.

Draw a path from each one to a runway, keep everyone apart, and land as many as you can, in that airport's real weather and time of day.

A real plane that lands says the city it flew in from, as it touches down: Arrived from Atlanta. And you can make a 20-second video of your best moment for TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels, with the game's music and the real flights by name. You choose where it goes.
