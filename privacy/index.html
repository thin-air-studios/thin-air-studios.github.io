Short Final privacy policy

Short Final is a game by Thin Air Studios LLC, Colorado, USA. Effective 10 October 2026.

The short version: the game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts of its own. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos or anything else on your phone, and it keeps your scores on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your scores, stars, achievements, settings and the airports you played recently.
  • Airport maps and weather downloaded for the airports you open, kept so they open quickly next time.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

What the game downloads

To draw an airport and fill its sky, the game asks public services for data about the airport you pick. The requests contain that airport's code or coordinates, not your location or any information about you.

  • OpenStreetMap data for the airport's map and surroundings, from public Overpass API servers: overpass-api.de, with overpass.kumi.systems and maps.mail.ru as fallbacks. The 50 busiest airports ship inside the game and need no download. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • The OpenSky Network for live flights near the airport. opensky-network.org.
  • aviationweather.gov (the US National Weather Service) for the airport's weather report. Privacy policy.
  • Google Play: when you open Share, the game checks whether Short Final's store page is live, so your shares link to it only once it is. The check asks for that one page and sends nothing about you.

As with any internet request, these services see your phone's IP address. The game doesn't send them anything else about you.

Google Play Games

If your phone has Google Play Games, the game signs in with it so your achievements and scores appear there. Google then receives your Play Games identity (gamer name and avatar), the achievements you unlock and the scores you post. Its software also collects diagnostics to keep the service working. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

We can see leaderboard entries the way any player can, and nothing more. You can control who sees your Play Games profile, and delete your Play Games data, in the Play Games app or your Google Account.

Radio voices

The radio calls are spoken by your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The game passes it only the radio lines, such as "Speedbird 12, cleared to land runway 27 left".

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game makes a picture or text of your result and hands it to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy then applies. The result contains your airport, score and game stats, and no name, location or account details. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Children

Short Final is not directed at children under 13, and the game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
