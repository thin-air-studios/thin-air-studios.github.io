Thanksgiving Rush

Coming in version 3.4

One of the busiest travel weeks of the year in the United States, planned for version 3.4.

Dates: 25 to 29 November 2026

Get it on Google Play Play free in your browser

This event is not in the game yet. The details on this page can still change before release.

The days around Thanksgiving are among the busiest travel days of the year in the United States. Thanksgiving Rush is planned for version 3.4 of Short Final: a holiday event built around that busy week.

What is planned

  • Holiday traffic at some of the busiest airports in the United States. The airports are named once they are confirmed.
  • Real flights from the same live feed the game uses every day. When few flights are flying near an airport, the sky stays quiet.

The final list of airports and the rules are set before the release. This page will say what the event does once it is in the game.
