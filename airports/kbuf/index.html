About KBUF

Buffalo Niagara International Airport (BUF) is a large airport in Buffalo, United States, with the ICAO code KBUF. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 8,829 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 519 routes through KBUF. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Philadelphia (PHL) and Dulles (IAD).
