About VIPK

Pathankot Airport (IXP) is a medium airport in Pathankot, India, with the ICAO code VIPK. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,970 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VIPK.
