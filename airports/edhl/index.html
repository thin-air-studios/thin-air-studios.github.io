About EDHL

Lübeck Blankensee Airport (LBC) is a medium airport in Lübeck, Germany, with the ICAO code EDHL. It has one runway, 07/25, 6,896 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 20 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 83 routes through EDHL. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Munich (MUC) and Antalya (AYT).
