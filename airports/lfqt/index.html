About LFQT

Merville-Calonne Airport (HZB) is a medium airport in Merville, Nord, France, with the ICAO code LFQT. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,037 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 108 taxiway segments, 47 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFQT.
