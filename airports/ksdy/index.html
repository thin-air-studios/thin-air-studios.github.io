About KSDY

Sidney - Richland Regional Airport (SDY) is a medium airport in Sidney, United States, with the ICAO code KSDY. Its two runways are 01/19 and 11/29. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,705 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Cape Air has 6 routes through KSDY. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Billings (BIL).
