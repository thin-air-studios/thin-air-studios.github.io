About FMNM

Amborovy Airport (MJN) is a large airport in Mahajanga, Madagascar, with the ICAO code FMNM. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 12 routes through FMNM. Its most frequent links are Antananarivo (TNR) and Dzaoudzi (DZA).
