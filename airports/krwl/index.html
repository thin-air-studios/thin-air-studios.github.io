About KRWL

Rawlins Municipal Airport/Harvey Field (RWL) is a medium airport in Rawlins, United States, with the ICAO code KRWL. Its two runways are 04/22 and 11/29. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,008 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through KRWL. Its most frequent links are Hayden (HDN) and Denver (DEN).
