About AYPY

Port Moresby Jacksons International Airport (POM) is a large airport in Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea, with the ICAO code AYPY. Its two runways are 14L/32R and 14R/32L. The longer one, 14L/32R, is 9,022 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 143 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 23 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 36 routes through AYPY. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Sydney (SYD) and Manila (MNL).
