About OIBK

Kish International Airport (KIH) is a large airport in Kish Island, Iran, with the ICAO code OIBK. It has one runway, 09R/27L, 12,004 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 74 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 89 routes through OIBK. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Mashhad (MHD) and Bandar Abbas (BND).
