About LPPD

João Paulo II Airport (PDL) is a large airport in Ponta Delgada, Portugal, with the ICAO code LPPD. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,192 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 52 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 32 airlines have 475 routes through LPPD. Its most frequent links are Lisbon (LIS), Praia da Vitória (TER) and Porto (OPO).
