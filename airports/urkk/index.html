About URKK

Krasnodar Pashkovsky International Airport (KRR) is a large airport in Krasnodar, Russia, with the ICAO code URKK. Its two runways are 05R/23L and 05L/23R. The longer one, 05R/23L, is 9,835 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 145 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 9 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 44 airlines have 490 routes through URKK. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (SVO) and Moscow (VKO).
