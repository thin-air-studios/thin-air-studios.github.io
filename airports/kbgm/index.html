About KBGM

Greater Binghamton/Edwin A Link field (BGM) is a medium airport in Binghamton, United States, with the ICAO code KBGM. Its two runways are 16/34 and 10/28. The longer one, 16/34, is 7,305 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 17 routes through KBGM. Its most frequent links are Detroit (DTW), Philadelphia (PHL) and Dulles (IAD).
