About ORBD

Balad Southeast Airport / Joint Base Balad (ORBD) is a medium airport in Al Bakr, Iraq. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 12/30 and 14/32. The longer one, 12/30, is 11,495 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 187 taxiway segments and 12 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ORBD.
