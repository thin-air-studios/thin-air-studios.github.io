About LFBH

La Rochelle Île de Ré Airport (LRH) is a medium airport in La Rochelle, France, with the ICAO code LFBH. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,398 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 169 routes through LFBH. Its most frequent links are London (STN), London (LGW) and Bristol (BRS).
