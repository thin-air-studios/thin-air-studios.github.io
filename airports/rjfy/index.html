About RJFY

JMSDF Kanoya Air Base (RJFY) is a medium airport in Kanoya, Japan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longer one, 08R/26L, is 7,382 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RJFY.
