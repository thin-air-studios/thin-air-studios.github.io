About KCTZ

Sampson County Airport (CTZ) is a small airport in Clinton, United States, with the ICAO code KCTZ. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,008 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCTZ.
