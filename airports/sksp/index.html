About SKSP

Gustavo Rojas Pinilla International Airport (ADZ) is a large airport in San Andrés, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKSP. It has one runway, 06/24, 7,808 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 156 routes through SKSP. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Medellín (MDE) and Cali (CLO).
