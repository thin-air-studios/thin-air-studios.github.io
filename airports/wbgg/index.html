About WBGG

Kuching International Airport (KCH) is a large airport in Kuching, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WBGG. It has one runway, 07/25, 12,402 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 81 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 374 routes through WBGG. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Kota Kinabalu (BKI) and Singapore (SIN).
