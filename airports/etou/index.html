About ETOU

Wiesbaden Army Airfield (WIE) is a medium airport in Wiesbaden, Germany, with the ICAO code ETOU. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,065 ft long and 120 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 161 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETOU.
