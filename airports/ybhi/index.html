About YBHI

Broken Hill Airport (BHQ) is a medium airport in Broken Hill, Australia, with the ICAO code YBHI. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 8,251 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 13 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 45 routes through YBHI. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Adelaide (ADL) and Dubbo (DBO).
