About LFLS

Grenoble Alpes Isère Airport (GNB) is a medium airport in Grenoble, France, with the ICAO code LFLS. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,007 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 405 routes through LFLS. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Luton (LTN) and Bristol (BRS).
