About KPIR

Pierre Regional Airport (PIR) is a medium airport in Pierre, United States, with the ICAO code KPIR. Its two runways are 13/31 and 07/25. The longer one, 13/31, is 6,900 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 26 routes through KPIR. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Sioux Falls (FSD) and Minneapolis (MSP).
