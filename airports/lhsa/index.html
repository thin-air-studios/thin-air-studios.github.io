About LHSA

Szentkirályszabadja Airfield (LHSA) is a small airport in Szentkirályszabadja, Hungary. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,562 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LHSA.
