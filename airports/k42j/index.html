About K42J

Keystone Heights Airport (K42J) is a small airport in Keystone Heights, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 05/23 and 11/29. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,044 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for K42J.
