About MYBS

South Bimini Airport (BIM) is a medium airport in South Bimini, Bahamas, with the ICAO code MYBS. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,409 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through MYBS. Its most frequent links are Nassau (NAS), Fort Lauderdale (FLL) and Miami (MIA).
