About WBKL

Labuan Airport (LBU) is a medium airport in Labuan, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WBKL. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,006 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 71 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 61 routes through WBKL. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Kota Kinabalu (BKI) and Miri (MYY).
