About LFTH

Toulon-Hyères Airport (TLN) is a medium airport in Hyères, Var, France, with the ICAO code LFTH. Its two runways are 05/23 and 13/31. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,955 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 303 routes through LFTH. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Brest (BES) and Rotterdam (RTM).
