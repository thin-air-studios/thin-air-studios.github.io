About VERB

Fursatganj Airport (VERB) is a small airport in Tarauna, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,650 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VERB.
