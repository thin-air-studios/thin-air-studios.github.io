About LZSL

Sliač Airport (SLD) is a medium airport in Sliač, Slovakia, with the ICAO code LZSL. It has one runway, 18/36, 7,874 ft long and 187 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 83 taxiway segments, 68 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 31 routes through LZSL. Its most frequent links are Burgas (BOJ), Antalya (AYT) and Budapest (BUD).
