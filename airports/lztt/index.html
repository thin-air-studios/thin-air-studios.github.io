About LZTT

Poprad-Tatry Airport (TAT) is a medium airport in Poprad, Slovakia, with the ICAO code LZTT. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 07L/25R and 07R/25L. The longest, 09/27, is 8,530 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 13 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 89 routes through LZTT. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Antalya (AYT) and Burgas (BOJ).
