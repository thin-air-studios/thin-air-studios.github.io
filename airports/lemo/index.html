About LEMO

Moron Air Base (OZP) is a medium airport in Morón, Spain, with the ICAO code LEMO. It has one runway, 02/20, 11,801 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 1 routes through LEMO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Cherry Point (KNKT).
