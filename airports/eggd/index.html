About EGGD

Bristol Airport (BRS) is a large airport in Bristol, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGGD. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,598 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 gates, 41 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 55 airlines have 5,279 routes through EGGD. Its most frequent links are Ingliston (EDI), Belfast (BFS) and Glasgow (GLA).
