About EGDR

RNAS Culdrose (EGDR) is a medium airport in Helston, United Kingdom. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 11/29, 18/36 and 06/24. The longest, 11/29, is 6,006 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 102 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EGDR.
