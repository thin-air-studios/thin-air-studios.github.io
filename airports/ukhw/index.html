About UKHW

Chuhuiv Air Base (UKHW) is a small airport in Chuhuiv, Ukraine. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,202 ft long and 155 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 104 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UKHW.
