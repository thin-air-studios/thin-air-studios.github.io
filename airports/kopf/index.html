About KOPF

Miami-Opa Locka Executive Airport (OPF) is a medium airport in Miami, United States, with the ICAO code KOPF. Its 3 runways are 09L/27R, 12/30 and 09R/27L. The longest, 09L/27R, is 8,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 214 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 31 routes through KOPF. Its most frequent links are White Plains (HPN), Dallas (DAL) and Teterboro (TEB).
