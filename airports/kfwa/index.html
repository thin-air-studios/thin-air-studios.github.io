About KFWA

Fort Wayne International Airport (FWA) is a medium airport in Fort Wayne, United States, with the ICAO code KFWA. Its 3 runways are 05/23, 14/32 and 09/27. The longest, 05/23, is 11,981 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 64 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 139 routes through KFWA. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Punta Gorda (PGD) and Detroit (DTW).
