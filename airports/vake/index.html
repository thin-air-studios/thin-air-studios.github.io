About VAKE

Kandla Airport (IXY) is a medium airport in Kandla, India, with the ICAO code VAKE. It has one runway, 05/23, 4,997 ft long and 94 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SEJ has 7 routes through VAKE. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM) and New Delhi (DEL).
