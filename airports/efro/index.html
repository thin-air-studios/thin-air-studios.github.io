About EFRO

Rovaniemi Airport (RVN) is a large airport in Rovaniemi, Finland, with the ICAO code EFRO. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,849 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 65 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 487 routes through EFRO. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Manchester (MAN) and London (LGW).
