About KATL

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL) is a large airport in Atlanta, United States, with the ICAO code KATL. It is number 1 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 106.3 million passengers in 2025. It has 5 runways, from 9,000 ft to 12,390 ft long. The longest is 09L/27R. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 201 gates, 437 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 24 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 90 airlines have 4,183 routes through KATL. Its most frequent links are New York (LGA), Chicago (ORD) and Philadelphia (PHL).
