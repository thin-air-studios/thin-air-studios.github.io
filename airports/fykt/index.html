About FYKT

Keetmanshoop Airport (KMP) is a medium airport in Keetmanshoop, Namibia, with the ICAO code FYKT. Its two runways are 04/22 and 18/36. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,598 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 14 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FYKT.
