About SBGR

São Paulo/Guarulhos–Governor André Franco Montoro International Airport (GRU) is a large airport in São Paulo, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBGR. It is number 50 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 47.2 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 12,139 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 171 gates, 480 taxiway segments, 25 aprons and 9 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 80 airlines have 2,952 routes through SBGR. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), Recife (REC) and Porto Alegre (POA).
