About LTCS

Şanlıurfa GAP Airport (GNY) is a large airport in Şanlıurfa, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTCS. It has one runway, 04/22, 13,123 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 118 routes through LTCS. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Gaziemir (ADB).
