About ZUBJ

Bijie Feixiong Airport (BFJ) is a medium airport in Bijie, China, with the ICAO code ZUBJ. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 22 routes through ZUBJ. Its most frequent links are Hangzhou (HGH), Tongren (TEN) and Xiamen (XMN).
