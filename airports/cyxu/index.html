About CYXU

London International Airport (YXU) is a medium airport in London, Canada, with the ICAO code CYXU. Its two runways are 15/33 and 09/27. The longer one, 15/33, is 8,800 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 119 routes through CYXU. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Calgary (YYC) and Cancún (CUN).
