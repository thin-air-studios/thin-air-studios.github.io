About AYKV

Kavieng Airport (KVG) is a medium airport in Kavieng, Papua New Guinea, with the ICAO code AYKV. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,592 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for AYKV.
