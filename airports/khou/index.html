About KHOU

William P. Hobby Airport (HOU) is a large airport in Houston, United States, with the ICAO code KHOU. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 13R/31L and 13L/31R. The longest, 04/22, is 7,602 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 gates, 169 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 686 routes through KHOU. Its most frequent links are Dallas (DAL), Denver (DEN) and Orlando (MCO).
