About EPKT

Katowice Wojciech Korfanty International Airport (KTW) is a large airport in Katowice, Poland, with the ICAO code EPKT. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 88 airlines have 2,413 routes through EPKT. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Dortmund (DTM) and Hurghada (HRG).
