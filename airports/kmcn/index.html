About KMCN

Middle Georgia Regional Airport (MCN) is a medium airport in Macon, United States, with the ICAO code KMCN. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 18 routes through KMCN. Its most frequent links are Baltimore (BWI), Dulles (IAD) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).
