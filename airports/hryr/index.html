About HRYR

Kigali International Airport (KGL) is a large airport in Kigali, Rwanda, with the ICAO code HRYR. It has one runway, 10/28, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 23 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 99 routes through HRYR. Its most frequent links are Entebbe (EBB), Nairobi (NBO) and Zaventem (BRU).
