About OS70

Al Qusayr Air Base (OS70) is a medium airport in Al-Qusayr, Syria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OS70.
