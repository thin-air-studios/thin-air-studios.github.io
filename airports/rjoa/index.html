About RJOA

Hiroshima Airport (HIJ) is a large airport in Hiroshima, Japan, with the ICAO code RJOA. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,842 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 129 routes through RJOA. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Seoul (ICN) and Narita (NRT).
