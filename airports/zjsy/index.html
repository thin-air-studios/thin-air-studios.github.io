About ZJSY

Sanya Phoenix International Airport (SYX) is a large airport in Sanya (Tianya), China, with the ICAO code ZJSY. It has one runway, 08/26, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 123 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 65 airlines have 886 routes through ZJSY. Its most frequent links are Beijing (PEK), Shanghai (PVG) and Chengdu (TFU).
