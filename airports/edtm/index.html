About EDTM

Mengen-Hohentengen Airfield (EDTM) is a medium airport in Mengen, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 07/25 and 07L/25R. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,138 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDTM.
