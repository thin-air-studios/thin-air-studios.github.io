About SKPE

Matecaña International Airport (PEI) is a medium airport in Pereira, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKPE. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,627 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 170 routes through SKPE. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Medellín (MDE) and Medellín (EOH).
