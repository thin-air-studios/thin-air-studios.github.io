About EGQS

RAF Lossiemouth (LMO) is a medium airport in Lossiemouth, Moray, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGQS. Its two runways are 05/23 and 10/28. The longer one, 05/23, is 9,091 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 130 taxiway segments and 33 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 4 routes through EGQS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Lincoln (QCY).
