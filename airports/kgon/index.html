About KGON

Groton New London Airport (GON) is a medium airport in Groton, United States, with the ICAO code KGON. Its two runways are 05/23 and 15/33. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGON.
