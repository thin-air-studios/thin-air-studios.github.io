About 14NC

Camp Davis Marine Corps Outlying Field (14NC) is a small airport in Holly Ridge, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 18/36 and 05/23. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for 14NC.
