About KRYN

Ryan Field (KRYN) is a small airport in Tucson, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 06R/24L, 06L/24R and 15/33. The longest, 06R/24L, is 5,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 111 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRYN.
