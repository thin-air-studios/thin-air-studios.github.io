About KOLM

Olympia Regional Airport (OLM) is a medium airport in Olympia, United States, with the ICAO code KOLM. Its two runways are 17/35 and 08/26. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOLM.
