About LICB

Comiso Airport (CIY) is a medium airport in Comiso, Italy, with the ICAO code LICB. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,070 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 178 routes through LICB. Its most frequent links are Charleroi (CRL), Rome (FCO) and Ferno (MXP).
