About DNYO

Yola Airport (YOL) is a medium airport in Yola, Nigeria, with the ICAO code DNYO. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,840 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 5 routes through DNYO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Abuja (ABV).
