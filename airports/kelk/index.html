About KELK

Elk City Regional Business Airport (ELK) is a small airport in Elk City, United States, with the ICAO code KELK. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,399 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KELK.
