About RPVI

Iloilo International Airport (ILO) is a large airport in Cabatuan, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPVI. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 194 routes through RPVI. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL), Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB) and Davao (DVO).
