About LFAT

Le Touquet-Côte d'Opale Airport (LTQ) is a medium airport in Le Touquet-Paris-Plage, France, with the ICAO code LFAT. It has one runway, 13/31, 6,070 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, ASL has 1 routes through LFAT. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Prague (PRG).
