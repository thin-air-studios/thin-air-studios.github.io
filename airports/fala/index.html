About FALA

Lanseria International Airport (HLA) is a large airport in Johannesburg, South Africa, with the ICAO code FALA. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,996 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 143 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 107 routes through FALA. Its most frequent links are Cape Town (CPT), Durban (DUR) and East London (ELS).
