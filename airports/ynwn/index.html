About YNWN

Newman Airport (ZNE) is a medium airport in Newman, Australia, with the ICAO code YNWN. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,798 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 78 routes through YNWN. Its most frequent links are Perth (PER) and Busselton (BQB).
