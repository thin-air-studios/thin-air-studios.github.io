About PTPN

Pohnpei International Airport (PNI) is a medium airport in Pohnpei Island, Micronesia, with the ICAO code PTPN. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,600 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through PTPN. Its most frequent links are Weno Island (TKK), Okat (KSA) and Majuro Atoll (MAJ).
