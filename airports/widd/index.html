About WIDD

Hang Nadim International Airport (BTH) is a large airport in Batam, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIDD. It has one runway, 04/22, 13,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 106 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 248 routes through WIDD. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Beringin (KNO) and Pekanbaru (PKU).
