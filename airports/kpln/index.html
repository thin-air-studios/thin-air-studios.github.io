About KPLN

Pellston Regional Airport of Emmet County Airport (PLN) is a medium airport in Pellston, United States, with the ICAO code KPLN. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,513 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 32 routes through KPLN. Its most frequent links are Grand Rapids (GRR), Detroit (DTW) and Chicago (ORD).
