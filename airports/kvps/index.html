About KVPS

Destin-Fort Walton Beach Airport (VPS) is a medium airport in Valparaiso, United States, with the ICAO code KVPS. Its two runways are 12/30 and 02/20. The longer one, 12/30, is 12,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 216 taxiway segments, 31 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 214 routes through KVPS. Its most frequent links are Cincinnati / Covington (CVG), Fayetteville/Springdale/Rogers (XNA) and Charlotte (CLT).
