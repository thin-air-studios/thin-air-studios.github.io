About KTPA

Tampa International Airport (TPA) is a large airport in Tampa, United States, with the ICAO code KTPA. Its 3 runways are 19R/01L, 19L/01R and 10/28. The longest, 19R/01L, is 11,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 gates, 346 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 49 airlines have 1,431 routes through KTPA. Its most frequent links are Atlanta (ATL), Baltimore (BWI) and Denver (DEN).
