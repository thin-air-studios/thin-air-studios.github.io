About OEYN

Prince Abdulmohsen Bin Abdulaziz International Airport (YNB) is a large airport in Yanbu, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEYN. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,532 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 42 routes through OEYN. Its most frequent links are Cairo (CAI), Riyadh (RUH) and Dubai (DXB).
