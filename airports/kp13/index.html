About KP13

San Carlos Apache Airport (GLB) is a small airport in Globe, United States, with the ICAO code KP13. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,500 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KP13.
