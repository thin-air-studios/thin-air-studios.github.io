About UKKE

Cherkasy International Airport (CKC) is a medium airport in Cherkasy, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKKE. It has one runway, 15/33, 8,180 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 85 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UKKE.
