About LROP

Bucharest Henri Coandă International Airport (OTP) is a large airport in Otopeni, Romania, with the ICAO code LROP. Its two runways are 08L/26R and 08R/26L. The longer one, 08L/26R, is 11,484 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 gates, 103 taxiway segments, 75 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 135 airlines have 5,782 routes through LROP. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Paris (CDG) and Luton (LTN).
