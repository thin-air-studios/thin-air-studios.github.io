About KLAL

Lakeland Linder International Airport (LAL) is a medium airport in Lakeland, United States, with the ICAO code KLAL. Its two runways are 10/28 and 05/23. The longer one, 10/28, is 8,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 92 taxiway segments, 34 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 108 routes through KLAL. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), San Juan (SJU) and Austin (AUS).
