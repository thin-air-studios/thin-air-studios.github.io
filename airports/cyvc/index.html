About CYVC

La Ronge Airport (YVC) is a medium airport in La Ronge, Canada, with the ICAO code CYVC. Its two runways are 18/36 and 11/29. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 5 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYVC.
