About SBSP

Congonhas–Deputado Freitas Nobre Airport (CGH) is a large airport in São Paulo, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBSP. Its two runways are 17R/35L and 17L/35R. The longer one, 17R/35L, is 6,365 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 gates, 91 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 1,457 routes through SBSP. Its most frequent links are Rio de Janeiro (SDU), Belo Horizonte (CNF) and Porto Alegre (POA).
