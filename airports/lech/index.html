About LECH

Castellón-Costa Azahar Airport (CDT) is a medium airport in Castellón de la Plana, Spain, with the ICAO code LECH. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,858 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 96 routes through LECH. Its most frequent links are Otopeni (OTP), Charleroi (CRL) and London (STN).
