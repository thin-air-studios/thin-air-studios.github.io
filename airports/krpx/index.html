About KRPX

Roundup Airport (RPX) is a small airport in Roundup, United States, with the ICAO code KRPX. Its two runways are 07/25 and 16/34. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,098 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRPX.
