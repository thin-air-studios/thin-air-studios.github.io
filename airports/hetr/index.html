About HETR

At Tur Airport (ELT) is a small airport in At Tur, Egypt, with the ICAO code HETR. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HETR.
