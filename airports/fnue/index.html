About FNUE

Luena Airport (LUO) is a medium airport in Luena, Angola, with the ICAO code FNUE. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,875 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, DTA has 1 routes through FNUE. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Luanda (NBJ).
