About ROAH

Naha International Airport (OKA) is a large airport in Naha, Japan, with the ICAO code ROAH. Its two runways are 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longer one, 18L/36R, is 9,840 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 gates, 138 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 872 routes through ROAH. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (KIX) and Tokoname (NGO).
