About LTAZ

Nevşehir Kapadokya Airport (NAV) is a large airport in Nevşehir, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAZ. It has one runway, 11/29, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 90 routes through LTAZ. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Pendik (SAW) and Sofia (SOF).
