About SVMI

Maiquetía Simón Bolívar International Airport (CCS) is a large airport in Maiquetía, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVMI. Its two runways are 10/28 and 09/27. The longer one, 10/28, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 232 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 62 airlines have 470 routes through SVMI. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Guyana City (PZO) and Maracaibo (MAR).
