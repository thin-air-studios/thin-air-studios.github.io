About VAJM

Jamnagar Airport (JGA) is a medium airport in Jamnagar, India, with the ICAO code VAJM. Its two runways are 06/24 and 12/30. The longer one, 06/24, is 8,242 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 18 routes through VAJM. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), Ahmedabad (AMD) and Surat (STV).
