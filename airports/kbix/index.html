About KBIX

Keesler Air Force Base (BIX) is a medium airport in Biloxi, United States, with the ICAO code KBIX. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,630 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 79 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBIX.
