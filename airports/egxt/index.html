About EGXT

RAF Wittering (EGXT) is a medium airport in Peterborough, Cambridgeshire, United Kingdom. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,052 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 62 taxiway segments and 21 aprons.
