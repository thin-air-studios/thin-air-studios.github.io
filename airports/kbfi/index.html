About KBFI

King County International Airport - Boeing Field (BFI) is a medium airport in Seattle, United States, with the ICAO code KBFI. Its two runways are 14R/32L and 14L/32R. The longer one, 14R/32L, is 10,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 78 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 141 routes through KBFI. Its most frequent links are Louisville (SDF), Ontario (ONT) and Dublin (DUB).
