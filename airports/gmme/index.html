About GMME

Rabat-Salé Airport (RBA) is a large airport in Rabat, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMME. It has one runway, 03/21, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 549 routes through GMME. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Paris (ORY) and Charleroi (CRL).
