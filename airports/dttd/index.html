About DTTD

Remada Air Base (DTTD) is a medium airport in Remada, Tunisia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,812 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DTTD.
