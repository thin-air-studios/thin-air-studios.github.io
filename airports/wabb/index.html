About WABB

Frans Kaisiepo Airport (BIK) is a medium airport in Biak, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WABB. It has one runway, 11/29, 11,715 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 4 routes through WABB. Its most frequent links are Sentani (DJJ), Makassar (UPG) and Brisbane (BNE).
