About KMKT

Mankato Regional Airport (MKT) is a small airport in Mankato, United States, with the ICAO code KMKT. Its two runways are 15/33 and 04/22. The longer one, 15/33, is 6,600 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMKT.
