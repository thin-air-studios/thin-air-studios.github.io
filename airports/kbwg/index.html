About KBWG

Bowling Green Warren County Regional Airport (BWG) is a medium airport in Bowling Green, United States, with the ICAO code KBWG. Its two runways are 03/21 and 12/30. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBWG.
