About FVFA

Victoria Falls International Airport (VFA) is a large airport in Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe, with the ICAO code FVFA. It has one runway, 12/30, 13,123 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 63 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 36 routes through FVFA. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Cape Town (CPT) and Harare (HRE).
