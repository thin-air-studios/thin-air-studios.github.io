About MYAP

Spring Point Airport (AXP) is a medium airport in Spring Point, Bahamas, with the ICAO code MYAP. It has one runway, 13/31, 5,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BHS has 1 routes through MYAP. Its most frequent links are Colonel Hill (CRI) and Nassau (NAS).
