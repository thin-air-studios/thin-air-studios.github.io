About OESK

Al-Jawf International Airport (AJF) is a large airport in Al-Jawf, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OESK. It has one runway, 10/28, 12,011 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 30 routes through OESK. Its most frequent links are Riyadh (RUH), Cairo (CAI) and Jeddah (JED).
