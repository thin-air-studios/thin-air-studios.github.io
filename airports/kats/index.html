About KATS

Artesia Municipal Airport (ATS) is a small airport in Artesia, United States, with the ICAO code KATS. Its two runways are 04/22 and 13/31. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,800 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KATS.
