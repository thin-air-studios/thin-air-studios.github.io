About VYMD

Mandalay International Airport (MDL) is a large airport in Mandalay, Myanmar (Burma), with the ICAO code VYMD. It has one runway, 17/35, 14,003 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 54 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 148 routes through VYMD. Its most frequent links are Yangon (RGN), Myitkyina (MYT) and Heho (HEH).
