About CYTS

Timmins/Victor M. Power (YTS) is a medium airport in Timmins, Canada, with the ICAO code CYTS. Its two runways are 03/21 and 10/28. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 50 routes through CYTS. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Toronto (YTZ) and Sudbury (YSB).
