About ESNS

Skellefteå Airport (SFT) is a medium airport in Skellefteå, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESNS. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,268 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 5 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 68 routes through ESNS. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Gran Canaria Island (LPA) and London (STN).
