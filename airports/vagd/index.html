About VAGD

Gondia Airport (GDB) is a medium airport in Gondia, India, with the ICAO code VAGD. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,500 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through VAGD. Its most frequent links are Hyderabad (HYD) and Indore (IDR).
