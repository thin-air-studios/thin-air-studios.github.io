About KHSV

Huntsville International Airport (HSV) is a medium airport in Huntsville, United States, with the ICAO code KHSV. Its two runways are 18R/36L and 18L/36R. The longer one, 18R/36L, is 12,600 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 135 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 218 routes through KHSV. Its most frequent links are Luxembourg (LUX), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Charlotte (CLT).
