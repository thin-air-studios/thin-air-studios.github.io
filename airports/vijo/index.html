About VIJO

Jodhpur Airport (JDH) is a medium airport in Jodhpur, India, with the ICAO code VIJO. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,005 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 65 routes through VIJO. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
