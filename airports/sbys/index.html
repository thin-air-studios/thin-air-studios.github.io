About SBYS

Campo Fontenelle (SBYS) is a medium airport in Pirassununga, Brazil. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 02C/20C, 02L/20R and 02R/20L. The longest, 02C/20C, is 6,923 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 81 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SBYS.
