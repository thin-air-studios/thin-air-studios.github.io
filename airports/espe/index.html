About ESPE

Vidsel Air Base (ESPE) is a small airport in Vidsel, Sweden. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,316 ft long and 115 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ESPE.
