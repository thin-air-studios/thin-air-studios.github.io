About USRK

Kogalym International Airport (KGP) is a medium airport in Kogalym, Russia, with the ICAO code USRK. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,225 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 19 routes through USRK. Its most frequent links are Ufa (UFA), Samara (KUF) and Moscow (VKO).
