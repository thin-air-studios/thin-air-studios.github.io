About SKPS

Antonio Nariño Airport (PSO) is a medium airport in Chachagüí, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKPS. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,585 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 23 routes through SKPS. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG) and Cali (CLO).
