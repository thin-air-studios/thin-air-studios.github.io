About EGXC

RAF Coningsby (QCY) is a medium airport in Lincoln, Lincolnshire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGXC. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,003 ft long and 187 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 111 taxiway segments and 33 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 6 routes through EGXC. Its most frequent links are Lossiemouth (LMO) and Warton (WRT).
