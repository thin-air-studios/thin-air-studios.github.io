About KLNK

Lincoln Airport (LNK) is a medium airport in Lincoln, United States, with the ICAO code KLNK. Its 3 runways are 18/36, 14/32 and 17/35. The longest, 18/36, is 12,901 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 172 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 69 routes through KLNK. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
