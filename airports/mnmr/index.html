About MNMR

Montelimar Airport (MNMR) is a small airport in Montelimar, Nicaragua. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,680 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MNMR.
