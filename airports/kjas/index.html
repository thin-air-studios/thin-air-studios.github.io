About KJAS

Jasper County Airport Bell Field (JAS) is a small airport in Jasper, United States, with the ICAO code KJAS. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,500 ft long and 70 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KJAS.
