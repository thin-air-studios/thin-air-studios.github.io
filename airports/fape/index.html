About FAPE

Chief Dawid Stuurman International Airport (PLZ) is a large airport in Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth), South Africa, with the ICAO code FAPE. Its two runways are 08/26 and 17/35. The longer one, 08/26, is 7,087 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 88 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 177 routes through FAPE. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Cape Town (CPT) and Durban (DUR).
