About YMHU

McArthur River Mine Airport (MCV) is a small airport in McArthur River Mine, Australia, with the ICAO code YMHU. It has one runway, 06/24, 4,931 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, ANO has 4 routes through YMHU. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Darwin (DRW).
