About KMYL

McCall Municipal Airport (MYL) is a medium airport in McCall, United States, with the ICAO code KMYL. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,101 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMYL.
