About KARR

Aurora Municipal Airport (AUZ) is a small airport in Chicago/Aurora, United States, with the ICAO code KARR. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 15/33 and 18/36. The longest, 09/27, is 6,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 62 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KARR.
