About VVCT

Can Tho International Airport (VCA) is a large airport in Can Tho, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVCT. It has one runway, 06/24, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 66 routes through VVCT. Its most frequent links are Hanoi (HAN), Da Nang (DAD) and Con Dao (VCS).
