About OIMB

Birjand International Airport (XBJ) is a large airport in Birjand, Iran, with the ICAO code OIMB. Its two runways are 10/28 and 08/26. The longer one, 10/28, is 9,521 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through OIMB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Tehran (THR).
