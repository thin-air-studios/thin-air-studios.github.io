About KBUR

Hollywood Burbank/Bob Hope Airport (BUR) is a large airport in Burbank, United States, with the ICAO code KBUR. Its two runways are 15/33 and 08/26. The longer one, 15/33, is 6,886 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 161 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 539 routes through KBUR. Its most frequent links are Las Vegas (LAS), San Francisco (SFO) and Oakland (OAK).
