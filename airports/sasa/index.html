About SASA

Martín Miguel de Güemes International Airport (SLA) is a large airport in Salta, Argentina, with the ICAO code SASA. Its two runways are 02/20 and 06/24. The longer one, 02/20, is 9,842 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 22 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 103 routes through SASA. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP), Puerto Iguazu (IGR) and Buenos Aires (EZE).
