About KTRK

Truckee Tahoe Airport (TKF) is a medium airport in Truckee, United States, with the ICAO code KTRK. Its two runways are 11/29 and 02/20. The longer one, 11/29, is 7,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 89 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, FDY has 4 routes through KTRK. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is San Carlos (SQL).
