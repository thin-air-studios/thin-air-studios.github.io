About KPDK

DeKalb Peachtree Airport (PDK) is a medium airport in Atlanta, United States, with the ICAO code KPDK. Its 3 runways are 3R/21L, 16/34 and 3L/21R. The longest, 3R/21L, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 79 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SKQ has 1 routes through KPDK. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Birmingham (BHM).
