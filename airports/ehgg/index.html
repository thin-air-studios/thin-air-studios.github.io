About EHGG

Groningen Airport Eelde (GRQ) is a large airport in Groningen, Netherlands, with the ICAO code EHGG. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 174 routes through EHGG. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Gran Canaria Island (LPA) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
