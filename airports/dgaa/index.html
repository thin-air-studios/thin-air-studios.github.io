About DGAA

Kotoka International Airport (ACC) is a large airport in Accra, Ghana, with the ICAO code DGAA. It has one runway, 03/21, 11,165 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 24 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 43 airlines have 210 routes through DGAA. Its most frequent links are Abidjan (ABJ), Zaventem (BRU) and Lagos (LOS).
