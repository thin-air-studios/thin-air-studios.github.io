About LIPC

Cervia Air Base (LIPC) is a medium airport in Cervia (RA), Italy. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 10,007 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIPC.
