About RCSS

Taipei Songshan International Airport (TSA) is a large airport in Taipei (Songshan), Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCSS. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,547 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 38 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 380 routes through RCSS. Its most frequent links are Huxi (MZG), Shang-I (KNH) and Matsu (LZN).
