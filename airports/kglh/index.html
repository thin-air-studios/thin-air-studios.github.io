About KGLH

Mid Delta Regional Airport (GLH) is a medium airport in Greenville, United States, with the ICAO code KGLH. Its two runways are 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longer one, 18L/36R, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 12 routes through KGLH. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Atlanta (ATL) and Chicago (ORD).
