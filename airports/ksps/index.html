About KSPS

Wichita Falls Municipal Airport / Sheppard Air Force Base (SPS) is a medium airport in Wichita Falls, United States, with the ICAO code KSPS. Its 4 runways are 15R/33L, 15C/33C, 17/35 and 15L/33R. The longest, 15R/33L, is 13,100 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 7 routes through KSPS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
