About KLNS

Lancaster Airport (LNS) is a medium airport in Lancaster, United States, with the ICAO code KLNS. Its two runways are 08/26 and 13/31. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,933 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 20 routes through KLNS. Its most frequent links are Dulles (IAD), Chicago (ORD) and Pittsburgh (PIT).
