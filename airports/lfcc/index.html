About LFCC

Cahors Lalbenque airport (ZAO) is a medium airport in Cahors, France, with the ICAO code LFCC. It has one runway, 13/31, 4,921 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFCC.
