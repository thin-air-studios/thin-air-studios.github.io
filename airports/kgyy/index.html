About KGYY

Gary/Chicago International Airport (GYY) is a medium airport in Gary, United States, with the ICAO code KGYY. Its two runways are 12/30 and 02/20. The longer one, 12/30, is 8,859 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, UPS has 8 routes through KGYY. Its most frequent links are New York (JFK), Chicago/Rockford (RFD) and Fargo (FAR).
