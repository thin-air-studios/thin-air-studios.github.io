About KRBW

Lowcountry Regional Airport (RBW) is a small airport in Walterboro, United States, with the ICAO code KRBW. Its two runways are 05/23 and 17/35. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRBW.
