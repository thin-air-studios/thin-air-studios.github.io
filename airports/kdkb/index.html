About KDKB

DeKalb Taylor Municipal Airport (KDKB) is a small airport in DeKalb, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 02/20 and 09/27. The longer one, 02/20, is 7,026 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDKB.
