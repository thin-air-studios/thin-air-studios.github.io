About HE40

Sidi EL Barrani Airport (SQK) is a small airport in Sidi El Barrani, Egypt, with the ICAO code HE40. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 9,882 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE40.
