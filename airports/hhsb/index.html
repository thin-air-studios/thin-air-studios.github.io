About HHSB

Assab International Airport (ASA) is a medium airport in Assab, Eritrea, with the ICAO code HHSB. It has one runway, 12/30, 11,531 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HHSB.
