About UARR

Manshuk Mametova International Airport (URA) is a large airport in Uralsk, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UARR. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,183 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 31 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 46 routes through UARR. Its most frequent links are Almaty (ALA), Astana (NQZ) and Aktau (SCO).
