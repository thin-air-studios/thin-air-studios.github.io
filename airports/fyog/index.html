About FYOG

Oranjemund Airport (OMD) is a medium airport in Oranjemund, Namibia, with the ICAO code FYOG. It has one runway, 02/20, 5,252 ft long and 59 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WAA has 3 routes through FYOG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Windhoek (ERS).
