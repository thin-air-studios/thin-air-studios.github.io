About ENDU

Bardufoss Airport (BDU) is a medium airport in Målselv, Norway, with the ICAO code ENDU. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,015 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 27 routes through ENDU. Its most frequent links are Oslo (OSL), Amsterdam (AMS) and Malmö (MMX).
