About RCKH

Kaohsiung International Airport (KHH) is a large airport in Kaohsiung (Xiaogang), Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCKH. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,335 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 gates, 44 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 476 routes through RCKH. Its most frequent links are Huxi (MZG), Hong Kong (HKG) and Shang-I (KNH).
