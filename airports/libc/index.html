About LIBC

Crotone Sant'Anna Pythagoras Airport (CRV) is a medium airport in Isola di Capo Rizzuto (KR), Italy, with the ICAO code LIBC. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 46 routes through LIBC. Its most frequent links are Orio al Serio (BGY), Bologna (BLQ) and Caselle Torinese (TRN).
