About KCIU

Chippewa County International Airport (CIU) is a medium airport in Kincheloe, United States, with the ICAO code KCIU. Its two runways are 16/34 and 09/27. The longer one, 16/34, is 7,203 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 14 routes through KCIU. Its most frequent links are Minneapolis (MSP), Detroit (DTW) and Grand Rapids (GRR).
