About HLLB

Benina International Airport (BEN) is a large airport in Benina, Libya, with the ICAO code HLLB. Its two runways are 15L/33R and 15R/33L. The longer one, 15L/33R, is 11,732 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 taxiway segments, 21 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 165 routes through HLLB. Its most frequent links are Tripoli (MJI), Tunis (TUN) and HEBA (HEBA).
