About LUBM

Mărculești Air Base (LUBM) is a medium airport in Mărculeşti, Moldova. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,235 ft long and 129 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LUBM.
