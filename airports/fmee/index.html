About FMEE

Roland Garros Airport (RUN) is a large airport in Sainte-Marie, Réunion, with the ICAO code FMEE. Its two runways are 12/30 and 14/32. The longer one, 12/30, is 10,499 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 94 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 224 routes through FMEE. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Plaine Magnien (MRU) and Dzaoudzi (DZA).
