About LEAS

Asturias Airport (OVD) is a large airport in Ranón, Spain, with the ICAO code LEAS. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 817 routes through LEAS. Its most frequent links are Barcelona (BCN), Madrid (MAD) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
