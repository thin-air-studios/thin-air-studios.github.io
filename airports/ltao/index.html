About LTAO

Malatya Tulga Airport (LTAO) is a small airport in Malatya, Türkiye. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 04L/22R and 04R/22L. The longer one, 04L/22R, is 7,874 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LTAO.
