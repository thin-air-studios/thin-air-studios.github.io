About KSGF

Springfield Branson National Airport (SGF) is a medium airport in Springfield, United States, with the ICAO code KSGF. Its two runways are 14/32 and 02/20. The longer one, 14/32, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 46 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 189 routes through KSGF. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Denver (DEN).
