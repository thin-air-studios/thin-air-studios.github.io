About EPDE

Deblin Military Air Base (EPDE) is a medium airport in Dęblin, Poland. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,202 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EPDE.
