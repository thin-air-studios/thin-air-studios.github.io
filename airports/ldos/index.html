About LDOS

Osijek Airport (OSI) is a medium airport in Osijek(Klisa), Croatia, with the ICAO code LDOS. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 23 routes through LDOS. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Velika Gorica (ZAG) and Köln (CGN).
