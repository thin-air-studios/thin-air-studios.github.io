About ZSFZ

Fuzhou Changle International Airport (FOC) is a large airport in Fuzhou (Changle), China, with the ICAO code ZSFZ. Its two runways are 03/21 and 04/22. The longer one, 03/21, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 135 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 849 routes through ZSFZ. Its most frequent links are Chongqing (CKG), Zhengzhou (CGO) and Nanning (NNG).
