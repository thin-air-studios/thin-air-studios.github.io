About KGGW

Glasgow Valley County Airport Wokal Field (GGW) is a medium airport in Glasgow, United States, with the ICAO code KGGW. Its two runways are 12/30 and 08/26. The longer one, 12/30, is 5,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGGW.
