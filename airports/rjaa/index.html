About RJAA

Narita International Airport (NRT) is a large airport in Narita, Japan, with the ICAO code RJAA. Its two runways are 16R/34L and 16L/34R. The longer one, 16R/34L, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 97 gates, 573 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 159 airlines have 2,804 routes through RJAA. Its most frequent links are Seoul (ICN), Hong Kong (HKG) and Shanghai (PVG).
