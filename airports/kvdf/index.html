About KVDF

Tampa Executive Airport (KVDF) is a small airport in Tampa, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 05/23 and 18/36. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 73 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 4 routes through KVDF. Its most frequent links are Tampa (TPA), Fort Lauderdale (FXE) and Gainesville (GNV).
