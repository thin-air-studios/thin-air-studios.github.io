About LAKU

Kukës International Airport (KFZ) is a medium airport in Kukës, Albania, with the ICAO code LAKU. It has one runway, 01/19, 7,211 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through LAKU. Its most frequent links are Bâle / Mulhouse (BSL) and Rheinmünster (FKB).
