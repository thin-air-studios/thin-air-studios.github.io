About LFBG

Cognac-Châteaubernard (BA 709) Air Base (CNG) is a medium airport in Cognac/Châteaubernard, France, with the ICAO code LFBG. Its two runways are 05/23 and 08/26. The longer one, 05/23, is 7,949 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFBG.
