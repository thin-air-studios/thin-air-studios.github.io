About TNCA

Queen Beatrix International Airport (AUA) is a large airport in Oranjestad, Aruba, with the ICAO code TNCA. It has one runway, 11/29, 9,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 37 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 16 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 44 airlines have 258 routes through TNCA. Its most frequent links are Willemstad (CUR), New York (JFK) and Toronto (YYZ).
