About KPRC

Prescott Regional Airport - Ernest A. Love Field (PRC) is a medium airport in Prescott, United States, with the ICAO code KPRC. Its 3 runways are 03R/21L, 12/30 and 03L/21R. The longest, 03R/21L, is 7,619 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 306 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 31 routes through KPRC. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Los Angeles (LAX) and Kingman (IGM).
