About KBED

Laurence G Hanscom Field (BED) is a medium airport in Bedford, United States, with the ICAO code KBED. Its two runways are 11/29 and 05/23. The longer one, 11/29, is 7,011 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 67 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 8 routes through KBED. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Martha's Vineyard (MVY) and Bloomington/Normal (BMI).
