About HEAT

Asyut International Airport (ATZ) is a large airport in Asyut, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEAT. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,905 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 50 routes through HEAT. Its most frequent links are Kuwait City (KWI), Jeddah (JED) and Sharjah (SHJ).
