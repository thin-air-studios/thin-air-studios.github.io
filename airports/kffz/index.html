About KFFZ

Falcon Field (MSC) is a small airport in Mesa, United States, with the ICAO code KFFZ. Its two runways are 04R/22L and 04L/22R. The longer one, 04R/22L, is 5,100 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 202 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFFZ.
