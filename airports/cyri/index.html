About CYRI

Rivière-du-Loup Airport (YRI) is a medium airport in Rivière-du-Loup, Canada, with the ICAO code CYRI. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, CNK has 1 routes through CYRI. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Quebec (YQB).
