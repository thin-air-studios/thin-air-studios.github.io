About RCYU

Hualien Chiashan Airport (HUN) is a large airport in Hualien City, Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCYU. Its two runways are 03/21 and 03R/21L. The longer one, 03/21, is 9,022 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 19 routes through RCYU. Its most frequent links are Taipei (TSA), Kaohsiung (KHH) and Taichung (RMQ).
