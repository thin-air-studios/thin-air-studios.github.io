About VVCM

Cà Mau Airport (CAH) is a medium airport in Ca Mau City, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVCM. It has one runway, 09/27, 4,921 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through VVCM. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Ho Chi Minh City (SGN).
