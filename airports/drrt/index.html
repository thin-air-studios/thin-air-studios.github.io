About DRRT

Tahoua Airport (THZ) is a medium airport in Tahoua, Niger, with the ICAO code DRRT. It has one runway, 06/24, 7,053 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DRRT.
