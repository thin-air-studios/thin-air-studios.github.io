About KCAK

Akron Canton Regional Airport (CAK) is a medium airport in Akron, United States, with the ICAO code KCAK. Its two runways are 05/23 and 01/19. The longer one, 05/23, is 8,204 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 78 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 235 routes through KCAK. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Charlotte (CLT) and New York (LGA).
