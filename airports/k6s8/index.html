About K6S8

Laurel Municipal Airport (K6S8) is a small airport in Laurel, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 14/32 and 09/27. The longest, 04/22, is 5,199 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for K6S8.
