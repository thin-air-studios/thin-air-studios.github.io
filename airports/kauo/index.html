About KAUO

Auburn University Regional Airport (AUO) is a small airport in Auburn, United States, with the ICAO code KAUO. Its two runways are 18/36 and 11/29. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,264 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAUO.
