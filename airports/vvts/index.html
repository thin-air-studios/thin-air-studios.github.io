About VVTS

Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) is a large airport in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVTS. Its two runways are 07R/25L and 07L/25R. The longer one, 07R/25L, is 12,468 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 117 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 111 airlines have 2,637 routes through VVTS. Its most frequent links are Hanoi (HAN), Da Nang (DAD) and Haiphong (HPH).
