About SEQM

Mariscal Sucre International Airport (UIO) is a large airport in Quito, Ecuador, with the ICAO code SEQM. It has one runway, 18/36, 13,445 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 55 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 53 airlines have 506 routes through SEQM. Its most frequent links are Guayaquil (GYE), Miami (MIA) and Bogota (BOG).
