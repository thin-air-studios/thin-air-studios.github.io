About WBKK

Kota Kinabalu International Airport (BKI) is a large airport in Kota Kinabalu, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WBKK. It has one runway, 02/20, 12,402 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 102 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 37 airlines have 626 routes through WBKK. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Seoul (ICN) and Tawau (TWU).
