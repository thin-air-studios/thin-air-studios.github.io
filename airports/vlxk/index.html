About VLXK

Xieng Khouang Airport (XKH) is a small airport in Xieng Khouang, Laos, with the ICAO code VLXK. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,555 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 4 routes through VLXK. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Vientiane (VTE).
