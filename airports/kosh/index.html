About KOSH

Wittman Regional Airport (OSH) is a medium airport in Oshkosh, United States, with the ICAO code KOSH. Its 4 runways are 18/36, 09/27, 05/23 and 13/31. The longest, 18/36, is 8,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 177 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOSH.
