About KMLB

Melbourne Orlando International Airport (MLB) is a medium airport in Melbourne, United States, with the ICAO code KMLB. Its 3 runways are 09R/27L, 09L/27R and 05/23. The longest, 09R/27L, is 10,181 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 105 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 80 routes through KMLB. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Charlotte (CLT) and Birmingham (BHX).
