About ZBYN

Taiyuan Wusu International Airport (TYN) is a large airport in Taiyuan, China, with the ICAO code ZBYN. Its two runways are 13L/31R and 13R/31L. The longer one, 13L/31R, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 118 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 47 airlines have 625 routes through ZBYN. Its most frequent links are Hangzhou (HGH), Chongqing (CKG) and Nanjing (NKG).
