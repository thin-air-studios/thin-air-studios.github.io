About RJOK

Kochi Ryoma Airport (KCZ) is a large airport in Nankoku, Japan, with the ICAO code RJOK. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,203 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 38 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 75 routes through RJOK. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (ITM) and Nagoya (NKM).
