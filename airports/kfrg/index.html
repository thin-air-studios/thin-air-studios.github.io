About KFRG

Republic Airport (FRG) is a medium airport in East Farmingdale, United States, with the ICAO code KFRG. Its two runways are 14/32 and 01/19. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,833 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 146 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 6 routes through KFRG. Its most frequent links are Columbus (CMH), Dulles (IAD) and New York (JFK).
