About VQPR

Paro International Airport (PBH) is a large airport in Paro, Bhutan, with the ICAO code VQPR. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,431 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 46 routes through VQPR. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (BKK), Kolkata (CCU) and Kathmandu (KTM).
