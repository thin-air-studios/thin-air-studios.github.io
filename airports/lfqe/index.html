About LFQE

Lieutenant Étienne Mantoux Air Base (LFQE) is a medium airport in Rouvres-en-Woëvre, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 01/19, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 128 taxiway segments and 59 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFQE.
