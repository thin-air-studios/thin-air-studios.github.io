About SBJP

Presidente Castro Pinto International Airport (JPA) is a large airport in João Pessoa, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBJP. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,251 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 152 routes through SBJP. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Rio De Janeiro (GIG) and Brasília (BSB).
