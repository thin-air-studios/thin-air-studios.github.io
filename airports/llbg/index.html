About LLBG

Ben Gurion International Airport (TLV) is a large airport in Tel Aviv, Israel, with the ICAO code LLBG. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 12/30 and 03/21. The longest, 08/26, is 13,327 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 gates, 207 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 9 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 189 airlines have 3,973 routes through LLBG. Its most frequent links are Larnaca (LCA), Spata-Artemida (ATH) and Otopeni (OTP).
