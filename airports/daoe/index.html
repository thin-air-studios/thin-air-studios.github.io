About DAOE

Bousfer Air Base (DAOE) is a small airport in Bousfer, Algeria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,834 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DAOE.
