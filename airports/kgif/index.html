About KGIF

Winter Haven Regional Airport - Gilbert Field (GIF) is a small airport in Winter Haven, United States, with the ICAO code KGIF. Its two runways are 05/23 and 11/29. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,005 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 159 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGIF.
