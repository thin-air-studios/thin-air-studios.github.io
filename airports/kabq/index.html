About KABQ

Albuquerque International Sunport (ABQ) is a large airport in Albuquerque, United States, with the ICAO code KABQ. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 03/21 and 12/30. The longest, 08/26, is 13,793 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 228 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 394 routes through KABQ. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Los Angeles (LAX) and Phoenix (PHX).
