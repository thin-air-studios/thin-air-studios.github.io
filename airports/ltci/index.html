About LTCI

Van Ferit Melen Airport (VAN) is a medium airport in Van, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTCI. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,022 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 183 routes through LTCI. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Ankara (ESB).
