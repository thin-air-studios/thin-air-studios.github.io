About LTBG

Bandırma Airport (BDM) is a medium airport in Bandırma, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTBG. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,875 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 112 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LTBG.
