About LOXZ

Hinterstoisser Air Base (LOXZ) is a medium airport in Zeltweg, Austria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longer one, 08R/26L, is 9,022 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 92 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LOXZ.
