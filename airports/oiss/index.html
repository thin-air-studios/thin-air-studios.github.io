About OISS

Shiraz Shahid Dastghaib International Airport (SYZ) is a large airport in Shiraz, Iran, with the ICAO code OISS. Its two runways are 11L/29R and 11R/29L. The longer one, 11L/29R, is 14,345 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 220 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 89 routes through OISS. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Kuwait City (KWI) and Muscat/Seeb (MCT).
