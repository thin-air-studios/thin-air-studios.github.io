About OETB

Prince Sultan bin Abdulaziz International Airport (TUU) is a large airport in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OETB. Its two runways are 06/24 and 13/31. The longer one, 06/24, is 10,991 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 112 routes through OETB. Its most frequent links are Jeddah (JED), Riyadh (RUH) and Cairo (CAI).
