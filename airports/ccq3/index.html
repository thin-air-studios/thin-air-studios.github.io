About CCQ3

Debert Airport (CCQ3) is a small airport in Debert, Canada. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 16/34 and 05/23. The longest, 09/27, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CCQ3.
