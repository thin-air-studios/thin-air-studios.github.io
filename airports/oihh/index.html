About OIHH

Hamadan Airport (HDM) is a medium airport in Hamadan, Iran, with the ICAO code OIHH. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,611 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OIHH.
