About KERI

Erie International Tom Ridge Field (ERI) is a medium airport in Erie, United States, with the ICAO code KERI. Its two runways are 06/24 and 02/20. The longer one, 06/24, is 8,420 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 47 routes through KERI. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Charlotte (CLT) and Chicago (ORD).
