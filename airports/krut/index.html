About KRUT

Rutland - Southern Vermont Regional Airport (RUT) is a medium airport in Rutland, United States, with the ICAO code KRUT. Its two runways are 01/19 and 13/31. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,304 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 15 routes through KRUT. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS) and Manchester (MHT).
