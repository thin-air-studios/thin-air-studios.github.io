About HEPS

Port Said International Airport (PSD) is a large airport in Port Said, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEPS. It has one runway, 10/28, 7,707 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HEPS.
