About LEVT

Vitoria Airport (VIT) is a medium airport in Alava, Spain, with the ICAO code LEVT. It has one runway, 04/22, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 47 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 212 routes through LEVT. Its most frequent links are Seville (SVQ), Schkeuditz (LEJ) and Köln (CGN).
