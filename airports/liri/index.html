About LIRI

Salerno Costa d'Amalfi Airport (QSR) is a medium airport in Salerno, Italy, with the ICAO code LIRI. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,437 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 72 routes through LIRI. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), London (LGW) and Caselle Torinese (TRN).
