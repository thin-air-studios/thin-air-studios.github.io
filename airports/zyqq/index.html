About ZYQQ

Qiqihar Sanjiazi Airport (NDG) is a large airport in Qiqihar, China, with the ICAO code ZYQQ. Its two runways are 17L/35R and 17R/35L. The longer one, 17L/35R, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 43 routes through ZYQQ. Its most frequent links are Qingdao (TAO), Beijing (PEK) and Beijing (PKX).
