About LOWW

Vienna International Airport (VIE) is a large airport in Vienna, Austria, with the ICAO code LOWW. Its two runways are 16/34 and 11/29. The longer one, 16/34, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 102 gates, 230 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 14 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 165 airlines have 8,195 routes through LOWW. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and Antalya (AYT).
