About SPUR

PAF Captain Guillermo Concha Iberico International Airport (PIU) is a medium airport in Piura, Peru, with the ICAO code SPUR. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 61 routes through SPUR. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Lima (LIM).
