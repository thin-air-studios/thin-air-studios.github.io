About URMM

Mineralnye Vody Airport (MRV) is a large airport in Mineralnyye Vody, Russia, with the ICAO code URMM. It has one runway, 11/29, 12,795 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 62 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 539 routes through URMM. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Moscow (DME) and Moscow (VKO).
