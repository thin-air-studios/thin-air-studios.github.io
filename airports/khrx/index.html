About KHRX

Hereford Municipal Airport (KHRX) is a small airport in Hereford, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 02/20 and 14/32. The longer one, 02/20, is 6,100 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHRX.
