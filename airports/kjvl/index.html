About KJVL

Southern Wisconsin Regional Airport (JVL) is a small airport in Janesville, United States, with the ICAO code KJVL. Its 3 runways are 14/32, 04/22 and 18/36. The longest, 14/32, is 7,302 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KJVL.
