About VERC

Birsa Munda Airport (IXR) is a medium airport in Ranchi, India, with the ICAO code VERC. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,855 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 38 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 160 routes through VERC. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR) and Hyderabad (HYD).
