About ZUGY

Guiyang Longdongbao International Airport (KWE) is a large airport in Guiyang (Nanming), China, with the ICAO code ZUGY. Its two runways are 01R/19L and 01L/19R. The longer one, 01R/19L, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 244 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 50 airlines have 1,005 routes through ZUGY. Its most frequent links are Hangzhou (HGH), Nanjing (NKG) and Wenzhou (WNZ).
