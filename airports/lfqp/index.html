About LFQP

Phalsbourg-Bourscheid Air Base (LFQP) is a medium airport in Bourscheid, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 06/24, 7,205 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 taxiway segments and 35 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFQP.
