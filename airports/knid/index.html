About KNID

China Lake Naws (Armitage Field) Airport (KNID) is a medium airport in China Lake, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 03/21 and 14/32. The longest, 08/26, is 11,199 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 174 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNID.
