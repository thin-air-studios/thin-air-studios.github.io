About UUOO

Voronezh International Airport (VOZ) is a medium airport in Voronezh, Russia, with the ICAO code UUOO. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,546 ft long and 161 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 12 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 80 routes through UUOO. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Antalya (AYT) and Moscow (SVO).
