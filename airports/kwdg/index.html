About KWDG

Enid Woodring Regional Airport (WDG) is a small airport in Enid, United States, with the ICAO code KWDG. Its two runways are 17/35 and 13/31. The longer one, 17/35, is 8,613 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, MRA has 1 routes through KWDG. Its most frequent links are Tulsa (TUL) and Woodward (WWR).
