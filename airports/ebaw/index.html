About EBAW

Antwerp International Airport (Deurne) (ANR) is a medium airport in Antwerp, Belgium, with the ICAO code EBAW. It has one runway, 11/29, 4,954 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 223 routes through EBAW. Its most frequent links are Málaga (AGP), Alicante (ALC) and London (LCY).
