About LKTB

Brno-Tuřany Airport (BRQ) is a medium airport in Brno, Czechia, with the ICAO code LKTB. Its two runways are 09/27 and 08/26. The longer one, 09/27, is 8,694 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 67 taxiway segments, 28 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 35 airlines have 328 routes through LKTB. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), London (STN) and Mošnov (OSR).
