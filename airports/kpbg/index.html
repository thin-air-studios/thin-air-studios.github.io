About KPBG

Plattsburgh International Airport (PBG) is a medium airport in Plattsburgh, United States, with the ICAO code KPBG. It has one runway, 17/35, 11,759 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 63 routes through KPBG. Its most frequent links are Orlando (SFB), Buffalo (BUF) and Albany (ALB).
