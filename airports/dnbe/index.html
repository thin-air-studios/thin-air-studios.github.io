About DNBE

Benin Airport (BNI) is a medium airport in Benin, Nigeria, with the ICAO code DNBE. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,870 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 17 routes through DNBE. Its most frequent links are Abuja (ABV) and Lagos (LOS).
