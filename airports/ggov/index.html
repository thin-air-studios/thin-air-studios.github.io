About GGOV

Osvaldo Vieira International Airport (OXB) is a large airport in Bissau, Guinea-Bissau, with the ICAO code GGOV. It has one runway, 03/21, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 17 routes through GGOV. Its most frequent links are Lisbon (LIS), Dakar (DSS) and Casablanca (CMN).
