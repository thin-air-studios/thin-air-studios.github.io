About OEAB

Abha International Airport (AHB) is a large airport in Abha, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEAB. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,991 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 149 routes through OEAB. Its most frequent links are Riyadh (RUH), Jeddah (JED) and Ad Dammam (DMM).
