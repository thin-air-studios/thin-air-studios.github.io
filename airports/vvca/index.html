About VVCA

Chu Lai Airport (VCL) is a small airport in Tam Nghĩa, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVCA. It has one runway, 14/32, 10,007 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 61 routes through VVCA. Its most frequent links are Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) and Hanoi (HAN).
