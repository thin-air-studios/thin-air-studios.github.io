About LTAE

Akıncı Air Base (LTAE) is a medium airport in Ankara, Türkiye. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 03/21, 10,991 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 159 taxiway segments and 12 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LTAE.
