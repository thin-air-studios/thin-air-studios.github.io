About KLZU

Gwinnett County Briscoe Field (LZU) is a small airport in Lawrenceville, United States, with the ICAO code KLZU. It has one runway, 07/25, 6,001 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 142 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, LBQ has 10 routes through KLZU. Its most frequent links are Greensboro (GSO), Nashville (KJWN) and Roanoke (ROA).
