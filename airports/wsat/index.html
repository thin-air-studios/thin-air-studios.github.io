About WSAT

Tengah Air Base (TGA) is a medium airport in Western Water Catchment, Singapore, with the ICAO code WSAT. Its 3 runways are 18/36, 01/19 and 18R/36L. The longest, 18/36, is 8,999 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for WSAT.
