About SBCX

Hugo Cantergiani Regional Airport (CXJ) is a medium airport in Caxias Do Sul, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBCX. It has one runway, 15/33, 5,479 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 20 routes through SBCX. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (CGH), São Paulo (GRU) and Campinas (VCP).
