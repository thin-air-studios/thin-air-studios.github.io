About SKTI

Tolemaida Air Base (SKTI) is a small airport in Tolemaida - Nilo, Colombia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 04/22 and 04L/22R. The longer one, 04/22, is 9,280 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 67 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SKTI.
