About LSZH

Zürich Airport (ZRH) is a large airport in Zurich, Switzerland, with the ICAO code LSZH. Its 4 runways are 16/34, 14/32, 10/28 and 01H/19H. The longest, 16/34, is 12,139 ft. Of these, 3 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 105 gates, 250 taxiway segments, 28 aprons and 22 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 140 airlines have 6,857 routes through LSZH. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Madrid (MAD) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
