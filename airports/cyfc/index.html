About CYFC

Fredericton International Airport (YFC) is a medium airport in Fredericton, Canada, with the ICAO code CYFC. Its two runways are 09/27 and 15/33. The longer one, 09/27, is 8,005 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 120 routes through CYFC. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Montréal (YUL) and Ottawa (YOW).
