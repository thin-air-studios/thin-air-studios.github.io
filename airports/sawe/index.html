About SAWE

Gobernador Ramón Trejo Noel International Airport (RGA) is a medium airport in Rio Grande, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAWE. It has one runway, 7/25, 6,562 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Aerolineas has 6 routes through SAWE. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (EZE) and Buenos Aires (AEP).
