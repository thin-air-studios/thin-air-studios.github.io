About OLBA

Beirut Rafic Hariri International Airport (BEY) is a large airport in Beirut, Lebanon, with the ICAO code OLBA. Its 3 runways are 03/21, 16/34 and 17/35. The longest, 03/21, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 118 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 85 airlines have 884 routes through OLBA. Its most frequent links are Doha (DOH), Istanbul (IST) and Dubai (DXB).
