About KMEI

Key Field / Meridian Regional Airport (MEI) is a medium airport in Meridian, United States, with the ICAO code KMEI. Its two runways are 01/19 and 04/22. The longer one, 01/19, is 10,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SkyWest has 47 routes through KMEI. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH) and Moselle (PIB).
