About OASD

Shindand Air Base (OAH) is a small airport in Shindand, Afghanistan, with the ICAO code OASD. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,140 ft long and 160 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 183 taxiway segments and 12 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OASD.
