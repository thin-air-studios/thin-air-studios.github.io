About LDDU

Dubrovnik Ruđer Bošković Airport (DBV) is a large airport in Dubrovnik, Croatia, with the ICAO code LDDU. It has one runway, 11/29, 10,597 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 83 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 82 airlines have 1,374 routes through LDDU. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Paris (ORY) and Manchester (MAN).
