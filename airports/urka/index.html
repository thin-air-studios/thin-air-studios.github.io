About URKA

Anapa Vityazevo Airport (AAQ) is a medium airport in Krasnyi Kurgan, Russia, with the ICAO code URKA. It has one runway, 04/22, 8,202 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 64 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 264 routes through URKA. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (VKO) and Moscow (SVO).
