About LFPK

Coulommiers-Voisins Aerodrome (LFPK) is a small airport in Pommeuse, Seine-et-Marne, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 4 runways are 09C/27C, 09/27, 09R/27L and 09L/27R. The longest, 09C/27C, is 5,217 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 67 taxiway segments and 17 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFPK.
