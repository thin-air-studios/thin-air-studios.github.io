About CYTN

Trenton Airport (CYTN) is a small airport in Trenton, Canada. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 06/24 and 10/28. The longer one, 06/24, is 5,377 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYTN.
