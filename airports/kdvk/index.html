About KDVK

Stuart Powell Field (KDVK) is a small airport in Danville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 13/31 and 01/19. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDVK.
