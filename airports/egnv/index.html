About EGNV

Teesside International Airport (MME) is a medium airport in Darlington, Durham, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGNV. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,516 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 12 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 184 routes through EGNV. Its most frequent links are Aberdeen (ABZ), Amsterdam (AMS) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
