About OEAH

Al-Ahsa International Airport (HOF) is a large airport in Hofuf, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEAH. It has one runway, 16/34, 10,039 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 15 routes through OEAH. Its most frequent links are Jeddah (JED), Dubai (DXB) and Medina (MED).
