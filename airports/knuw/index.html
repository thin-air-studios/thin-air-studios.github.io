About KNUW

Whidbey Island Naval Air Station (Ault Field) (NUW) is a medium airport in Oak Harbor, United States, with the ICAO code KNUW. Its two runways are 14/32 and 07/25. The longer one, 14/32, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 76 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 1 routes through KNUW. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Frankfurt am Main (HHN).
