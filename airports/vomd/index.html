About VOMD

Madurai Airport (IXM) is a medium airport in Madurai, India, with the ICAO code VOMD. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,990 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 159 routes through VOMD. Its most frequent links are Chennai (MAA), Bengaluru (BLR) and New Delhi (DEL).
