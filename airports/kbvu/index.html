About KBVU

Boulder City Municipal Airport (BLD) is a medium airport in Boulder City, United States, with the ICAO code KBVU. Its two runways are 09/27 and 15/33. The longer one, 09/27, is 5,103 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBVU.
