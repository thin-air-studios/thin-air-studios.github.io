About CYYZ

Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) is a large airport in Toronto, Canada, with the ICAO code CYYZ. It is number 49 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 47.3 million passengers in 2025. It has 5 runways, from 9,000 ft to 11,120 ft long. The longest is 05/23. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 152 gates, 401 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 137 airlines have 5,775 routes through CYYZ. Its most frequent links are Montréal (YUL), New York (LGA) and Vancouver (YVR).
