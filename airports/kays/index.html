About KAYS

Waycross Ware County Airport (AYS) is a small airport in Waycross, United States, with the ICAO code KAYS. Its 3 runways are 01/19, 05/23 and 13/31. The longest, 01/19, is 5,992 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, OHY has 1 routes through KAYS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Istanbul (ISL).
