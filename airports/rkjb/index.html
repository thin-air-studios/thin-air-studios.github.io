About RKJB

Muan International Airport (MWX) is a large airport in Muan (Piseo-ri), South Korea, with the ICAO code RKJB. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,186 ft long, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 151 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 89 routes through RKJB. Its most frequent links are Da Nang (DAD), Nha Trang/nha Trang aiurportCam Ranh (CXR) and Taoyuan (TPE).
