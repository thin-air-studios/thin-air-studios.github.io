About GOBD

Blaise Diagne International Airport (DSS) is a large airport in Dakar, Senegal, with the ICAO code GOBD. It has one runway, 01/19, 11,483 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 72 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 312 routes through GOBD. Its most frequent links are Zaventem (BRU), Istanbul (IST) and Bamako (BKO).
