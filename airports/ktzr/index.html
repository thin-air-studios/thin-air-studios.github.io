About KTZR

Bolton Field (KTZR) is a small airport in Columbus, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 04/22, 5,500 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTZR.
