About KLWM

Lawrence Municipal Airport (LWM) is a medium airport in Lawrence, United States, with the ICAO code KLWM. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 55 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLWM.
