About LPBG

Bragança Airport (BGC) is a medium airport in Bragança, Portugal, with the ICAO code LPBG. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,600 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, RVP has 4 routes through LPBG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Vila Real (VRL).
