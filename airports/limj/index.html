About LIMJ

Genoa Cristoforo Colombo Airport (GOA) is a large airport in Genova (GE), Italy, with the ICAO code LIMJ. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,564 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 43 airlines have 477 routes through LIMJ. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Napoli (NAP) and Munich (MUC).
