About CYRT

Rankin Inlet Airport (YRT) is a medium airport in Rankin Inlet, Canada, with the ICAO code CYRT. It has one runway, 13T/31T, 6,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 21 routes through CYRT. Its most frequent links are Winnipeg (YWG), Val-d'Or (YVO) and Iqaluit (YFB).
