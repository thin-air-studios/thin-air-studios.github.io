About OIKB

Bandar Abbas International Airport (BND) is a large airport in Bandar Abbas, Iran, with the ICAO code OIKB. Its two runways are 03R/21L and 03L/21R. The longer one, 03R/21L, is 12,008 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 186 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 71 routes through OIKB. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Dubai (DXB) and Kish Island (KIH).
