About SVPR

General Manuel Carlos Piar International Airport (PZO) is a large airport in Guyana City, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVPR. It has one runway, 07/25, 6,726 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 131 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 61 routes through SVPR. Its most frequent links are Maiquetía (CCS), Isla Margarita (PMV) and Barcelona (BLA).
