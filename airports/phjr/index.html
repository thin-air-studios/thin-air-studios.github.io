About PHJR

Kalaeloa Airport (JRF) is a medium airport in Kapolei, United States, with the ICAO code PHJR. Its 3 runways are 11/29, 04R/22L and 04L/22R. The longest, 11/29, is 8,365 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for PHJR.
