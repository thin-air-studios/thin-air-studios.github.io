About UBBB

Heydar Aliyev International Airport (GYD) is a large airport in Baku, Azerbaijan, with the ICAO code UBBB. Its two runways are 16/34 and 17/35. The longer one, 16/34, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 177 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 13 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 95 airlines have 1,347 routes through UBBB. Its most frequent links are Luxembourg (LUX), Frankfurt am Main (HHN) and Istanbul (IST).
