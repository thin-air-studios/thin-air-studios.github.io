About KRKW

Rockwood Municipal Airport (RKW) is a small airport in Rockwood, United States, with the ICAO code KRKW. It has one runway, 04/22, 5,000 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRKW.
