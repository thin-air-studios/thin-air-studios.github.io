About SLLP

El Alto International Airport (LPB) is a large airport in La Paz / El Alto, Bolivia, with the ICAO code SLLP. Its two runways are 10/28 and 10L/28R. The longer one, 10/28, is 13,123 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 40 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 109 routes through SLLP. Its most frequent links are Santa Cruz (VVI), Cochabamba (CBB) and Lima (LIM).
