About LFBO

Toulouse-Blagnac Airport (TLS) is a large airport in Toulouse/Blagnac, France, with the ICAO code LFBO. Its two runways are 14R/32L and 14L/32R. The longer one, 14R/32L, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 gates, 259 taxiway segments, 43 aprons and 16 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 104 airlines have 3,981 routes through LFBO. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS).
