About FBTL

Tuli Lodge Airport (TLD) is a small airport in Tuli Lodge, Botswana, with the ICAO code FBTL. It has one runway, 12/30, 4,938 ft long, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FBTL.
