About ETAD

Spangdahlem Air Base (SPM) is a medium airport in Trier, Germany, with the ICAO code ETAD. It has one runway, 05/23, 10,007 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 121 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETAD.
