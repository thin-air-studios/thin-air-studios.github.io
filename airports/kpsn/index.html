About KPSN

Palestine Municipal Airport (PSN) is a small airport in Palestine, United States, with the ICAO code KPSN. Its two runways are 18/36 and 09/27. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,005 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPSN.
