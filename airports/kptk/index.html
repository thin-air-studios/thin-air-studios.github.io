About KPTK

Oakland County International Airport (PTK) is a medium airport in Pontiac, United States, with the ICAO code KPTK. Its 3 runways are 09R/27L, 09L/27R and 18/36. The longest, 09R/27L, is 6,521 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 202 taxiway segments, 68 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, CTL has 3 routes through KPTK. Its most frequent links are Jackson (JXN), Chicago/West Chicago (DPA) and Rochester (RST).
