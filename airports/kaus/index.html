About KAUS

Austin Bergstrom International Airport (AUS) is a large airport in Austin, United States, with the ICAO code KAUS. Its two runways are 18R/36L and 18L/36R. The longer one, 18R/36L, is 12,250 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 gates, 174 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 56 airlines have 1,134 routes through KAUS. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Houston (IAH) and Chicago (ORD).
