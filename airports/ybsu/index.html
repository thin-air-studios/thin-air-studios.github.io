About YBSU

Sunshine Coast Airport (MCY) is a large airport in Maroochydore, Australia, with the ICAO code YBSU. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 171 routes through YBSU. Its most frequent links are Melbourne (MEL), Sydney (SYD) and Cairns (CNS).
