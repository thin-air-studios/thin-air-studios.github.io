About LIBV

Gioia del Colle Antonio Ramirez Air Base (LIBV) is a medium airport in Gioia del Colle (BA), Italy. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 14R/32L and 14L/32R. The longer one, 14R/32L, is 9,846 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIBV.
