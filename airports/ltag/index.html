About LTAG

İncirlik Air Base (UAB) is a medium airport in Sarıçam, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAG. It has one runway, 05/23, 10,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 268 taxiway segments and 26 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through LTAG. Its most frequent links are Dover (DOV), Frankfurt am Main (HHN) and Grâce-Hollogne (LGG).
