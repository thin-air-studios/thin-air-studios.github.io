About FNLU

Quatro de Fevereiro International Airport (LAD) is a large airport in Luanda, Angola, with the ICAO code FNLU. Its two runways are 05/23 and 07/25. The longer one, 05/23, is 12,190 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 24 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 95 routes through FNLU. Its most frequent links are Lisbon (LIS), Paris (CDG) and Kinshasa (FIH).
