About USSS

Koltsovo Airport (SVX) is a large airport in Yekaterinburg, Russia, with the ICAO code USSS. Its two runways are 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longer one, 08R/26L, is 9,925 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 242 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 71 airlines have 1,132 routes through USSS. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Moscow (SVO) and Novosibirsk (OVB).
