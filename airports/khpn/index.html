About KHPN

Westchester County Airport (HPN) is a medium airport in White Plains, United States, with the ICAO code KHPN. Its two runways are 16/34 and 11/29. The longer one, 16/34, is 6,549 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 49 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 309 routes through KHPN. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), West Palm Beach (DJT) and Atlanta (ATL).
