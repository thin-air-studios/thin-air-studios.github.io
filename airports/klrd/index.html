About KLRD

Laredo International Airport (LRD) is a medium airport in Laredo, United States, with the ICAO code KLRD. Its 3 runways are 18R/36L, 18L/36R and 14/32. The longest, 18R/36L, is 8,743 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 74 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 56 routes through KLRD. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Las Vegas (LAS).
