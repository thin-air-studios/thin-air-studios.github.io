About MMOX

Xoxocotlán International Airport (OAX) is a large airport in Oaxaca, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMOX. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,038 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 163 routes through MMOX. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Mexico City (NLU) and Tijuana (TIJ).
