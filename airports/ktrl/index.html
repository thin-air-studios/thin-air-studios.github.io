About KTRL

Terrell Municipal Airport (TRL) is a small airport in Terrell, United States, with the ICAO code KTRL. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,006 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTRL.
