About LICT

Vincenzo Florio Airport Trapani-Birgi (TPS) is a medium airport in Trapani (TP), Italy, with the ICAO code LICT. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,852 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 49 taxiway segments, 21 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 390 routes through LICT. Its most frequent links are Orio al Serio (BGY), Bologna (BLQ) and Caselle Torinese (TRN).
