About SARE

Resistencia International Airport (RES) is a large airport in Resistencia, Argentina, with the ICAO code SARE. It has one runway, 3/21, 9,088 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 13 routes through SARE. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Buenos Aires (AEP).
