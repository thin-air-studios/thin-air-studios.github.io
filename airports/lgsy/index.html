About LGSY

Skiros Airport (SKU) is a small airport in Skiros Island, Greece, with the ICAO code LGSY. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,849 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 12 routes through LGSY. Its most frequent links are Thessaloniki (SKG), Paris (CDG) and Spata-Artemida (ATH).
