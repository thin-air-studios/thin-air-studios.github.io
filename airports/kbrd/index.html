About KBRD

Brainerd Lakes Regional Airport (BRD) is a medium airport in Brainerd, United States, with the ICAO code KBRD. Its 3 runways are 16/34, 05/23 and 12/30. The longest, 16/34, is 7,100 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 10 routes through KBRD. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Minneapolis (MSP).
