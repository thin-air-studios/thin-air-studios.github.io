About KOWB

Owensboro Daviess County Airport (OWB) is a medium airport in Owensboro, United States, with the ICAO code KOWB. Its two runways are 18/36 and 06/24. The longer one, 18/36, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, VTE has 6 routes through KOWB. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD) and Charlotte (CLT).
