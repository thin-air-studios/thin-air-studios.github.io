About HTDA

Julius Nyerere International Airport (DAR) is a large airport in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, with the ICAO code HTDA. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 69 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 265 routes through HTDA. Its most frequent links are Zanzibar (ZNZ), Nairobi (NBO) and Arusha (JRO).
