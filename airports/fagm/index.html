About FAGM

Rand Airport (QRA) is a medium airport in Johannesburg, South Africa, with the ICAO code FAGM. Its two runways are 11/29 and 17/35. The longer one, 11/29, is 5,617 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 131 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FAGM.
