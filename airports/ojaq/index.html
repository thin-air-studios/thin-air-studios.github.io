About OJAQ

King Hussein International Airport (AQJ) is a large airport in Aqaba, Jordan, with the ICAO code OJAQ. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,855 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 97 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 102 routes through OJAQ. Its most frequent links are Amman (AMM), Zaventem (BRU) and Budapest (BUD).
