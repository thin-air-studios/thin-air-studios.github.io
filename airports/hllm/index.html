About HLLM

Mitiga International Airport (MJI) is a large airport in Tripoli, Libya, with the ICAO code HLLM. It has one runway, 10/28, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 126 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 231 routes through HLLM. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Benina (BEN) and Tunis (TUN).
