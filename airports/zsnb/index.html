About ZSNB

Ningbo Lishe International Airport (NGB) is a large airport in Ningbo, China, with the ICAO code ZSNB. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 gates, 88 taxiway segments and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 71 airlines have 1,028 routes through ZSNB. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Chongqing (CKG) and Guiyang (KWE).
