About EBLG

Liège Airport (LGG) is a medium airport in Grâce-Hollogne, Belgium, with the ICAO code EBLG. Its two runways are 04R/22L and 04L/22R. The longer one, 04R/22L, is 12,106 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 61 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 106 airlines have 2,132 routes through EBLG. Its most frequent links are Tel Aviv (TLV), New York (JFK) and Doha (DOH).
