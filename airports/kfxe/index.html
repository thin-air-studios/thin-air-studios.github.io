About KFXE

Fort Lauderdale Executive Airport (FXE) is a medium airport in Fort Lauderdale, United States, with the ICAO code KFXE. Its two runways are 09/27 and 13/31. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments and 20 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 7 routes through KFXE. Its most frequent links are White Plains (HPN), Teterboro (TEB) and Quebec (YQB).
