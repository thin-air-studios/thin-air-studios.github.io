About OEDF

King Fahd International Airport (DMM) is a large airport in Ad Dammam, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEDF. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 13,124 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 105 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 831 routes through OEDF. Its most frequent links are Jeddah (JED), Cairo (CAI) and Riyadh (RUH).
