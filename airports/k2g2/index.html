About K2G2

Jefferson County Airpark (K2G2) is a small airport in Steubenville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,001 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for K2G2.
