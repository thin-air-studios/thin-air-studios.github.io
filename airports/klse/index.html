About KLSE

La Crosse Regional Airport (LSE) is a medium airport in La Crosse, United States, with the ICAO code KLSE. Its 3 runways are 18/36, 13/31 and 04/22. The longest, 18/36, is 8,742 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 45 routes through KLSE. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Minneapolis (MSP) and Mesa (AZA).
