About KCOS

City of Colorado Springs Municipal Airport (COS) is a large airport in Colorado Springs, United States, with the ICAO code KCOS. Its 3 runways are 17L/35R, 17R/35L and 12/30. The longest, 17L/35R, is 13,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 239 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 241 routes through KCOS. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Chicago (ORD) and Houston (IAH).
