About KAIK

Aiken Regional Airport (AIK) is a small airport in Aiken, United States, with the ICAO code KAIK. Its two runways are 07/25 and 01/19. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAIK.
