About KLVM

Mission Field (LVM) is a medium airport in Livingston, United States, with the ICAO code KLVM. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 08/26 and 13/31. The longest, 04/22, is 5,701 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLVM.
