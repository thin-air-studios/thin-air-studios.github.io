About SLTJ

Capitan Oriel Lea Plaza Airport (TJA) is a medium airport in Tarija, Bolivia, with the ICAO code SLTJ. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,007 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 16 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BOV has 8 routes through SLTJ. Its most frequent links are Cochabamba (CBB), Santa Cruz (VVI) and La Paz / El Alto (LPB).
