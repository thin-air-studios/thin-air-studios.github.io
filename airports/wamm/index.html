About WAMM

Sam Ratulangi International Airport (MDC) is a large airport in Manado, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAMM. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,693 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 98 routes through WAMM. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Makassar (UPG) and Guangzhou (CAN).
