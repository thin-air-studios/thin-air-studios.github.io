About EDDS

Stuttgart Airport (STR) is a large airport in Stuttgart, Germany, with the ICAO code EDDS. It has one runway, 07/25, 10,974 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 102 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 107 airlines have 3,979 routes through EDDS. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Hamburg (HAM) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
