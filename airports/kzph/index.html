About KZPH

Zephyrhills Municipal Airport (ZPH) is a small airport in Zephyrhills, United States, with the ICAO code KZPH. Its two runways are 19/1 and 5/23. The longer one, 19/1, is 5,072 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 55 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KZPH.
