About LFML

Marseille Provence Airport (MRS) is a large airport in Marignane, Bouches-du-Rhône, France, with the ICAO code LFML. Its two runways are 13L/31R and 13R/31L. The longer one, 13L/31R, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 gates, 127 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 86 airlines have 3,715 routes through LFML. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Nantes (NTE).
