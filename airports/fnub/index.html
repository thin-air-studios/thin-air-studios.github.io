About FNUB

Lubango Mukanka International Airport (SDD) is a medium airport in Lubango, Angola, with the ICAO code FNUB. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,335 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 77 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, DTA has 4 routes through FNUB. Its most frequent links are Luanda (LAD) and Luanda (NBJ).
