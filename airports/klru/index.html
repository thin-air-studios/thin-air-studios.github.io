About KLRU

Las Cruces International Airport (LRU) is a medium airport in Las Cruces, United States, with the ICAO code KLRU. Its 3 runways are 12/30, 04/22 and 08/26. The longest, 12/30, is 7,506 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLRU.
