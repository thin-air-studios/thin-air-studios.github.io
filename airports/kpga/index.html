About KPGA

Page Municipal Airport (PGA) is a medium airport in Page, United States, with the ICAO code KPGA. Its two runways are 15/33 and 07/25. The longer one, 15/33, is 5,950 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, VTE has 7 routes through KPGA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Phoenix (PHX).
