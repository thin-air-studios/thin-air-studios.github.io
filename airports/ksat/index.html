About KSAT

San Antonio International Airport (SAT) is a large airport in San Antonio, United States, with the ICAO code KSAT. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 13R/31L and 13L/31R. The longest, 04/22, is 8,505 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 gates, 249 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 46 airlines have 661 routes through KSAT. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Denver (DEN) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
