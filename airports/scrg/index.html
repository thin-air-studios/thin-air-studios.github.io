About SCRG

La Independencia Airport (SCRG) is a medium airport in Rancagua, Chile. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,258 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SCRG.
