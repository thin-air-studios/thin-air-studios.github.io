About FMCZ

Dzaoudzi Pamandzi International Airport (DZA) is a large airport in Dzaoudzi, Mayotte, with the ICAO code FMCZ. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,330 ft long and 92 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 78 routes through FMCZ. Its most frequent links are Sainte-Marie (RUN), Moroni (HAH) and Nairobi (NBO).
