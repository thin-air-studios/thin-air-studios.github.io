About KFPY

Perry-Foley Airport (FPY) is a small airport in Perry, United States, with the ICAO code KFPY. Its two runways are 18/36 and 12/30. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,013 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFPY.
