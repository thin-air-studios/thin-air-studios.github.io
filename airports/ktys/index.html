About KTYS

McGhee Tyson Airport (TYS) is a large airport in Knoxville/Maryville, United States, with the ICAO code KTYS. Its two runways are 05L/23R and 05R/23L. The longer one, 05L/23R, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 97 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 472 routes through KTYS. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Dulles (IAD) and Charlotte (CLT).
