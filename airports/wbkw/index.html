About WBKW

Tawau Airport (TWU) is a medium airport in Tawau, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WBKW. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,800 ft long and 155 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 101 routes through WBKW. Its most frequent links are Kota Kinabalu (BKI), Sepang (KUL) and Nossa Senhora do Carmo (MFM).
