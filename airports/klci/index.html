About KLCI

Laconia Municipal Airport (LCI) is a small airport in Laconia / Gilford, United States, with the ICAO code KLCI. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,890 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLCI.
