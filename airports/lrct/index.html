About LRCT

Câmpia Turzii Air Base (LRCT) is a medium airport in Câmpia Turzii, Romania. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 8,202 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LRCT.
