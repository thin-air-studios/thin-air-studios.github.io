About RPMP

Pagadian Airport (PAG) is a medium airport in Pagadian, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPMP. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,574 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 20 routes through RPMP. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL), Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB) and Dumaguete City (DGT).
