About ZHEC

Ezhou Huahu International Airport (EHU) is a large airport in Ezhou, China, with the ICAO code ZHEC. Its two runways are 01L/19R and 01R/19L. The longer one, 01L/19R, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 319 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 425 routes through ZHEC. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), Seoul (ICN) and New Delhi (DEL).
