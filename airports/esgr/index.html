About ESGR

Skövde Airport (KVB) is a medium airport in Skövde, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESGR. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,696 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through ESGR. Its most frequent links are Visby (VBY) and Burgas (BOJ).
