About MDSD

Las Américas International Airport (SDQ) is a large airport in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, with the ICAO code MDSD. Its two runways are 17/35 and 18/36. The longer one, 17/35, is 11,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 85 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 590 routes through MDSD. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), New York (JFK) and San Juan (SJU).
