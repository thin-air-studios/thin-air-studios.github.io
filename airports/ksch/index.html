About KSCH

Schenectady County Airport (SCH) is a medium airport in Schenectady, United States, with the ICAO code KSCH. Its two runways are 04/22 and 10/28. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSCH.
