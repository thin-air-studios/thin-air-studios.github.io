About LFSO

Nancy-Ochey (BA 133) Air Base (LFSO) is a medium airport in Thuilley-aux-Groseilles, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,878 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 255 taxiway segments and 53 aprons.
