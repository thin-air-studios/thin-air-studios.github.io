About KDAA

Davison Army Air Field (DAA) is a medium airport in Fort Belvoir, United States, with the ICAO code KDAA. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,421 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDAA.
