About KPNS

Pensacola International Airport (PNS) is a large airport in Pensacola, United States, with the ICAO code KPNS. Its two runways are 17/35 and 08/26. The longer one, 17/35, is 7,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 135 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 203 routes through KPNS. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Atlanta (ATL) and Orlando (MCO).
