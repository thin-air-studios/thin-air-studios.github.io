About DAAD

Bou Saada Airport (BUJ) is a medium airport in Ouled Sidi Brahim, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAAD. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,218 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 155 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DAAD.
