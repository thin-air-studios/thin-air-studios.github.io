About EGNO

Warton Aerodrome (WRT) is a medium airport in Warton, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGNO. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,946 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 14 routes through EGNO. Its most frequent links are Munich (MUC), Farnborough (FAB) and Lincoln (QCY).
