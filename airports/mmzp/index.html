About MMZP

Zapopan Air Force Base (MMZP) is a small airport in Zapopan, Mexico. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longer one, 08R/26L, is 8,860 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MMZP.
