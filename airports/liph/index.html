About LIPH

Treviso Airport (TSF) is a large airport in Treviso (TV), Italy, with the ICAO code LIPH. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,941 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 16 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 884 routes through LIPH. Its most frequent links are Otopeni (OTP), Rinas (TIA) and Charleroi (CRL).
