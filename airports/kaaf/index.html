About KAAF

Apalachicola Regional Airport (AAF) is a small airport in Apalachicola, United States, with the ICAO code KAAF. Its 3 runways are 14/32, 06/24 and 18/36. The longest, 14/32, is 5,425 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAAF.
