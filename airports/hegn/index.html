About HEGN

Hurghada International Airport (HRG) is a large airport in Hurghada, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEGN. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 13,171 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 105 taxiway segments, 30 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 153 airlines have 3,480 routes through HEGN. Its most frequent links are Düsseldorf (DUS), Amsterdam (AMS) and Berlin (BER).
