About EDLW

Dortmund Airport (DTM) is a large airport in Dortmund, Germany, with the ICAO code EDLW. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 20 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 32 airlines have 1,230 routes through EDLW. Its most frequent links are Katowice (KTW), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Munich (MUC).
