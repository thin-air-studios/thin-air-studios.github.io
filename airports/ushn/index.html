About USHN

Nyagan Airport (NYA) is a medium airport in Nyagan, Russia, with the ICAO code USHN. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,307 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for USHN.
