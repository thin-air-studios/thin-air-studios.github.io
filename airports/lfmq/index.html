About LFMQ

Le Castellet Airport (CTT) is a medium airport in Le Castellet, Var, France, with the ICAO code LFMQ. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,069 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFMQ.
