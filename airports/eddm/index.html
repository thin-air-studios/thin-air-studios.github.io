About EDDM

Munich Airport (MUC) is a large airport in Munich, Germany, with the ICAO code EDDM. Its two runways are 08L/26R and 08R/26L. The longer one, 08L/26R, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 165 gates, 606 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 211 airlines have 8,679 routes through EDDM. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Düsseldorf (DUS) and Antalya (AYT).
