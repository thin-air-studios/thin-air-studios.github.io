About SBBG

Comandante Gustavo Kraemer Airport (BGX) is a medium airport in Bagé, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBBG. Its two runways are 6/24 and 14/32. The longer one, 6/24, is 4,921 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through SBBG. Its most frequent links are Geneva (GVA) and Porto Alegre (POA).
