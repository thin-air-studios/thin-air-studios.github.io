About EGPO

Stornoway Airport (SYY) is a medium airport in Stornoway, Western Isles, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGPO. Its two runways are 18/36 and 06/24. The longer one, 18/36, is 7,218 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 47 routes through EGPO. Its most frequent links are Glasgow (GLA), Inverness (INV) and Ingliston (EDI).
