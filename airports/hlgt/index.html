About HLGT

Ghat Airport (GHT) is a medium airport in Ghat, Libya, with the ICAO code HLGT. Its two runways are 17/35 and 06/24. The longer one, 17/35, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HLGT.
