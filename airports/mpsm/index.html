About MPSM

Scarlett Martinez International Airport (RIH) is a small airport in Río Hato, Panama, with the ICAO code MPSM. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,038 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 26 routes through MPSM. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Montréal (YUL) and Quebec (YQB).
