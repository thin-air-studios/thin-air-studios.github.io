About VTUU

Ubon Ratchathani Airport (UBP) is a medium airport in Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTUU. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,848 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 59 routes through VTUU. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (DMK), Bangkok (BKK) and Chiang Mai (CNX).
