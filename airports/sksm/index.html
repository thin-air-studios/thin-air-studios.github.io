About SKSM

Simón Bolívar International Airport (SMR) is a medium airport in Santa Marta, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKSM. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,577 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 168 routes through SKSM. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Medellín (MDE) and Cali (CLO).
