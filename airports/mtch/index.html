About MTCH

Cap Haitien International Airport (CAP) is a large airport in Cap Haitien, Haiti, with the ICAO code MTCH. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,701 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 20 routes through MTCH. Its most frequent links are Providenciales (PLS), Miami (MIA) and Tocumen (PTY).
