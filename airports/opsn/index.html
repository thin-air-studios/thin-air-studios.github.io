About OPSN

Sehwan Sharif Airport (SYW) is a medium airport in Sehwan Sharif, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPSN. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,562 ft long, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OPSN.
