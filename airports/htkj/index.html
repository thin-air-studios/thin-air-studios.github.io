About HTKJ

Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) is a large airport in Arusha, Tanzania, with the ICAO code HTKJ. It has one runway, 09/27, 11,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 68 routes through HTKJ. Its most frequent links are Dar es Salaam (DAR), Zanzibar (ZNZ) and Istanbul (IST).
