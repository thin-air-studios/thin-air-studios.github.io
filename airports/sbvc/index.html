About SBVC

Glauber de Andrade Rocha Airport (VDC) is a medium airport in Vitória da Conquista, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBVC. It has one runway, 15/33, 6,890 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SBVC.
