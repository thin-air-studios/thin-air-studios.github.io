About UBBN

Nakhchivan International Airport (NAJ) is a large airport in Nakhchivan, Azerbaijan, with the ICAO code UBBN. Its two runways are 14L/32R and 14R/32L. The longer one, 14L/32R, is 10,826 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 42 routes through UBBN. Its most frequent links are Baku (GYD), Moscow (VKO) and Ganja (GNJ).
