About CYRQ

Trois-Rivières Airport (YRQ) is a medium airport in Trois-Rivières, Canada, with the ICAO code CYRQ. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,006 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 5 routes through CYRQ. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Montréal (YUL) and Zaventem (BRU).
