About KODO

Odessa Schlemeyer Field (ODT) is a small airport in Odessa, United States, with the ICAO code KODO. Its 3 runways are 11/29, 02/20 and 16/34. The longest, 11/29, is 6,200 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KODO.
