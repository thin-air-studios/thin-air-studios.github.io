About NZWN

Wellington International Airport (WLG) is a large airport in Wellington, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZWN. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,352 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 gates, 81 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 1,306 routes through NZWN. Its most frequent links are Christchurch (CHC), Auckland (AKL) and Nelson (NSN).
