About KDOV

Dover Civil Air Terminal/Dover Air Force Base (DOV) is a medium airport in Dover, United States, with the ICAO code KDOV. Its two runways are 14/32 and 01/19. The longer one, 14/32, is 12,903 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 135 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 97 routes through KDOV. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (HHN), Köln (CGN) and Ramstein-Miesenbach (RMS).
