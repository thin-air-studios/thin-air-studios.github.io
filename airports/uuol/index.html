About UUOL

Lipetsk Airport (LPK) is a medium airport in Lipetsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UUOL. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,546 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 15 routes through UUOL. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Sochi (AER) and Yekaterinburg (SVX).
