About LGLM

Limnos Airport (LXS) is a small airport in Limnos Island, Greece, with the ICAO code LGLM. It has one runway, 04R/22L, 9,895 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 44 routes through LGLM. Its most frequent links are Spata-Artemida (ATH), Prague (PRG) and London (LGW).
