About ESSL

Linköping City Airport (LPI) is a large airport in Linköping, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESSL. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,004 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 27 routes through ESSL. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Copenhagen (CPH) and Örebro (ORB).
