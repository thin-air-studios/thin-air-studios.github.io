About UT1E

Gazadzhak Airport (UT1E) is a small airport in Gazadzhak, Turkmenistan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,576 ft long and 120 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UT1E.
