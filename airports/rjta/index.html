About RJTA

JMSDF Atsugi Air Base / Naval Air Facility Atsugi (NJA) is a medium airport in Ayase / Yamato, Japan, with the ICAO code RJTA. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RJTA.
