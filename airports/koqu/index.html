About KOQU

Quonset State Airport (NCO) is a medium airport in North Kingstown, United States, with the ICAO code KOQU. Its two runways are 16/34 and 05/23. The longer one, 16/34, is 7,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 70 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOQU.
