About SABE

Aeroparque Jorge Newbery (AEP) is a large airport in Buenos Aires, Argentina, with the ICAO code SABE. It has one runway, 13/31, 7,710 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 55 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 1,367 routes through SABE. Its most frequent links are Cordoba (COR), Mendoza (MDZ) and San Carlos de Bariloche (BRC).
