About KFEP

Albertus Airport (FEP) is a small airport in Freeport, United States, with the ICAO code KFEP. Its 3 runways are 06/24, 18/36 and 13/31. The longest, 06/24, is 5,504 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFEP.
