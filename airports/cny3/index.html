About CNY3

Collingwood Regional Airport (CNY3) is a small airport in Collingwood, Canada. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 13/31 and 01/19. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CNY3.
