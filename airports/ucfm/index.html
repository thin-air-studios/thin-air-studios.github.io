About UCFM

Manas International Airport (BSZ) is a large airport in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, with the ICAO code UCFM. It has one runway, 07/25, 13,780 ft long and 180 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 302 routes through UCFM. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Osh (OSS) and Moscow (DME).
