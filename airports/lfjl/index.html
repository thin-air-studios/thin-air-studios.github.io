About LFJL

Metz-Nancy-Lorraine Airport (ETZ) is a medium airport in Goin, France, with the ICAO code LFJL. It has one runway, 04/22, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 77 routes through LFJL. Its most frequent links are Casablanca (CMN), Algiers (ALG) and Toulouse/Blagnac (TLS).
