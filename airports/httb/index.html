About HTTB

Tabora Airport (TBO) is a small airport in Tabora, Tanzania, with the ICAO code HTTB. Its two runways are 13/31 and 08/26. The longer one, 13/31, is 6,234 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, ATC has 1 routes through HTTB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Dar es Salaam (DAR).
