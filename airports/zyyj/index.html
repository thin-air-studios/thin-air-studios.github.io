About ZYYJ

Yanji Chaoyangchuan Airport (YNJ) is a medium airport in Yanji, China, with the ICAO code ZYYJ. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 38 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 104 routes through ZYYJ. Its most frequent links are Seoul (ICN), Qingdao (TAO) and Beijing (PKX).
