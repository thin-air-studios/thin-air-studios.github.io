About KAGS

Augusta Regional At Bush Field (AGS) is a medium airport in Augusta, United States, with the ICAO code KAGS. Its two runways are 17/35 and 08/26. The longer one, 17/35, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 65 routes through KAGS. Its most frequent links are Atlanta (ATL), Charlotte (CLT) and Washington (DCA).
