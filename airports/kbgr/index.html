About KBGR

Bangor International Airport (BGR) is a medium airport in Bangor, United States, with the ICAO code KBGR. It has one runway, 15/33, 11,440 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 114 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 40 airlines have 161 routes through KBGR. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), New York (LGA) and Orlando (SFB).
