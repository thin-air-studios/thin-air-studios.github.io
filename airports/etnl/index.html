About ETNL

Rostock-Laage Airport (RLG) is a medium airport in Laage, Germany, with the ICAO code ETNL. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 178 routes through ETNL. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Köln (CGN) and Munich (MUC).
