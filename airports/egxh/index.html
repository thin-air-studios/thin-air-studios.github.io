About EGXH

RAF Honington (BEQ) is a medium airport in Bury Saint Edmunds, Suffolk, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGXH. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,012 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EGXH.
