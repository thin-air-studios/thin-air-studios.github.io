About KCOD

Yellowstone Regional Airport (COD) is a medium airport in Cody, United States, with the ICAO code KCOD. It has one runway, 04/22, 8,268 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 15 routes through KCOD. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN) and Salt Lake City (SLC).
