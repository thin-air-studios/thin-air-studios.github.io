About CYQR

Regina International Airport (YQR) is a medium airport in Regina, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQR. Its two runways are 13/31 and 08/26. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,900 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 248 routes through CYQR. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC), Toronto (YYZ) and Vancouver (YVR).
