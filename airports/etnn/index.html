About ETNN

Nörvenich Air Base (ETNN) is a medium airport in Nörvenich, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,003 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 249 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETNN.
