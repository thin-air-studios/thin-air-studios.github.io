About FBSN

Sua Pan Airport (SXN) is a medium airport in Sowa, Botswana, with the ICAO code FBSN. It has one runway, 11/29, 5,381 ft long and 66 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FBSN.
