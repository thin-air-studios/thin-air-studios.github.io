About KGRK

Killeen Regional Airport / Robert Gray Army Airfield (GRK) is a medium airport in Fort Cavazos, United States, with the ICAO code KGRK. It has one runway, 15/33, 9,997 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 96 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 53 routes through KGRK. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Houston (IAH) and Frankfurt am Main (HHN).
