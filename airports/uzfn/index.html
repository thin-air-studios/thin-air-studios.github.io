About UZFN

Namangan International Airport (NMA) is a large airport in Namangan, Uzbekistan, with the ICAO code UZFN. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,698 ft long and 160 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UZFN.
