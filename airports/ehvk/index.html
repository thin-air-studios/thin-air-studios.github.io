About EHVK

Volkel Air Base (UDE) is a medium airport in Uden, Netherlands, with the ICAO code EHVK. Its two runways are 06R/24L and 06L/24R. The longer one, 06R/24L, is 9,931 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 108 taxiway segments and 49 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EHVK.
