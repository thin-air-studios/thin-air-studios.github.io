About CYYL

Lynn Lake Airport (YYL) is a medium airport in Lynn Lake, Canada, with the ICAO code CYYL. Its two runways are 17/35 and 08/26. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYYL.
