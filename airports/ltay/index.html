About LTAY

Çardak Airport (DNZ) is a medium airport in Denizli, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAY. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 65 routes through LTAY. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Istanbul (ISL).
