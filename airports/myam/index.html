About MYAM

Leonard M. Thompson International Airport (MHH) is a medium airport in Marsh Harbour, Bahamas, with the ICAO code MYAM. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,100 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 34 routes through MYAM. Its most frequent links are Nassau (NAS), Fort Lauderdale (FLL) and West Palm Beach (DJT).
