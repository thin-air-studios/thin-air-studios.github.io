About RCFG

Matsu Nangan Airport (LZN) is a medium airport in Matsu (Nangan), Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCFG. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,180 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 38 routes through RCFG. Its most frequent links are Taipei (TSA), Taichung (RMQ) and Kaohsiung (KHH).
