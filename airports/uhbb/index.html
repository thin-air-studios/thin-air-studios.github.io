About UHBB

Ignatyevo Airport (BQS) is a medium airport in Blagoveschensk, Russia, with the ICAO code UHBB. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,256 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 73 routes through UHBB. Its most frequent links are Novosibirsk (OVB), Moscow (DME) and Artyom (VVO).
