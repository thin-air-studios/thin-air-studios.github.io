About YABA

Albany Airport (ALH) is a medium airport in Albany, Australia, with the ICAO code YABA. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 5,906 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 13 routes through YABA. Its most frequent links are Perth (PER), YBGD (YBGD) and Moonyoonooka (GET).
