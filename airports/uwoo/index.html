About UWOO

Orenburg Central Airport (REN) is a medium airport in Orenburg, Russia, with the ICAO code UWOO. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,212 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 49 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 102 routes through UWOO. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Antalya (AYT) and St. Petersburg (LED).
