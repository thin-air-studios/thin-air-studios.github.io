About KRNO

Reno Tahoe International Airport (RNO) is a large airport in Reno, United States, with the ICAO code KRNO. Its 3 runways are 17R/35L, 17L/35R and 08/26. The longest, 17R/35L, is 11,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 196 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 369 routes through KRNO. Its most frequent links are Phoenix (PHX), Las Vegas (LAS) and Los Angeles (LAX).
