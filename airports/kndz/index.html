About KNDZ

Whiting Field Naval Air Station South Airport (KNDZ) is a medium airport in Milton, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNDZ.
