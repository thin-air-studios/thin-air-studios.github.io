About KRBG

Roseburg Regional Airport (RBG) is a small airport in Roseburg, United States, with the ICAO code KRBG. It has one runway, 16/34, 5,003 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through KRBG. Its most frequent links are Portland (PDX) and Eugene (EUG).
