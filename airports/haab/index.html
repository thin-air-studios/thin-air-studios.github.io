About HAAB

Addis Ababa Bole International Airport (ADD) is a large airport in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, with the ICAO code HAAB. Its two runways are 07R/25L and 07L/25R. The longer one, 07R/25L, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 59 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 777 routes through HAAB. Its most frequent links are Bahir Dar (BJR), Jeddah (JED) and Dire Dawa (DIR).
