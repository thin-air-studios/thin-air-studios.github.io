About EGNM

Leeds Bradford Airport (LBA) is a large airport in Leeds, West Yorkshire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGNM. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,381 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 62 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 1,727 routes through EGNM. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Alicante (ALC) and Málaga (AGP).
