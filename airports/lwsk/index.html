About LWSK

Skopje International Airport (SKP) is a large airport in Ilinden, North Macedonia, with the ICAO code LWSK. It has one runway, 16/34, 9,678 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 83 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 968 routes through LWSK. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Luton (LTN) and Bâle / Mulhouse (BSL).
