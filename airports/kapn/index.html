About KAPN

Alpena County Regional Airport (APN) is a medium airport in Alpena, United States, with the ICAO code KAPN. Its two runways are 01/19 and 07/25. The longer one, 01/19, is 9,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 6 routes through KAPN. Its most frequent links are Detroit (DTW) and Flint (FNT).
