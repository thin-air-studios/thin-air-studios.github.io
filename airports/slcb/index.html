About SLCB

Jorge Wilsterman International Airport (CBB) is a large airport in Cochabamba, Bolivia, with the ICAO code SLCB. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 12,460 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 79 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 81 routes through SLCB. Its most frequent links are Santa Cruz (VVI), La Paz / El Alto (LPB) and Tarija (TJA).
