About WAUU

Rendani Airport (MKW) is a medium airport in Manokwari, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAUU. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 4 routes through WAUU. Its most frequent links are Sorong (SOQ), Makassar (UPG) and Jakarta (CGK).
