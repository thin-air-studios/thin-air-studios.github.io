About KGUC

Gunnison Crested Butte Regional Airport (GUC) is a medium airport in Gunnison, United States, with the ICAO code KGUC. Its two runways are 7/25 and 17/35. The longer one, 7/25, is 9,400 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 28 routes through KGUC. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Dallas (DAL) and Houston (IAH).
