About MUHA

José Martí International Airport (HAV) is a large airport in Havana, Cuba, with the ICAO code MUHA. It has one runway, 06/24, 13,123 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 79 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 412 routes through MUHA. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), Ferno (MXP) and Cancún (CUN).
