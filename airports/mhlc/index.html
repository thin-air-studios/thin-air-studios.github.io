About MHLC

Golosón International Airport (LCE) is a medium airport in La Ceiba, Honduras, with the ICAO code MHLC. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,875 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 8 routes through MHLC. Its most frequent links are George Town (GCM), Tegucigalpa (TGU) and Toronto (YYZ).
