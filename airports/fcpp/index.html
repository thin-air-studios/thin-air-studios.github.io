About FCPP

Antonio Agostinho-Neto International Airport (PNR) is a large airport in Pointe Noire, Congo - Brazzaville, with the ICAO code FCPP. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 49 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 30 routes through FCPP. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Brazzaville (BZV) and Libreville (LBV).
