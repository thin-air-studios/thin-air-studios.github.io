About OIMM

Mashhad International Airport (MHD) is a large airport in Mashhad, Iran, with the ICAO code OIMM. Its two runways are 13R/31L and 13L/31R. The longer one, 13R/31L, is 12,877 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 110 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 147 routes through OIMM. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Kuwait City (KWI) and Kish Island (KIH).
