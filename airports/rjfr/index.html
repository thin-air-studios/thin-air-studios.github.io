About RJFR

Kitakyushu Airport (KKJ) is a large airport in Kitakyushu, Japan, with the ICAO code RJFR. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,202 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 107 routes through RJFR. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Narita (NRT) and Seoul (ICN).
