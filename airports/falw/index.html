About FALW

Langebaanweg Airport (SDB) is a medium airport in Langebaanweg, South Africa, with the ICAO code FALW. Its 4 runways are 02R/20L, 02L/20R, 16/34 and 07/25. The longest, 02R/20L, is 7,689 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 170 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FALW.
