About KHVN

Tweed New Haven Airport (HVN) is a medium airport in New Haven, United States, with the ICAO code KHVN. It has one runway, 02/20, 5,600 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 268 routes through KHVN. Its most frequent links are Orlando (MCO), Myrtle Beach (MYR) and Greenville/Greer/Spartanburg (GSP).
