About KBNA

Nashville International Airport (BNA) is a large airport in Nashville, United States, with the ICAO code KBNA. Its 4 runways are 13/31, 02C/20C, 02R/20L and 02L/20R. The longest, 13/31, is 11,030 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 gates, 263 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 50 airlines have 1,698 routes through KBNA. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN) and New York (LGA).
