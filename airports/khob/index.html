About KHOB

Lea County Regional Airport (HOB) is a medium airport in Hobbs, United States, with the ICAO code KHOB. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 13/31 and 17/35. The longest, 04/22, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 13 routes through KHOB. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Denver (DEN) and Dallas (DAL).
