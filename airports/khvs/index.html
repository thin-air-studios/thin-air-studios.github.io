About KHVS

Hartsville Regional Airport (HVS) is a small airport in Hartsville, United States, with the ICAO code KHVS. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,000 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHVS.
