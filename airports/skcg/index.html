About SKCG

Rafael Nuñez International Airport (CTG) is a large airport in Cartagena, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKCG. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 22 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 369 routes through SKCG. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Medellín (MDE) and Cali (CLO).
