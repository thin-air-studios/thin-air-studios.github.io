About EKCH

Copenhagen Kastrup Airport (CPH) is a large airport in Copenhagen, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKCH. Its 3 runways are 04L/22R, 04R/22L and 12/30. The longest, 04L/22R, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 88 gates, 219 taxiway segments, 22 aprons and 12 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 195 airlines have 7,878 routes through EKCH. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Stockholm (ARN) and Aalborg (AAL).
