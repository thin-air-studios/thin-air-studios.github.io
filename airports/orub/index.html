About ORUB

Ubaydah Bin Al Jarrah Airport (ORUB) is a medium airport in Al Kut, Iraq. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 11R/29L and 11L/29R. The longer one, 11R/29L, is 11,666 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ORUB.
