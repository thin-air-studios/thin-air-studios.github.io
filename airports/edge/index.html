About EDGE

Eisenach-Kindel Airport (EIB) is a medium airport in Hörselberg-Hainich, Germany, with the ICAO code EDGE. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,643 ft long and 180 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDGE.
