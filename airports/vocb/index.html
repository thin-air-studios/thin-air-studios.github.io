About VOCB

Coimbatore International Airport (CJB) is a large airport in Coimbatore, India, with the ICAO code VOCB. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,480 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 251 routes through VOCB. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), Chennai (MAA) and Bengaluru (BLR).
