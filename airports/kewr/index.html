About KEWR

Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) is a large airport in Newark, United States, with the ICAO code KEWR. Its 3 runways are 4L/22R, 4R/22L and 11/29. The longest, 4L/22R, is 11,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 101 gates, 173 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 101 airlines have 4,348 routes through KEWR. Its most frequent links are Louisville (SDF), Chicago (ORD) and Boston (BOS).
