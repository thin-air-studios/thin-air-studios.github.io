About KSFO

San Francisco International Airport (SFO) is a large airport in San Francisco, United States, with the ICAO code KSFO. It is number 32 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 54.5 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 10L/28R, 10R/28L, 1R/19L and 1L/19R. The longest, 10L/28R, is 11,870 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 121 gates, 241 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 92 airlines have 3,276 routes through KSFO. Its most frequent links are Los Angeles (LAX), San Diego (SAN) and Las Vegas (LAS).
