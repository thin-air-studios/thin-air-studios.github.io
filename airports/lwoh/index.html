About LWOH

Ohrid St. Paul the Apostle Airport (OHD) is a large airport in Ohrid, North Macedonia, with the ICAO code LWOH. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,366 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 183 routes through LWOH. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Dortmund (DTM) and Zurich (ZRH).
