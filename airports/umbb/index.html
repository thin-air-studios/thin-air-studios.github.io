About UMBB

Brest International Airport (BQT) is a large airport in Brest, Belarus, with the ICAO code UMBB. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,596 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BRU has 16 routes through UMBB. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH) and Burgas (BOJ).
