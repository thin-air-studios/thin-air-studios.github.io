About NZQN

Queenstown Airport (ZQN) is a large airport in Queenstown, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZQN. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,204 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 256 routes through NZQN. Its most frequent links are Christchurch (CHC), Auckland (AKL) and Wellington (WLG).
