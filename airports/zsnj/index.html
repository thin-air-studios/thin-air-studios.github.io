About ZSNJ

Nanjing Lukou International Airport (NKG) is a large airport in Nanjing, China, with the ICAO code ZSNJ. Its two runways are 06/24 and 07/25. The longer one, 06/24, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 gates, 265 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 84 airlines have 1,578 routes through ZSNJ. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Shenzhen (SZX) and Kunming (KMG).
