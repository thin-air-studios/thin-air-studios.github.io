About CYSM

Fort Smith Airport (YSM) is a medium airport in Fort Smith, Canada, with the ICAO code CYSM. Its two runways are 12/30 and 03/21. The longer one, 12/30, is 6,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYSM.
