About EGKK

London Gatwick Airport (LGW) is a large airport in London, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGKK. Its two runways are 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longer one, 08R/26L, is 10,883 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 73 gates, 214 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 178 airlines have 16,993 routes through EGKK. Its most frequent links are Geneva (GVA), Barcelona (BCN) and Málaga (AGP).
