About KBDL

Bradley International Airport (BDL) is a large airport in Hartford, United States, with the ICAO code KBDL. Its two runways are 06/24 and 15/33. The longer one, 06/24, is 9,510 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 153 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 660 routes through KBDL. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Chicago (ORD) and Dulles (IAD).
