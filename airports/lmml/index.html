About LMML

Malta International Airport (MLA) is a large airport in Valletta, Malta, with the ICAO code LMML. Its two runways are 13/31 and 05/23. The longer one, 13/31, is 10,991 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 94 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 80 airlines have 2,269 routes through LMML. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Manchester (MAN) and London (STN).
