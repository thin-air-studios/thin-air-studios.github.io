About OYGD

Al Ghaydah International Airport (AAY) is a medium airport in Al Ghaydah, Yemen, with the ICAO code OYGD. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OYGD.
