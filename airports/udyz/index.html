About UDYZ

Zvartnots International Airport (EVN) is a large airport in Yerevan, Armenia, with the ICAO code UDYZ. It has one runway, 08/26, 12,631 ft long and 184 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 24 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 95 airlines have 921 routes through UDYZ. Its most frequent links are Moscow (VKO), Moscow (DME) and Moscow (SVO).
