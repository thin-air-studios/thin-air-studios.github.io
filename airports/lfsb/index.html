About LFSB

EuroAirport Basel–Mulhouse–Freiburg (BSL) is a large airport in Bâle / Mulhouse, France, with the ICAO code LFSB. Its two runways are 15/33 and 07/25. The longer one, 15/33, is 12,795 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 gates, 143 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 119 airlines have 5,009 routes through LFSB. Its most frequent links are Berlin (BER), Prishtina (PRN) and Amsterdam (AMS).
