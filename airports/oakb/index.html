About OAKB

Kabul International Airport (KBL) is a large airport in Kabul, Afghanistan, with the ICAO code OAKB. It has one runway, 11/29, 11,483 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 216 taxiway segments, 21 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 119 routes through OAKB. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Istanbul (IST) and Mazar-i-Sharif (MZR).
