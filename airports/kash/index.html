About KASH

Nashua Airport / Boire Field (ASH) is a small airport in Nashua, United States, with the ICAO code KASH. It has one runway, 14/32, 6,000 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KASH.
