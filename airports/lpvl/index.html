About LPVL

Vilar de Luz Airfield (LPVL) is a medium airport in Maia, Portugal. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 16/34, 5,606 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LPVL.
