About KS21

Sunriver Airport (SUO) is a small airport in Sunriver, United States, with the ICAO code KS21. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,460 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KS21.
