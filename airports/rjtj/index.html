About RJTJ

Iruma Air Base (RJTJ) is a medium airport in Sayama, Japan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RJTJ.
