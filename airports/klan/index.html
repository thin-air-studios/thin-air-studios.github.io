About KLAN

Capital Region International Airport (LAN) is a medium airport in Lansing, United States, with the ICAO code KLAN. Its 3 runways are 10R/28L, 06/24 and 10L/28R. The longest, 10R/28L, is 8,506 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 37 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 95 routes through KLAN. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Detroit (DTW) and Louisville (SDF).
