About LFBA

Agen La Garenne airport (AGF) is a medium airport in Agen, France, with the ICAO code LFBA. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,103 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 7 routes through LFBA. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY) and Bergerac (EGC).
