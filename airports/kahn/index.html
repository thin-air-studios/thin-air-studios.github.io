About KAHN

Athens Ben Epps Airport (AHN) is a medium airport in Athens, United States, with the ICAO code KAHN. Its two runways are 09/27 and 02/20. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,122 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAHN.
