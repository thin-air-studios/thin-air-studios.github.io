About KUIN

Quincy Regional Airport Baldwin Field (UIN) is a medium airport in Quincy, United States, with the ICAO code KUIN. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 18/36 and 13/31. The longest, 04/22, is 7,098 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 25 routes through KUIN. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), St Louis (STL) and Burlington (BRL).
