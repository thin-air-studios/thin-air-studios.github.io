About HE38

Abu Rish Valley Airport (HE38) is a small airport in Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 02L/20R and 02R/20L. The longer one, 02L/20R, is 9,886 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE38.
