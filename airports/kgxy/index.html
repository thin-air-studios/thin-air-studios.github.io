About KGXY

Greeley–Weld County Airport (GXY) is a small airport in Greeley, United States, with the ICAO code KGXY. Its two runways are 17/35 and 10/28. The longer one, 17/35, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGXY.
