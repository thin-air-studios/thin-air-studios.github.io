About NZRO

Rotorua Regional Airport (ROT) is a medium airport in Rotorua, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZRO. Its two runways are 18R/36L and 18L/36R. The longer one, 18R/36L, is 5,321 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 106 routes through NZRO. Its most frequent links are Auckland (AKL), Wellington (WLG) and Christchurch (CHC).
