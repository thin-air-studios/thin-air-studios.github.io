About HEMA

Marsa Alam International Airport (RMF) is a large airport in Marsa Alam, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEMA. It has one runway, 15/33, 11,253 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 68 airlines have 792 routes through HEMA. Its most frequent links are Prague (PRG), Katowice (KTW) and Düsseldorf (DUS).
