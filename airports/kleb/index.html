About KLEB

Lebanon Municipal Airport (LEB) is a medium airport in Lebanon, United States, with the ICAO code KLEB. Its two runways are 07/25 and 18/36. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,496 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 15 routes through KLEB. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS), White Plains (HPN) and Orio al Serio (BGY).
