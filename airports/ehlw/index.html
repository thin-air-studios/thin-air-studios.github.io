About EHLW

Leeuwarden Air Base (LWR) is a medium airport in Leeuwarden, Netherlands, with the ICAO code EHLW. Its two runways are 05/23 and 09/27. The longer one, 05/23, is 8,045 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 122 taxiway segments and 40 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EHLW.
