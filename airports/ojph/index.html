About OJPH

Prince Hassan Air Base (OJPH) is a small airport in Jordan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,802 ft long and 140 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 108 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OJPH.
