About HSSM

Malakal International Airport (MAK) is a medium airport in Malakal, South Sudan, with the ICAO code HSSM. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,562 ft long and 125 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BDR has 1 routes through HSSM. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is HSSJ (HSSJ).
