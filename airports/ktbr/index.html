About KTBR

Statesboro Bulloch County Airport (TBR) is a small airport in Statesboro, United States, with the ICAO code KTBR. Its two runways are 14/32 and 06/24. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTBR.
