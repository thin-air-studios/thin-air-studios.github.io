About KALW

Walla Walla Regional Airport (ALW) is a medium airport in Walla Walla, United States, with the ICAO code KALW. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,527 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 8 routes through KALW. Its most frequent links are Seattle (SEA), Spokane (GEG) and Pasco (PSC).
