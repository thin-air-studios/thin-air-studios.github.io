About KMOD

Modesto City Co-Harry Sham Field (MOD) is a medium airport in Modesto, United States, with the ICAO code KMOD. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 5,904 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 10 routes through KMOD. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Merced (MCE) and Sacramento (MHR).
