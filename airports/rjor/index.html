About RJOR

Tottori Sand Dunes Conan Airport (TTJ) is a medium airport in Tottori, Japan, with the ICAO code RJOR. It has one runway, 10/28, 6,562 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 16 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 14 routes through RJOR. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND) and Taoyuan (TPE).
