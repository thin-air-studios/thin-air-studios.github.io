About EDAH

Heringsdorf Airport (HDF) is a medium airport in Zirchow, Germany, with the ICAO code EDAH. Its 3 runways are 10/28, 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longest, 10/28, is 7,562 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 25 routes through EDAH. Its most frequent links are Stuttgart (STR), Zurich (ZRH) and Frankfurt am Main (FRA).
