About ESNO

Örnsköldsvik Airport (OER) is a medium airport in Örnsköldsvik, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESNO. It has one runway, 12/30, 6,607 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 4 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 18 routes through ESNO. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Split (SPU) and Kokkola / Kruunupyy (KOK).
