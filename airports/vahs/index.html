About VAHS

Rajkot International Airport (HSR) is a large airport in Rajkot, India, with the ICAO code VAHS. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,974 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 69 routes through VAHS. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), New Delhi (DEL) and Bengaluru (BLR).
