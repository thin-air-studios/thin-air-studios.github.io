About HSMN

Merowe Airport (MWE) is a medium airport in Merowe, Sudan, with the ICAO code HSMN. It has one runway, 02/20, 13,186 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HSMN.
