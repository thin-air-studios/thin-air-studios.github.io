About KFNT

Bishop International Airport (FNT) is a medium airport in Flint, United States, with the ICAO code KFNT. Its two runways are 18/36 and 09/27. The longer one, 18/36, is 7,852 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 138 routes through KFNT. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Orlando (SFB) and Punta Gorda (PGD).
