About VGEG

Shah Amanat International Airport (CGP) is a large airport in Chattogram (Chittagong), Bangladesh, with the ICAO code VGEG. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,646 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 31 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 92 routes through VGEG. Its most frequent links are Dhaka (DAC), Muscat/Seeb (MCT) and Dubai (DXB).
