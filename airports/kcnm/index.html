About KCNM

Cavern City Air Terminal (CNM) is a medium airport in Carlsbad, United States, with the ICAO code KCNM. Its 4 runways are 03/21, 14R/32L, 08/26 and 14L/32R. The longest, 03/21, is 7,854 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 6 routes through KCNM. Its most frequent links are Albuquerque (ABQ), Denver (DEN) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
