About KSUU

Travis Air Force Base (SUU) is a medium airport in Fairfield, United States, with the ICAO code KSUU. Its two runways are 03L/21R and 03R/21L. The longer one, 03L/21R, is 11,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 160 taxiway segments and 16 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 19 routes through KSUU. Its most frequent links are Honolulu (HNL), Anchorage (EDF) and Anchorage (ANC).
