About FQMA

Maputo Airport (MPM) is a large airport in Maputo, Mozambique, with the ICAO code FQMA. Its two runways are 05/23 and 10/28. The longer one, 05/23, is 12,008 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 82 routes through FQMA. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Lisbon (LIS) and Doha (DOH).
