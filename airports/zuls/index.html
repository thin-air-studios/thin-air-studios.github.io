About ZULS

Lhasa Gonggar International Airport (LXA) is a large airport in Shannan (Gonggar), China, with the ICAO code ZULS. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longest, 09/27, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 158 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 277 routes through ZULS. Its most frequent links are Chengdu (CTU), Chengdu (TFU) and Chongqing (CKG).
