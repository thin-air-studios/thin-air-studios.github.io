About GVBA

Aristides Pereira International Airport (BVC) is a large airport in Rabil, Cape Verde, with the ICAO code GVBA. It has one runway, 03/21, 6,890 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 189 routes through GVBA. Its most frequent links are Espargos (SID), London (LGW) and Manchester (MAN).
