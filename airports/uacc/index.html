About UACC

Nursultan Nazarbayev International Airport (NQZ) is a large airport in Astana, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UACC. It has one runway, 04/22, 11,484 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 119 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 59 airlines have 620 routes through UACC. Its most frequent links are Almaty (ALA), Shymkent (CIT) and Grâce-Hollogne (LGG).
