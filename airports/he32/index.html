About HE32

Al Mansurah Airbase (HE32) is a medium airport in Mansoura, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 14/32 and 17/35. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,084 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE32.
