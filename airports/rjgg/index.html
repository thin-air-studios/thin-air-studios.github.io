About RJGG

Chubu Centrair International Airport (NGO) is a large airport in Tokoname, Japan, with the ICAO code RJGG. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,483 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 gates, 199 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 71 airlines have 866 routes through RJGG. Its most frequent links are Sapporo (CTS), Naha (OKA) and Hong Kong (HKG).
