About MUSC

Abel Santamaria International Airport (SNU) is a large airport in Santa Clara, Cuba, with the ICAO code MUSC. It has one runway, 08/26, 9,898 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 73 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 185 routes through MUSC. Its most frequent links are Montréal (YUL), Toronto (YYZ) and Miami (MIA).
