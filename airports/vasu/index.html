About VASU

Surat International Airport (STV) is a large airport in Surat, India, with the ICAO code VASU. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 125 routes through VASU. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR) and Hyderabad (HYD).
