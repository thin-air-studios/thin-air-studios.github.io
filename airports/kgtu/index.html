About KGTU

Georgetown Executive Airport at Johnny Gantt Field (KGTU) is a small airport in Georgetown, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 18/36 and 11/29. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 78 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGTU.
