About KALS

San Luis Valley Regional Airport/Bergman Field (ALS) is a medium airport in Alamosa, United States, with the ICAO code KALS. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,521 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 12 routes through KALS. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Zaventem (BRU) and Salida (SLT).
