About DGSI

Prempeh I International Airport (KMS) is a large airport in Kumasi, Ghana, with the ICAO code DGSI. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,612 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 9 routes through DGSI. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Accra (ACC).
