About MMLO

Guanajuato International Airport (BJX) is a large airport in Silao, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMLO. It has one runway, 13/31, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 193 routes through MMLO. Its most frequent links are Tijuana (TIJ), Mexico City (MEX) and Houston (IAH).
