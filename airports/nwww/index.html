About NWWW

La Tontouta International Airport (NOU) is a large airport in Nouméa (La Tontouta), New Caledonia, with the ICAO code NWWW. It has one runway, 11/29, 10,663 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 28 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 114 routes through NWWW. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Auckland (AKL) and Port Vila (VLI).
