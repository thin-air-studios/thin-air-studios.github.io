About CYHY

Hay River / Merlyn Carter Airport (YHY) is a medium airport in Hay River, Canada, with the ICAO code CYHY. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYHY.
