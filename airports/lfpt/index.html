About LFPT

Pontoise-Cormeilles Aerodrome (POX) is a medium airport in Cormeilles-en-Vexin, Val-d'Oise, France, with the ICAO code LFPT. Its two runways are 04/22 and 12/30. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,541 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 86 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFPT.
