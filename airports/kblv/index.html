About KBLV

Scott AFB/Midamerica Airport (BLV) is a medium airport in Belleville, United States, with the ICAO code KBLV. Its two runways are 14L/32R and 14R/32L. The longer one, 14L/32R, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 55 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 53 routes through KBLV. Its most frequent links are Punta Gorda (PGD), Valparaiso (VPS) and Sarasota/Bradenton (SRQ).
