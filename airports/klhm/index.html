About KLHM

Lincoln Regional Karl Harder Field (KLHM) is a small airport in Lincoln, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 6,001 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments and 3 aprons.
