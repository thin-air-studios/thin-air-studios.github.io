About VTBU

U-Tapao–Rayong–Pattaya International Airport (UTP) is a large airport in Rayong, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTBU. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,500 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 89 routes through VTBU. Its most frequent links are Irkutsk (IKT), Novosibirsk (OVB) and Chiang Mai (CNX).
