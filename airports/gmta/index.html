About GMTA

Cherif Al Idrissi Airport (AHU) is a medium airport in Al Hoceima, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMTA. It has one runway, 17/35, 10,511 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 79 routes through GMTA. Its most frequent links are Zaventem (BRU), Rotterdam (RTM) and Amsterdam (AMS).
