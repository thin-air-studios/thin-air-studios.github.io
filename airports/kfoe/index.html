About KFOE

Topeka Regional Airport (FOE) is a medium airport in Topeka, United States, with the ICAO code KFOE. Its two runways are 13/31 and 03/21. The longer one, 13/31, is 12,803 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through KFOE. Its most frequent links are Waco (ACT) and Victorville (VCV).
