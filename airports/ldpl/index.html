About LDPL

Pula Airport (PUY) is a large airport in Pula, Croatia, with the ICAO code LDPL. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,678 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 46 airlines have 449 routes through LDPL. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), London (STN) and Amsterdam (AMS).
