About LTAC

Esenboğa International Airport (ESB) is a large airport in Ankara, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAC. Its two runways are 03L/21R and 03R/21L. The longer one, 03L/21R, is 12,303 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 192 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 50 airlines have 2,236 routes through LTAC. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Gaziemir (ADB).
