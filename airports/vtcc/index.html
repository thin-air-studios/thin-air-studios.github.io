About VTCC

Chiang Mai International Airport (CNX) is a large airport in Chiang Mai, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTCC. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 43 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 518 routes through VTCC. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (DMK), Bangkok (BKK) and Seoul (ICN).
