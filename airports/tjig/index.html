About TJIG

Fernando Luis Ribas Dominicci Airport (SIG) is a medium airport in San Juan, Puerto Rico, with the ICAO code TJIG. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,317 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WAF has 3 routes through TJIG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Culebra (CPX).
