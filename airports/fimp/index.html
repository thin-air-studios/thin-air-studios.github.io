About FIMP

Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU) is a large airport in Plaine Magnien, Mauritius, with the ICAO code FIMP. It has one runway, 14/32, 11,056 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 59 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 40 airlines have 363 routes through FIMP. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Sainte-Marie (RUN) and Frankfurt am Main (FRA).
