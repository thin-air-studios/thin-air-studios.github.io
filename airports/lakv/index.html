About LAKV

Kuçovë Air Base (LAKV) is a medium airport in Kuçovë, Albania. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,318 ft long and 220 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 32 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LAKV.
