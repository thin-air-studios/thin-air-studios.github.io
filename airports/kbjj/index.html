About KBJJ

Wayne County Airport (BJJ) is a small airport in Wooster, United States, with the ICAO code KBJJ. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,190 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBJJ.
