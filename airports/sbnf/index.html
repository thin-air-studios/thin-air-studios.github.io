About SBNF

Ministro Victor Konder International Airport (NVT) is a large airport in Navegantes, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBNF. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,906 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 161 routes through SBNF. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (CGH), São Paulo (GRU) and Campinas (VCP).
