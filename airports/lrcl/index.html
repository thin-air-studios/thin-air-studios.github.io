About LRCL

Avram Iancu Cluj International Airport (CLJ) is a large airport in Cluj-Napoca, Romania, with the ICAO code LRCL. It has one runway, 07/25, 6,693 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 37 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 52 airlines have 1,382 routes through LRCL. Its most frequent links are Otopeni (OTP), Luton (LTN) and Antalya (AYT).
