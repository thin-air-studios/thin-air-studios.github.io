About VIDP

Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) is a large airport in New Delhi, India, with the ICAO code VIDP. It is number 11 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 78.1 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 11R/29L, 11L/29R, 10/28 and 09/27. The longest, 11R/29L, is 14,534 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 gates, 536 taxiway segments, 32 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 126 airlines have 6,302 routes through VIDP. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), Bengaluru (BLR) and Kolkata (CCU).
