About EKTS

Thisted Airport (TED) is a medium airport in Thisted, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKTS. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,249 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EKTS.
