About LELC

San Javier Airport (LELC) is a medium airport in San Javier, Spain. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 05R/23L and 05L/23R. The longer one, 05R/23L, is 7,612 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 271 routes through LELC. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Bristol (BRS) and Manchester (MAN).
