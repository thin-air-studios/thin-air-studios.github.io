About KMHL

Marshall Memorial Municipal Airport (MHL) is a small airport in Marshall, United States, with the ICAO code KMHL. Its two runways are 18/36 and 09/27. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,005 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMHL.
