About EPKS

Krzesiny Military Air Base (EPKS) is a medium airport in Poznań, Poland. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,202 ft long and 196 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EPKS.
