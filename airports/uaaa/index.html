About UAAA

Almaty International Airport (ALA) is a large airport in Almaty, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UAAA. Its two runways are 05L/23R and 05R/23L. The longer one, 05L/23R, is 14,764 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 285 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 108 airlines have 1,265 routes through UAAA. Its most frequent links are Astana (NQZ), Hong Kong (HKG) and Istanbul (IST).
