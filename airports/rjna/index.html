About RJNA

Nagoya Airport / JASDF Komaki Air Base (NKM) is a medium airport in Nagoya, Japan, with the ICAO code RJNA. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,990 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 35 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, FDA has 70 routes through RJNA. Its most frequent links are Fukuoka (FUK), Hanamaki (HNA) and Izumo (IZO).
