About UMMG

Hrodna Airport (GNA) is a medium airport in Hrodna, Belarus, with the ICAO code UMMG. Its two runways are 17/35 and 09/27. The longer one, 17/35, is 8,399 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BRU has 5 routes through UMMG. Its most frequent links are Burgas (BOJ), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH) and Antalya (AYT).
