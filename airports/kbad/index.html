About KBAD

Barksdale Air Force Base (BAD) is a medium airport in Bossier City, United States, with the ICAO code KBAD. It has one runway, 15/33, 11,758 ft long and 299 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through KBAD. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC) and Philadelphia (PHL).
