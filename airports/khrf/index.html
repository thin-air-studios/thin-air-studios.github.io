About KHRF

Ravalli County Airport (KHRF) is a small airport in Hamilton, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,200 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 72 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHRF.
