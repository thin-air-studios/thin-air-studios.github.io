About KMDW

Chicago Midway International Airport (MDW) is a large airport in Chicago, United States, with the ICAO code KMDW. Its 4 runways are 13L/31R, 04R/22L, 04L/22R and 13R/31L. The longest, 13L/31R, is 6,522 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 gates, 216 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 1,079 routes through KMDW. Its most frequent links are Atlanta (ATL), Orlando (MCO) and Denver (DEN).
