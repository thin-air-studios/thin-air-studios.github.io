About ZSAM

Xiamen Gaoqi International Airport (XMN) is a large airport in Xiamen, China, with the ICAO code ZSAM. It has one runway, 05/23, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 gates, 160 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 74 airlines have 1,246 routes through ZSAM. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (SHA), Zhengzhou (CGO) and Beijing (PEK).
