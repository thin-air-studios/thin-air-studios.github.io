About KCCR

Buchanan Field (CCR) is a medium airport in Concord, United States, with the ICAO code KCCR. Its 4 runways are 01L/19R, 14L/32R, 14R/32L and 01R/19L. The longest, 01L/19R, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 76 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JSX has 17 routes through KCCR. Its most frequent links are Santa Ana (SNA), Las Vegas (LAS) and Burbank (BUR).
