About LTBA

İstanbul Atatürk Airport (ISL) is a large airport in Istanbul(Bakırköy), Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTBA. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,530 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 gates, 92 taxiway segments and 17 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 85 airlines have 2,136 routes through LTBA. Its most frequent links are Ankara (ESB), Gaziemir (ADB) and Antalya (AYT).
