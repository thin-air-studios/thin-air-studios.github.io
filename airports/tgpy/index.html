About TGPY

Maurice Bishop International Airport (GND) is a large airport in Saint George's, Grenada, with the ICAO code TGPY. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,003 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 74 routes through TGPY. Its most frequent links are Bridgetown (BGI), New York (JFK) and Port of Spain (POS).
