About KSRQ

Sarasota Bradenton International Airport (SRQ) is a large airport in Sarasota/Bradenton, United States, with the ICAO code KSRQ. Its two runways are 14/32 and 04/22. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 339 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 352 routes through KSRQ. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS), Charlotte (CLT) and Atlanta (ATL).
