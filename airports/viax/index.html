About VIAX

Adampur Airport (AIP) is a small airport in Adampur, India, with the ICAO code VIAX. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,039 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 175 taxiway segments and 17 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 8 routes through VIAX. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), Ghaziabad (HDO) and Nanded (NDC).
