About KTYR

Tyler Pounds Regional Airport (TYR) is a medium airport in Tyler, United States, with the ICAO code KTYR. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 13/31 and 18/36. The longest, 04/22, is 8,334 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 68 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 15 routes through KTYR. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Houston (IAH).
