About ESNZ

Åre Östersund Airport (OSD) is a medium airport in Östersund, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESNZ. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 68 routes through ESNZ. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Umeå (UME) and Stockholm (BMA).
