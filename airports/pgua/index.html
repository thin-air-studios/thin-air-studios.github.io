About PGUA

Andersen Air Force Base (UAM) is a medium airport in Yigo, Guam, with the ICAO code PGUA. Its two runways are 06R/24L and 06L/24R. The longer one, 06R/24L, is 11,185 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 5 routes through PGUA. Its most frequent links are Honolulu (HNL), Fussa (OKO) and Sharjah (SHJ).
