About ESGG

Göteborg Landvetter Airport (GOT) is a large airport in Göteborg, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESGG. It has one runway, 03/21, 10,823 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 58 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 103 airlines have 2,325 routes through ESGG. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Paris (CDG) and Antalya (AYT).
