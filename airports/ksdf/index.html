About KSDF

Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport (SDF) is a large airport in Louisville, United States, with the ICAO code KSDF. Its 3 runways are 17R/35L, 17L/35R and 11/29. The longest, 17R/35L, is 11,887 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 235 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 49 airlines have 2,535 routes through KSDF. Its most frequent links are Newark (EWR), Philadelphia (PHL) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
