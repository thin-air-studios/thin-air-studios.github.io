About SKRG

Jose Maria Córdova International Airport (MDE) is a large airport in Medellín, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKRG. It has one runway, 01/19, 11,286 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 100 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 656 routes through SKRG. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Cartagena (CTG) and Cali (CLO).
