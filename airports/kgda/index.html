About KGDA

Marion-Crittenden County James C Johnson Regional Airport (KGDA) is a small airport in Marion, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,001 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 aprons.
