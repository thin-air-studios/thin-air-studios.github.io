About KTCL

Tuscaloosa National Airport (TCL) is a medium airport in Tuscaloosa, United States, with the ICAO code KTCL. Its two runways are 04/22 and 12/30. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,498 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 106 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTCL.
