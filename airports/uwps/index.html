About UWPS

Saransk International Airport (SKX) is a large airport in Saransk, Russia, with the ICAO code UWPS. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,186 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 19 routes through UWPS. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Sochi (AER) and St. Petersburg (LED).
