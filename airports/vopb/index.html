About VOPB

Veer Savarkar International Airport / INS Utkrosh (IXZ) is a large airport in Port Blair, India, with the ICAO code VOPB. It has one runway, 04/22, 10,795 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 97 routes through VOPB. Its most frequent links are Kolkata (CCU), Chennai (MAA) and Bengaluru (BLR).
