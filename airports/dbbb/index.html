About DBBB

Cotonou Cadjehoun International Airport (COO) is a large airport in Cotonou, Benin, with the ICAO code DBBB. It has one runway, 06/24, 7,906 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 96 routes through DBBB. Its most frequent links are Abidjan (ABJ), Lomé (LFW) and Libreville (LBV).
