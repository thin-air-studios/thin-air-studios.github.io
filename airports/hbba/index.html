About HBBA

Bujumbura Melchior Ndadaye International Airport (BJM) is a large airport in Bujumbura, Burundi, with the ICAO code HBBA. It has one runway, 17/35, 11,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 15 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 27 routes through HBBA. Its most frequent links are Zaventem (BRU), Nairobi (NBO) and Kigali (KGL).
