About KSWF

New York Stewart International Airport (SWF) is a medium airport in Newburgh, United States, with the ICAO code KSWF. Its two runways are 09/27 and 16/34. The longer one, 09/27, is 11,817 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 53 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 77 routes through KSWF. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Louisville (SDF) and Detroit (DTW).
