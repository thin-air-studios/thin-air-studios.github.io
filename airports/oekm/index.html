About OEKM

King Khalid Air Base (KMX) is a medium airport in Khamis Mushait, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEKM. Its two runways are 06/24 and 14/32. The longer one, 06/24, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 194 taxiway segments and 26 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OEKM.
