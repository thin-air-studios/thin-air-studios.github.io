About KLTS

Altus Air Force Base (LTS) is a medium airport in Altus, United States, with the ICAO code KLTS. Its 3 runways are 18R/36L, 18L/36R and 176/356. The longest, 18R/36L, is 13,440 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 127 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLTS.
