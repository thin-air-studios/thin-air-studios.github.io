About SEAM

Chachoán Regional Airport (ATF) is a medium airport in Ambato, Ecuador, with the ICAO code SEAM. It has one runway, 01/19, 6,316 ft long and 82 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SEAM.
