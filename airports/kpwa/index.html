About KPWA

Wiley Post Airport (PWA) is a small airport in Oklahoma City, United States, with the ICAO code KPWA. Its 3 runways are 17L/35R, 17R/35L and 13/31. The longest, 17L/35R, is 7,199 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 166 taxiway segments and 14 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPWA.
