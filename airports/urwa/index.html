About URWA

Astrakhan Narimanovo Boris M. Kustodiev International Airport (ASF) is a large airport in Astrakhan, Russia, with the ICAO code URWA. Its two runways are 09/27 and 11/29. The longer one, 09/27, is 10,499 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 125 routes through URWA. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Moscow (DME) and Baku (GYD).
