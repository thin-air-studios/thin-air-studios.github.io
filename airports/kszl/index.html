About KSZL

Whiteman Air Force Base (SZL) is a medium airport in Knob Noster, United States, with the ICAO code KSZL. It has one runway, 01/19, 12,400 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 1 routes through KSZL. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Baltimore (BWI).
