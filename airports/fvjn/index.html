About FVJN

Joshua Mqabuko Nkomo International Airport (BUQ) is a large airport in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, with the ICAO code FVJN. Its two runways are 13/31 and 02/20. The longer one, 13/31, is 8,491 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 16 routes through FVJN. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB) and Addis Ababa (ADD).
