About WMKI

Sultan Azlan Shah Airport (IPH) is a large airport in Ipoh, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WMKI. It has one runway, 04/22, 5,900 ft long and 121 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 36 routes through WMKI. Its most frequent links are Singapore (SIN), Johor Bahru (JHB) and Langkawi (LGK).
