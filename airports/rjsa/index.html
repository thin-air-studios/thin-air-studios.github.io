About RJSA

Aomori Airport (AOJ) is a large airport in Aomori, Japan, with the ICAO code RJSA. It has one runway, 06/24, 9,846 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 79 routes through RJSA. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (ITM) and Sapporo (CTS).
