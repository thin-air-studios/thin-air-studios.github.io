About EGYP

Mount Pleasant Airport / RAF Mount Pleasant (MPN) is a medium airport in Mount Pleasant, Falkland Islands, with the ICAO code EGYP. Its two runways are 10/28 and 05/23. The longer one, 10/28, is 8,497 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 22 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 12 routes through EGYP. Its most frequent links are Espargos (SID), Rio Gallegos (RGL) and Punta Arenas (PUQ).
