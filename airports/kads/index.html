About KADS

Addison Airport (ADS) is a small airport in Dallas, United States, with the ICAO code KADS. It has one runway, 16/34, 7,203 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through KADS. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Abilene (ABI) and El Paso (ELP).
