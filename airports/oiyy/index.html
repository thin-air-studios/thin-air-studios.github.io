About OIYY

Shahid Sadooghi Airport (AZD) is a medium airport in Yazd, Iran, with the ICAO code OIYY. It has one runway, 13/31, 13,446 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 12 routes through OIYY. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Bandar Abbas (BND) and Ahvaz (AWZ).
