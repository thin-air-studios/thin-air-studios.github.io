About KSLE

Salem-Willamette Valley Airport/McNary Field (SLE) is a medium airport in Salem, United States, with the ICAO code KSLE. Its two runways are 13/31 and 16/34. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 164 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 16 routes through KSLE. Its most frequent links are Burbank (BUR), Las Vegas (LAS) and North Bend (OTH).
