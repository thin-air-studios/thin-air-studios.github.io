About LLHS

Hatzor Air Base (LLHS) is a small airport in Hatzor, Israel. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 11R/29L, 11L/29R and 05/23. The longest, 11R/29L, is 8,040 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LLHS.
