About DAOF

Tindouf Airport (TIN) is a medium airport in Tindouf, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAOF. Its two runways are 08L/26R and 08R/26L. The longer one, 08L/26R, is 9,840 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 18 routes through DAOF. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG), Béchar (CBH) and Adrar (AZR).
