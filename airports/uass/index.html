About UASS

Semei International Airport (PLX) is a large airport in Semey, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UASS. Its two runways are 08/26 and 02/20. The longer one, 08/26, is 10,159 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 28 routes through UASS. Its most frequent links are Almaty (ALA), Astana (NQZ) and Shymkent (CIT).
