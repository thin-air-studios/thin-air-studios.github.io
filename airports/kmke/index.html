About KMKE

General Mitchell International Airport (MKE) is a large airport in Milwaukee, United States, with the ICAO code KMKE. It has 5 runways, from 4,182 ft to 9,990 ft long. The longest is 01L/19R. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 gates, 145 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 1,056 routes through KMKE. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), New York (LGA) and Denver (DEN).
