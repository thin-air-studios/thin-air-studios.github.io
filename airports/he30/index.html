About HE30

Kawm Ushim Airport (HE30) is a small airport in Tamiya, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 16/34 and 15/33. The longer one, 16/34, is 9,078 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE30.
