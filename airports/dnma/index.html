About DNMA

Maiduguri International Airport (MIU) is a large airport in Maiduguri, Nigeria, with the ICAO code DNMA. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,846 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, APO has 2 routes through DNMA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Abuja (ABV).
