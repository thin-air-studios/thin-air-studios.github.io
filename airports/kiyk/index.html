About KIYK

Inyokern Airport (IYK) is a small airport in Inyokern, United States, with the ICAO code KIYK. Its 3 runways are 15/33, 02/20 and 10/28. The longest, 15/33, is 7,100 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through KIYK. Its most frequent links are Los Angeles (LAX), Ontario (ONT) and Bishop (BIH).
