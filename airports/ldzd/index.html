About LDZD

Zadar Airport (ZAD) is a large airport in Zadar, Croatia, with the ICAO code LDZD. Its two runways are 13/31 and 04/22. The longer one, 13/31, is 8,202 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 37 airlines have 637 routes through LDZD. Its most frequent links are Berlin (BER), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and London (STN).
