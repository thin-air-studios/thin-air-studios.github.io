About LEGR

F.G.L. Airport Granada-Jaén Airport (GRX) is a medium airport in Granada, Spain, with the ICAO code LEGR. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,514 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 37 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 369 routes through LEGR. Its most frequent links are Barcelona (BCN), Madrid (MAD) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
