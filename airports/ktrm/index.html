About KTRM

Jacqueline Cochran Regional Airport (TRM) is a medium airport in Palm Springs, United States, with the ICAO code KTRM. Its two runways are 17/35 and 12/30. The longer one, 17/35, is 8,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTRM.
