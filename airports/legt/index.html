About LEGT

Getafe Air Base (LEGT) is a medium airport in Getafe, Spain. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 04/22 and 05R/23L. The longer one, 04/22, is 8,136 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 36 routes through LEGT. Its most frequent links are Toulouse/Blagnac (TLS), Broughton (CEG) and Hamburg (XFW).
