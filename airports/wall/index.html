About WALL

Sultan Aji Muhammad Sulaiman Sepinggan International Airport (BPN) is a large airport in Balikpapan, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WALL. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 95 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 219 routes through WALL. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Surabaya (SUB) and Yogyakarta (YIA).
