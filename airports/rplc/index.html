About RPLC

Clark International Airport / Clark Air Base (CRK) is a large airport in Mabalacat, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPLC. It has one runway, 02R/20L, 10,499 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 107 taxiway segments, 24 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 447 routes through RPLC. Its most frequent links are Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB), Seoul (ICN) and El Nido (ENI).
