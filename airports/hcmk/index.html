About HCMK

Kismayo Airport (KMU) is a medium airport in Kismayo, Somalia, with the ICAO code HCMK. It has one runway, 4/22, 12,139 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HCMK.
