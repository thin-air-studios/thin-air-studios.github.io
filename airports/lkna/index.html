About LKNA

Náměšť Air Base (LKNA) is a medium airport in Sedlec, Czechia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 132 taxiway segments and 28 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LKNA.
