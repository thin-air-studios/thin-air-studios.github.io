About KMQY

Smyrna Airport (MQY) is a medium airport in Smyrna, United States, with the ICAO code KMQY. Its two runways are 14/32 and 01/19. The longer one, 14/32, is 8,038 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMQY.
