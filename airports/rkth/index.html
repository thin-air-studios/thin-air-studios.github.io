About RKTH

Pohang Airport (G-815/K-3) (KPO) is a medium airport in Pohang, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKTH. It has one runway, 10/28, 7,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 115 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 16 routes through RKTH. Its most frequent links are Seoul (GMP) and Jeju City (CJU).
