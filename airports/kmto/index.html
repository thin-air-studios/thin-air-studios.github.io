About KMTO

Coles County Memorial Airport (MTO) is a small airport in Mattoon, United States, with the ICAO code KMTO. Its 3 runways are 11/29, 06/24 and 18/36. The longest, 11/29, is 6,501 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMTO.
