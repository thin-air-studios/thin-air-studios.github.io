About YPAD

Adelaide International Airport (ADL) is a large airport in Adelaide, Australia, with the ICAO code YPAD. Its two runways are 05/23 and 12/30. The longer one, 05/23, is 10,171 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 gates, 104 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 32 airlines have 1,164 routes through YPAD. Its most frequent links are Melbourne (MEL), Sydney (SYD) and Olympic Dam (OLP).
