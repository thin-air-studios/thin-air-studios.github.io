About GCXO

Tenerife Norte-Ciudad de La Laguna Airport (TFN) is a large airport in Tenerife, Spain, with the ICAO code GCXO. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,404 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 1,678 routes through GCXO. Its most frequent links are Madrid (MAD), Gran Canaria Island (LPA) and Barcelona (BCN).
