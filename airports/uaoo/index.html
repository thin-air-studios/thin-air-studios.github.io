About UAOO

Korkyt Ata International Airport (KZO) is a large airport in Kyzylorda, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UAOO. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 36 routes through UAOO. Its most frequent links are Almaty (ALA), Astana (NQZ) and Atyrau (GUW).
