About OITP

Parsabad-Moghan Airport (PFQ) is a small airport in Parsabad, Iran, with the ICAO code OITP. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,515 ft long and 115 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, IRA has 1 routes through OITP. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Tehran (THR).
