About YPPH

Perth International Airport (PER) is a large airport in Perth, Australia, with the ICAO code YPPH. Its two runways are 03/21 and 06/24. The longer one, 03/21, is 11,299 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 82 gates, 159 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 15 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 48 airlines have 1,688 routes through YPPH. Its most frequent links are Melbourne (MEL), Auckland (AKL) and Sydney (SYD).
