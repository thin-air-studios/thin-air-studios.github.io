About HEBR

Berenice International Airport / Banas Cape Air Base (EES) is a large airport in Berenice Troglodytica, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEBR. Its 3 runways are 13L/31R, 13R/31L and 15/33. The longest, 13L/31R, is 9,930 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 108 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HEBR.
