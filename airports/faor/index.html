About FAOR

O.R. Tambo International Airport (JNB) is a large airport in Johannesburg, South Africa, with the ICAO code FAOR. Its two runways are 03L/21R and 03R/21L. The longer one, 03L/21R, is 14,495 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 gates, 336 taxiway segments, 28 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 76 airlines have 1,888 routes through FAOR. Its most frequent links are Cape Town (CPT), Durban (DUR) and Gqeberha (PLZ).
