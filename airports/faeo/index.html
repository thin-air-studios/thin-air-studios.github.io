About FAEO

Ermelo Airport (FAEO) is a medium airport in Ermelo, South Africa. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 13/31, 5,085 ft long and 33 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FAEO.
