About UOHH

Khatanga Airport (HTG) is a medium airport in Khatanga, Russia, with the ICAO code UOHH. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,872 ft long and 158 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SSJ has 2 routes through UOHH. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Krasnoyarsk (KJA).
