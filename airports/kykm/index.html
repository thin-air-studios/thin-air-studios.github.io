About KYKM

Yakima Air Terminal McAllister Field (YKM) is a medium airport in Yakima, United States, with the ICAO code KYKM. Its two runways are 09/27 and 04/22. The longer one, 09/27, is 7,604 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 16 routes through KYKM. Its most frequent links are Spokane (GEG), Seattle (SEA) and Pasco (PSC).
