About GMMW

Nador Al Aaroui International Airport (NDR) is a large airport in Al Aaroui, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMMW. It has one runway, 08/26, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 493 routes through GMMW. Its most frequent links are Zaventem (BRU), Barcelona (BCN) and Amsterdam (AMS).
