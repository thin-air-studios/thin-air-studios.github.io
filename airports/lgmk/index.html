About LGMK

Mykonos Island National Airport (JMK) is a medium airport in Mykonos, Greece, with the ICAO code LGMK. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,240 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 64 airlines have 702 routes through LGMK. Its most frequent links are Spata-Artemida (ATH), Paris (ORY) and London (LGW).
