About EFKA

Kauhava Airfield (KAU) is a medium airport in Kauhava, Finland, with the ICAO code EFKA. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,858 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 66 taxiway segments. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Finnair has 2 routes through EFKA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Helsinki (HEL).
