About SKBG

Palonegro Airport (BGA) is a medium airport in Bucaramanga, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKBG. It has one runway, 17/35, 7,381 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 111 routes through SKBG. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Medellín (EOH) and Cartagena (CTG).
