About OENG

Najran Domestic Airport (EAM) is a medium airport in Najran, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OENG. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,007 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 40 routes through OENG. Its most frequent links are Riyadh (RUH), Jeddah (JED) and Ad Dammam (DMM).
