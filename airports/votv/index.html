About VOTV

Thiruvananthapuram International Airport (TRV) is a large airport in Thiruvananthapuram, India, with the ICAO code VOTV. It has one runway, 14/32, 11,148 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 gates, 54 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 9 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 375 routes through VOTV. Its most frequent links are Bengaluru (BLR), New Delhi (DEL) and Mumbai (BOM).
