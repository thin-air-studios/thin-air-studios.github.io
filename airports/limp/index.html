About LIMP

Parma Airport (PMF) is a medium airport in Parma (PR), Italy, with the ICAO code LIMP. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,962 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 56 routes through LIMP. Its most frequent links are Cagliari (CAG), Trapani (TPS) and London (STN).
