About HSOB

El-Obeid Airport (EBD) is a medium airport in El-Obeid, Sudan, with the ICAO code HSOB. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, NOV has 2 routes through HSOB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is HSSS (HSSS).
