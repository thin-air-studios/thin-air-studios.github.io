About LFMY

Salon-de-Provence (BA 701) Air Base (LFMY) is a medium airport in Salon, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 4 runways are 16/34, 16R/34L, 16L/34R and 09/27. The longest, 16/34, is 6,565 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 3 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFMY.
