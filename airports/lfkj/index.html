About LFKJ

Ajaccio Napoléon Bonaparte airport (AJA) is a medium airport in Ajaccio, France, with the ICAO code LFKJ. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,897 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 78 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 836 routes through LFKJ. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Nantes (NTE).
