About WAJJ

Dortheys Hiyo Eluay International Airport (DJJ) is a large airport in Sentani, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAJJ. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 47 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 30 routes through WAJJ. Its most frequent links are Makassar (UPG), Timika (TIM) and Jakarta (CGK).
