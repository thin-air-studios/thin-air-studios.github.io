About KRNH

New Richmond Regional Airport (RNH) is a medium airport in New Richmond, United States, with the ICAO code KRNH. Its two runways are 14/32 and 04/22. The longer one, 14/32, is 5,507 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRNH.
