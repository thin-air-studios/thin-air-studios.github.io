About RKPK

Gimhae International Airport (PUS) is a large airport in Busan, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKPK. Its two runways are 18R/36L and 18L/36R. The longer one, 18R/36L, is 10,499 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 gates, 189 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 951 routes through RKPK. Its most frequent links are Seoul (GMP), Jeju City (CJU) and Taoyuan (TPE).
