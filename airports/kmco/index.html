About KMCO

Orlando International Airport (MCO) is a large airport in Orlando, United States, with the ICAO code KMCO. It is number 25 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 57.7 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 18L/36R, 18R/36L, 17R/35L and 17L/35R. The longest, 18L/36R, is 12,005 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 114 gates, 310 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 95 airlines have 3,173 routes through KMCO. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Newark (EWR) and Toronto (YYZ).
