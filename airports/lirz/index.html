About LIRZ

Perugia San Francesco d'Assisi – Umbria International Airport (PEG) is a large airport in Perugia (PG), Italy, with the ICAO code LIRZ. It has one runway, 01/19, 7,215 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 11 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 178 routes through LIRZ. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Charleroi (CRL) and Rotterdam (RTM).
