About UAAT

Taldykorgan Airport (TDK) is a medium airport in Taldykorgan, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UAAT. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,846 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, QAZ has 4 routes through UAAT. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Astana (NQZ).
