About NCRG

Rarotonga International Airport (RAR) is a large airport in Avarua, Cook Islands, with the ICAO code NCRG. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,638 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 66 routes through NCRG. Its most frequent links are Auckland (AKL), Aitutaki (AIT) and Sydney (SYD).
