About VDSA

Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport (SAI) is a large airport in Siem Reap, Cambodia, with the ICAO code VDSA. It has one runway, 05/23, 11,827 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 68 routes through VDSA. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (BKK), Hanoi (HAN) and Ho Chi Minh City (SGN).
