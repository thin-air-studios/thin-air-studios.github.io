About KCEF

Westover Metropolitan Airport / Westover Air Reserve Base (CEF) is a medium airport in Chicopee, United States, with the ICAO code KCEF. Its two runways are 05/23 and 15/33. The longer one, 05/23, is 11,597 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 111 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through KCEF. Its most frequent links are Fort Cavazos (GRK), New York (JFK) and Madison (MSN).
