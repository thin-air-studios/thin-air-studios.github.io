About MMQT

Querétaro Intercontinental Airport (QRO) is a large airport in Querétaro, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMQT. It has one runway, 09/27, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 105 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 203 routes through MMQT. Its most frequent links are Monterrey (MTY), Cancún (CUN) and Houston (IAH).
