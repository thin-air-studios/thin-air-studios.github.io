About KCRW

Yeager Airport (CRW) is a medium airport in Charleston, United States, with the ICAO code KCRW. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,715 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 32 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 81 routes through KCRW. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Charlotte (CLT) and Washington (DCA).
