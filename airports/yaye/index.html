About YAYE

Ayers Rock Connellan Airport (AYQ) is a medium airport in Yulara, Australia, with the ICAO code YAYE. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,527 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 31 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 21 routes through YAYE. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Brisbane (BNE) and Melbourne (MEL).
