About KDNA

Doña Ana County International Jetport (KDNA) is a small airport in Santa Teresa, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,550 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDNA.
