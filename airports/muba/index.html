About MUBA

Gustavo Rizo Airport (BCA) is a medium airport in Baracoa, Cuba, with the ICAO code MUBA. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,070 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, NWS has 1 routes through MUBA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Murmansk (MMK).
