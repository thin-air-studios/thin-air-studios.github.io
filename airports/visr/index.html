About VISR

Srinagar International Airport (SXR) is a large airport in Srinagar, India, with the ICAO code VISR. It has one runway, 13/31, 12,090 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 161 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 283 routes through VISR. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Jammu (IXJ) and Mumbai (BOM).
