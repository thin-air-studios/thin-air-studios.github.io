About KART

Watertown International Airport (ART) is a medium airport in Watertown, United States, with the ICAO code KART. Its two runways are 10/28 and 07/25. The longer one, 10/28, is 7,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 16 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 14 routes through KART. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL) and Chicago (ORD).
