About LDZA

Zagreb Franjo Tuđman International Airport (ZAG) is a large airport in Velika Gorica, Croatia, with the ICAO code LDZA. It has one runway, 04/22, 10,669 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 gates, 30 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 64 airlines have 1,064 routes through LDZA. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Belgrade (BEG) and Vienna (VIE).
