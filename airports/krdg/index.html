About KRDG

Reading Regional Airport (Carl A Spaatz Field) (RDG) is a medium airport in Reading, United States, with the ICAO code KRDG. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 6,350 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 9 routes through KRDG. Its most frequent links are Manassas (MNZ), Teterboro (TEB) and Bangor (BGR).
