About PADQ

Kodiak Airport (ADQ) is a medium airport in Kodiak, United States, with the ICAO code PADQ. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 11/29 and 01/19. The longest, 08/26, is 7,534 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 55 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 17 routes through PADQ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Anchorage (ANC).
