About ZBDT

Datong Yungang International Airport (DAT) is a large airport in Datong, China, with the ICAO code ZBDT. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 58 routes through ZBDT. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (PVG), Shanghai (SHA) and Nanjing (NKG).
