About LEGE

Girona-Costa Brava Airport (GRO) is a large airport in Girona, Spain, with the ICAO code LEGE. It has one runway, 01/19, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 846 routes through LEGE. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Amsterdam (AMS) and Weeze (NRN).
