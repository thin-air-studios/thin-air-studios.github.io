About LIMS

Piacenza San Damiano Air Base (LIMS) is a medium airport in San Damiano (PC), Italy. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,828 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIMS.
