About KGFK

Grand Forks International Airport (GFK) is a medium airport in Grand Forks, United States, with the ICAO code KGFK. Its 4 runways are 17R/35L, 09L/27R, 17L/35R and 09R/27L. The longest, 17R/35L, is 7,351 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 195 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 15 routes through KGFK. Its most frequent links are Minneapolis (MSP), Denver (DEN) and Las Vegas (LAS).
