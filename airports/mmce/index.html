About MMCE

Ciudad del Carmen International Airport (CME) is a medium airport in Ciudad del Carmen, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMCE. It has one runway, 13/31, 7,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 54 routes through MMCE. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Veracruz (VER) and Houston (IAH).
