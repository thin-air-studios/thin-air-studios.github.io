About KBOI

Boise Air Terminal/Gowen Field (BOI) is a large airport in Boise, United States, with the ICAO code KBOI. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 184 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 450 routes through KBOI. Its most frequent links are Seattle (SEA), Denver (DEN) and Portland (PDX).
