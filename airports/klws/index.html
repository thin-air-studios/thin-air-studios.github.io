About KLWS

Lewiston Nez Perce County Airport (LWS) is a medium airport in Lewiston, United States, with the ICAO code KLWS. Its two runways are 08/26 and 12/30. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,511 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 99 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 12 routes through KLWS. Its most frequent links are Salt Lake City (SLC), Seattle (SEA) and Spokane (GEG).
