About KCSM

Clinton Sherman Airport (CSM) is a small airport in Clinton, United States, with the ICAO code KCSM. Its two runways are 17R/35L and 17L/35R. The longer one, 17R/35L, is 13,503 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCSM.
