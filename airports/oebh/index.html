About OEBH

Bisha Airport (BHH) is a medium airport in Bisha, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEBH. It has one runway, 18/36, 10,007 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 16 routes through OEBH. Its most frequent links are Riyadh (RUH), Jeddah (JED) and Ad Dammam (DMM).
