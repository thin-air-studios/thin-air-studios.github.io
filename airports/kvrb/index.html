About KVRB

Vero Beach Regional Airport (VRB) is a medium airport in Vero Beach, United States, with the ICAO code KVRB. Its 3 runways are 12R/30L, 04/22 and 12L/30R. The longest, 12R/30L, is 7,314 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 38 routes through KVRB. Its most frequent links are White Plains (HPN), Providence/Warwick (PVD) and Hartford (BDL).
