About MMCS

Abraham González International Airport (CJS) is a large airport in Ciudad Juárez, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMCS. Its two runways are 03/21 and 14/32. The longer one, 03/21, is 8,858 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 154 routes through MMCS. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Guadalajara (GDL) and Monterrey (MTY).
