About HETB

Taba International Airport (TCP) is a medium airport in Taba, Egypt, with the ICAO code HETB. It has one runway, 04L/22R, 13,123 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 32 routes through HETB. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Prague (PRG) and Manchester (MAN).
