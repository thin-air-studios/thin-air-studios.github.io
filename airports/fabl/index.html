About FABL

Bram Fischer International Airport (BFN) is a large airport in Bloemfontein, South Africa, with the ICAO code FABL. Its two runways are 02/20 and 12/30. The longer one, 02/20, is 8,396 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 239 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 53 routes through FABL. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Cape Town (CPT) and Durban (DUR).
