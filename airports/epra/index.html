About EPRA

Warsaw Radom Airport (RDO) is a medium airport in Radom, Poland, with the ICAO code EPRA. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 71 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 65 routes through EPRA. Its most frequent links are Larnaca (LCA), Antalya (AYT) and Gdańsk (GDN).
