About VIBT

Bhatinda Air Force Station (BUP) is a medium airport in India, with the ICAO code VIBT. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,200 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 82 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 6 routes through VIBT. Its most frequent links are Ghaziabad (HDO) and New Delhi (DEL).
