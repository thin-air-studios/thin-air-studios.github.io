About KOGA

Ogallala Municipal Airport Searle Field (OGA) is a small airport in Ogallala, United States, with the ICAO code KOGA. Its two runways are 08/26 and 13/31. The longer one, 08/26, is 5,102 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOGA.
