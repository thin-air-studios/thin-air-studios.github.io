About UUOK

Kursk East Airport (URS) is a medium airport in Kursk, Russia, with the ICAO code UUOK. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,202 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 33 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 12 routes through UUOK. Its most frequent links are Moscow (VKO), Simferopol (SIP) and St. Petersburg (LED).
