About CYQT

Thunder Bay International Airport (YQT) is a medium airport in Thunder Bay, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQT. Its two runways are 07/25 and 12/30. The longer one, 07/25, is 7,318 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 178 routes through CYQT. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Toronto (YTZ) and Winnipeg (YWG).
