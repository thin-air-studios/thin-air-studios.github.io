About YMAY

Albury Airport (ABX) is a medium airport in East Albury, Australia, with the ICAO code YMAY. It has one runway, 07/25, 6,234 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 105 routes through YMAY. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Melbourne (MEL) and Brisbane (BNE).
