About LTBL

Çiğli Airbase (IGL) is a medium airport in Çiğli, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTBL. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,821 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 107 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LTBL.
