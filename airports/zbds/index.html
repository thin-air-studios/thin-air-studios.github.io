About ZBDS

Ordos Ejin Horo International Airport (DSN) is a large airport in Ordos, China, with the ICAO code ZBDS. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 183 routes through ZBDS. Its most frequent links are Beijing (PKX), Beijing (PEK) and Chengdu (TFU).
