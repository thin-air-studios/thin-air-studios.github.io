About ETNS

Schleswig Air Base (WBG) is a medium airport in Jagel, Germany, with the ICAO code ETNS. Its two runways are 05/23 and 07/25. The longer one, 05/23, is 8,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 143 taxiway segments and 13 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETNS.
