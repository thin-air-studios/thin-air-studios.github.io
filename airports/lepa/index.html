About LEPA

Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI) is a large airport in Palma de Mallorca, Spain, with the ICAO code LEPA. Its two runways are 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06L/24R, is 10,728 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 65 gates, 168 taxiway segments, 28 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 172 airlines have 12,018 routes through LEPA. Its most frequent links are Düsseldorf (DUS), Madrid (MAD) and Barcelona (BCN).
