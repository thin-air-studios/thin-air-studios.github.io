About LFPR

Plan-de-Dieu-Orange Airfield (LFPR) is a small airport in Travaillan, Vaucluse, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,562 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFPR.
