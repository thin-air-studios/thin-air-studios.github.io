About EGWU

RAF Northolt (NHT) is a medium airport in Northolt, Greater London, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGWU. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,545 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EGWU.
