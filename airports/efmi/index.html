About EFMI

Mikkeli Airport (MIK) is a medium airport in Mikkeli, Finland, with the ICAO code EFMI. It has one runway, 11/29, 5,584 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EFMI.
