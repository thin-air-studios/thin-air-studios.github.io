About VY02

New Magway Airport (VY02) is a small airport in Magway, Myanmar (Burma). Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,500 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VY02.
