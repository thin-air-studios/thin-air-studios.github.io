About MSLP

El Salvador International Airport Saint Óscar Arnulfo Romero y Galdámez (SAL) is a large airport in San Salvador (San Luis Talpa), El Salvador, with the ICAO code MSLP. It has one runway, 07/25, 10,500 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 88 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 436 routes through MSLP. Its most frequent links are Guatemala City (GUA), San José (SJO) and Los Angeles (LAX).
