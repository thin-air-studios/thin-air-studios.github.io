About HLTS

Tebesty-V9 Airport (HLTS) is a small airport in Libya. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 01/19 and 09/27. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,160 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HLTS.
