About DGLE

Yakubu Tali International Airport (TML) is a large airport in Tamale, Ghana, with the ICAO code DGLE. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,999 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AFW has 5 routes through DGLE. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Accra (ACC).
