About KFTY

Fulton County Airport Brown Field (FTY) is a medium airport in Atlanta, United States, with the ICAO code KFTY. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,797 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 97 taxiway segments and 11 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SUB has 2 routes through KFTY. Its most frequent links are St Louis (SUS), Charlotte (CLT) and Columbus (LCK).
