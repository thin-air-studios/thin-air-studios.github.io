About SBAQ

Araraquara Airport (AQA) is a medium airport in Araraquara, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBAQ. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,907 ft long and 99 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 9 routes through SBAQ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Campinas (VCP).
