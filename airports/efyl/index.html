About EFYL

Ylivieska Airfield (YLI) is a medium airport in Ylivieska, Finland, with the ICAO code EFYL. It has one runway, 15/33, 4,921 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EFYL.
