About ZWAL

Aral Tarim Airport (ACF) is a small airport in Aral, China, with the ICAO code ZWAL. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 29 routes through ZWAL. Its most frequent links are Ürümqi (URC), Zhengzhou (CGO) and Lanzhou (LHW).
