About HESC

Saint Catherine International Airport (SKV) is a medium airport in Saint Catherine, Egypt, with the ICAO code HESC. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,939 ft long and 118 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HESC.
