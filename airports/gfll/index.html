About GFLL

Lungi International Airport (FNA) is a large airport in Freetown (Lungi-Town), Sierra Leone, with the ICAO code GFLL. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,498 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 49 routes through GFLL. Its most frequent links are Monrovia (ROB), Zaventem (BRU) and Banjul (BJL).
