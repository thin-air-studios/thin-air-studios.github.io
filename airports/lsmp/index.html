About LSMP

Payerne Air Base (VIP) is a medium airport in Payerne, Switzerland, with the ICAO code LSMP. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,376 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 182 taxiway segments and 17 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LSMP.
