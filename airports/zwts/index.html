About ZWTS

Tumxuk Tangwangcheng Airport (TWC) is a small airport in Tumxuk, China, with the ICAO code ZWTS. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 31 routes through ZWTS. Its most frequent links are Ürümqi (URC), Guangzhou (CAN) and Zhengzhou (CGO).
