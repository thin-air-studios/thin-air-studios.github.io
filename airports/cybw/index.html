About CYBW

Calgary / Springbank Airport (CYBW) is a medium airport in Calgary, Canada. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 17/35 and 08/26. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 115 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 17 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYBW.
