About EDLE

Essen-Mülheim Airport (ESS) is a small airport in Essen, Germany, with the ICAO code EDLE. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,095 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDLE.
