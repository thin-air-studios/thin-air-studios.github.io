About KMEM

Frederick W. Smith International Airport (MEM) is a large airport in Memphis, United States, with the ICAO code KMEM. Its 4 runways are 18C/36C, 18R/36L, 18L/36R and 09/27. The longest, 18C/36C, is 11,120 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 gates, 250 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 44 airlines have 1,659 routes through KMEM. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), Newark (EWR) and Chicago (ORD).
