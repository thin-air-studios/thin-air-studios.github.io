About KUGN

Waukegan National Airport (UGN) is a small airport in Chicago/Waukegan, United States, with the ICAO code KUGN. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 86 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KUGN.
