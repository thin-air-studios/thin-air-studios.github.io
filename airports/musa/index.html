About MUSA

San Antonio de los Baños Airfield (MUSA) is a small airport in San Antonio de los Baños, Cuba. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 05/23, 12/30 and 01/19. The longest, 05/23, is 11,799 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 122 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MUSA.
