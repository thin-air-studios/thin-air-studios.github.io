About YGIG

RAAF Gingin (YGIG) is a small airport in Australia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,997 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YGIG.
