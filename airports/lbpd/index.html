About LBPD

Plovdiv International Airport (PDV) is a large airport in Plovdiv, Bulgaria, with the ICAO code LBPD. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 93 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 119 routes through LBPD. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), London (STN) and Antalya (AYT).
