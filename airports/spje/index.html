About SPJE

Shumba Airport (JAE) is a medium airport in Jaén, Peru, with the ICAO code SPJE. It has one runway, 16/34, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 7 routes through SPJE. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Lima (LIM).
