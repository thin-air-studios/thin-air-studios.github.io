About KVQQ

Cecil Airport (VQQ) is a medium airport in Jacksonville, United States, with the ICAO code KVQQ. Its 4 runways are 18L/36R, 09R/27L, 18R/36L and 09L/27R. The longest, 18L/36R, is 12,504 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 1 routes through KVQQ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Cincinnati / Covington (CVG).
