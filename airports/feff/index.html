About FEFF

Bangui M'Poko International Airport (BGF) is a large airport in Bangui, Central African Republic, with the ICAO code FEFF. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 21 routes through FEFF. Its most frequent links are Douala (DLA), Yaoundé (NSI) and Paris (CDG).
