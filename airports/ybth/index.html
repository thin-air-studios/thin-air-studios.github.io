About YBTH

Bathurst Airport (BHS) is a medium airport in Bathurst, Australia, with the ICAO code YBTH. Its two runways are 17/35 and 08/26. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,594 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Rex has 24 routes through YBTH. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD) and Parkes (PKE).
