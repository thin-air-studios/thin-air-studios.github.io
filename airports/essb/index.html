About ESSB

Stockholm-Bromma Airport (BMA) is a medium airport in Stockholm, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESSB. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,472 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 52 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 505 routes through ESSB. Its most frequent links are Malmö (MMX), Visby (VBY) and Ängelholm (AGH).
