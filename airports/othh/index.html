About OTHH

Hamad International Airport (DOH) is a large airport in Doha, Qatar, with the ICAO code OTHH. It is number 33 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 54.3 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 15,912 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 107 gates, 580 taxiway segments, 50 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 89 airlines have 4,833 routes through OTHH. Its most frequent links are London (LHR), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and Amsterdam (AMS).
