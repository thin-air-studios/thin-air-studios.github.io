About KGCC

Northeast Wyoming Regional Airport (GCC) is a medium airport in Gillette, United States, with the ICAO code KGCC. Its two runways are 16/34 and 03/21. The longer one, 16/34, is 7,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 18 routes through KGCC. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN) and Billings (BIL).
