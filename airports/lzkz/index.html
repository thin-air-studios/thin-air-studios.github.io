About LZKZ

Košice International Airport (KSC) is a medium airport in Košice, Slovakia, with the ICAO code LZKZ. It has one runway, 01/19, 10,171 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 35 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 319 routes through LZKZ. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Luton (LTN) and Burgas (BOJ).
