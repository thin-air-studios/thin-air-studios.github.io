About KMFE

McAllen Miller International Airport (MFE) is a medium airport in McAllen, United States, with the ICAO code KMFE. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,120 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 37 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 108 routes through KMFE. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Las Vegas (LAS).
