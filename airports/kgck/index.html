About KGCK

Garden City Regional Airport (GCK) is a medium airport in Garden City, United States, with the ICAO code KGCK. Its two runways are 17/35 and 12/30. The longer one, 17/35, is 7,299 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 14 routes through KGCK. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Wichita (ICT).
