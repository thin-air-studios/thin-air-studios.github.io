About YPDN

Darwin International Airport / RAAF Darwin (DRW) is a large airport in Darwin, Australia, with the ICAO code YPDN. Its two runways are 11/29 and 18/36. The longer one, 11/29, is 11,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 260 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 238 routes through YPDN. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Melbourne (MEL) and Sydney (SYD).
