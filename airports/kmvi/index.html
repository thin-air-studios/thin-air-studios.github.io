About KMVI

Monte Vista Municipal Airport (KMVI) is a small airport in Monte Vista, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 02/20, 16/34 and 10/28. The longest, 02/20, is 5,902 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMVI.
