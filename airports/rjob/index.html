About RJOB

Okayama Momotaro Airport (OKJ) is a large airport in Okayama, Japan, with the ICAO code RJOB. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,843 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 31 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 78 routes through RJOB. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Naha (OKA) and Taoyuan (TPE).
