About KMUO

Mountain Home Air Force Base (MUO) is a medium airport in Mountain Home, United States, with the ICAO code KMUO. It has one runway, 12/30, 13,510 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMUO.
