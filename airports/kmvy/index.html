About KMVY

Martha's Vineyard Airport (MVY) is a small airport in Martha's Vineyard, United States, with the ICAO code KMVY. Its two runways are 06/24 and 15/33. The longer one, 06/24, is 5,504 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 83 routes through KMVY. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS), New York (JFK) and Washington (DCA).
