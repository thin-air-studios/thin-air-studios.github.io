About SERO

Santa Rosa - Artillery Colonel Victor Larrea International Airport (ETR) is a medium airport in Santa Rosa, Ecuador, with the ICAO code SERO. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,625 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, RER has 3 routes through SERO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Quito (UIO).
