About LGBL

Nea Anchialos National Airport (VOL) is a medium airport in Nea Anchialos, Greece, with the ICAO code LGBL. It has one runway, 08/26, 9,052 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 128 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 90 routes through LGBL. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Vienna (VIE) and London (LGW).
