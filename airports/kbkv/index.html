About KBKV

Brooksville–Tampa Bay Regional Airport (KBKV) is a small airport in Brooksville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 09/27 and 03/21. The longer one, 09/27, is 7,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments and 9 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBKV.
