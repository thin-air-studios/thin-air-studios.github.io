About RPMN

Sanga Sanga Airport (TWT) is a medium airport in Bongao, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPMN. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,102 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through RPMN. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Zamboanga (ZAM).
