About KMSL

Northwest Alabama Regional Airport (MSL) is a medium airport in Muscle Shoals, United States, with the ICAO code KMSL. Its two runways are 11/29 and 18/36. The longer one, 11/29, is 6,693 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 11 routes through KMSL. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Atlanta (ATL) and Pensacola (PNS).
