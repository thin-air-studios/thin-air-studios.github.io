About LEVD

Valladolid Airport (VLL) is a medium airport in Valladolid, Spain, with the ICAO code LEVD. Its two runways are 05/23 and 15/33. The longer one, 05/23, is 9,843 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 119 routes through LEVD. Its most frequent links are Barcelona (BCN), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Gran Canaria Island (LPA).
