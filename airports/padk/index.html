About PADK

Adak Airport (ADK) is a medium airport in Adak, United States, with the ICAO code PADK. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,790 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Alaska has 1 routes through PADK. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Cold Bay (CDB).
