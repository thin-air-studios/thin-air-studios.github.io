About KTVR

Vicksburg Tallulah Regional Airport (KTVR) is a small airport in Tallulah, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,002 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTVR.
