About RJSC

Yamagata Airport (GAJ) is a medium airport in Higashine, Japan, with the ICAO code RJSC. It has one runway, 01/19, 6,560 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 6 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 36 routes through RJSC. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (ITM) and Nagoya (NKM).
