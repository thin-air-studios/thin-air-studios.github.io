About VDPP

Phnom Penh International Airport (PNH) is a large airport in Phnom Penh (Pou Senchey), Cambodia, with the ICAO code VDPP. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,843 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 32 airlines have 173 routes through VDPP. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (BKK), Sepang (KUL) and Shenzhen (SZX).
