About VIJU

Jammu Airport (IXJ) is a medium airport in Jammu, India, with the ICAO code VIJU. It has one runway, 18/36, 6,700 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 81 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 100 routes through VIJU. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Srinagar (SXR) and Mumbai (BOM).
