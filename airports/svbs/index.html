About SVBS

Escuela Mariscal Sucre Airport (MYC) is a medium airport in Maracay, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVBS. Its two runways are 11/29 and 06/24. The longer one, 11/29, is 6,846 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SVBS.
