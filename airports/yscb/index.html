About YSCB

Canberra Airport (CBR) is a medium airport in Canberra, Australia, with the ICAO code YSCB. Its two runways are 17/35 and 12/30. The longer one, 17/35, is 10,771 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 111 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 648 routes through YSCB. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Melbourne (MEL) and Brisbane (BNE).
