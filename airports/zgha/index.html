About ZGHA

Changsha Huanghua International Airport (CSX) is a large airport in Changsha (Changsha), China, with the ICAO code ZGHA. Its two runways are 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longer one, 18L/36R, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 gates, 246 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 66 airlines have 1,285 routes through ZGHA. Its most frequent links are Xi'an (XIY), Haikou (HAK) and Kunming (KMG).
