About KNYL

Yuma International Airport / Marine Corps Air Station Yuma (YUM) is a medium airport in Yuma, United States, with the ICAO code KNYL. Its 3 runways are 03L/21R, 03R/21L and 08/26. The longest, 03L/21R, is 13,300 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 127 taxiway segments, 25 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 35 routes through KNYL. Its most frequent links are Phoenix (PHX), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Los Angeles (LAX).
