About KVNY

Van Nuys Airport (VNY) is a medium airport in Van Nuys, United States, with the ICAO code KVNY. Its two runways are 16R/34L and 16L/34R. The longer one, 16R/34L, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 168 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, UPA has 1 routes through KVNY. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Santa Ana (SNA).
