About UUDD

Domodedovo International Airport (DME) is a large airport in Moscow, Russia, with the ICAO code UUDD. Its two runways are 13R/31L and 13C/31C. The longer one, 13R/31L, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 gates, 544 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 135 airlines have 3,778 routes through UUDD. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Simferopol (SIP) and Sochi (AER).
