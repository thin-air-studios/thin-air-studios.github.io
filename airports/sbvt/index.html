About SBVT

Eurico de Aguiar Salles International Airport (VIX) is a large airport in Vitória, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBVT. Its two runways are 02/20 and 06/24. The longer one, 02/20, is 6,752 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 76 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 256 routes through SBVT. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Rio De Janeiro (GIG) and Belo Horizonte (CNF).
