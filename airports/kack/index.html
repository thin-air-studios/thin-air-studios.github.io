About KACK

Nantucket Memorial Airport (ACK) is a medium airport in Nantucket, United States, with the ICAO code KACK. Its two runways are 06/24 and 15/33. The longer one, 06/24, is 6,303 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 53 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 112 routes through KACK. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS), New York (JFK) and New York (LGA).
