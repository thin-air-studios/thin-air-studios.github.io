About RJSM

Misawa Airport / Misawa Air Base (MSJ) is a medium airport in Misawa, Japan, with the ICAO code RJSM. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 139 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 26 routes through RJSM. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (ITM) and Sapporo (OKD).
