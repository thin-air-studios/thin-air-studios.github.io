About VIDX

Hindon Airport / Hindon Air Force Station (HDO) is a small airport in Ghaziabad, India, with the ICAO code VIDX. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 68 routes through VIDX. Its most frequent links are Bengaluru (BLR), Chennai (MAA) and Mumbai (BOM).
