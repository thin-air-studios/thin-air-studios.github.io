About EKBI

Billund Airport (BLL) is a large airport in Billund, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKBI. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,172 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 52 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 106 airlines have 1,935 routes through EKBI. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Copenhagen (CPH) and Paris (CDG).
