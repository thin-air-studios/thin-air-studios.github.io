About OIAI

Shahid Asiyaee Airport (QMJ) is a medium airport in Masjed Soleyman, Iran, with the ICAO code OIAI. Its two runways are 14/32 and 12/30. The longer one, 14/32, is 5,907 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 29 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OIAI.
