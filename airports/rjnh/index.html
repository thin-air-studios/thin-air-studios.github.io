About RJNH

JASDF Hamamatsu Air Base (RJNH) is a medium airport in Hamamatsu, Japan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,366 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 78 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RJNH.
