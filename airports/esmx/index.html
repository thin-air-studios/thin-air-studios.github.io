About ESMX

Växjö Kronoberg Airport (VXO) is a medium airport in Växjö, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESMX. It has one runway, 01/19, 6,900 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 116 routes through ESMX. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (BMA), Alicante (ALC) and Stockholm (ARN).
