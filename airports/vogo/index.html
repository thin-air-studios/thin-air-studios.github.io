About VOGO

Goa Dabolim International Airport (GOI) is a large airport in Vasco da Gama, India, with the ICAO code VOGO. It has one runway, 08/26, 11,345 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 447 routes through VOGO. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
