About KXMR

Cape Canaveral SFS Skid Strip (KXMR) is a medium airport in Cocoa Beach, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,999 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 19 aprons.
