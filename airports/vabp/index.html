About VABP

Raja Bhoj International Airport (BHO) is a large airport in Bhopal, India, with the ICAO code VABP. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,022 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 115 routes through VABP. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
