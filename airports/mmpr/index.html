About MMPR

Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR) is a large airport in Puerto Vallarta, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMPR. It has one runway, 04/22, 10,171 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 82 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 32 airlines have 466 routes through MMPR. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Vancouver (YVR) and Calgary (YYC).
