About KSFB

Orlando Sanford International Airport (SFB) is a large airport in Orlando, United States, with the ICAO code KSFB. Its 4 runways are 09L/27R, 18/36, 09R/27L and 09C/27C. The longest, 09L/27R, is 11,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 140 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 551 routes through KSFB. Its most frequent links are Allentown/Bethlehem (ABE), Grand Rapids (GRR) and Asheville (AVL).
