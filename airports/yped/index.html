About YPED

RAAF Base Edinburgh (YPED) is a medium airport in Adelaide, Australia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,399 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YPED.
