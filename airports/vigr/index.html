About VIGR

Gwalior Airport (GWL) is a medium airport in Gwalior, India, with the ICAO code VIGR. It has one runway, 06/24, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 577 taxiway segments and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 26 routes through VIGR. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
