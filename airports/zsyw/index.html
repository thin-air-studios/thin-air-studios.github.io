About ZSYW

Yiwu Airport (YIW) is a large airport in Yiwu/Jinhua, China, with the ICAO code ZSYW. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 143 routes through ZSYW. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Beijing (PKX) and Chengdu (TFU).
