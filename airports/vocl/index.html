About VOCL

Calicut International Airport (CCJ) is a large airport in Calicut, India, with the ICAO code VOCL. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,383 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 22 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 257 routes through VOCL. Its most frequent links are Bengaluru (BLR), Abu Dhabi (AUH) and Dubai (DXB).
