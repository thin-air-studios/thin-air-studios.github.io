About USNN

Nizhnevartovsk Airport (NJC) is a large airport in Nizhnevartovsk, Russia, with the ICAO code USNN. It has one runway, 03/21, 10,499 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 131 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 117 routes through USNN. Its most frequent links are Ufa (UFA), Novosibirsk (OVB) and Moscow (SVO).
