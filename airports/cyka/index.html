About CYKA

Kamloops John Moose Fulton Field Regional Airport (YKA) is a medium airport in Kamloops, Canada, with the ICAO code CYKA. Its two runways are 09/27 and 05/23. The longer one, 09/27, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 82 routes through CYKA. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC), Vancouver (YVR) and Kelowna (YLW).
