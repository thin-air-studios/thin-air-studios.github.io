About KJKA

Jack Edwards National Airport (GUF) is a small airport in Gulf Shores, United States, with the ICAO code KJKA. Its two runways are 09/27 and 17/35. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,962 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 109 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Allegiant has 37 routes through KJKA. Its most frequent links are Nashville (BNA), Fayetteville/Springdale/Rogers (XNA) and Cincinnati / Covington (CVG).
