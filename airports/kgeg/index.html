About KGEG

Spokane International Airport (GEG) is a large airport in Spokane, United States, with the ICAO code KGEG. Its two runways are 03/21 and 08/26. The longer one, 03/21, is 11,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 gates, 161 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 358 routes through KGEG. Its most frequent links are Seattle (SEA), Denver (DEN) and Portland (PDX).
