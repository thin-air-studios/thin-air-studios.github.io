About NZHN

Hamilton International Airport (HLZ) is a medium airport in Hamilton, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZHN. Its 4 runways are 18L/36R, 18R/36L, 07/25 and 07R/25L. The longest, 18L/36R, is 6,755 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 156 routes through NZHN. Its most frequent links are Wellington (WLG), Christchurch (CHC) and Auckland (AKL).
