About KIOW

Iowa City Municipal Airport (IOW) is a small airport in Iowa City, United States, with the ICAO code KIOW. Its two runways are 07/25 and 12/30. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KIOW.
