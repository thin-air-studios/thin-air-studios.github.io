About SAEZ

Ezeiza International Airport - Ministro Pistarini (EZE) is a large airport in Buenos Aires (Ezeiza), Argentina, with the ICAO code SAEZ. Its two runways are 11/29 and 17/35. The longer one, 11/29, is 10,827 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 50 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 77 airlines have 888 routes through SAEZ. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), São Paulo (GRU) and Rio De Janeiro (GIG).
