About KFAT

Fresno Yosemite International Airport (FAT) is a large airport in Fresno, United States, with the ICAO code KFAT. Its two runways are 11L/29R and 11R/29L. The longer one, 11L/29R, is 9,539 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 114 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 250 routes through KFAT. Its most frequent links are Las Vegas (LAS), Guadalajara (GDL) and Denver (DEN).
