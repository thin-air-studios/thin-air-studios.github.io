About UZTT

Tashkent International Airport (TAS) is a large airport in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, with the ICAO code UZTT. Its two runways are 08L/26R and 08R/26L. The longer one, 08L/26R, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 112 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UZTT.
