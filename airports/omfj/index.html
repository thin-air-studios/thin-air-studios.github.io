About OMFJ

Fujairah International Airport (FJR) is a large airport in Fujairah, United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMFJ. It has one runway, 11R/29L, 10,007 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 31 routes through OMFJ. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), Muscat/Seeb (MCT) and New Delhi (DEL).
