About MDLR

Casa De Campo International Airport (LRM) is a large airport in La Romana, Dominican Republic, with the ICAO code MDLR. It has one runway, 11/29, 9,678 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 141 routes through MDLR. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (FRA), Ferno (MXP) and Rome (FCO).
