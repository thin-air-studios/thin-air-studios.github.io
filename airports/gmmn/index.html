About GMMN

Mohammed V International Airport (CMN) is a large airport in Casablanca, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMMN. Its two runways are 17L/35R and 17R/35L. The longer one, 17L/35R, is 12,205 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 99 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 57 airlines have 1,749 routes through GMMN. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Zaventem (BRU).
