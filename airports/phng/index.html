About PHNG

Kaneohe Bay MCAS (Marion E. Carl Field) Airport (NGF) is a medium airport in Kaneohe, United States, with the ICAO code PHNG. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,771 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 168 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 3 routes through PHNG. Its most frequent links are Riverside (RIV), Victorville (VCV) and Iwakuni (IWK).
