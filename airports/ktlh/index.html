About KTLH

Tallahassee International Airport (TLH) is a medium airport in Tallahassee, United States, with the ICAO code KTLH. Its two runways are 09/27 and 18/36. The longer one, 09/27, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 171 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 82 routes through KTLH. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Atlanta (ATL) and Miami (MIA).
