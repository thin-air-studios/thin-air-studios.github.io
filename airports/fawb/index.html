About FAWB

Wonderboom Airport (PRY) is a medium airport in Pretoria, South Africa, with the ICAO code FAWB. Its two runways are 11/29 and 06/24. The longer one, 11/29, is 5,998 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 188 taxiway segments, 24 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, South African has 4 routes through FAWB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Cape Town (CPT).
