About KSCK

Stockton Metropolitan Airport (SCK) is a medium airport in Stockton, United States, with the ICAO code KSCK. Its two runways are 11L/29R and 11R/29L. The longer one, 11L/29R, is 10,245 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 58 routes through KSCK. Its most frequent links are Cincinnati / Covington (CVG), Las Vegas (LAS) and Chicago/Rockford (RFD).
