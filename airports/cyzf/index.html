About CYZF

Yellowknife International Airport (YZF) is a medium airport in Yellowknife, Canada, with the ICAO code CYZF. Its two runways are 16/34 and 10/28. The longer one, 16/34, is 7,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 31 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 76 routes through CYZF. Its most frequent links are Edmonton (YEG), Calgary (YYC) and Vancouver (YVR).
