About EDXW

Westerland Sylt Airport (GWT) is a medium airport in Sylt, Germany, with the ICAO code EDXW. Its two runways are 14/32 and 06/24. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,955 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 41 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 64 routes through EDXW. Its most frequent links are Düsseldorf (DUS), Zurich (ZRH) and Stuttgart (STR).
