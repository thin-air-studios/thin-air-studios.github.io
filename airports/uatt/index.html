About UATT

Aktobe International Airport (AKX) is a large airport in Aktobe, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UATT. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,505 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 58 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 80 routes through UATT. Its most frequent links are Almaty (ALA), Astana (NQZ) and Antalya (AYT).
