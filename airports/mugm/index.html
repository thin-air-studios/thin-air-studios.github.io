About MUGM

Leeward Point Field (NBW) is a medium airport in Guantanamo Bay Naval Station, Cuba, with the ICAO code MUGM. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,000 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 8 routes through MUGM. Its most frequent links are Norfolk (NGU), Jacksonville (NIP) and Fort Bliss/El Paso (BIF).
