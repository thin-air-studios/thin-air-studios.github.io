About KAWO

Arlington Municipal Airport (KAWO) is a medium airport in Arlington, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 16/34 and 11/29. The longer one, 16/34, is 5,332 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 92 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAWO.
