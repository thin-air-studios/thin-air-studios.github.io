About KSBD

San Bernardino International Airport (SBD) is a large airport in San Bernardino, United States, with the ICAO code KSBD. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,000 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 73 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 103 routes through KSBD. Its most frequent links are Honolulu (HNL), Austin (AUS) and Portland (PDX).
