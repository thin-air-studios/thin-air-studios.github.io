About KMCC

McClellan Airfield (MCC) is a medium airport in Sacramento, United States, with the ICAO code KMCC. It has one runway, 16/34, 10,599 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 158 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMCC.
