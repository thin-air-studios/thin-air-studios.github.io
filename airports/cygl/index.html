About CYGL

La Grande Rivière Airport (YGL) is a medium airport in La Grande Rivière, Canada, with the ICAO code CYGL. It has one runway, 13/31, 6,500 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 11 routes through CYGL. Its most frequent links are Rouyn-Noranda (YUY), La Grande-3 (YAR) and La Grande-4 (YAH).
