About KGED

Delaware Coastal Airport (GED) is a small airport in Georgetown, United States, with the ICAO code KGED. Its two runways are 04/22 and 10/28. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Kuwait has 1 routes through KGED. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Bangor (BGR).
