About EGPD

Aberdeen International Airport (ABZ) is a large airport in Aberdeen, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGPD. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,407 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 142 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 50 airlines have 1,378 routes through EGPD. Its most frequent links are Lerwick (LSI), London (LHR) and London (LGW).
