About OISL

Lar Airport (LRR) is a medium airport in Lar, Iran, with the ICAO code OISL. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,397 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 15 routes through OISL. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Kuwait City (KWI) and Doha (DOH).
