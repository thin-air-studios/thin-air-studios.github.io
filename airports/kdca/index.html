About KDCA

Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA) is a large airport in Washington, United States, with the ICAO code KDCA. Its 3 runways are 01/19, 15/33 and 04/22. The longest, 01/19, is 7,169 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 gates, 134 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 2,291 routes through KDCA. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS), Chicago (ORD) and New York (LGA).
