About ENSB

Svalbard Airport, Longyear (LYR) is a medium airport in Longyearbyen, Norway, with the ICAO code ENSB. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,608 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 33 routes through ENSB. Its most frequent links are Tromsø (TOS), Oslo (OSL) and Paris (CDG).
