About PABE

Bethel Airport (BET) is a medium airport in Bethel, United States, with the ICAO code PABE. Its 3 runways are 01L/19R, 01R/19L and 12/30. The longest, 01L/19R, is 6,400 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 45 routes through PABE. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), King Salmon (AKN) and Seattle (SEA).
