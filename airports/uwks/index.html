About UWKS

Cheboksary Airport (CSY) is a medium airport in Cheboksary, Russia, with the ICAO code UWKS. Its two runways are 06/24 and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06/24, is 8,241 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 29 routes through UWKS. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Moscow (VKO) and Sochi (AER).
