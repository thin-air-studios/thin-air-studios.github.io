About HLWN

Waddan Airport (HLWN) is a small airport in Libya. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 6,515 ft long and 70 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HLWN.
