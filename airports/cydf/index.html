About CYDF

Deer Lake Airport (YDF) is a medium airport in Deer Lake, Canada, with the ICAO code CYDF. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,005 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 69 routes through CYDF. Its most frequent links are Halifax (YHZ), Toronto (YYZ) and St. John's (YYT).
