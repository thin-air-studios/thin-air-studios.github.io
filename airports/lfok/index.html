About LFOK

Chalons Vatry airport (XCR) is a medium airport in Chalons en Champagne, France, with the ICAO code LFOK. It has one runway, 10/28, 12,664 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 135 taxiway segments, 52 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 32 airlines have 112 routes through LFOK. Its most frequent links are Porto (OPO), Marrakesh (RAK) and Moscow (SVO).
