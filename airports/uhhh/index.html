About UHHH

Khabarovsk Novy Airport (KHV) is a medium airport in Khabarovsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UHHH. Its two runways are 05R/23L and 05L/23R. The longer one, 05R/23L, is 13,124 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 176 routes through UHHH. Its most frequent links are Novosibirsk (OVB), Moscow (SVO) and Irkutsk (IKT).
