About KTMB

Miami Executive Airport (TMB) is a medium airport in Miami, United States, with the ICAO code KTMB. Its 3 runways are 09R/27L, 09L/27R and 13/31. The longest, 09R/27L, is 6,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 151 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Solairus has 1 routes through KTMB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is North Eleuthera (ELH).
