About BGSF

Kangerlussuaq International Airport (SFJ) is a medium airport in Kangerlussuaq, Greenland, with the ICAO code BGSF. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,219 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 19 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 54 routes through BGSF. Its most frequent links are Nuuk (GOH), Copenhagen (CPH) and Ilulissat (JAV).
