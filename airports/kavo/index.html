About KAVO

Avon Park Executive Airport (AVO) is a small airport in Avon Park, United States, with the ICAO code KAVO. Its two runways are 05/23 and 10/28. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,374 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAVO.
