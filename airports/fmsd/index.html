About FMSD

Tôlanaro Airport (FTU) is a medium airport in Tôlanaro, Madagascar, with the ICAO code FMSD. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,280 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 19 routes through FMSD. Its most frequent links are Antananarivo (TNR), Toliara (TLE) and Sainte-Marie (RUN).
