About KDET

Coleman A. Young Municipal Airport (DET) is a medium airport in Detroit, United States, with the ICAO code KDET. It has one runway, 15/33, 5,092 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDET.
