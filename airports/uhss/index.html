About UHSS

Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk International Airport (UUS) is a large airport in Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UHSS. It has one runway, 01/19, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 91 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 80 routes through UHSS. Its most frequent links are Artyom (VVO), Moscow (SVO) and Khabarovsk (KHV).
