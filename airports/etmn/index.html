About ETMN

Sea-Airport Cuxhaven/Nordholz / Nordholz Naval Airbase (FCN) is a medium airport in Wurster Nordseeküste, Germany, with the ICAO code ETMN. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,002 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 119 taxiway segments, 43 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETMN.
