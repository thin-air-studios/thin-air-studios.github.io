About KNZY

North Island Naval Air Station-Halsey Field (NZY) is a medium airport in San Diego, United States, with the ICAO code KNZY. Its two runways are 18/36 and 11/29. The longer one, 18/36, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through KNZY. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), Fort Bliss/El Paso (BIF) and Baltimore (BWI).
