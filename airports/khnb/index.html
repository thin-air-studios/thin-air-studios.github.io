About KHNB

Huntingburg Airport (HNB) is a small airport in Huntingburg, United States, with the ICAO code KHNB. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,501 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments and 9 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHNB.
