About URWI

Elista Airport (ESL) is a medium airport in Elista, Russia, with the ICAO code URWI. Its two runways are 09/27 and 09L/27R. The longer one, 09/27, is 10,499 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through URWI. Its most frequent links are Moscow (VKO), St. Petersburg (LED) and Moscow (DME).
