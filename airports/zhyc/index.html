About ZHYC

Yichang Sanxia Airport (YIH) is a medium airport in Yichang (Xiaoting), China, with the ICAO code ZHYC. It has one runway, 14/32, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 55 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 157 routes through ZHYC. Its most frequent links are Shenzhen (SZX), Guangzhou (CAN) and Beijing (PEK).
