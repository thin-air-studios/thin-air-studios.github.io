About KGLR

Gaylord Regional Airport (GLR) is a small airport in Gaylord, United States, with the ICAO code KGLR. Its two runways are 09/27 and 18/36. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,579 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, IRO has 2 routes through KGLR. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Flint (FNT).
