About KLCQ

Lake City Gateway Airport (LCQ) is a small airport in Lake City, United States, with the ICAO code KLCQ. Its two runways are 10/28 and 05/23. The longer one, 10/28, is 8,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 4 routes through KLCQ. Its most frequent links are New York (JFK), Gainesville (GNV) and Minneapolis (MSP).
