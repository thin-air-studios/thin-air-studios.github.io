About RJEC

Asahikawa Airport (AKJ) is a medium airport in Higashikagura, Japan, with the ICAO code RJEC. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,200 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 82 routes through RJEC. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Narita (NRT) and Taoyuan (TPE).
