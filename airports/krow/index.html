About KROW

Roswell Air Center Airport (ROW) is a medium airport in Roswell, United States, with the ICAO code KROW. Its two runways are 03/21 and 17/35. The longer one, 03/21, is 13,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 30 routes through KROW. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and New York (JFK).
