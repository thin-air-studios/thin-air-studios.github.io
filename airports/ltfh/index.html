About LTFH

Samsun-Çarşamba Airport (SZF) is a medium airport in Samsun, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTFH. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 380 routes through LTFH. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Düsseldorf (DUS).
