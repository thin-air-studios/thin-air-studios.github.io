About KMZJ

Pinal Airpark (MZJ) is a small airport in Marana, United States, with the ICAO code KMZJ. It has one runway, 12/30, 6,849 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 100 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMZJ.
