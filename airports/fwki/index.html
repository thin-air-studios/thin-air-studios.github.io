About FWKI

Kamuzu International Airport (LLW) is a large airport in Lumbadzi, Malawi, with the ICAO code FWKI. It has one runway, 14/32, 11,614 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 20 routes through FWKI. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Nairobi (NBO) and Blantyre (BLZ).
