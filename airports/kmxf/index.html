About KMXF

Maxwell Air Force Base (MXF) is a medium airport in Montgomery, United States, with the ICAO code KMXF. Its two runways are 15/33 and 009/189. The longer one, 15/33, is 8,008 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMXF.
