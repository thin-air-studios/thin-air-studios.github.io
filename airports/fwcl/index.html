About FWCL

Chileka International Airport (BLZ) is a large airport in Blantyre, Malawi, with the ICAO code FWCL. Its two runways are 10/28 and 15/33. The longer one, 10/28, is 7,628 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through FWCL. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Lumbadzi (LLW) and Addis Ababa (ADD).
