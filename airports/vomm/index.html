About VOMM

Chennai International Airport (MAA) is a large airport in Chennai, India, with the ICAO code VOMM. Its two runways are 07/25 and 12/30. The longer one, 07/25, is 12,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 104 gates, 113 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 73 airlines have 1,794 routes through VOMM. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
