About KRQO

El Reno Regional Airport (RQO) is a small airport in El Reno, United States, with the ICAO code KRQO. Its two runways are 17/35 and 18/36. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,600 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRQO.
