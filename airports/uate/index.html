About UATE

Aktau International Airport (SCO) is a large airport in Aktau, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UATE. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,013 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 148 routes through UATE. Its most frequent links are Almaty (ALA), Atyrau (GUW) and Astana (NQZ).
