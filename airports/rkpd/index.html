About RKPD

Jeongseok Airport (JDG) is a medium airport in Jeju Island, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKPD. Its two runways are 01/19 and 15/33. The longer one, 01/19, is 7,589 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RKPD.
