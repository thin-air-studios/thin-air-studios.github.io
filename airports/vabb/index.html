About VABB

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) is a large airport in Mumbai, India, with the ICAO code VABB. It is number 28 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 55.5 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 09/27 and 14/32. The longer one, 09/27, is 11,511 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 gates, 317 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 106 airlines have 4,113 routes through VABB. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR) and Kolkata (CCU).
