About KOMA

Eppley Airfield (OMA) is a large airport in Omaha, United States, with the ICAO code KOMA. Its 3 runways are 14R/32L, 14L/32R and 18/36. The longest, 14R/32L, is 9,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 138 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 515 routes through KOMA. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Chicago (ORD) and Houston (IAH).
