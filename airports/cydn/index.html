About CYDN

Dauphin Barker Airport (YDN) is a medium airport in Dauphin, Canada, with the ICAO code CYDN. Its 3 runways are 14/32, 08/26 and 02/20. The longest, 14/32, is 5,000 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYDN.
