About KINS

Creech Air Force Base (INS) is a small airport in Indian Springs, United States, with the ICAO code KINS. Its two runways are 08/26 and 13/31. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 90 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KINS.
