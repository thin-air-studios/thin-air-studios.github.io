About VTSF

Nakhon Si Thammarat Airport (NST) is a medium airport in Nakhon Si Thammarat, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTSF. It has one runway, 01/19, 6,890 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 58 routes through VTSF. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (DMK) and Bangkok (BKK).
