About LFRS

Nantes Atlantique Airport (NTE) is a large airport in Nantes, France, with the ICAO code LFRS. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,514 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 71 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 83 airlines have 3,738 routes through LFRS. Its most frequent links are Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS), Nice (NCE) and Paris (CDG).
