About RJOH

Yonago Kitaro Airport / JASDF Miho Air Base (YGJ) is a medium airport in Yonago, Japan, with the ICAO code RJOH. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,202 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 55 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 36 routes through RJOH. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Hong Kong (HKG) and Narita (NRT).
