About KBIE

Beatrice Municipal Airport (BIE) is a small airport in Beatrice, United States, with the ICAO code KBIE. Its two runways are 18/36 and 14/32. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,602 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AMF has 1 routes through KBIE. Its most frequent links are Kearney (EAR) and Omaha (OMA).
