About SVMG

Del Caribe Santiago Mariño International Airport (PMV) is a large airport in Isla Margarita, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVMG. Its two runways are 09/27 and 09L/27R. The longer one, 09/27, is 10,433 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 100 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 100 routes through SVMG. Its most frequent links are Maiquetía (CCS), Guyana City (PZO) and Maracaibo (MAR).
