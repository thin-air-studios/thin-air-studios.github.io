About KCXY

Capital City Airport (HAR) is a small airport in Harrisburg, United States, with the ICAO code KCXY. Its two runways are 08/26 and 12/30. The longer one, 08/26, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 80 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCXY.
