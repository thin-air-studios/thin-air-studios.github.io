About KNEN

Whitehouse Naval Outlying Field (NEN) is a small airport in Jacksonville, United States, with the ICAO code KNEN. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,001 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNEN.
