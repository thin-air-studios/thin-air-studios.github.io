About SCTC

Maquehue Airport (PZS) is a medium airport in Temuco, Chile, with the ICAO code SCTC. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,577 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 21 routes through SCTC. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL) and Concepcion (CCP).
