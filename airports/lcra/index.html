About LCRA

RAF Akrotiri (AKT) is a medium airport in Akrotiri, Cyprus, with the ICAO code LCRA. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,002 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 114 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 27 routes through LCRA. Its most frequent links are Carterton (BZZ), Warton (WRT) and Darlington (MME).
