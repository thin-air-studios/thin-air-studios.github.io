About CYVV

Wiarton Airport (YVV) is a medium airport in Wiarton, Canada, with the ICAO code CYVV. Its two runways are 05/23 and 11/29. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,021 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYVV.
