About OERK

King Khalid International Airport (RUH) is a large airport in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OERK. Its two runways are 15L/33R and 15R/33L. The longer one, 15L/33R, is 13,796 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 155 taxiway segments, 24 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 94 airlines have 1,866 routes through OERK. Its most frequent links are Jeddah (JED), Dubai (DXB) and Cairo (CAI).
