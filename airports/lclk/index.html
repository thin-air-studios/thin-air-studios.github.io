About LCLK

Larnaca International Airport (LCA) is a large airport in Larnaca, Cyprus, with the ICAO code LCLK. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,823 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 78 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 150 airlines have 2,936 routes through LCLK. Its most frequent links are Tel Aviv (TLV), London (LGW) and Spata-Artemida (ATH).
