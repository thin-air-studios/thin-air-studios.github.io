About KMRY

Monterey Regional Airport (MRY) is a medium airport in Monterey, United States, with the ICAO code KMRY. Its two runways are 10R/28L and 10L/28R. The longer one, 10R/28L, is 7,175 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 109 routes through KMRY. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Los Angeles (LAX) and Phoenix (PHX).
