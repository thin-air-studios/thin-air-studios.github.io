About DAAP

Illizi Takhamalt Airport (VVZ) is a medium airport in Illizi, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAAP. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Air Algerie has 8 routes through DAAP. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG), Tamanrasset (TMR) and Es-Sénia (ORN).
