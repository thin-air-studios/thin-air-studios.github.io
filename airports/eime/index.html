About EIME

Casement Air Base (EIME) is a medium airport in Baldonnel, Ireland. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10/28 and 04/22. The longer one, 10/28, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EIME.
