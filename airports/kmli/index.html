About KMLI

Quad City International Airport (MLI) is a medium airport in Moline, United States, with the ICAO code KMLI. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 13/31 and 05/23. The longest, 09/27, is 10,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 93 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 154 routes through KMLI. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Atlanta (ATL) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
