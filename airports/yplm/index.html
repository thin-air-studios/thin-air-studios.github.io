About YPLM

Learmonth Airport (LEA) is a medium airport in Exmouth, Australia, with the ICAO code YPLM. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,997 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 13 routes through YPLM. Its most frequent links are Perth (PER) and Melbourne (MEL).
