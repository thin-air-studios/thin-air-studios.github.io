About SBPS

Porto Seguro International Airport (BPS) is a large airport in Porto Seguro, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBPS. It has one runway, 10/28, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 14 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 212 routes through SBPS. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Belo Horizonte (CNF) and São Paulo (CGH).
