About KDAG

Barstow Daggett Airport (DAG) is a medium airport in Daggett, United States, with the ICAO code KDAG. Its two runways are 08/26 and 04/22. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,402 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDAG.
