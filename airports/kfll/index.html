About KFLL

Fort Lauderdale Hollywood International Airport (FLL) is a large airport in Fort Lauderdale, United States, with the ICAO code KFLL. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 67 gates, 257 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 52 airlines have 1,887 routes through KFLL. Its most frequent links are Newark (EWR), Atlanta (ATL) and Toronto (YYZ).
