About KNBC

Beaufort MCAS - Merritt Field (KNBC) is a medium airport in Beaufort, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 12,202 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 116 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNBC.
