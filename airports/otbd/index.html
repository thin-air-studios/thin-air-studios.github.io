About OTBD

Doha International Airport (DIA) is a large airport in Doha, Qatar, with the ICAO code OTBD. It has one runway, 15/33, 15,000 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 407 taxiway segments, 32 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 29 routes through OTBD. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DWC), Doha (DOH) and Abu Dhabi (AUH).
