About BIRK

Reykjavík Domestic Airport (RKV) is a medium airport in Reykjavík, Iceland, with the ICAO code BIRK. Its two runways are 01/19 and 13/31. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,138 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 93 routes through BIRK. Its most frequent links are Akureyri (AEY), Egilsstaðir (EGS) and Ísafjörður (IFJ).
