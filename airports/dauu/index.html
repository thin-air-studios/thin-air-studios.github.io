About DAUU

Ain Beida Airport (OGX) is a medium airport in Ouargla, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAUU. Its two runways are 18/36 and 01/19. The longer one, 18/36, is 10,171 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 12 routes through DAUU. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG), Illizi (VVZ) and Tamanrasset (TMR).
