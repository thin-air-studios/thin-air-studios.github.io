About YBBN

Brisbane International Airport (BNE) is a large airport in Brisbane, Australia, with the ICAO code YBBN. Its two runways are 01R/19L and 01L/19R. The longer one, 01R/19L, is 11,680 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 gates, 326 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 56 airlines have 2,377 routes through YBBN. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Melbourne (MEL) and Cairns (CNS).
