About YSSY

Sydney Kingsford Smith International Airport (SYD) is a large airport in Sydney (Mascot), Australia, with the ICAO code YSSY. Its 3 runways are 16R/34L, 07/25 and 16L/34R. The longest, 16R/34L, is 12,999 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 73 gates, 319 taxiway segments, 49 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 102 airlines have 3,579 routes through YSSY. Its most frequent links are Melbourne (MEL), Brisbane (BNE) and Canberra (CBR).
