About VICG

Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport (IXC) is a large airport in Chandigarh, India, with the ICAO code VICG. It has one runway, 11/29, 12,467 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 94 taxiway segments, 22 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 317 routes through VICG. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
