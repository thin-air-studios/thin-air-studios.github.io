About UNBB

Barnaul Gherman Titov International Airport (BAX) is a large airport in Barnaul, Russia, with the ICAO code UNBB. It has one runway, 06/24, 9,350 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 91 routes through UNBB. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), St. Petersburg (LED) and Irkutsk (IKT).
