About HE28

Gebel El Basur Air Base (HE28) is a medium airport in Markaz Ad Delingat, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 18/36 and 15/33. The longer one, 18/36, is 10,568 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates and 128 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE28.
