About UEEE

Platon Oyunsky Yakutsk International Airport (YKS) is a large airport in Yakutsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UEEE. It has one runway, 05R/23L, 11,155 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 93 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 132 routes through UEEE. Its most frequent links are Novosibirsk (OVB), Moscow (DME) and Moscow (VKO).
