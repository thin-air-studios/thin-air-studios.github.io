About ZLXY

Xi'an Xianyang International Airport (XIY) is a large airport in Xi'an, China, with the ICAO code ZLXY. It is number 45 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 48.5 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 05L/23R, 05R/23L, 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longest, 05L/23R, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 105 gates, 498 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 79 airlines have 2,113 routes through ZLXY. Its most frequent links are Shenzhen (SZX), Hangzhou (HGH) and Beijing (PEK).
