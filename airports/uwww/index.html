About UWWW

Kurumoch International Airport (KUF) is a large airport in Samara, Russia, with the ICAO code UWWW. Its two runways are 15/33 and 05/23. The longer one, 15/33, is 9,846 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 147 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 44 airlines have 424 routes through UWWW. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Moscow (DME) and Moscow (SVO).
