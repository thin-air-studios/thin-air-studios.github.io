About OESH

Sharurah Domestic Airport (SHW) is a medium airport in Sharurah, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OESH. It has one runway, 08/26, 11,975 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Saudia has 10 routes through OESH. Its most frequent links are Riyadh (RUH), Jeddah (JED) and Abha (AHB).
