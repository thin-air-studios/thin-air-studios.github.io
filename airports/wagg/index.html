About WAGG

Tjilik Riwut Airport (PKY) is a medium airport in Palangkaraya, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAGG. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for WAGG.
