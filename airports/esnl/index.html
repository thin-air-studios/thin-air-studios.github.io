About ESNL

Lycksele Airport (LYC) is a medium airport in Lycksele, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESNL. It has one runway, 14/32, 6,564 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 27 routes through ESNL. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Vilhelmina (VHM) and Arvidsjaur (AJR).
