About KGRI

Central Nebraska Regional Airport (GRI) is a medium airport in Grand Island, United States, with the ICAO code KGRI. Its two runways are 17/35 and 13/31. The longer one, 17/35, is 7,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 19 routes through KGRI. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Omaha (OMA) and Mesa (AZA).
