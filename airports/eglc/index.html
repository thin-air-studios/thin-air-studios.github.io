About EGLC

London City Airport (LCY) is a medium airport in London, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGLC. It has one runway, 09/27, 4,948 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 37 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 1,756 routes through EGLC. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Zurich (ZRH) and Ingliston (EDI).
