About ZSOF

Hefei Xinqiao International Airport (HFE) is a large airport in Hefei, China, with the ICAO code ZSOF. It has one runway, 15/33, 11,155 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 155 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 49 airlines have 593 routes through ZSOF. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Shenzhen (SZX) and Kunming (KMG).
