About ZPNL

Ninglang Luguhu Airport (NLH) is a medium airport in Ninglang, China, with the ICAO code ZPNL. It has one runway, 05/23, 11,155 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 4 routes through ZPNL. Its most frequent links are Kunming (KMG) and Chengdu (CTU).
