About WSSS

Singapore Changi Airport (SIN) is a large airport in Singapore, Singapore, with the ICAO code WSSS. It is number 16 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 70.0 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 02C/20C and 02L/20R. The longer one, 02C/20C, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 132 gates, 829 taxiway segments, 181 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 154 airlines have 3,337 routes through WSSS. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Hong Kong (HKG) and Bangkok (BKK).
