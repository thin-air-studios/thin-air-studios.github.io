About CYWK

Wabush Airport (YWK) is a medium airport in Wabush, Canada, with the ICAO code CYWK. It has one runway, 18/36, 6,002 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 32 routes through CYWK. Its most frequent links are Sept-Îles (YZV), Montréal (YUL) and Saguenay (YBG).
