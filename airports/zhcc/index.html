About ZHCC

Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport (CGO) is a large airport in Zhengzhou, China, with the ICAO code ZHCC. Its two runways are 12L/30R and 12R/30L. The longer one, 12L/30R, is 11,811 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 with no surface listed. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 377 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 105 airlines have 1,557 routes through ZHCC. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Shenzhen (SZX) and Kunming (KMG).
