About KRHP

Western Carolina Regional Airport (KRHP) is a medium airport in Andrews, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,500 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 aprons.
