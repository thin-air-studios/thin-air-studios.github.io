About KIND

Indianapolis International Airport (IND) is a large airport in Indianapolis, United States, with the ICAO code KIND. Its 3 runways are 05L/23R, 05R/23L and 14/32. The longest, 05L/23R, is 11,200 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 gates, 260 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 45 airlines have 1,390 routes through KIND. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Newark (EWR) and Denver (DEN).
