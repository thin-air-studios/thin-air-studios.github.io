About KWRB

Robins Air Force Base (WRB) is a medium airport in Warner Robins, United States, with the ICAO code KWRB. It has one runway, 15/33, 12,001 ft long and 300 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 1 routes through KWRB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is New York (JFK).
