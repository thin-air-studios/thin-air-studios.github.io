About KSTC

Saint Cloud Regional Airport (STC) is a medium airport in Saint Cloud, United States, with the ICAO code KSTC. Its two runways are 13/31 and 05/23. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Allegiant has 6 routes through KSTC. Its most frequent links are Mesa (AZA) and Punta Gorda (PGD).
