About KIAG

Niagara Falls International Airport (IAG) is a medium airport in Niagara Falls, United States, with the ICAO code KIAG. Its two runways are 10/28 and 06/24. The longer one, 10/28, is 9,826 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 19 routes through KIAG. Its most frequent links are Pinellas Park (PIE), Punta Gorda (PGD) and Myrtle Beach (MYR).
