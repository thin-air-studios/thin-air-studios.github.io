About WICC

Husein Sastranegara International Airport (BDO) is a medium airport in Bandung, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WICC. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,381 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 111 routes through WICC. Its most frequent links are Surabaya (SUB), Kuta (DPS) and Sepang (KUL).
