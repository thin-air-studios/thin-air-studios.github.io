About KNTU

Oceana Naval Air Station (NTU) is a medium airport in Virginia Beach, United States, with the ICAO code KNTU. Its 4 runways are 05R/23L, 14L/32R, 05L/23R and 14R/32L. The longest, 05R/23L, is 12,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 61 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNTU.
