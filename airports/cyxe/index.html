About CYXE

Saskatoon John G. Diefenbaker International Airport (YXE) is a large airport in Saskatoon, Canada, with the ICAO code CYXE. Its two runways are 09/27 and 15/33. The longer one, 09/27, is 8,300 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 18 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 282 routes through CYXE. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC), Vancouver (YVR) and Toronto (YYZ).
