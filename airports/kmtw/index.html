About KMTW

Manitowoc County Airport (MTW) is a small airport in Manitowoc, United States, with the ICAO code KMTW. Its two runways are 17/35 and 07/25. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMTW.
