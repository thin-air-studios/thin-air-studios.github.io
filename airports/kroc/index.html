About KROC

Frederick Douglass Greater Rochester International Airport (ROC) is a large airport in Rochester, United States, with the ICAO code KROC. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 10/28 and 07/25. The longest, 04/22, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 gates, 67 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 460 routes through KROC. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Dulles (IAD) and Newark (EWR).
