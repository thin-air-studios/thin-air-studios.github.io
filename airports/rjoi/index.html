About RJOI

Iwakuni Kintaikyo Airport (IWK) is a medium airport in Iwakuni, Japan, with the ICAO code RJOI. Its two runways are 02/20 and 02R/20L. The longer one, 02/20, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 30 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 19 routes through RJOI. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Naha (OKA) and Kaneohe (NGF).
