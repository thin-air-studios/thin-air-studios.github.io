About KPWK

Chicago Executive Airport (PWK) is a medium airport in Chicago/Prospect Heights/Wheeling, United States, with the ICAO code KPWK. Its two runways are 16/34 and 12/30. The longer one, 16/34, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 142 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SKQ has 4 routes through KPWK. Its most frequent links are Columbus (OSU), Burlington (KBUY) and St Louis (STL).
