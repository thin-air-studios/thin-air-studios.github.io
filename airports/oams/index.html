About OAMS

Mazar-i-Sharif International Airport (MZR) is a large airport in Mazar-i-Sharif, Afghanistan, with the ICAO code OAMS. It has one runway, 06R/24L, 9,843 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 23 routes through OAMS. Its most frequent links are Kabul (KBL), Istanbul (ISL) and Istanbul (IST).
