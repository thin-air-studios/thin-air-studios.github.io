About CYHZ

Halifax / Stanfield International Airport (YHZ) is a large airport in Halifax, Canada, with the ICAO code CYHZ. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 10,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 51 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 49 airlines have 852 routes through CYHZ. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Ottawa (YOW) and St. John's (YYT).
