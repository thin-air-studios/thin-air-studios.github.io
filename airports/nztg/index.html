About NZTG

Tauranga Airport (TRG) is a medium airport in Tauranga, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZTG. It has 5 runways, from 1,886 ft to 5,988 ft long. The longest is 07/25. Of these, 2 hard and 3 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 177 routes through NZTG. Its most frequent links are Auckland (AKL), Wellington (WLG) and Christchurch (CHC).
