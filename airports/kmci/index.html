About KMCI

Kansas City International Airport (MCI) is a large airport in Kansas City, United States, with the ICAO code KMCI. Its 3 runways are 01L/19R, 09/27 and 01R/19L. The longest, 01L/19R, is 10,801 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 gates, 290 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 938 routes through KMCI. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), New York (LGA) and Chicago (ORD).
