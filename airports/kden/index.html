About KDEN

Denver International Airport (DEN) is a large airport in Denver, United States, with the ICAO code KDEN. It is number 10 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 82.4 million passengers in 2025. It has 6 runways, from 12,000 ft to 16,000 ft long. The longest is 16R/34L. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 177 gates, 393 taxiway segments, 24 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 63 airlines have 5,427 routes through KDEN. Its most frequent links are Phoenix (PHX), Colorado Springs (COS) and Salt Lake City (SLC).
