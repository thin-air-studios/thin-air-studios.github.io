About LRTC

Tulcea Danube Delta Airport (TCE) is a medium airport in Mihail Kogălniceanu, Romania, with the ICAO code LRTC. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,517 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LRTC.
