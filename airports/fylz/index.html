About FYLZ

Luderitz Airport (LUD) is a medium airport in Luderitz, Namibia, with the ICAO code FYLZ. Its two runways are 04/22 and 12/30. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,004 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FYLZ.
