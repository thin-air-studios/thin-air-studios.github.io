About FGMY

President Obiang Nguema International Airport (GEM) is a medium airport in Mengomeyén, Equatorial Guinea, with the ICAO code FGMY. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,843 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FGMY.
