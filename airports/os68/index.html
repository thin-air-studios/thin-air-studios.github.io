About OS68

Saiqal Air Base (OS68) is a small airport in Saiqal, Syria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08/26 and 05/23. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,820 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OS68.
