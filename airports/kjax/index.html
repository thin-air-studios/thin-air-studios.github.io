About KJAX

Jacksonville International Airport (JAX) is a large airport in Jacksonville, United States, with the ICAO code KJAX. Its two runways are 08/26 and 14/32. The longer one, 08/26, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 141 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 589 routes through KJAX. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Philadelphia (PHL) and Atlanta (ATL).
