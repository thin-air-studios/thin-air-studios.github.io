About KSHV

Shreveport Regional Airport (SHV) is a medium airport in Shreveport, United States, with the ICAO code KSHV. Its two runways are 14/32 and 06/24. The longer one, 14/32, is 8,348 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 64 taxiway segments, 21 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 96 routes through KSHV. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Atlanta (ATL).
