About UAAH

Balkhash Airport (BXH) is a medium airport in Balkhash, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UAAH. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,208 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 138 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, QAZ has 2 routes through UAAH. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Almaty (ALA).
