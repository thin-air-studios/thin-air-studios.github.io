About LFBL

Limoges Airport (LIG) is a medium airport in Limoges/Bellegarde, France, with the ICAO code LFBL. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 163 routes through LFBL. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Manchester (MAN) and Nottingham (EMA).
