About KOFP

Hanover County Municipal Airport (KOFP) is a small airport in Hanover, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 16/34, 5,402 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments and 2 aprons.
