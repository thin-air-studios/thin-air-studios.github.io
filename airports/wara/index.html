About WARA

Abdul Rachman Saleh Airport (MLG) is a medium airport in Malang, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WARA. Its two runways are 17/35 and 17L/35R. The longer one, 17/35, is 8,202 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 128 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 18 routes through WARA. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK) and Jakarta (HLP).
