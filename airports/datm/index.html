About DATM

Bordj Badji Mokhtar Airport (BMW) is a medium airport in Bordj Badji Mokhtar, Algeria, with the ICAO code DATM. It has one runway, 08/26, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, DTH has 4 routes through DATM. Its most frequent links are Constantine (CZL) and Es-Sénia (ORN).
