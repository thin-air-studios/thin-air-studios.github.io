About ETNG

Geilenkirchen Air Base (GKE) is a medium airport in Geilenkirchen, Germany, with the ICAO code ETNG. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,009 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 12 routes through ETNG. Its most frequent links are Bangor (BGR), Konya (KYA) and Mazar-i-Sharif (MZR).
