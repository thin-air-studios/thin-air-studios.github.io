About LGEL

Elefsis Air Base (LGEL) is a medium airport in Elefsina, Greece. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,985 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LGEL.
