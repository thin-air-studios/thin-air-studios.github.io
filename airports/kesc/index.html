About KESC

Delta County Airport (ESC) is a medium airport in Escanaba, United States, with the ICAO code KESC. Its two runways are 10/28 and 01/19. The longer one, 10/28, is 6,498 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 9 routes through KESC. Its most frequent links are Detroit (DTW), Lansing (LAN) and Milwaukee (MKE).
