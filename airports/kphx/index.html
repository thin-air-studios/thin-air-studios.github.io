About KPHX

Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX) is a large airport in Phoenix, United States, with the ICAO code KPHX. It is number 39 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 51.6 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 07L/25R and 07R/25L. The longest, 08/26, is 11,489 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 111 gates, 556 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 3,024 routes through KPHX. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Los Angeles (LAX) and San Diego (SAN).
