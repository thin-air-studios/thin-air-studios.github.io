About LGST

Sitia Airport (JSH) is a medium airport in Crete Island, Greece, with the ICAO code LGST. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,804 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 20 routes through LGST. Its most frequent links are Spata-Artemida (ATH), Billund (BLL) and Bergen (BGO).
