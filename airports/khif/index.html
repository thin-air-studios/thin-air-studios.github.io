About KHIF

Hill Air Force Base (HIF) is a medium airport in Ogden, United States, with the ICAO code KHIF. It has one runway, 14/32, 13,500 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 149 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHIF.
