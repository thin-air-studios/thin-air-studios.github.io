About SLPS

Capitán Av. Salvador Ogaya G. airport (PSZ) is a medium airport in Puerto Suárez, Bolivia, with the ICAO code SLPS. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,562 ft long and 118 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SLPS.
