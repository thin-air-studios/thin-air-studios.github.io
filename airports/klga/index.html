About KLGA

LaGuardia Airport (LGA) is a large airport in New York, United States, with the ICAO code KLGA. Its two runways are 04/22 and 13/31. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 80 gates, 157 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 9 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 2,694 routes through KLGA. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Boston (BOS) and Chicago (ORD).
