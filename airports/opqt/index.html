About OPQT

Quetta International Airport (UET) is a large airport in Quetta, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPQT. Its two runways are 13R/31L and 13L/31R. The longer one, 13R/31L, is 12,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 155 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 26 routes through OPQT. Its most frequent links are Karachi (KHI), Attock (ISB) and Dubai (DXB).
