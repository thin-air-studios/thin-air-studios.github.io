About LEZL

Seville Airport (SVQ) is a large airport in Seville, Spain, with the ICAO code LEZL. It has one runway, 09/27, 11,030 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 113 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 70 airlines have 2,749 routes through LEZL. Its most frequent links are Barcelona (BCN), Paris (ORY) and London (LGW).
