About KMYV

Yuba County Airport (MYV) is a medium airport in Marysville, United States, with the ICAO code KMYV. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMYV.
