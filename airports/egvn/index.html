About EGVN

RAF Brize Norton (BZZ) is a medium airport in Carterton, Oxfordshire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGVN. It has one runway, 07/25, 10,007 ft long and 184 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 137 routes through EGVN. Its most frequent links are Hannover (HAJ), Akrotiri (AKT) and Prestwick (PIK).
