About KMTH

Florida Keys Marathon International Airport (MTH) is a medium airport in Marathon, United States, with the ICAO code KMTH. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,008 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 70 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Mountain Air Cargo has 3 routes through KMTH. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Fort Lauderdale (FLL).
