About EGPC

Wick John O'Groats Airport (WIC) is a medium airport in Wick, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGPC. It has one runway, 13/31, 6,007 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 31 routes through EGPC. Its most frequent links are Aberdeen (ABZ), Ingliston (EDI) and Reykjavík (KEF).
