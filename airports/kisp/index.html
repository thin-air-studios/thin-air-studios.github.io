About KISP

Long Island MacArthur Airport (ISP) is a medium airport in Islip, United States, with the ICAO code KISP. Its 3 runways are 06/24, 15R/33L and 15L/33R. The longest, 06/24, is 7,006 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 78 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 175 routes through KISP. Its most frequent links are Orlando (MCO), Fort Myers (RSW) and Tampa (TPA).
