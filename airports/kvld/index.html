About KVLD

Valdosta Regional Airport (VLD) is a medium airport in Valdosta, United States, with the ICAO code KVLD. Its two runways are 18/36 and 04/22. The longer one, 18/36, is 8,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through KVLD. Its most frequent links are Atlanta (ATL), Lawrenceville (LZU) and Orlando (MCO).
