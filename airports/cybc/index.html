About CYBC

Baie-Comeau Airport (YBC) is a medium airport in Baie-Comeau, Canada, with the ICAO code CYBC. It has one runway, 10/28, 6,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 4 routes through CYBC. Its most frequent links are Montréal (YUL), Havre-Saint-Pierre (YGV) and Port-Menier (YPN).
