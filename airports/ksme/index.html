About KSME

Lake Cumberland Regional Airport (SME) is a medium airport in Somerset, United States, with the ICAO code KSME. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,801 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSME.
