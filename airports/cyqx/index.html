About CYQX

Gander International Airport (YQX) is a medium airport in Gander, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQX. Its two runways are 03/21 and 13/31. The longer one, 03/21, is 10,200 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 49 routes through CYQX. Its most frequent links are Halifax (YHZ), Toronto (YYZ) and Montréal (YUL).
