About GOTB

Bakel Airport (BXE) is a medium airport in Bakel, Senegal, with the ICAO code GOTB. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,741 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for GOTB.
