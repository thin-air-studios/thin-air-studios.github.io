About CYHU

Montréal / Saint-Hubert Metropolitan Airport (YHU) is a medium airport in Montréal, Canada, with the ICAO code CYHU. Its 3 runways are 06L/24R, 06R/24L and 10/28. The longest, 06L/24R, is 7,840 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 107 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 49 routes through CYHU. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YTZ), Toronto (YYZ) and Hamilton (YHM).
