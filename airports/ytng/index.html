About YTNG

Thangool Airport (THG) is a medium airport in Biloela, Australia, with the ICAO code YTNG. Its two runways are 10/28 and 14/32. The longer one, 10/28, is 4,993 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YTNG.
