About KPPA

Perry Lefors Field (PPA) is a small airport in Pampa, United States, with the ICAO code KPPA. Its two runways are 17/35 and 05/23. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,862 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AMF has 2 routes through KPPA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Lubbock (LBB).
