About SBKP

Viracopos International Airport (VCP) is a large airport in Campinas, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBKP. It has one runway, 15/33, 10,630 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 gates, 91 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 1,308 routes through SBKP. Its most frequent links are Curitiba (CWB), Porto Alegre (POA) and Manaus (MAO).
