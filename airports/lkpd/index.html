About LKPD

Pardubice Airport (PED) is a large airport in Pardubice, Czechia, with the ICAO code LKPD. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,203 ft long and 246 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 104 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 88 routes through LKPD. Its most frequent links are Burgas (BOJ), Antalya (AYT) and Alicante (ALC).
