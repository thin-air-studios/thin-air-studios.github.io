About FBJW

Jwaneng Airport (JWA) is a medium airport in Jwaneng, Botswana, with the ICAO code FBJW. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,495 ft long and 82 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FBJW.
