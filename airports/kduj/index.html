About KDUJ

DuBois Regional Airport (DUJ) is a medium airport in Dubois, United States, with the ICAO code KDUJ. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,503 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 22 routes through KDUJ. Its most frequent links are Pittsburgh (PIT), Dulles (IAD) and Cleveland (CLE).
