About KBYH

Arkansas International Airport (BYH) is a medium airport in Blytheville, United States, with the ICAO code KBYH. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,602 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBYH.
