About MMPA

El Tajín National Airport (PAZ) is a medium airport in Poza Rica, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMPA. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,906 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, RFD has 2 routes through MMPA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Veracruz (VER).
