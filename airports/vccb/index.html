About VCCB

Batticaloa International Airport (BTC) is a medium airport in Batticaloa, Sri Lanka, with the ICAO code VCCB. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,118 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VCCB.
