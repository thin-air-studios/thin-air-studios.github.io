About WAAA

Sultan Hasanuddin International Airport (UPG) is a large airport in Makassar, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAAA. Its two runways are 03/21 and 13/31. The longer one, 03/21, is 10,171 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 231 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 309 routes through WAAA. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Surabaya (SUB) and Kuta (DPS).
