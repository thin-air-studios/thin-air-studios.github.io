About SKCC

Camilo Daza International Airport (CUC) is a medium airport in Cúcuta, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKCC. Its two runways are 16/34 and 03/21. The longer one, 16/34, is 7,700 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 19 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 70 routes through SKCC. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Tocumen (PTY) and Medellín (MDE).
