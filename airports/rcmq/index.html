About RCMQ

Taichung International Airport / Ching Chuang Kang Air Base (RMQ) is a large airport in Taichung (Qingshui), Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCMQ. It has one runway, 18/36, 12,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 153 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 259 routes through RCMQ. Its most frequent links are Huxi (MZG), Shang-I (KNH) and Hong Kong (HKG).
