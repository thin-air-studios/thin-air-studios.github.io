About KSAW

Marquette Sawyer Regional Airport (MQT) is a medium airport in Gwinn, United States, with the ICAO code KSAW. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,072 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 11 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 31 routes through KSAW. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Milwaukee (MKE) and Minneapolis (MSP).
