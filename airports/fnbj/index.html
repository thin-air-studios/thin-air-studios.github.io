About FNBJ

Dr. Antonio Agostinho Neto International Airport (NBJ) is a large airport in Luanda (Ícolo e Bengo), Angola, with the ICAO code FNBJ. Its two runways are 06R/24L and 06L/24R. The longer one, 06R/24L, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 62 routes through FNBJ. Its most frequent links are Cabinda (CAB), Johannesburg (JNB) and Lisbon (LIS).
