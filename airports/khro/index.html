About KHRO

Boone County Airport (HRO) is a medium airport in Harrison, United States, with the ICAO code KHRO. It has one runway, 18/36, 6,161 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, FDY has 6 routes through KHRO. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Memphis (MEM).
