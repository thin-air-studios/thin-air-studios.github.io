About KBAB

Beale Air Force Base (BAB) is a medium airport in Beale Air Force Base, United States, with the ICAO code KBAB. It has one runway, 15/33, 12,001 ft long and 300 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 86 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBAB.
