About YTNK

Tennant Creek Airport (TCA) is a medium airport in Tennant Creek, Australia, with the ICAO code YTNK. Its two runways are 07/25 and 11/29. The longer one, 07/25, is 6,427 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YTNK.
