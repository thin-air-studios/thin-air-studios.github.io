About KBLM

Monmouth Executive Airport (BLM) is a small airport in Belmar/Farmingdale, United States, with the ICAO code KBLM. Its two runways are 14/32 and 03/21. The longer one, 14/32, is 7,345 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBLM.
