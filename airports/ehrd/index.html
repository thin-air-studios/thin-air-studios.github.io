About EHRD

Rotterdam The Hague Airport (RTM) is a large airport in Rotterdam, Netherlands, with the ICAO code EHRD. It has one runway, 06/24, 7,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 43 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 1,263 routes through EHRD. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Faro (FAO) and London (LCY).
