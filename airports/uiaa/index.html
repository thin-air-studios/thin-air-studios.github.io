About UIAA

Chita-Kadala International Airport (HTA) is a large airport in Chita, Russia, with the ICAO code UIAA. Its two runways are 11/29 and 11L/29R. The longer one, 11/29, is 9,430 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 61 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 75 routes through UIAA. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Novosibirsk (OVB) and Irkutsk (IKT).
