About RJCH

Hakodate Airport (HKD) is a large airport in Hakodate, Japan, with the ICAO code RJCH. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 35 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 107 routes through RJCH. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Sapporo (OKD) and Sapporo (CTS).
