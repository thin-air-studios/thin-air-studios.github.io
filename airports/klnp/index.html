About KLNP

Lonesome Pine Airport (LNP) is a small airport in Wise, United States, with the ICAO code KLNP. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,131 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLNP.
