About FQBR

Beira International Airport (BEW) is a large airport in Beira, Mozambique, with the ICAO code FQBR. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 91 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 9 routes through FQBR. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Maputo (MPM) and Nairobi (NBO).
