About LFFN

Brienne-le-Château Airfield (LFFN) is a small airport in Saint-Christophe-Dodinicourt, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,038 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments and 33 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFFN.
