About KBRO

Brownsville South Padre Island International Airport (BRO) is a medium airport in Brownsville, United States, with the ICAO code KBRO. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,399 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 42 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 57 routes through KBRO. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Monterrey (MTY).
