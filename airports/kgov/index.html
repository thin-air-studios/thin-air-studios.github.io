About KGOV

Grayling Army Air Field (KGOV) is a small airport in Grayling, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 5,005 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGOV.
