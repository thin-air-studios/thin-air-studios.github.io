About KPVD

Rhode Island T. F. Green International Airport (PVD) is a large airport in Providence/Warwick, United States, with the ICAO code KPVD. Its two runways are 05/23 and 16/34. The longer one, 05/23, is 8,700 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 79 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 412 routes through KPVD. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Washington (DCA) and Charlotte (CLT).
