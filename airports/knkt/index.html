About KNKT

Cherry Point MCAS / Cunningham Field/ (KNKT) is a medium airport in Cherry Point, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 4 runways are 14L/32R, 05L/23R, 14R/32L and 05R/23L. The longest, 14L/32R, is 8,978 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 20 routes through KNKT. Its most frequent links are Riverside (RIV), Schkeuditz (LEJ) and Ontario (ONT).
