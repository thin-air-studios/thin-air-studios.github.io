About SBPV

Governador Jorge Teixeira de Oliveira International Airport (PVH) is a large airport in Porto Velho, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBPV. It has one runway, 1/19, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 52 routes through SBPV. Its most frequent links are Brasília (BSB), Cuiabá (CGB) and Manaus (MAO).
