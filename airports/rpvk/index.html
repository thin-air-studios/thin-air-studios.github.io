About RPVK

Kalibo International Airport (KLO) is a large airport in Kalibo, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPVK. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,175 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 190 routes through RPVK. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL), Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB) and Seoul (ICN).
