About KTEB

Teterboro Airport (TEB) is a medium airport in Teterboro, United States, with the ICAO code KTEB. Its two runways are 1/19 and 6/24. The longer one, 1/19, is 6,997 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 38 routes through KTEB. Its most frequent links are Miami (OPF), West Palm Beach (DJT) and Reading (RDG).
