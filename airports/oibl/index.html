About OIBL

Bandar Lengeh International Airport (BDH) is a medium airport in Bandar Lengeh, Iran, with the ICAO code OIBL. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,203 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, IRA has 2 routes through OIBL. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Shiraz (SYZ).
