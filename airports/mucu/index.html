About MUCU

Antonio Maceo International Airport (SCU) is a large airport in Santiago, Cuba, with the ICAO code MUCU. Its two runways are 10/28 and 19/01. The longer one, 10/28, is 13,130 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 29 routes through MUCU. Its most frequent links are Havana (HAV), Rome (FCO) and Miami (MIA).
