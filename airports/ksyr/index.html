About KSYR

Syracuse Hancock International Airport (SYR) is a large airport in Syracuse, United States, with the ICAO code KSYR. Its two runways are 10/28 and 15/33. The longer one, 10/28, is 9,013 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 110 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 405 routes through KSYR. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Dulles (IAD) and Philadelphia (PHL).
