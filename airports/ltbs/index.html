About LTBS

Dalaman International Airport (DLM) is a large airport in Dalaman, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTBS. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 89 airlines have 2,074 routes through LTBS. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Manchester (MAN) and Pendik (SAW).
