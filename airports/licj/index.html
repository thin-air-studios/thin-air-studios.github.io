About LICJ

Falcone–Borsellino Airport (PMO) is a large airport in Palermo, Italy, with the ICAO code LICJ. Its two runways are 07/25 and 02/20. The longer one, 07/25, is 10,912 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 41 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 78 airlines have 2,229 routes through LICJ. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Rome (FCO) and Napoli (NAP).
