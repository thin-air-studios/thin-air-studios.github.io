About LGKP

Karpathos Airport (AOK) is a medium airport in Karpathos Island, Greece, with the ICAO code LGKP. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,871 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 159 routes through LGKP. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Vienna (VIE) and Stockholm (ARN).
