About KPRB

Paso Robles Municipal Airport (PRB) is a medium airport in Paso Robles, United States, with the ICAO code KPRB. Its two runways are 01/19 and 13/31. The longer one, 01/19, is 6,008 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPRB.
