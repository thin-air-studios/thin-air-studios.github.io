About KVNW

Van Wert County Airport (KVNW) is a small airport in Van Wert, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 09/27 and 18/36. The longer one, 09/27, is 5,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KVNW.
