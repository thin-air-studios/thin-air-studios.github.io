About KSEZ

Sedona Airport (SDX) is a small airport in Sedona, United States, with the ICAO code KSEZ. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,132 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSEZ.
