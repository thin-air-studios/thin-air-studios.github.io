About KMSN

Dane County Regional Truax Field (MSN) is a medium airport in Madison, United States, with the ICAO code KMSN. Its 3 runways are 18/36, 03/21 and 14/32. The longest, 18/36, is 9,006 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 86 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 257 routes through KMSN. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN) and Detroit (DTW).
