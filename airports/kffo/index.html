About KFFO

Wright-Patterson Air Force Base (FFO) is a medium airport in Dayton, United States, with the ICAO code KFFO. Its two runways are 05L/23R and 05R/23L. The longer one, 05L/23R, is 12,600 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 193 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFFO.
