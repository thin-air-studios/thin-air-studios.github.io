About KIDA

Idaho Falls Regional Airport (IDA) is a medium airport in Idaho Falls, United States, with the ICAO code KIDA. Its two runways are 02/20 and 17/35. The longer one, 02/20, is 9,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 81 routes through KIDA. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Salt Lake City (SLC) and Seattle (SEA).
