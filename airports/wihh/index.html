About WIHH

Halim Perdanakusuma International Airport (HLP) is a large airport in Jakarta, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIHH. It has one runway, 06/24, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 90 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 182 routes through WIHH. Its most frequent links are Surabaya (SUB), Yogyakarta (JOG) and Beringin (KNO).
