About ZBER

Erenhot Saiwusu International Airport (ERL) is a medium airport in Erenhot, China, with the ICAO code ZBER. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 12 routes through ZBER. Its most frequent links are Hohhot (HET), Beijing (PKX) and Chifeng (CIF).
