About KMBY

Omar N Bradley Airport (MBY) is a small airport in Moberly, United States, with the ICAO code KMBY. Its two runways are 13/31 and 05/23. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMBY.
