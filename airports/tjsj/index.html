About TJSJ

Luis Munoz Marin International Airport (SJU) is a large airport in San Juan, Puerto Rico, with the ICAO code TJSJ. Its two runways are 08/26 and 10/28. The longer one, 08/26, is 10,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 gates, 116 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 1,061 routes through TJSJ. Its most frequent links are Orlando (MCO), Miami (MIA) and Gustavia (SBH).
