About KPBH

Price County Airport (KPBH) is a small airport in Phillips, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 01/19 and 06/24. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,220 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPBH.
