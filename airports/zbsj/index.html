About ZBSJ

Shijiazhuang Zhengding International Airport (SJW) is a large airport in Shijiazhuang, China, with the ICAO code ZBSJ. It has one runway, 15/33, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 91 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 619 routes through ZBSJ. Its most frequent links are Chengdu (TFU), Guangzhou (CAN) and Kunming (KMG).
