About KJMS

Jamestown Regional Airport (JMS) is a medium airport in Jamestown, United States, with the ICAO code KJMS. Its two runways are 13/31 and 04/22. The longer one, 13/31, is 6,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SkyWest has 4 routes through KJMS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Denver (DEN).
