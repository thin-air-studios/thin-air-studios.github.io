About HE35

Abu Suwayr Air Base (HE35) is a medium airport in Abu Suwayr, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 09/27 and 11/29. The longer one, 09/27, is 9,752 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 70 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE35.
