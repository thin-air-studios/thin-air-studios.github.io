About OOTH

Thumrait Air Base (TTH) is a medium airport in Thumrait, Oman, with the ICAO code OOTH. It has one runway, 17/35, 13,123 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, RFR has 3 routes through OOTH. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Carterton (BZZ).
