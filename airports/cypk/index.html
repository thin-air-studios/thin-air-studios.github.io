About CYPK

Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (CYPK) is a medium airport in Pitt Meadows, Canada. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 08R/26L, 08L/26R and 18/36. The longest, 08R/26L, is 5,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 101 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, ASP has 1 routes through CYPK. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Tofino (YAZ).
