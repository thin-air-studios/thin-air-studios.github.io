About LBRS

Ruse Airport (ROU) is a small airport in Shtraklevo, Bulgaria, with the ICAO code LBRS. Its two runways are 05/23 and 05L/23R. The longer one, 05/23, is 8,210 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 61 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LBRS.
