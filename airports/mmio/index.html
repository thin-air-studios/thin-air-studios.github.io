About MMIO

Plan de Guadalupe International Airport (SLW) is a medium airport in Saltillo, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMIO. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,506 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 17 routes through MMIO. Its most frequent links are Monterrey (MTY), Mexico City (NLU) and Houston (IAH).
