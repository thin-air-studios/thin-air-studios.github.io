About EFKE

Kemi-Tornio Airport (KEM) is a medium airport in Kemi / Tornio, Finland, with the ICAO code EFKE. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,212 ft long and 157 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 89 routes through EFKE. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Kokkola / Kruunupyy (KOK) and Spata-Artemida (ATH).
