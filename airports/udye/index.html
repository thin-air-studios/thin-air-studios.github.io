About UDYE

Erebuni Airport (UDYE) is a medium airport in Yerevan, Armenia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,134 ft long and 132 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UDYE.
