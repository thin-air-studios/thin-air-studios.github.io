About LTAV

Sivrihisar Airport (LTAV) is a medium airport in Sivrihisar, Türkiye. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 11,156 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 23 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LTAV.
