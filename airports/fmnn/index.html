About FMNN

Nosy Be International Airport (NOS) is a large airport in Nosy Be, Madagascar, with the ICAO code FMNN. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,185 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 54 routes through FMNN. Its most frequent links are Antananarivo (TNR), Johannesburg (JNB) and Dzaoudzi (DZA).
