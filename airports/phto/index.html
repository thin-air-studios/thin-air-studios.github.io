About PHTO

Hilo International Airport (ITO) is a medium airport in Hilo, United States, with the ICAO code PHTO. Its two runways are 08/26 and 03/21. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,800 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 195 routes through PHTO. Its most frequent links are Honolulu (HNL), Kahului (OGG) and Los Angeles (LAX).
