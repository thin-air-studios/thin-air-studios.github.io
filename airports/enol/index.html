About ENOL

Ørland Airport (OLA) is a medium airport in Ørland, Norway, with the ICAO code ENOL. It has one runway, 15/33, 10,755 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 238 taxiway segments, 94 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 37 routes through ENOL. Its most frequent links are Oslo (OSL) and Evenes (EVE).
