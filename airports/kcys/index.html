About KCYS

Cheyenne Regional Jerry Olson Field (CYS) is a medium airport in Cheyenne, United States, with the ICAO code KCYS. Its two runways are 09/27 and 13/31. The longer one, 09/27, is 9,270 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 96 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 15 routes through KCYS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Denver (DEN).
