About LTCG

Trabzon International Airport (TZX) is a medium airport in Trabzon, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTCG. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,661 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 807 routes through LTCG. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Düsseldorf (DUS).
