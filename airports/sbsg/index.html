About SBSG

Rio Grande do Norte/São Gonçalo do Amarante–Governador Aluízio Alves International Airport (NAT) is a large airport in Natal, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBSG. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,843 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 170 routes through SBSG. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Recife (REC) and Belo Horizonte (CNF).
