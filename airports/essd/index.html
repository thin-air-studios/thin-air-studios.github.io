About ESSD

Dala Airport (BLE) is a medium airport in Borlange, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESSD. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,579 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 28 routes through ESSD. Its most frequent links are Rhodes (RHO), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Gran Canaria Island (LPA).
