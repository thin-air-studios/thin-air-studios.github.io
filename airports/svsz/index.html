About SVSZ

Miguel Urdaneta Fernández Airport (STB) is a medium airport in San Carlos del Zulia, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVSZ. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,835 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SVSZ.
