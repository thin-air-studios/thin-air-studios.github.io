About LIBN

Lecce Galatina Air Base / Galatina Fortunato Cesari Airport (LCC) is a medium airport in Galatina (LE), Italy, with the ICAO code LIBN. It has one runway, 14/32, 6,240 ft long and 190 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 9 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIBN.
