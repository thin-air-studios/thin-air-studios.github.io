About RKJK

Gunsan Airport / Gunsan Air Base (KUV) is a medium airport in Gunsan, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKJK. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 148 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 18 routes through RKJK. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Jeju City (CJU).
