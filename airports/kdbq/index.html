About KDBQ

Dubuque Regional Airport (DBQ) is a medium airport in Dubuque, United States, with the ICAO code KDBQ. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 6,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 65 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 12 routes through KDBQ. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD) and Orlando (MCO).
