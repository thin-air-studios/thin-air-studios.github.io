About NZNV

Invercargill Airport (IVC) is a medium airport in Invercargill, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZNV. Its 4 runways are 04/22, 12/30, 04R/22L and 07/25. The longest, 04/22, is 7,251 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 3 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 117 routes through NZNV. Its most frequent links are Christchurch (CHC), Wellington (WLG) and Auckland (AKL).
