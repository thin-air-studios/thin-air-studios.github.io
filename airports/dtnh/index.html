About DTNH

Enfidha - Hammamet International Airport (NBE) is a medium airport in Enfidha, Tunisia, with the ICAO code DTNH. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,827 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 88 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 19 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 49 airlines have 556 routes through DTNH. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Zaventem (BRU) and Manchester (MAN).
