About SBFZ

Pinto Martins International Airport (FOR) is a large airport in Fortaleza, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBFZ. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,039 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 gates, 92 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 481 routes through SBFZ. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Rio De Janeiro (GIG) and Recife (REC).
