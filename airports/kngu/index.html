About KNGU

Norfolk Naval Station (Chambers Field) (NGU) is a medium airport in Norfolk, United States, with the ICAO code KNGU. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,370 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 569 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 28 routes through KNGU. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (HHN), Guantanamo Bay Naval Station (NBW) and Baltimore (BWI).
