About OIBP

Persian Gulf International Airport (PGU) is a medium airport in Khiyaroo, Iran, with the ICAO code OIBP. It has one runway, 13/31, 13,115 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 19 routes through OIBP. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Sari (SRY) and Isfahan (IFN).
