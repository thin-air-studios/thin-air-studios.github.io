About KPGV

Pitt-Greenville Airport (PGV) is a medium airport in Greenville, United States, with the ICAO code KPGV. Its two runways are 02/20 and 08/26. The longer one, 02/20, is 7,175 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 27 routes through KPGV. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Burlington (KBUY) and Richmond (RIC).
