About PAGS

Gustavus Airport (GST) is a medium airport in Gustavus, United States, with the ICAO code PAGS. Its two runways are 11/29 and 02/20. The longer one, 11/29, is 6,720 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for PAGS.
