About CYYF

Penticton Airport (YYF) is a medium airport in Penticton, Canada, with the ICAO code CYYF. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 31 routes through CYYF. Its most frequent links are Vancouver (YVR), Calgary (YYC) and Kelowna (YLW).
