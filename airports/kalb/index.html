About KALB

Albany International Airport (ALB) is a large airport in Albany, United States, with the ICAO code KALB. Its two runways are 01/19 and 10/28. The longer one, 01/19, is 8,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 99 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 35 airlines have 329 routes through KALB. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Chicago (ORD) and Washington (DCA).
