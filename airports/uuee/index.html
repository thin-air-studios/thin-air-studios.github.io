About UUEE

Sheremetyevo International Airport (SVO) is a large airport in Moscow, Russia, with the ICAO code UUEE. Its 3 runways are 06R/24L, 06C/24C and 06L/24R. The longest, 06R/24L, is 12,139 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 92 gates, 330 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 122 airlines have 4,178 routes through UUEE. Its most frequent links are St. Petersburg (LED), Antalya (AYT) and Sochi (AER).
