About VAPO

Pune International Airport (PNQ) is a large airport in Pune, India, with the ICAO code VAPO. Its two runways are 10/28 and 14/32. The longer one, 10/28, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 40 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 800 routes through VAPO. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR) and Chennai (MAA).
