About LTCE

Erzurum International Airport (ERZ) is a medium airport in Erzurum, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTCE. Its two runways are 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longer one, 08R/26L, is 12,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 150 routes through LTCE. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Ankara (ESB).
