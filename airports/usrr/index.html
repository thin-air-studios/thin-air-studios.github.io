About USRR

Surgut International Airport (SGC) is a large airport in Surgut, Russia, with the ICAO code USRR. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,154 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 201 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 216 routes through USRR. Its most frequent links are Samara (KUF), Novosibirsk (OVB) and Ufa (UFA).
