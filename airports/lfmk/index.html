About LFMK

Carcassonne Salvaza Airport (CCF) is a medium airport in Carcassonne, France, with the ICAO code LFMK. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,726 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 50 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 157 routes through LFMK. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Charleroi (CRL) and Manchester (MAN).
