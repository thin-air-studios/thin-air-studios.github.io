About KRYM

Ray S Miller Army Air Field (KRYM) is a small airport in Fort Ripley, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 13/31 and 132/312. The longer one, 13/31, is 6,100 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRYM.
