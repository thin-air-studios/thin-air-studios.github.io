About ENBR

Bergen Airport, Flesland (BGO) is a large airport in Bergen, Norway, with the ICAO code ENBR. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,810 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 109 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 62 airlines have 2,065 routes through ENBR. Its most frequent links are Stavanger (SVG), Oslo (OSL) and Trondheim (TRD).
