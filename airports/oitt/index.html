About OITT

Tabriz International Airport (TBZ) is a large airport in Tabriz, Iran, with the ICAO code OITT. Its two runways are 12L/30R and 12R/30L. The longer one, 12L/30R, is 11,825 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 94 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 42 routes through OITT. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Tehran (THR) and Istanbul (ISL).
