About EBBR

Brussels Airport (BRU) is a large airport in Zaventem, Belgium, with the ICAO code EBBR. Its 3 runways are 07L/25R, 07R/25L and 01/19. The longest, 07L/25R, is 11,936 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 70 gates, 254 taxiway segments, 23 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 228 airlines have 9,207 routes through EBBR. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Geneva (GVA) and Barcelona (BCN).
