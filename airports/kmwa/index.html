About KMWA

Veterans Airport of Southern Illinois (MWA) is a medium airport in Marion, United States, with the ICAO code KMWA. Its two runways are 02/20 and 11/29. The longer one, 02/20, is 8,012 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 7 routes through KMWA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Chicago (ORD).
