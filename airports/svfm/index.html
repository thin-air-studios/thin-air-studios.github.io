About SVFM

Generalissimo Francisco de Miranda Air Base (SVFM) is a medium airport in Caracas, Venezuela. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 6,475 ft long and 140 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SVFM.
