About KMPV

Edward F Knapp State Airport (MPV) is a medium airport in Barre/Montpelier, United States, with the ICAO code KMPV. Its two runways are 17/35 and 05/23. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AMF has 1 routes through KMPV. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Manchester (MHT).
