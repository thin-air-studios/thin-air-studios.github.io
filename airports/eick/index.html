About EICK

Cork International Airport (ORK) is a large airport in Cork, Ireland, with the ICAO code EICK. Its two runways are 16/34 and 07/25. The longer one, 16/34, is 6,998 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 21 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 1,001 routes through EICK. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Manchester (MAN) and Málaga (AGP).
