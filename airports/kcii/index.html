About KCII

Choteau Airport (KCII) is a small airport in Choteau, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 15/33 and 05/23. The longer one, 15/33, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCII.
