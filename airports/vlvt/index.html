About VLVT

Wattay International Airport (VTE) is a large airport in Vientiane, Laos, with the ICAO code VLVT. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 117 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 207 routes through VLVT. Its most frequent links are Seoul (ICN), Bangkok (BKK) and Kunming (KMG).
