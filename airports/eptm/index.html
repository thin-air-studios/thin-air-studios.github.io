About EPTM

Tomaszów Mazowiecki Military Air Base (EPTM) is a medium airport in Nowy Glinnik, Poland. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 6,560 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EPTM.
