About MMMY

Monterrey International Airport (MTY) is a large airport in Monterrey, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMMY. Its two runways are 12/30 and 16/34. The longer one, 12/30, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 41 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 1,035 routes through MMMY. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Cancún (CUN) and Guadalajara (GDL).
