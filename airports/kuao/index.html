About KUAO

Aurora State Airport (KUAO) is a medium airport in Aurora, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,003 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 114 taxiway segments and 11 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KUAO.
