About HE21

Ras Shukeir Airport (HE21) is a small airport in Ras Gharib, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,000 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE21.
