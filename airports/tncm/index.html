About TNCM

Princess Juliana International Airport (SXM) is a large airport in Sint Maarten, Sint Maarten, with the ICAO code TNCM. It has one runway, 10/28, 7,546 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 36 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 40 airlines have 312 routes through TNCM. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Gustavia (SBH) and Beef Island (EIS).
