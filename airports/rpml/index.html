About RPML

Lumbia Airfield (RPML) is a small airport in Cagayan de Oro, Philippines. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,050 ft long and 118 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 211 routes through RPML. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL), Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB) and Davao (DVO).
