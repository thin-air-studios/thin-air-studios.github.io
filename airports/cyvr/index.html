About CYVR

Vancouver International Airport (YVR) is a large airport in Vancouver, Canada, with the ICAO code CYVR. Its 3 runways are 08R/26L, 08L/26R and 13/31. The longest, 08R/26L, is 11,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 106 gates, 290 taxiway segments, 34 aprons and 9 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 83 airlines have 2,552 routes through CYVR. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC), Toronto (YYZ) and Edmonton (YEG).
