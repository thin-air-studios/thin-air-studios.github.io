About TXKF

L.F. Wade International Airport (BDA) is a large airport in Hamilton, Bermuda, with the ICAO code TXKF. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,705 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 67 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 94 routes through TXKF. Its most frequent links are Newark (EWR), Toronto (YYZ) and New York (JFK).
