About KWJF

General William J Fox Airfield (WJF) is a medium airport in Lancaster, United States, with the ICAO code KWJF. It has one runway, 06/24, 7,201 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 106 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KWJF.
