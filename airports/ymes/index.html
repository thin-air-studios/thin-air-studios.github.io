About YMES

RAAF Base East Sale (YMES) is a medium airport in Australia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 04/22 and 09/27. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,995 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YMES.
