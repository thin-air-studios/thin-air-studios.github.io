About BGTL

Pituffik Space Base (THU) is a medium airport in Pituffik, Greenland, with the ICAO code BGTL. It has one runway, 08T/26T, 9,997 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 124 taxiway segments, 27 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 4 routes through BGTL. Its most frequent links are Copenhagen (CPH) and Baltimore (BWI).
