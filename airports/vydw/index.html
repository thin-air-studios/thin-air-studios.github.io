About VYDW

Dawei Airport (TVY) is a medium airport in Dawei, Myanmar (Burma), with the ICAO code VYDW. It has one runway, 16/34, 12,013 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 21 routes through VYDW. Its most frequent links are Yangon (RGN), Kawthoung (KAW) and Mkeik (MGZ).
