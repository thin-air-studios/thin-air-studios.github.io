About KIWA

Mesa Gateway Airport (AZA) is a medium airport in Mesa, United States, with the ICAO code KIWA. Its 3 runways are 12R/30L, 12C/30C and 12L/30R. The longest, 12R/30L, is 10,401 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 112 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 287 routes through KIWA. Its most frequent links are Provo (PVU), Peoria (PIA) and Fargo (FAR).
