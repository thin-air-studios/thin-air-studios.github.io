About EDBN

Neubrandenburg Trollenhagen Airport (FNB) is a medium airport in Trollenhagen, Germany, with the ICAO code EDBN. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,522 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDBN.
