About GAGO

Gao International Airport (GAQ) is a medium airport in Gao, Mali, with the ICAO code GAGO. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for GAGO.
