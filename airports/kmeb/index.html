About KMEB

Laurinburg Maxton Airport (MXE) is a small airport in Maxton, United States, with the ICAO code KMEB. Its two runways are 05/23 and 13/31. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,503 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMEB.
