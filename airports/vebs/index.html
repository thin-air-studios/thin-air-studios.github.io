About VEBS

Biju Patnaik International Airport (BBI) is a large airport in Bhubaneswar, India, with the ICAO code VEBS. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 354 routes through VEBS. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR) and Kolkata (CCU).
