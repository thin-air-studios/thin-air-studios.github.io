About LKPO

Přerov Air Base (PRV) is a medium airport in Přerov, Czechia, with the ICAO code LKPO. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,123 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LKPO.
