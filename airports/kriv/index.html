About KRIV

March Air Reserve Base (RIV) is a medium airport in Riverside, United States, with the ICAO code KRIV. It has one runway, 14/32, 13,302 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 56 routes through KRIV. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Nashville (BNA) and Kansas City (MCI).
