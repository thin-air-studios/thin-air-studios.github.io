About LBIA

Bezmer Air Base (JAM) is a medium airport in Bezmer, Bulgaria, with the ICAO code LBIA. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,193 ft long and 260 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 82 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LBIA.
