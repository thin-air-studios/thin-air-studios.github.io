About LIRN

Naples International Airport (NAP) is a large airport in Napoli, Italy, with the ICAO code LIRN. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,622 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 88 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 113 airlines have 4,281 routes through LIRN. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Paris (ORY) and London (LGW).
