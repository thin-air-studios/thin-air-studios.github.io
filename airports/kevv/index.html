About KEVV

Evansville Regional Airport (EVV) is a medium airport in Evansville, United States, with the ICAO code KEVV. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 18/36 and 09/27. The longest, 04/22, is 8,021 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 68 routes through KEVV. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Detroit (DTW).
