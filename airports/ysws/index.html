About YSWS

Western Sydney International (Nancy-Bird Walton) Airport (WSI) is a large airport in Sydney, Australia, with the ICAO code YSWS. It has one runway, 05/23, 12,139 ft long and 1,562 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 56 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YSWS.
