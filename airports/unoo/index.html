About UNOO

Omsk Central Airport (OMS) is a large airport in Omsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UNOO. Its 3 runways are 06/24, 07/25 and 15/33. The longest, 06/24, is 9,435 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 50 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 243 routes through UNOO. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Antalya (AYT) and Sochi (AER).
