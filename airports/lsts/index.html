About LSTS

Sankt Stephan Airfield (LSTS) is a small airport in Sankt Stephan, Switzerland. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,708 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 11 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LSTS.
