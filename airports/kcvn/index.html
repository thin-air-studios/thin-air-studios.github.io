About KCVN

Clovis Municipal Airport (CVN) is a medium airport in Clovis, United States, with the ICAO code KCVN. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 12/30 and 08/26. The longest, 04/22, is 7,200 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, LYM has 4 routes through KCVN. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
