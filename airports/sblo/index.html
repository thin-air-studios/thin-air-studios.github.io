About SBLO

Governor José Richa Airport (LDB) is a medium airport in Londrina, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBLO. It has one runway, 13/31, 6,890 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 36 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 69 routes through SBLO. Its most frequent links are Campinas (VCP), Curitiba (CWB) and São Paulo (GRU).
