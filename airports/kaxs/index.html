About KAXS

Altus Quartz Mountain Regional Airport (AXS) is a small airport in Altus, United States, with the ICAO code KAXS. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,501 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAXS.
