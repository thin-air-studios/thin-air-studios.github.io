About RJFT

Kumamoto Airport (KMJ) is a large airport in Kumamoto, Japan, with the ICAO code RJFT. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,840 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 183 routes through RJFT. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (ITM) and Tokoname (NGO).
