About KBTR

Baton Rouge Metropolitan Airport (BTR) is a medium airport in Baton Rouge, United States, with the ICAO code KBTR. Its two runways are 4/22 and 13/31. The longer one, 4/22, is 7,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 141 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 83 routes through KBTR. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Charlotte (CLT) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
