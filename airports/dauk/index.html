About DAUK

Touggourt Sidi Madhi Airport (TGR) is a medium airport in Touggourt, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAUK. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Air Algerie has 5 routes through DAUK. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG) and Constantine (CZL).
