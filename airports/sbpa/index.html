About SBPA

Porto Alegre-Salgado Filho International Airport (POA) is a large airport in Porto Alegre, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBPA. It has one runway, 11/29, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 710 routes through SBPA. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), São Paulo (CGH) and Rio De Janeiro (GIG).
