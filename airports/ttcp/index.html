About TTCP

A.N.R. Robinson International Airport (TAB) is a large airport in Scarborough, Trinidad & Tobago, with the ICAO code TTCP. It has one runway, 11/29, 9,002 ft long and 159 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 74 routes through TTCP. Its most frequent links are Port of Spain (POS), Bridgetown (BGI) and Frankfurt am Main (FRA).
