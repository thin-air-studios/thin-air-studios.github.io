About HKJK

Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO) is a large airport in Nairobi, Kenya, with the ICAO code HKJK. It has one runway, 06/24, 13,507 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 68 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 78 airlines have 798 routes through HKJK. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Amsterdam (AMS) and Mombasa (MBA).
