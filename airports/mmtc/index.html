About MMTC

Francisco Sarabia Tinoco International Airport (TRC) is a medium airport in Torreón, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMTC. Its two runways are 13/31 and 08/26. The longer one, 13/31, is 9,039 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 41 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 65 routes through MMTC. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Guadalajara (GDL) and Tijuana (TIJ).
