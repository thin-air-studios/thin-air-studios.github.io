About EGNX

East Midlands Airport (EMA) is a large airport in Nottingham, Leicestershire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGNX. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,495 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 93 airlines have 2,851 routes through EGNX. Its most frequent links are Schkeuditz (LEJ), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Alicante (ALC).
