About CYHM

John C. Munro Hamilton International Airport (YHM) is a large airport in Hamilton, Canada, with the ICAO code CYHM. Its two runways are 12/30 and 06/24. The longer one, 12/30, is 10,006 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 231 routes through CYHM. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC), Vancouver (YVR) and Louisville (SDF).
