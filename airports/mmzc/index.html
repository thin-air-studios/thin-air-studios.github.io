About MMZC

General Leobardo C. Ruiz International Airport (ZCL) is a medium airport in Zacatecas, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMZC. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 42 routes through MMZC. Its most frequent links are Tijuana (TIJ), Los Angeles (LAX) and Chicago (MDW).
