About HLGD

Sirt International Airport / Ghardabiya Airbase (SRX) is a large airport in Sirt, Libya, with the ICAO code HLGD. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 11,807 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 taxiway segments, 23 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AAW has 1 routes through HLGD. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is HLLT (HLLT).
