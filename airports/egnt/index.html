About EGNT

Newcastle International Airport (NCL) is a large airport in Newcastle upon Tyne, Tyne and Wear, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGNT. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,644 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 67 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 67 airlines have 2,695 routes through EGNT. Its most frequent links are Belfast (BFS), Alicante (ALC) and Paris (CDG).
