About ZGSZ

Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport (SZX) is a large airport in Shenzhen, China, with the ICAO code ZGSZ. It is number 19 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 66.5 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 16L/34R, 16R/34L and 15/33. The longest, 16L/34R, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 101 gates, 617 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 116 airlines have 3,008 routes through ZGSZ. Its most frequent links are Chongqing (CKG), Hangzhou (HGH) and Shanghai (SHA).
