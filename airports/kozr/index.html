About KOZR

Cairns AAF (Fort Rucker) Air Field (OZR) is a medium airport in Fort Rucker/Ozark, United States, with the ICAO code KOZR. Its two runways are 18/36 and 06/24. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,025 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 23 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOZR.
