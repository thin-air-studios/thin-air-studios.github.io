About LFBF

Toulouse-Francazal (BA 101) Air Base (LFBF) is a medium airport in Toulouse/Francazal, Haute-Garonne, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,906 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFBF.
