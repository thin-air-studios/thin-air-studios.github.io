About KARM

Wharton Regional Airport (WHT) is a small airport in Wharton, United States, with the ICAO code KARM. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,004 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments and 11 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KARM.
