About SASJ

Gobernador Horacio Guzman International Airport (JUJ) is a large airport in San Salvador de Jujuy, Argentina, with the ICAO code SASJ. It has one runway, 16/34, 9,698 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 31 routes through SASJ. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP) and Buenos Aires (EZE).
