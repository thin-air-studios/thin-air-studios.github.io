About ENZV

Stavanger Airport, Sola (SVG) is a large airport in Stavanger, Norway, with the ICAO code ENZV. Its two runways are 18/36 and 10/28. The longer one, 18/36, is 9,369 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 123 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 59 airlines have 1,011 routes through ENZV. Its most frequent links are Bergen (BGO), Oslo (OSL) and Aberdeen (ABZ).
