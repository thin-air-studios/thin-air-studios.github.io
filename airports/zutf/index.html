About ZUTF

Chengdu Tianfu International Airport (TFU) is a large airport in Chengdu (Jianyang), China, with the ICAO code ZUTF. It is number 27 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 56.7 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 01/19, 11/29 and 02/20. The longest, 01/19, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 88 gates, 664 taxiway segments, 27 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 62 airlines have 2,541 routes through ZUTF. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Wuhan (WUH) and Kunming (KMG).
