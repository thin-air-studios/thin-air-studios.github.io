About OPPS

Bacha Khan International Airport (PEW) is a large airport in Peshawar, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPPS. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 143 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 82 routes through OPPS. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Riyadh (RUH) and Doha (DOH).
