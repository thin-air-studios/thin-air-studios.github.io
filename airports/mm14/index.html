About MM14

Campo Ríoverde National Airport (MM14) is a small airport in Rioverde, Mexico. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,325 ft long and 76 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows only the runways. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MM14.
