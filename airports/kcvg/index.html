About KCVG

Cincinnati Northern Kentucky International Airport (CVG) is a large airport in Cincinnati / Covington, United States, with the ICAO code KCVG. Its 4 runways are 09/27, 18C/36C, 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longest, 09/27, is 12,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 gates, 309 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 69 airlines have 2,001 routes through KCVG. Its most frequent links are Schkeuditz (LEJ), Chicago (ORD) and Miami (MIA).
