About LICC

Catania-Fontanarossa Airport (CTA) is a large airport in Catania, Italy, with the ICAO code LICC. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,989 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 gates, 84 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 106 airlines have 2,951 routes through LICC. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Rome (FCO) and London (LGW).
