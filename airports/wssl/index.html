About WSSL

Seletar Airport (XSP) is a medium airport in Seletar, Singapore, with the ICAO code WSSL. It has one runway, 03/21, 6,023 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 155 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 24 routes through WSSL. Its most frequent links are Subang (SZB), Melbourne (MEL) and Phuket (HKT).
