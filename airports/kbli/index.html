About KBLI

Bellingham International Airport (BLI) is a medium airport in Bellingham, United States, with the ICAO code KBLI. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,700 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 11 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 63 routes through KBLI. Its most frequent links are Seattle (SEA), Las Vegas (LAS) and Mesa (AZA).
