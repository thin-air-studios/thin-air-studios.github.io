About KFMN

Four Corners Regional Airport (FMN) is a medium airport in Farmington, United States, with the ICAO code KFMN. Its two runways are 07/25 and 05/23. The longer one, 07/25, is 6,704 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 58 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through KFMN. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Albuquerque (ABQ) and Durango (DRO).
