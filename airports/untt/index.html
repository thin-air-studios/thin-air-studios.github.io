About UNTT

Tomsk Kamov Airport (TOF) is a large airport in Tomsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UNTT. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,202 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 80 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 143 routes through UNTT. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Antalya (AYT) and Moscow (SVO).
