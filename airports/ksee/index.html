About KSEE

Gillespie Field (SEE) is a small airport in San Diego/El Cajon, United States, with the ICAO code KSEE. Its 3 runways are 09L/27R, 17/35 and 09R/27L. The longest, 09L/27R, is 5,342 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 79 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSEE.
