About VODG

Dundigul Air Force Academy (VODG) is a medium airport in Hyderabad, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 8,250 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VODG.
