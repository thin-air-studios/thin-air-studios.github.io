About KASW

Warsaw Municipal Airport (KASW) is a small airport in Warsaw, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 09/27 and 18/36. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments and 25 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SNC has 1 routes through KASW. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Louisville (SDF).
