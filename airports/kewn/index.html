About KEWN

Coastal Carolina Regional Airport (EWN) is a medium airport in New Bern, United States, with the ICAO code KEWN. Its two runways are 04/22 and 14/32. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,452 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 68 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 39 routes through KEWN. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Raleigh/Durham (RDU) and Hartford (BDL).
