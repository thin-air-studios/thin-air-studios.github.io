About SCTE

El Tepual International Airport (PMC) is a large airport in Puerto Montt, Chile, with the ICAO code SCTE. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,694 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 50 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 204 routes through SCTE. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), Punta Arenas (PUQ) and Balmaceda (BBA).
