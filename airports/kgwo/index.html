About KGWO

Greenwood–Leflore Airport (GWO) is a medium airport in Greenwood, United States, with the ICAO code KGWO. Its 3 runways are 18/36, 05/23 and 09/27. The longest, 18/36, is 6,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SBI has 1 routes through KGWO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Albany (ALB).
