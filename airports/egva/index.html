About EGVA

RAF Fairford (FFD) is a medium airport in Fairford, Gloucestershire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGVA. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,994 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 33 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 4 routes through EGVA. Its most frequent links are Grâce-Hollogne (LGG), New York (JFK) and Portsmouth (PSM).
