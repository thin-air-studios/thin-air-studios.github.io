About KPTB

Dinwiddie County Airport (PTB) is a small airport in Petersburg, United States, with the ICAO code KPTB. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPTB.
