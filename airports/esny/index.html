About ESNY

Söderhamn Airport (SOO) is a medium airport in Söderhamn, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESNY. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,281 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ESNY.
