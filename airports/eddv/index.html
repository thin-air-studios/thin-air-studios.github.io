About EDDV

Hannover Airport (HAJ) is a large airport in Hannover, Germany, with the ICAO code EDDV. Its 3 runways are 09L/27R, 09R/27L and 09C/27C. The longest, 09L/27R, is 12,434 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 116 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 125 airlines have 2,724 routes through EDDV. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Paris (CDG).
