About RJCJ

JASDF Chitose Air Base (RJCJ) is a medium airport in Chitose, Japan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longer one, 18L/36R, is 13,124 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 gates, 194 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Emirates has 1 routes through RJCJ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Okinawa (DNA).
