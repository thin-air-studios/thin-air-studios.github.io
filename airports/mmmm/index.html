About MMMM

General Francisco J. Mujica International Airport (MLM) is a large airport in Morelia, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMMM. It has one runway, 05/23, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 49 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 152 routes through MMMM. Its most frequent links are Tijuana (TIJ), Chicago (ORD) and Mexico City (MEX).
