About VEJT

Jorhat Airport (JRH) is a medium airport in Jorhat, India, with the ICAO code VEJT. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, IndiGo has 6 routes through VEJT. Its most frequent links are Kolkata (CCU), Guwahati (GAU) and New Delhi (DEL).
