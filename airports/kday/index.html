About KDAY

James M. Cox Dayton International Airport (DAY) is a medium airport in Dayton, United States, with the ICAO code KDAY. Its 3 runways are 06L/24R, 18/36 and 06R/24L. The longest, 06L/24R, is 10,901 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 316 routes through KDAY. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Dulles (IAD) and Charlotte (CLT).
