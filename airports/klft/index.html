About KLFT

Lafayette Regional Airport (LFT) is a medium airport in Lafayette, United States, with the ICAO code KLFT. Its 3 runways are 04R/22L, 11/29 and 04L/22R. The longest, 04R/22L, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 76 routes through KLFT. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Atlanta (ATL).
