About DABB

Annaba Rabah Bitat Airport (AAE) is a large airport in Annaba, Algeria, with the ICAO code DABB. Its two runways are 18/36 and 05/23. The longer one, 18/36, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 58 routes through DABB. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG), Marignane (MRS) and Es-Sénia (ORN).
