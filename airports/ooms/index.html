About OOMS

Muscat International Airport (MCT) is a large airport in Muscat/Seeb, Oman, with the ICAO code OOMS. Its two runways are 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longer one, 08R/26L, is 13,386 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 234 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 69 airlines have 1,034 routes through OOMS. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Doha (DOH) and Salalah (SLL).
