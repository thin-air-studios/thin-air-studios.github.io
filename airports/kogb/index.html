About KOGB

Orangeburg Municipal Airport (OGB) is a medium airport in Orangeburg, United States, with the ICAO code KOGB. Its two runways are 17/35 and 05/23. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,399 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOGB.
