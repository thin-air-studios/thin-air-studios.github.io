About KMTC

Selfridge Air National Guard Base Airport (MTC) is a medium airport in Mount Clemens, United States, with the ICAO code KMTC. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 197 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMTC.
