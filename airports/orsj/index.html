About ORSJ

Jalal Talabani International Airport (ISU) is a medium airport in Sulaymaniyah, Iraq, with the ICAO code ORSJ. It has one runway, 13/31, 11,481 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ORSJ.
