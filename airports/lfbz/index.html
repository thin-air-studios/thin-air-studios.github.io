About LFBZ

Biarritz Pays Basque airport (BIQ) is a medium airport in Biarritz, France, with the ICAO code LFBZ. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,382 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 677 routes through LFBZ. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS).
