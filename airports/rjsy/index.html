About RJSY

Shonai Airport (SYO) is a medium airport in Shonai, Japan, with the ICAO code RJSY. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,560 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 20 routes through RJSY. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Narita (NRT) and Taoyuan (TPE).
