About MMSD

Los Cabos International Airport (SJD) is a large airport in San José del Cabo, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMSD. It has one runway, 16/34, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 140 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 395 routes through MMSD. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Guadalajara (GDL) and Tijuana (TIJ).
