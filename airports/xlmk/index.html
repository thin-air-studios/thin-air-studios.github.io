About XLMK

Kirovsk-Apatity Airport (KVK) is a small airport in Apatity, Russia, with the ICAO code XLMK. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,202 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for XLMK.
