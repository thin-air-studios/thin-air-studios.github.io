About PGUM

Antonio B. Won Pat International Airport (GUM) is a large airport in Hagåtña, Guam, with the ICAO code PGUM. Its two runways are 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06L/24R, is 12,015 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 234 routes through PGUM. Its most frequent links are Narita (NRT), Seoul (ICN) and Osaka (KIX).
