About KACV

California Redwood Coast-Humboldt County Airport (ACV) is a medium airport in Arcata/Eureka, United States, with the ICAO code KACV. Its two runways are 14/32 and 01/19. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,046 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 59 routes through KACV. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Sacramento (SMF) and Burbank (BUR).
