About KSUS

Spirit of St Louis Airport (SUS) is a medium airport in St Louis, United States, with the ICAO code KSUS. Its two runways are 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longer one, 08R/26L, is 7,486 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 146 taxiway segments, 22 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 6 routes through KSUS. Its most frequent links are Atlanta (FTY), Columbus (LCK) and Akron (CAK).
