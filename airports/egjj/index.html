About EGJJ

Jersey Airport (JER) is a medium airport in St. Peter, Jersey, with the ICAO code EGJJ. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,594 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 20 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 37 airlines have 841 routes through EGJJ. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Liverpool (LPL) and Saint Peter Port (GCI).
