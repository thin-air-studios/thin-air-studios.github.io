About EGXW

RAF Waddington (WTN) is a medium airport in Lincoln, Lincolnshire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGXW. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,642 ft long and 198 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 73 taxiway segments and 26 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EGXW.
