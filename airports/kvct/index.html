About KVCT

Victoria Regional Airport (VCT) is a medium airport in Victoria, United States, with the ICAO code KVCT. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 9,111 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 15 routes through KVCT. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
