About KLBF

North Platte Regional Airport Lee Bird Field (LBF) is a medium airport in North Platte, United States, with the ICAO code KLBF. Its two runways are 12/30 and 17/35. The longer one, 12/30, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 14 routes through KLBF. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN) and Omaha (OMA).
