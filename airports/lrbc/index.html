About LRBC

Bacău George Enescu International Airport (BCM) is a large airport in Bacău, Romania, with the ICAO code LRBC. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,203 ft long and 262 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 319 routes through LRBC. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Rome (FCO) and Zaventem (BRU).
