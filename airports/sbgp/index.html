About SBGP

EMBRAER - Unidade Gavião Peixoto Airport (SBGP) is a small airport in Gavião Peixoto, Brazil. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 02R/20L and 02L/20R. The longer one, 02R/20L, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SBGP.
