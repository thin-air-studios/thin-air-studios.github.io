About ZBTJ

Tianjin Binhai International Airport (TSN) is a large airport in Tianjin, China, with the ICAO code ZBTJ. Its two runways are 16R/34L and 16L/34R. The longer one, 16R/34L, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 gates, 277 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 71 airlines have 1,164 routes through ZBTJ. Its most frequent links are Chengdu (TFU), Shanghai (PVG) and Chongqing (CKG).
