About RCPO

Hsinchu Air Base (HSZ) is a medium airport in Hsinchu City, Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCPO. It has one runway, 05/23, 11,955 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 80 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RCPO.
