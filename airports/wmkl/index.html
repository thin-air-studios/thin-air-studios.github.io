About WMKL

Langkawi International Airport (LGK) is a large airport in Langkawi, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WMKL. It has one runway, 03/21, 12,500 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 155 routes through WMKL. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Subang (SZB) and Penang (PEN).
