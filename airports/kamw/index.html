About KAMW

Ames Municipal Airport (AMW) is a small airport in Ames, United States, with the ICAO code KAMW. Its two runways are 01/19 and 13/31. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,701 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAMW.
