About KGXF

Gila Bend Air Force Auxiliary Airport (KGXF) is a small airport in Gila Bend, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,503 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 4 aprons.
