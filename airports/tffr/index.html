About TFFR

Maryse Condé International Airport (PTP) is a large airport in Pointe-à-Pitre, Guadeloupe, with the ICAO code TFFR. It has one runway, 12/30, 11,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 101 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 13 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 356 routes through TFFR. Its most frequent links are Fort-de-France (FDF), Paris (ORY) and Grand Case (SFG).
