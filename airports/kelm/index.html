About KELM

Elmira Corning Regional Airport (ELM) is a medium airport in Elmira/Corning, United States, with the ICAO code KELM. Its 3 runways are 06/24, 10/28 and 05/23. The longest, 06/24, is 8,001 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 52 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 33 routes through KELM. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Detroit (DTW) and Rochester (ROC).
