About VOTR

Tiruchirappalli International Airport (TRZ) is a large airport in Tiruchirappalli, India, with the ICAO code VOTR. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,115 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 199 routes through VOTR. Its most frequent links are Chennai (MAA), Bengaluru (BLR) and Singapore (SIN).
