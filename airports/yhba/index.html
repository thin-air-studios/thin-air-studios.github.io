About YHBA

Hervey Bay Airport (HVB) is a medium airport in Hervey Bay, Australia, with the ICAO code YHBA. It has one runway, 11/29, 6,561 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 39 routes through YHBA. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Sydney (SYD) and Melbourne (MEL).
