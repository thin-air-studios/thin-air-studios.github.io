About KSBN

South Bend International Airport (SBN) is a medium airport in South Bend, United States, with the ICAO code KSBN. Its 3 runways are 09R/27L, 18/36 and 09L/27R. The longest, 09R/27L, is 8,412 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 146 routes through KSBN. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Punta Gorda (PGD) and Orlando (SFB).
