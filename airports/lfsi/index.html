About LFSI

Saint-Dizier – Robinson Air Base (LFSI) is a medium airport in Saint-Dizier, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,913 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 120 taxiway segments, 57 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFSI.
