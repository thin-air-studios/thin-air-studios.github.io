About KOEB

Branch County Memorial Airport (KOEB) is a small airport in Coldwater, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 07/25, 04/22 and 16/34. The longest, 07/25, is 5,350 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOEB.
