About KP33

Cochise County Airport (CWX) is a small airport in Willcox, United States, with the ICAO code KP33. It has one runway, 3/21, 6,095 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KP33.
