About NSTU

Pago Pago International Airport (PPG) is a large airport in Pago Pago, American Samoa, with the ICAO code NSTU. Its two runways are 05/23 and 08/26. The longer one, 05/23, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 5 routes through NSTU. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Honolulu (HNL).
