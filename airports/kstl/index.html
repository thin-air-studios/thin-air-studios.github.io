About KSTL

St. Louis Lambert International Airport (STL) is a large airport in St Louis, United States, with the ICAO code KSTL. Its 4 runways are 12R/30L, 12L/30R, 11/29 and 06/24. The longest, 12R/30L, is 11,020 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 gates, 280 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 1,045 routes through KSTL. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN) and Orlando (MCO).
