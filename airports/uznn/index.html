About UZNN

Nukus International Airport (NCU) is a large airport in Nukus, Uzbekistan, with the ICAO code UZNN. Its two runways are 15/33 and 07/25. The longer one, 15/33, is 9,865 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UZNN.
