About SBRP

Leite Lopes Airport (RAO) is a medium airport in Ribeirão Preto, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBRP. It has one runway, 18/36, 6,890 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 114 routes through SBRP. Its most frequent links are Campinas (VCP), São Paulo (GRU) and São Paulo (CGH).
