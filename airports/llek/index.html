About LLEK

Tel Nof Air Base (LLEK) is a medium airport in Rehovot, Israel. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 18/36, 15R/33L and 15L/33R. The longest, 18/36, is 7,880 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LLEK.
