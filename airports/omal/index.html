About OMAL

Al Ain International Airport (AAN) is a large airport in Al Ain, United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMAL. It has one runway, 01/19, 13,123 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 139 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 19 routes through OMAL. Its most frequent links are Amman (AMM), Calicut (CCJ) and Cairo (CAI).
