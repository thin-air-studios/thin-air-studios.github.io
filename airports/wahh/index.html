About WAHH

Adisutjipto International Airport (JOG) is a medium airport in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAHH. Its two runways are 09/27 and 09R/27L. The longer one, 09/27, is 7,215 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 81 routes through WAHH. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (HLP), Jakarta (CGK) and Surabaya (SUB).
