About SAZR

Santa Rosa Airport (RSA) is a medium airport in Santa Rosa, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAZR. It has one runway, 1/19, 7,546 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Aerolineas has 2 routes through SAZR. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Buenos Aires (AEP).
