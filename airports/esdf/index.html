About ESDF

Ronneby Airport (RNB) is a medium airport in Ronneby, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESDF. It has one runway, 01/19, 7,648 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 56 routes through ESDF. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Stockholm (BMA) and Antalya (AYT).
