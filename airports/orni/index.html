About ORNI

Al Najaf International Airport (NJF) is a large airport in Najaf, Iraq, with the ICAO code ORNI. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 141 routes through ORNI. Its most frequent links are Tehran (IKA), Kuwait City (KWI) and Baghdad (BGW).
