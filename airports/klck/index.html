About KLCK

Rickenbacker International Airport (LCK) is a medium airport in Columbus, United States, with the ICAO code KLCK. Its two runways are 05R/23L and 05L/23R. The longer one, 05R/23L, is 12,103 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 55 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 172 routes through KLCK. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), Punta Gorda (PGD) and Pinellas Park (PIE).
