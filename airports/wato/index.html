About WATO

Komodo Airport (LBJ) is a small airport in Labuan Bajo, Manggarai Barat, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WATO. It has one runway, 17/35, 7,381 ft long and 145 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 39 routes through WATO. Its most frequent links are Kuta (DPS), Jakarta (CGK) and Surabaya (SUB).
