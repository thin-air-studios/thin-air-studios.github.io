About HAGO

Gode Airport (GDE) is a medium airport in Gode, Ethiopia, with the ICAO code HAGO. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,505 ft long and 115 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Ethiopian has 3 routes through HAGO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Jijiga (JIJ).
