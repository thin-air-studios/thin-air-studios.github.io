About LFOC

Châteaudun Airfield (LFOC) is a medium airport in Châteaudun, Eure-et-Loir, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 7,552 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments and 13 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFOC.
