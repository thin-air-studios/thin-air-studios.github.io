About KMDT

Harrisburg International Airport (MDT) is a medium airport in Harrisburg, United States, with the ICAO code KMDT. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,001 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 43 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 396 routes through KMDT. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Chicago (ORD) and Charlotte (CLT).
