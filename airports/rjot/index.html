About RJOT

Takamatsu Airport (TAK) is a large airport in Takamatsu, Japan, with the ICAO code RJOT. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,200 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 93 routes through RJOT. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Narita (NRT) and Seoul (ICN).
