About RJSS

Sendai Airport (SDJ) is a large airport in Natori, Japan, with the ICAO code RJSS. Its two runways are 09/27 and 12/30. The longer one, 09/27, is 9,842 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 67 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 286 routes through RJSS. Its most frequent links are Osaka (ITM), Sapporo (CTS) and Tokoname (NGO).
