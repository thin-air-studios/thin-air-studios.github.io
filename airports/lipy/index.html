About LIPY

Marche Airport (AOI) is a medium airport in Falconara Marittima (AN), Italy, with the ICAO code LIPY. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,718 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 248 routes through LIPY. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Munich (MUC) and Charleroi (CRL).
