About ESGJ

Jönköping Airport (JKG) is a medium airport in Jönköping, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESGJ. Its two runways are 01/19 and 11/29. The longer one, 01/19, is 7,228 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 66 routes through ESGJ. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Gran Canaria Island (LPA) and Helsinki (HEL).
