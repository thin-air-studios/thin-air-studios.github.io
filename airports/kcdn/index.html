About KCDN

Woodward Field (CDN) is a small airport in Camden, United States, with the ICAO code KCDN. Its two runways are 06/24 and 14/32. The longer one, 06/24, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Ryanair has 1 routes through KCDN. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Birmingham (BHX).
