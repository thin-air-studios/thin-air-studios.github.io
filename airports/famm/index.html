About FAMM

Mmabatho International Airport (MBD) is a medium airport in Mafeking, South Africa, with the ICAO code FAMM. It has one runway, 04/22, 15,157 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FAMM.
