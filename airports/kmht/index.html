About KMHT

Manchester-Boston Regional Airport (MHT) is a medium airport in Manchester, United States, with the ICAO code KMHT. Its two runways are 17/35 and 06/24. The longer one, 17/35, is 9,250 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 78 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 332 routes through KMHT. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Louisville (SDF) and Dulles (IAD).
