About EGPI

Islay Airport (ILY) is a medium airport in Isle of Islay, Argyll and Bute, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGPI. Its two runways are 12/30 and 07/25. The longer one, 12/30, is 5,098 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Loganair has 12 routes through EGPI. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Glasgow (GLA).
