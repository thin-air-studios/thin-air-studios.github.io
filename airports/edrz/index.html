About EDRZ

Zweibrücken Airport (ZQW) is a small airport in Zweibrücken, Germany, with the ICAO code EDRZ. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,776 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 15 routes through EDRZ. Its most frequent links are Berlin (BER), Luxembourg (LUX) and Burgas (BOJ).
