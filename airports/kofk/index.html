About KOFK

Karl Stefan Memorial Airport (OFK) is a medium airport in Norfolk, United States, with the ICAO code KOFK. Its two runways are 14/32 and 02/20. The longer one, 14/32, is 5,806 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AMF has 1 routes through KOFK. Its most frequent links are Omaha (OMA) and O'Neill (ONL).
