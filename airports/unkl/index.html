About UNKL

Krasnoyarsk International Airport (KJA) is a large airport in Krasnoyarsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UNKL. It has one runway, 11/29, 12,139 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 272 taxiway segments, 48 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 51 airlines have 649 routes through UNKL. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and Sochi (AER).
