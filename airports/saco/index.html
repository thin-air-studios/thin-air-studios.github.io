About SACO

Ingeniero Aeronáutico Ambrosio L.V. Taravella International Airport (COR) is a large airport in Cordoba, Argentina, with the ICAO code SACO. Its two runways are 01/19 and 05/23. The longer one, 01/19, is 10,499 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 273 routes through SACO. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP), Santiago (SCL) and São Paulo (GRU).
