About ZBAA

Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) is a large airport in Beijing, China, with the ICAO code ZBAA. It is number 15 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 70.7 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 01/19, 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longest, 01/19, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 126 gates, 628 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 15 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 113 airlines have 2,880 routes through ZBAA. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (SHA), Hangzhou (HGH) and Shenzhen (SZX).
