About KOGS

Ogdensburg International Airport (OGS) is a medium airport in Ogdensburg, United States, with the ICAO code KOGS. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,400 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 17 routes through KOGS. Its most frequent links are Dulles (IAD), Philadelphia (PHL) and Orlando (MCO).
