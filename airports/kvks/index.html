About KVKS

Vicksburg Municipal Airport (VKS) is a small airport in Vicksburg, United States, with the ICAO code KVKS. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,000 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KVKS.
