About OEJN

King Abdulaziz International Airport (JED) is a large airport in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEJN. It is number 36 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 53.4 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 16C/34C, 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longest, 16C/34C, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 320 taxiway segments, 36 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 116 airlines have 2,490 routes through OEJN. Its most frequent links are Riyadh (RUH), Cairo (CAI) and Dubai (DXB).
