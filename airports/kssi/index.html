About KSSI

St Simons Island Airport (SSI) is a medium airport in St Simons Island, United States, with the ICAO code KSSI. Its two runways are 04/22 and 16/34. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,584 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSSI.
