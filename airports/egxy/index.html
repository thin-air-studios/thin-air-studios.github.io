About EGXY

RAF Syerston (EGXY) is a small airport in Newark on Trent, Nottinghamshire, United Kingdom. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 06/24 and 15/33. The longer one, 06/24, is 5,994 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EGXY.
