About OPNH

Shaheed Benazirabad Airport (WNS) is a medium airport in Nawabashah, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPNH. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,999 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OPNH.
