About KEFK

Northeast Kingdom International Airport (EFK) is a small airport in Newport, United States, with the ICAO code KEFK. Its two runways are 18/36 and 05/23. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,301 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KEFK.
