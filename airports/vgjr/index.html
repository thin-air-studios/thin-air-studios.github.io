About VGJR

Jessore Airport (JSR) is a medium airport in Jashore (Jessore), Bangladesh, with the ICAO code VGJR. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 141 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 26 routes through VGJR. Its most frequent links are Dhaka (DAC), Cox's Bazar (CXB) and Chattogram (CGP).
