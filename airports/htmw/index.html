About HTMW

Mwanza International Airport (MWZ) is a large airport in Mwanza, Tanzania, with the ICAO code HTMW. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,212 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 15 routes through HTMW. Its most frequent links are Dar es Salaam (DAR), Bukoba (BKZ) and Arusha (JRO).
