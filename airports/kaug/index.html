About KAUG

Augusta State Airport (AUG) is a medium airport in Augusta, United States, with the ICAO code KAUG. Its two runways are 17/35 and 08/26. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 21 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Cape Air has 7 routes through KAUG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Boston (BOS).
