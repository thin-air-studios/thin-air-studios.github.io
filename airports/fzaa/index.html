About FZAA

Ndjili International Airport (FIH) is a large airport in Kinshasa, Congo - Kinshasa, with the ICAO code FZAA. It has one runway, 06/24, 13,123 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 87 routes through FZAA. Its most frequent links are Zaventem (BRU), Brazzaville (BZV) and Paris (CDG).
