About KIAH

George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH) is a large airport in Houston, United States, with the ICAO code KIAH. It is number 47 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 48.1 million passengers in 2025. It has 5 runways, from 9,000 ft to 12,001 ft long. The longest is 15L/33R. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 148 gates, 420 taxiway segments, 23 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 90 airlines have 4,003 routes through KIAH. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Chicago (ORD) and Los Angeles (LAX).
