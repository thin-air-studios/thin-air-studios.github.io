About KSGJ

Northeast Florida Regional Airport (UST) is a medium airport in St Augustine, United States, with the ICAO code KSGJ. Its 3 runways are 13/31, 06/24 and 02/20. The longest, 13/31, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 125 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Frontier has 1 routes through KSGJ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Ewing Township (TTN).
