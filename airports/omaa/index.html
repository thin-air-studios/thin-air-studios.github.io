About OMAA

Zayed International Airport (AUH) is a large airport in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMAA. Its two runways are 13R/31L and 13L/31R. The longer one, 13R/31L, is 13,471 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 95 gates, 409 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 91 airlines have 2,159 routes through OMAA. Its most frequent links are Doha (DOH), Kochi (COK) and Mumbai (BOM).
