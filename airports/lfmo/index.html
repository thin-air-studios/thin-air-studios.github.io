About LFMO

Orange-Caritat (BA 115) Air Base (LFMO) is a medium airport in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,909 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 114 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines.
