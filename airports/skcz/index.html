About SKCZ

Las Brujas Airport (CZU) is a medium airport in Corozal, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKCZ. It has one runway, 02/20, 4,930 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 14 routes through SKCZ. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG) and Medellín (EOH).
