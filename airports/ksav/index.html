About KSAV

Savannah Hilton Head International Airport (SAV) is a large airport in Savannah, United States, with the ICAO code KSAV. Its two runways are 10/28 and 01/19. The longer one, 10/28, is 9,351 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 49 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 37 airlines have 344 routes through KSAV. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Dulles (IAD) and Atlanta (ATL).
