About ZLIC

Yinchuan Hedong International Airport (INC) is a large airport in Yinchuan, China, with the ICAO code ZLIC. It has one runway, 03/21, 11,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 82 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 550 routes through ZLIC. Its most frequent links are Hangzhou (HGH), Shanghai (PVG) and Chengdu (TFU).
