About KAGR

MacDill Air Force Base Auxiliary Field (KAGR) is a small airport in Avon Park, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAGR.
