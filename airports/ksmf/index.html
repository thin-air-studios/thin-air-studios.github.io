About KSMF

Sacramento International Airport (SMF) is a large airport in Sacramento, United States, with the ICAO code KSMF. Its two runways are 17L/35R and 17R/35L. The longer one, 17L/35R, is 8,605 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 gates, 119 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 885 routes through KSMF. Its most frequent links are Los Angeles (LAX), San Diego (SAN) and Denver (DEN).
