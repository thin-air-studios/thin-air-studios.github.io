About KSAF

Santa Fe Municipal Airport (SAF) is a medium airport in Santa Fe, United States, with the ICAO code KSAF. Its 3 runways are 02/20, 15/33 and 10/28. The longest, 02/20, is 8,366 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 48 routes through KSAF. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Los Angeles (LAX).
