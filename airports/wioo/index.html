About WIOO

Supadio International Airport (PNK) is a large airport in Pontianak, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIOO. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,380 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 85 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 141 routes through WIOO. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Batam (BTH) and Surabaya (SUB).
