About KLWC

Lawrence Municipal Airport (LWC) is a small airport in Lawrence, United States, with the ICAO code KLWC. Its two runways are 15/33 and 01/19. The longer one, 15/33, is 5,701 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLWC.
