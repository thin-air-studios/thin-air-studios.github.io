About KHEZ

Natchez–Adams County Airport / Hardy-Anders Field (HEZ) is a small airport in Natchez, United States, with the ICAO code KHEZ. Its two runways are 14/32 and 18/36. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SkyWest has 3 routes through KHEZ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Houston (IAH).
