About ESSV

Visby Airport (VBY) is a large airport in Visby, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESSV. Its two runways are 03/21 and 10/28. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,562 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 190 routes through ESSV. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (BMA), Stockholm (ARN) and Göteborg (GOT).
