About LIRP

Pisa International Airport (PSA) is a large airport in Pisa (PI), Italy, with the ICAO code LIRP. Its two runways are 03R/21L and 03L/21R. The longer one, 03R/21L, is 9,820 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 42 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 64 airlines have 1,901 routes through LIRP. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), London (STN) and Paris (ORY).
