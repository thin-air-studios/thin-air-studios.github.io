About WMKP

Penang International Airport (PEN) is a large airport in Penang, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WMKP. It has one runway, 04/22, 10,997 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 78 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 52 airlines have 694 routes through WMKP. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Subang (SZB) and Singapore (SIN).
