About UKFF

Simferopol International Airport (SIP) is a large airport in Simferopol, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKFF. It has one runway, 01L/19R, 12,142 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 32 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 32 airlines have 648 routes through UKFF. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (SVO) and St. Petersburg (LED).
