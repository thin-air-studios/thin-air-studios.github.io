About EYKA

Kaunas International Airport (KUN) is a large airport in Kaunas, Lithuania, with the ICAO code EYKA. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,663 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 532 routes through EYKA. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Dublin (DUB) and London (STN).
