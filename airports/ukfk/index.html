About UKFK

Kerch Airport (KHC) is a small airport in Kerch, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKFK. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,420 ft long and 115 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UKFK.
