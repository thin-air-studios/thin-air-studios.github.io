About ENGM

Oslo-Gardermoen International Airport (OSL) is a large airport in Oslo (Gardermoen), Norway, with the ICAO code ENGM. Its two runways are 01L/19R and 01R/19L. The longer one, 01L/19R, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 gates, 317 taxiway segments, 21 aprons and 45 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 152 airlines have 4,595 routes through ENGM. Its most frequent links are Trondheim (TRD), Copenhagen (CPH) and Stockholm (ARN).
