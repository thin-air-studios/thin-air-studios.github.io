About OIKQ

Qeshm International Airport (GSM) is a large airport in Qeshm(Dayrestan), Iran, with the ICAO code OIKQ. It has one runway, 05/23, 13,864 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 29 routes through OIKQ. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Dubai (DXB) and Kish Island (KIH).
