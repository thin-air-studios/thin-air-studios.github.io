About KLUM

Menomonie Municipal Score Field (KLUM) is a small airport in Menomonie, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 09/27 and 18/36. The longer one, 09/27, is 5,074 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLUM.
