About GOGS

Cap Skirring Airport (CSK) is a medium airport in Cap Skirring, Senegal, with the ICAO code GOGS. It has one runway, 14/32, 6,573 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 12 routes through GOGS. Its most frequent links are Dakar (DSS), Marrakesh (RAK) and Paris (ORY).
