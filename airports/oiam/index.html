About OIAM

Mahshahr Airport (MRX) is a medium airport in Mahshahr, Iran, with the ICAO code OIAM. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 10 routes through OIAM. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Mashhad (MHD) and Isfahan (IFN).
