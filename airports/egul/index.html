About EGUL

RAF Lakenheath (LKZ) is a medium airport in Brandon, Suffolk, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGUL. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,998 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 378 taxiway segments and 63 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Kalitta has 1 routes through EGUL. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Kuwait City (KWI).
