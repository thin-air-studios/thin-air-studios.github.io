About LIPQ

Trieste Airport (TRS) is a large airport in Ronchi dei Legionari/Trieste, Italy, with the ICAO code LIPQ. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 349 routes through LIPQ. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and Valencia (VLC).
