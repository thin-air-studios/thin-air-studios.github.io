About YMIA

Mildura Airport (MQL) is a medium airport in Mildura, Australia, with the ICAO code YMIA. Its two runways are 09/27 and 18/36. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 63 routes through YMIA. Its most frequent links are Melbourne (MEL), Sydney (SYD) and Broken Hill (BHQ).
