About CYND

Ottawa / Gatineau Airport (YND) is a medium airport in Gatineau, Canada, with the ICAO code CYND. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, PRO has 1 routes through CYND. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Rouyn-Noranda (YUY).
