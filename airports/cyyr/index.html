About CYYR

Goose Bay Airport (YYR) is a medium airport in Goose Bay, Canada, with the ICAO code CYYR. Its two runways are 08/26 and 15/33. The longer one, 08/26, is 11,046 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 126 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 37 routes through CYYR. Its most frequent links are Halifax (YHZ), Reykjavík (KEF) and St. John's (YYT).
