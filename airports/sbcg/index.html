About SBCG

Campo Grande Airport (CGR) is a medium airport in Campo Grande, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBCG. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 113 routes through SBCG. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), São Paulo (CGH) and Cuiabá (CGB).
