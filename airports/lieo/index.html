About LIEO

Olbia Costa Smeralda Airport (OLB) is a large airport in Olbia (SS), Italy, with the ICAO code LIEO. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,006 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 9 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 73 airlines have 1,549 routes through LIEO. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Rome (FCO) and London (LGW).
