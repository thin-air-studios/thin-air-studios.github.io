About LGAD

Andravida Air Base (PYR) is a medium airport in Andravida, Greece, with the ICAO code LGAD. It has one runway, 16/34, 10,299 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LGAD.
