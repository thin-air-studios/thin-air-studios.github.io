About RJSN

Niigata Airport (KIJ) is a large airport in Niigata, Japan, with the ICAO code RJSN. Its two runways are 10/28 and 04/22. The longer one, 10/28, is 8,200 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 48 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 127 routes through RJSN. Its most frequent links are Osaka (ITM), Sapporo (CTS) and Tokoname (NGO).
