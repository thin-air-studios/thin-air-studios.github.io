About ZWTN

Hotan Airport (HTN) is a medium airport in Hotan, China, with the ICAO code ZWTN. It has one runway, 11/29, 10,499 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 71 routes through ZWTN. Its most frequent links are Ürümqi (URC), Lanzhou (LHW) and Zhengzhou (CGO).
