About USDD

Salekhard Airport (SLY) is a medium airport in Salekhard, Russia, with the ICAO code USDD. It has one runway, 04/22, 8,917 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 32 routes through USDD. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Tyumen (TJM) and Sochi (AER).
