About OIZI

Iranshahr Airport (IHR) is a medium airport in Iranshahr, Iran, with the ICAO code OIZI. It has one runway, 17/35, 7,711 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OIZI.
