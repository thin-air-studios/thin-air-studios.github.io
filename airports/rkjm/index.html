About RKJM

Mokpo Air Base (RKJM) is a small airport in Gonghang-ro (Mokpo), South Korea. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,249 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RKJM.
