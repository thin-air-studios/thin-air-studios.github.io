About UACK

Kokshetau International Airport (KOV) is a large airport in Kokshetau, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UACK. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,325 ft long and 125 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 15 routes through UACK. Its most frequent links are Almaty (ALA), Aktau (SCO) and Moscow (DME).
