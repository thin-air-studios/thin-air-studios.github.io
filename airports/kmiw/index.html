About KMIW

Marshalltown Municipal Airport (MIW) is a small airport in Marshalltown, United States, with the ICAO code KMIW. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMIW.
