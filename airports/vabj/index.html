About VABJ

Bhuj Airport (BHJ) is a medium airport in Bhuj, India, with the ICAO code VABJ. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,205 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 123 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 31 routes through VABJ. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), New Delhi (DEL) and Surat (STV).
