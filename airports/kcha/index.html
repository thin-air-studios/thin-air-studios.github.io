About KCHA

Chattanooga Metropolitan Airport (Lovell Field) (CHA) is a medium airport in Chattanooga, United States, with the ICAO code KCHA. Its two runways are 02/20 and 15/33. The longer one, 02/20, is 7,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 54 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 127 routes through KCHA. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Charlotte (CLT) and Atlanta (ATL).
