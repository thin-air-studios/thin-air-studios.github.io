About SMJP

Johan Adolf Pengel International Airport (PBM) is a large airport in Paramaribo, Suriname, with the ICAO code SMJP. It has one runway, 11/29, 11,417 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 51 routes through SMJP. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Miami (MIA) and Georgetown (GEO).
