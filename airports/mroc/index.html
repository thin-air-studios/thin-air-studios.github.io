About MROC

Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) is a large airport in San José (Alajuela), Costa Rica, with the ICAO code MROC. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,882 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 137 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 62 airlines have 691 routes through MROC. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), Tocumen (PTY) and Liberia (LIR).
