About LGKO

Kos International Airport "Ippokratis" (KGS) is a large airport in Kos Island, Greece, with the ICAO code LGKO. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,841 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 8 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 89 airlines have 1,760 routes through LGKO. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Zaventem (BRU) and London (LGW).
