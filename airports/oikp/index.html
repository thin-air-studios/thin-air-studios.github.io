About OIKP

Havadarya Airport (HDR) is a small airport in Bandar Abbas, Iran, with the ICAO code OIKP. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,525 ft long and 115 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OIKP.
