About KGCN

Grand Canyon National Park Airport (GCN) is a medium airport in Grand Canyon - Tusayan, United States, with the ICAO code KGCN. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,999 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through KGCN. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Girona (GRO).
