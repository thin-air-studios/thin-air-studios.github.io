About LIPR

Federico Fellini International Airport (RMI) is a large airport in Rimini (RN), Italy, with the ICAO code LIPR. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,828 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 206 routes through LIPR. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Rinas (TIA) and Luxembourg (LUX).
