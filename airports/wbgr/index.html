About WBGR

Miri Airport (MYY) is a medium airport in Miri, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WBGR. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,006 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 59 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 122 routes through WBGR. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Kuching (KCH) and Labuan (LBU).
