About ORTL

Ali Air Base (XNH) is a medium airport in Nasiriyah, Iraq, with the ICAO code ORTL. Its two runways are 12L/30R and 12R/30L. The longer one, 12L/30R, is 10,935 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 64 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ORTL.
