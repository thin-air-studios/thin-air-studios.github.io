About UNEE

Alexei Leonov Kemerovo International Airport (KEJ) is a large airport in Kemerovo, Russia, with the ICAO code UNEE. It has one runway, 05/23, 10,499 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 151 routes through UNEE. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Moscow (DME) and Antalya (AYT).
