About LTBT

Akhisar Airport / Akhisar Air Base (LTBT) is a medium airport in Akhisar, Türkiye. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 14L/32R and 14R/32L. The longer one, 14L/32R, is 9,815 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LTBT.
