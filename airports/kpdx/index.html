About KPDX

Portland International Airport (PDX) is a large airport in Portland, United States, with the ICAO code KPDX. Its 3 runways are 10R/28L, 10L/28R and 3/21. The longest, 10R/28L, is 11,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 gates, 230 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 46 airlines have 1,456 routes through KPDX. Its most frequent links are Seattle (SEA), San Francisco (SFO) and Denver (DEN).
