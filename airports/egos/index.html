About EGOS

RAF Shawbury (EGOS) is a medium airport in Shrewsbury, Shropshire, United Kingdom. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 18/36 and 04/22. The longer one, 18/36, is 6,018 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EGOS.
