About LSGG

Geneva International Airport (GVA) is a large airport in Geneva, Switzerland, with the ICAO code LSGG. It has one runway, 04/22, 12,795 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 gates, 155 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 131 airlines have 8,248 routes through LSGG. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Paris (CDG) and Porto (OPO).
