About ZSQD

Qingdao Jiaodong International Airport (TAO) is a large airport in Qingdao (Jiaozhou), China, with the ICAO code ZSQD. Its two runways are 16/34 and 17/35. The longer one, 16/34, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 83 gates, 485 taxiway segments, 35 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 70 airlines have 1,419 routes through ZSQD. Its most frequent links are Seoul (ICN), Hangzhou (HGH) and Harbin (HRB).
