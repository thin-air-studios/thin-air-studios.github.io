About KILG

Wilmington Airport (ILG) is a medium airport in Wilmington, United States, with the ICAO code KILG. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 01/19 and 14/32. The longest, 09/27, is 7,275 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 185 taxiway segments, 25 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 70 routes through KILG. Its most frequent links are Orlando (MCO), San Juan (SJU) and Wilmington (ILM).
