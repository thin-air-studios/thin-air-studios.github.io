About KMDD

Midland Airpark (MDD) is a small airport in Midland, United States, with the ICAO code KMDD. Its two runways are 07/25 and 16/34. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,571 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMDD.
