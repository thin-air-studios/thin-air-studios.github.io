About LKPR

Václav Havel Airport Prague (PRG) is a large airport in Prague, Czechia, with the ICAO code LKPR. Its two runways are 06/24 and 12/30. The longer one, 06/24, is 12,189 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 gates, 209 taxiway segments, 52 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 180 airlines have 5,470 routes through LKPR. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Antalya (AYT) and Amsterdam (AMS).
