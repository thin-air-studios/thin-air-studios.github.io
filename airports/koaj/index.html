About KOAJ

Albert J Ellis Airport (OAJ) is a medium airport in Richlands, United States, with the ICAO code KOAJ. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,100 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 38 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 54 routes through KOAJ. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Atlanta (ATL) and Washington (DCA).
