About SKPC

German Olano Airport (PCR) is a medium airport in Puerto Carreño, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKPC. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,907 ft long and 69 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through SKPC. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Bogota (BOG).
