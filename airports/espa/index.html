About ESPA

Luleå Airport (LLA) is a large airport in Luleå, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESPA. It has one runway, 14/32, 10,990 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 78 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 194 routes through ESPA. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Gran Canaria Island (LPA) and Paris (ORY).
