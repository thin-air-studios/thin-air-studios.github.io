About ZSSS

Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA) is a large airport in Shanghai (Minhang), China, with the ICAO code ZSSS. It is number 42 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 50.2 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longer one, 18L/36R, is 11,154 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 gates, 304 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 1,370 routes through ZSSS. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Shenzhen (SZX) and Beijing (PEK).
