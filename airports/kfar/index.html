About KFAR

Hector International Airport (FAR) is a medium airport in Fargo, United States, with the ICAO code KFAR. Its 3 runways are 18/36, 09/27 and 13/31. The longest, 18/36, is 9,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 113 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 172 routes through KFAR. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN) and Minneapolis (MSP).
