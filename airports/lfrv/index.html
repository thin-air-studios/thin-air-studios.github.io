About LFRV

Vannes-Meucon Airport (VNE) is a medium airport in Vannes/Meucon, France, with the ICAO code LFRV. Its two runways are 04/22 and 08/26. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,020 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFRV.
