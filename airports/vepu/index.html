About VEPU

Purnea Airport (VEPU) is a small airport in India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,700 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 14 routes through VEPU. Its most frequent links are Kolkata (CCU), New Delhi (DEL) and Hyderabad (HYD).
