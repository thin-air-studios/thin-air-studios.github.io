About VTBS

Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) is a large airport in Bangkok, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTBS. It is number 22 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 62.9 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 01/19, 02L/20R and 02R/20L. The longest, 01/19, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 81 gates, 522 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 202 airlines have 3,257 routes through VTBS. Its most frequent links are Hong Kong (HKG), Na Thon (USM) and Singapore (SIN).
