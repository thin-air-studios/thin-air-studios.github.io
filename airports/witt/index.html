About WITT

Sultan Iskandar Muda International Airport (BTJ) is a large airport in Banda Aceh, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WITT. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 42 routes through WITT. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Sepang (KUL) and Beringin (KNO).
