About KJVY

Clark Regional Airport (KJVY) is a small airport in Jeffersonville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 18/36 and 14/32. The longer one, 18/36, is 7,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KJVY.
