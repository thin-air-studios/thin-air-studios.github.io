About LTBR

Bursa Yenişehir Airport (YEI) is a medium airport in Yenişehir, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTBR. Its two runways are 07L/25R and 07R/25L. The longer one, 07L/25R, is 9,818 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 62 routes through LTBR. Its most frequent links are Muş (MSR), Erzurum (ERZ) and Trabzon (TZX).
