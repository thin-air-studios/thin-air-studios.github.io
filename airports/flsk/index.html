About FLSK

Simon Mwansa Kapwepwe International Airport (NLA) is a large airport in Ndola, Zambia, with the ICAO code FLSK. It has one runway, 09/27, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 19 routes through FLSK. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Lubumbashi (FBM) and Addis Ababa (ADD).
