About KBFD

Bradford Regional Airport (BFD) is a medium airport in Bradford, United States, with the ICAO code KBFD. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,307 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 17 routes through KBFD. Its most frequent links are Dulles (IAD), Pittsburgh (PIT) and Jamestown (JHW).
