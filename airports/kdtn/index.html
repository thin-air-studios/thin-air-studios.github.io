About KDTN

Shreveport Downtown Airport (DTN) is a small airport in Shreveport, United States, with the ICAO code KDTN. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 5,016 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 81 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Ryanair has 1 routes through KDTN. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Dinard (DNR).
