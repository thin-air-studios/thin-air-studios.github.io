About OPKC

Jinnah International Airport (KHI) is a large airport in Karachi, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPKC. Its two runways are 07R/25L and 07L/25R. The longer one, 07R/25L, is 11,155 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 105 taxiway segments and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 379 routes through OPKC. Its most frequent links are Lahore (LHE), Dubai (DXB) and Attock (ISB).
