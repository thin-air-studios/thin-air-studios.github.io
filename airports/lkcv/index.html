About LKCV

Čáslav Air Base (LKCV) is a medium airport in Chotusice, Czechia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 13/31 and 12/30. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,875 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 118 taxiway segments and 11 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LKCV.
