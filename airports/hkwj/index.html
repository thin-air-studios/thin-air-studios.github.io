About HKWJ

Wajir Airport (WJR) is a medium airport in Wajir, Kenya, with the ICAO code HKWJ. It has one runway, 15/33, 9,193 ft long and 104 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, RNG has 2 routes through HKWJ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Nairobi (WIL).
