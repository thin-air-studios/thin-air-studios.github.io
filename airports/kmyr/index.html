About KMYR

Myrtle Beach International Airport (MYR) is a large airport in Myrtle Beach, United States, with the ICAO code KMYR. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,503 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 61 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 290 routes through KMYR. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Newark (EWR) and Philadelphia (PHL).
