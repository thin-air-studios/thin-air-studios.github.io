About LGSK

Skiathos Island National Airport (JSI) is a medium airport in Skiathos, Greece, with the ICAO code LGSK. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,341 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 25 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 292 routes through LGSK. Its most frequent links are Manchester (MAN), London (LGW) and Belgrade (BEG).
