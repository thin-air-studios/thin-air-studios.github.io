About ESMV

Hagshult Air Base (ESMV) is a small airport in Sweden. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,726 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments and 18 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ESMV.
