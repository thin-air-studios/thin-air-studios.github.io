About SEMT

Eloy Alfaro International Airport (MEC) is a medium airport in Manta, Ecuador, with the ICAO code SEMT. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,383 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 23 routes through SEMT. Its most frequent links are Quito (UIO) and Tocumen (PTY).
