About EHAM

Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS) is a large airport in Amsterdam, Netherlands, with the ICAO code EHAM. It is number 17 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 68.8 million passengers in 2025. It has 6 runways, from 6,627 ft to 12,467 ft long. The longest is 18R/36L. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 140 gates, 527 taxiway segments, 44 aprons and 20 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 227 airlines have 15,829 routes through EHAM. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), London (LGW) and Paris (CDG).
