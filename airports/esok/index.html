About ESOK

Karlstad Airport (KSD) is a medium airport in Karlstad, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESOK. Its two runways are 03/21 and 03L/21R. The longer one, 03/21, is 8,255 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 45 routes through ESOK. Its most frequent links are Gran Canaria Island (LPA), Rhodes (RHO) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
