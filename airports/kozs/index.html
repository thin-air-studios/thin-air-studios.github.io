About KOZS

Camdenton Memorial Airport (KOZS) is a small airport in Camdenton, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 5,002 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOZS.
