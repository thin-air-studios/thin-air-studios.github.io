About KPOU

Dutchess County Airport (POU) is a medium airport in Poughkeepsie, United States, with the ICAO code KPOU. Its 3 runways are 06/24, 15/33 and 07/25. The longest, 06/24, is 5,001 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPOU.
