About KATW

Appleton International Airport (ATW) is a medium airport in Appleton, United States, with the ICAO code KATW. Its two runways are 03/21 and 12/30. The longer one, 03/21, is 8,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 97 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 190 routes through KATW. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Detroit (DTW) and Minneapolis (MSP).
