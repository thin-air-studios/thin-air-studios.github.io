About KWRI

Mc Guire Air Force Base (WRI) is a medium airport in Wrightstown, United States, with the ICAO code KWRI. Its two runways are 06/24 and 18/36. The longer one, 06/24, is 11,451 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 66 routes through KWRI. Its most frequent links are Ramstein-Miesenbach (RMS), New York (JFK) and Frankfurt am Main (HHN).
