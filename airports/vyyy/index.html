About VYYY

Yangon International Airport (RGN) is a large airport in Yangon, Myanmar (Burma), with the ICAO code VYYY. It has one runway, 03/21, 11,200 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 56 airlines have 665 routes through VYYY. Its most frequent links are Mandalay (MDL), Bangkok (BKK) and Heho (HEH).
