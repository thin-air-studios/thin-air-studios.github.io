About EGGP

Liverpool John Lennon Airport (LPL) is a large airport in Liverpool, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGGP. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,497 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 47 airlines have 3,062 routes through EGGP. Its most frequent links are Belfast (BFS), Alicante (ALC) and Málaga (AGP).
