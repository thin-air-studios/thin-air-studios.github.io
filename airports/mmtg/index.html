About MMTG

Angel Albino Corzo International Airport (TGZ) is a medium airport in Tuxtla Gutiérrez, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMTG. It has one runway, 15/33, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 195 routes through MMTG. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Cancún (CUN) and Guadalajara (GDL).
