About CYSU

Summerside Airport (YSU) is a medium airport in Slemon Park, Canada, with the ICAO code CYSU. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,000 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 7 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYSU.
