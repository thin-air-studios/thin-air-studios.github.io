About KEAR

Kearney Regional Airport (EAR) is a medium airport in Kearney, United States, with the ICAO code KEAR. Its two runways are 18/36 and 13/31. The longer one, 18/36, is 7,094 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 15 routes through KEAR. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Omaha (OMA) and Beatrice (BIE).
