About EKKA

Midtjyllands Airport / Air Base Karup (KRP) is a medium airport in Karup, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKKA. It has 5 runways, from 2,296 ft to 9,816 ft long. The longest is 09L/27R. Of these, 4 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 93 routes through EKKA. Its most frequent links are Copenhagen (CPH), Köln (CGN) and Rønne (RNN).
