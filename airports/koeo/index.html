About KOEO

L O Simenstad Municipal Airport (OEO) is a small airport in Osceola, United States, with the ICAO code KOEO. Its two runways are 10/28 and 04/22. The longer one, 10/28, is 5,006 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOEO.
