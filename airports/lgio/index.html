About LGIO

Ioannina King Pyrrhus National Airport (IOA) is a medium airport in Ioannina, Greece, with the ICAO code LGIO. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 35 routes through LGIO. Its most frequent links are Spata-Artemida (ATH), Billund (BLL) and Oslo (OSL).
