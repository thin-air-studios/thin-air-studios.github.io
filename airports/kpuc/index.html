About KPUC

Carbon County Regional Airport / Buck Davis Field (PUC) is a small airport in Price, United States, with the ICAO code KPUC. Its 3 runways are 18/36, 14/32 and 07/25. The longest, 18/36, is 8,319 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AIP has 1 routes through KPUC. Its most frequent links are Salt Lake City (SLC) and Vernal (VEL).
