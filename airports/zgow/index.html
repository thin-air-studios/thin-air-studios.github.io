About ZGOW

Jieyang Chaoshan International Airport (SWA) is a large airport in Jieyang (Rongcheng), China, with the ICAO code ZGOW. It has one runway, 04/22, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 gates, 89 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 622 routes through ZGOW. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (PVG), Shanghai (SHA) and Beijing (PKX).
