About MMMD

Manuel Crescencio Rejón International Airport (MID) is a large airport in Mérida, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMMD. Its two runways are 10/28 and 18/36. The longer one, 10/28, is 10,499 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 93 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 367 routes through MMMD. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Mexico City (NLU) and Guadalajara (GDL).
