About VOHY

Begumpet Airport (BPM) is a medium airport in Hyderabad, India, with the ICAO code VOHY. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,600 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 55 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 7 routes through VOHY. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and Ad Dammam (DMM).
