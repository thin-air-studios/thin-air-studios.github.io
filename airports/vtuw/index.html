About VTUW

Nakhon Phanom Airport (KOP) is a medium airport in Nakhon Phanom, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTUW. It has one runway, 15/33, 8,203 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 16 routes through VTUW. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Bangkok (DMK).
