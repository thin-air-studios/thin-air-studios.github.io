About CYVO

Val-d'Or Airport (YVO) is a medium airport in Val-d'Or, Canada, with the ICAO code CYVO. It has one runway, 18/36, 10,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 34 routes through CYVO. Its most frequent links are Montréal (YUL), Montréal (YMX) and Rankin Inlet (YRT).
