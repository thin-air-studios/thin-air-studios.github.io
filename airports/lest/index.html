About LEST

Santiago-Rosalía de Castro Airport (SCQ) is a large airport in Santiago de Compostela, Spain, with the ICAO code LEST. It has one runway, 17/35, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 30 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 1,002 routes through LEST. Its most frequent links are Barcelona (BCN), Madrid (MAD) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
