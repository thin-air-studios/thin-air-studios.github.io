About LPFR

Faro - Gago Coutinho International Airport (FAO) is a large airport in Faro, Portugal, with the ICAO code LPFR. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,169 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 82 airlines have 3,855 routes through LPFR. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Amsterdam (AMS) and Manchester (MAN).
