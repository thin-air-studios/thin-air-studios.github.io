About EDLP

Paderborn Lippstadt Airport (PAD) is a large airport in Büren, Germany, with the ICAO code EDLP. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,152 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 96 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 362 routes through EDLP. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Munich (MUC).
