About OMAD

Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI) is a large airport in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMAD. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,499 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 25 routes through OMAD. Its most frequent links are Das Island (OMAS), Zirku Island (OMAZ) and Delma Island (ZDY).
