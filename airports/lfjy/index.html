About LFJY

Chambley airfield (LFJY) is a small airport in Chambley-Bussières, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 93 taxiway segments and 31 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFJY.
