About UIBB

Bratsk Airport (BTK) is a medium airport in Bratsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UIBB. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,368 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 178 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 24 routes through UIBB. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Novosibirsk (OVB) and Irkutsk (IKT).
