About KTIK

Tinker Air Force Base (TIK) is a medium airport in Oklahoma City, United States, with the ICAO code KTIK. Its two runways are 18/36 and 13/31. The longer one, 18/36, is 11,100 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTIK.
