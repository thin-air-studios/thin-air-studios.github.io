About KSRB

Upper Cumberland Regional Airport (KSRB) is a small airport in Sparta, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,704 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments and 1 aprons.
