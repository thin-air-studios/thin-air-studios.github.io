About URML

Makhachkala Uytash International Airport (MCX) is a large airport in Makhachkala, Russia, with the ICAO code URML. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,662 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 221 routes through URML. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (SVO) and Moscow (VKO).
