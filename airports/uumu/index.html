About UUMU

Chkalovskiy Air Base (CKL) is a medium airport in Moscow, Russia, with the ICAO code UUMU. Its two runways are 12R/30L and 12L/30R. The longer one, 12R/30L, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments and 12 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SJV has 1 routes through UUMU. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is WIPK (WIPK).
