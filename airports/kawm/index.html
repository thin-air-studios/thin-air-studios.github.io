About KAWM

West Memphis Municipal Airport (AWM) is a small airport in West Memphis, United States, with the ICAO code KAWM. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,003 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, FDY has 2 routes through KAWM. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Memphis (MEM).
