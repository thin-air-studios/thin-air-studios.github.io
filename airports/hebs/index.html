About HEBS

Bani Sweif Air Base (HEBS) is a medium airport in Bani Sweif, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 01/19 and 06/24. The longer one, 01/19, is 11,565 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 82 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HEBS.
