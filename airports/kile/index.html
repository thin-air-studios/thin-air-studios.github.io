About KILE

Skylark Field (ILE) is a small airport in Killeen, United States, with the ICAO code KILE. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,495 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KILE.
