About LFKC

Calvi Sainte Catherine Airport (CLY) is a medium airport in Calvi, France, with the ICAO code LFKC. It has one runway, 18/36, 7,579 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 370 routes through LFKC. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS).
