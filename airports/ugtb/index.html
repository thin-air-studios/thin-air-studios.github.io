About UGTB

Tbilisi International Airport (TBS) is a large airport in Tbilisi, Georgia, with the ICAO code UGTB. It has one runway, 13R/31L, 9,843 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 160 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 103 airlines have 787 routes through UGTB. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Baku (GYD).
