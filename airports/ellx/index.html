About ELLX

Luxembourg-Findel International Airport (LUX) is a large airport in Luxembourg, Luxembourg, with the ICAO code ELLX. It has one runway, 06/24, 13,123 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 114 taxiway segments, 57 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 62 airlines have 3,351 routes through ELLX. Its most frequent links are Porto (OPO), Ferno (MXP) and Chicago (ORD).
