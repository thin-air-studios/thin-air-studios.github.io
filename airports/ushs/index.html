About USHS

Sovetskiy Airport (OVS) is a medium airport in Sovetskiy, Russia, with the ICAO code USHS. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,202 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 5 routes through USHS. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Valencia (VLC) and Krasnyi Kurgan (AAQ).
