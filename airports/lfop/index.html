About LFOP

Rouen Vallée de Seine Airport (URO) is a medium airport in Boos, France, with the ICAO code LFOP. Its two runways are 04/22 and 05/23. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,577 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SUS has 1 routes through LFOP. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Augsburg (AGB).
