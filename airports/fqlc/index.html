About FQLC

Lichinga Airport (VXC) is a medium airport in Lichinga, Mozambique, with the ICAO code FQLC. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,300 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, LAM has 2 routes through FQLC. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Nampula (APL).
