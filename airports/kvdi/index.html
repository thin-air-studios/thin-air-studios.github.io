About KVDI

Vidalia Regional Airport (VDI) is a small airport in Vidalia, United States, with the ICAO code KVDI. Its two runways are 07/25 and 14/32. The longer one, 07/25, is 6,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, DAG has 1 routes through KVDI. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Atlanta (ATL).
