About KCXO

Conroe-North Houston Regional Airport (CXO) is a medium airport in Houston, United States, with the ICAO code KCXO. Its two runways are 14/32 and 01/19. The longer one, 14/32, is 7,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 80 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCXO.
