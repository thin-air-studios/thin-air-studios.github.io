About EGAA

Belfast International Airport (BFS) is a large airport in Belfast, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGAA. Its two runways are 07/25 and 17/35. The longer one, 07/25, is 9,121 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 77 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 59 airlines have 3,927 routes through EGAA. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), London (STN) and Liverpool (LPL).
