About LEVC

Valencia Airport (VLC) is a large airport in Valencia, Spain, with the ICAO code LEVC. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 105 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 81 airlines have 3,052 routes through LEVC. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Paris (ORY) and London (LGW).
