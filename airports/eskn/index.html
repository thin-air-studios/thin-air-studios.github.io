About ESKN

Stockholm Skavsta Airport (NYO) is a large airport in Nyköping, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESKN. Its two runways are 08/26 and 16/34. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,442 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 450 routes through ESKN. Its most frequent links are Warsaw (WAW), London (STN) and Belgrade (BEG).
