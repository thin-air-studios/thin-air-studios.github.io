About EGNR

Hawarden Airport (CEG) is a medium airport in Broughton, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGNR. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,867 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 114 routes through EGNR. Its most frequent links are Toulouse/Blagnac (TLS), Hamburg (XFW) and Bremen (BRE).
