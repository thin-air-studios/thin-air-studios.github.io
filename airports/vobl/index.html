About VOBL

Kempegowda International Airport Bengaluru (BLR) is a large airport in Bengaluru, India, with the ICAO code VOBL. Its two runways are 09L/27R and 09R/27L. The longer one, 09L/27R, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 gates, 169 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 64 airlines have 3,671 routes through VOBL. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Kolkata (CCU).
