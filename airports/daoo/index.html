About DAOO

Oran Es-Sénia (Ahmed Ben Bella) International Airport (ORN) is a large airport in Es-Sénia, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAOO. Its two runways are 07L/25R and 07R/25L. The longer one, 07L/25R, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 365 routes through DAOO. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Algiers (ALG) and Marignane (MRS).
