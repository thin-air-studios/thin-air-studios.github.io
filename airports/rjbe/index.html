About RJBE

Kobe Airport (UKB) is a large airport in Kobe, Japan, with the ICAO code RJBE. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,202 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 177 routes through RJBE. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Naha (OKA) and Sapporo (CTS).
