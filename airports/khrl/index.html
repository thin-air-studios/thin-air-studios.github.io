About KHRL

Valley International Airport (HRL) is a medium airport in Harlingen, United States, with the ICAO code KHRL. Its 3 runways are 18R/36L, 13/31 and 18L/36R. The longest, 18R/36L, is 9,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 22 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 80 routes through KHRL. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Houston (HOU) and Cincinnati / Covington (CVG).
