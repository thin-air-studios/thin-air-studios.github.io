About VVVH

Vinh Airport (VII) is a medium airport in Vinh, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVVH. It has one runway, 17/35, 7,875 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 144 routes through VVVH. Its most frequent links are Ho Chi Minh City (SGN), Hanoi (HAN) and Da Lat (DLI).
