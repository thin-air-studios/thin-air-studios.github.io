About SAZB

Comandante Espora Airport (BHI) is a medium airport in Bahía Blanca, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAZB. Its 3 runways are 17L/35R, 06/24 and 17R/35L. The longest, 17L/35R, is 7,907 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 51 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 21 routes through SAZB. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP) and El Palomar (EPA).
