About KINL

Falls International Airport (INL) is a medium airport in International Falls, United States, with the ICAO code KINL. Its two runways are 13/31 and 04/22. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through KINL. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Minneapolis (MSP).
