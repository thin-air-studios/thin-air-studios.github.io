About EFTP

Tampere-Pirkkala Airport (TMP) is a large airport in Tampere / Pirkkala, Finland, with the ICAO code EFTP. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 104 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 141 routes through EFTP. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Stockholm (ARN) and Copenhagen (CPH).
