About KHEF

Washington Manassas Harry P. Davis Field (MNZ) is a medium airport in Manassas, United States, with the ICAO code KHEF. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 6,200 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 10 routes through KHEF. Its most frequent links are Reading (RDG), Pittsburgh (AGC) and Burlington (KBUY).
