About KMAF

Midland International Air and Space Port (MAF) is a medium airport in Midland, United States, with the ICAO code KMAF. Its 4 runways are 16R/34L, 10/28, 04/22 and 16L/34R. The longest, 16R/34L, is 9,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 142 routes through KMAF. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Houston (IAH) and Dallas (DAL).
