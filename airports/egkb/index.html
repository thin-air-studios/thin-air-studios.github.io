About EGKB

London Biggin Hill Airport (BQH) is a medium airport in London, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGKB. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,925 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 6 routes through EGKB. Its most frequent links are Belfast (BFS), Lerwick (LSI) and Dublin (DUB).
