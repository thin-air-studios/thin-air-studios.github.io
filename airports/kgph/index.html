About KGPH

Midwest National Air Center Airport (KGPH) is a small airport in Excelsior Springs, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,502 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, CTL has 4 routes through KGPH. Its most frequent links are Kansas City (MKC), Little Rock (LIT) and Moline (MLI).
