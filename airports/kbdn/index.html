About KBDN

Bend Municipal Airport (KBDN) is a small airport in Bend, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,201 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBDN.
