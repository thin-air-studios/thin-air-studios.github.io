About SCSE

La Florida Airport (LSC) is a medium airport in La Serena-Coquimbo, Chile, with the ICAO code SCSE. It has one runway, 12/30, 6,358 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 164 routes through SCSE. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), Calama (CJC) and Iquique (IQQ).
