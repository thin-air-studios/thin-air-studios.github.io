About LIPE

Bologna Guglielmo Marconi Airport (BLQ) is a large airport in Bologna, Italy, with the ICAO code LIPE. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,196 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 80 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 21 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 102 airlines have 2,802 routes through LIPE. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Catania (CTA) and Barcelona (BCN).
