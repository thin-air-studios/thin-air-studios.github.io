About SESM

Rio Amazonas Airport (PTZ) is a small airport in Shell Mera, Ecuador, with the ICAO code SESM. It has one runway, 12A/30D, 5,052 ft long and 82 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 24 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SESM.
