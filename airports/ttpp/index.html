About TTPP

Piarco International Airport (POS) is a large airport in Port of Spain, Trinidad & Tobago, with the ICAO code TTPP. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,500 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 325 routes through TTPP. Its most frequent links are Scarborough (TAB), Bridgetown (BGI) and New York (JFK).
