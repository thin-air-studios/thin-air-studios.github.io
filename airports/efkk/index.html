About EFKK

Kokkola-Pietarsaari Airport (KOK) is a medium airport in Kokkola / Kruunupyy, Finland, with the ICAO code EFKK. Its two runways are 01/19 and 11/29. The longer one, 01/19, is 8,202 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 94 routes through EFKK. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Kemi / Tornio (KEM) and Pori (POR).
