About VILH

Leh Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport (IXL) is a medium airport in Leh, India, with the ICAO code VILH. It has one runway, 07L/25R, 9,040 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 162 taxiway segments, 25 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 81 routes through VILH. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Chandigarh (IXC) and Mumbai (BOM).
