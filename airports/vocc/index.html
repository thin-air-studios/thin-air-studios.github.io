About VOCC

INS Garuda / Willingdon Island Naval Air Station (VOCC) is a small airport in Kochi, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 17/35 and 13/31. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,960 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 19 routes through VOCC. Its most frequent links are Thiruvananthapuram (TRV), Calicut (CCJ) and Riyadh (RUH).
