About KEFD

Ellington Airport (EFD) is a medium airport in Houston, United States, with the ICAO code KEFD. Its two runways are 17R/35L and 17L/35R. The longer one, 17R/35L, is 9,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments and 15 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KEFD.
