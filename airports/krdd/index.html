About KRDD

Redding Municipal Airport (RDD) is a medium airport in Redding, United States, with the ICAO code KRDD. Its two runways are 16/34 and 12/30. The longer one, 16/34, is 7,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 115 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 49 routes through KRDD. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Sacramento (SMF) and Denver (DEN).
