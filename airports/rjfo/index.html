About RJFO

Oita Airport (OIT) is a medium airport in Oita, Japan, with the ICAO code RJFO. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,840 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 43 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 124 routes through RJFO. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (ITM) and Narita (NRT).
