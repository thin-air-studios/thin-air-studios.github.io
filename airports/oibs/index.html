About OIBS

Siri Airport (SXI) is a medium airport in Siri, Iran, with the ICAO code OIBS. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,345 ft long and 175 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, KRU has 5 routes through OIBS. Its most frequent links are Ahvaz (AWZ), Isfahan (IFN) and Tehran (THR).
