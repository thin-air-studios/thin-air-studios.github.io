About SLUY

Joya Andina International Airport (UYU) is a large airport in Quijarro, Bolivia, with the ICAO code SLUY. It has one runway, 13/31, 13,123 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BOV has 4 routes through SLUY. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is La Paz / El Alto (LPB).
