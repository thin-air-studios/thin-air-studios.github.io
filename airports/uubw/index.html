About UUBW

Zhukovsky International Airport (ZIA) is a large airport in Moscow, Russia, with the ICAO code UUBW. It has one runway, 12/30, 15,092 ft long and 230 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 117 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 199 routes through UUBW. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Dushanbe (DYU) and Osh (OSS).
