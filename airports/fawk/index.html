About FAWK

Waterkloof Air Force Base (WKF) is a medium airport in Pretoria, South Africa, with the ICAO code FAWK. Its two runways are 01/19 and 06/24. The longer one, 01/19, is 11,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 312 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FAWK.
