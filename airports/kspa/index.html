About KSPA

Spartanburg Downtown Memorial Airport (SPA) is a small airport in Spartanburg, United States, with the ICAO code KSPA. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,852 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSPA.
