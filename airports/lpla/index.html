About LPLA

Lajes Airport (TER) is a medium airport in Praia da Vitória, Portugal, with the ICAO code LPLA. It has one runway, 15/33, 10,870 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 180 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 192 routes through LPLA. Its most frequent links are Ponta Delgada (PDL), Lisbon (LIS) and Santa Cruz da Graciosa (GRW).
