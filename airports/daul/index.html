About DAUL

Laghouat - Molay Ahmed Medeghri Airport (LOO) is a medium airport in Laghouat, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAUL. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 12,486 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 339 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Air Algerie has 2 routes through DAUL. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Algiers (ALG).
