About KSBY

Salisbury Ocean City Wicomico Regional Airport (SBY) is a medium airport in Salisbury, United States, with the ICAO code KSBY. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 50 routes through KSBY. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Charlotte (CLT) and Orlando (MCO).
