About MDPC

Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ) is a large airport in Punta Cana, Dominican Republic, with the ICAO code MDPC. Its two runways are 08/26 and 09/27. The longer one, 08/26, is 10,171 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 gates, 52 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 106 airlines have 1,165 routes through MDPC. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Montréal (YUL) and Paris (CDG).
