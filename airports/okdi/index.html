About OKDI

Udairi Army Air Field (OKDI) is a small airport in Camp Buehring, Kuwait. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,215 ft long and 80 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments and 14 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OKDI.
