About DAUG

Noumérat - Moufdi Zakaria Airport (GHA) is a medium airport in El Atteuf, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAUG. Its two runways are 12/30 and 18/36. The longer one, 12/30, is 10,171 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 13 routes through DAUG. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG), Tamanrasset (TMR) and Es-Sénia (ORN).
