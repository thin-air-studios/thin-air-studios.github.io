About ZKPY

Pyongyang Sunan International Airport (FNJ) is a large airport in Pyongyang, North Korea, with the ICAO code ZKPY. It has one runway, 17/35, 11,490 ft long and 230 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 55 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 7 routes through ZKPY. Its most frequent links are Beijing (PEK) and Artyom (VVO).
