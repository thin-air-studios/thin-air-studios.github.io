About VVPQ

Phú Quốc International Airport (PQC) is a large airport in Phu Quoc Island, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVPQ. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 10,827 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 70 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 399 routes through VVPQ. Its most frequent links are Ho Chi Minh City (SGN), Hanoi (HAN) and Seoul (ICN).
