About DABS

Cheikh Larbi Tébessi Airport (TEE) is a medium airport in Tébessi, Algeria, with the ICAO code DABS. Its two runways are 11/29 and 12/30. The longer one, 11/29, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Air Algerie has 4 routes through DABS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Algiers (ALG).
