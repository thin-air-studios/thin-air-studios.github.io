About HLMB

Marsa al Brega Airport (LMQ) is a medium airport in Marsa al Brega, Libya, with the ICAO code HLMB. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,220 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HLMB.
