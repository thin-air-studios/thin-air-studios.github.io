About URWW

Volgograd International Airport (VOG) is a large airport in Volgograd, Russia, with the ICAO code URWW. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 144 routes through URWW. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Antalya (AYT) and Moscow (DME).
