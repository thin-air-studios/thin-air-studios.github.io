About HSPN

Port Sudan New International Airport (PZU) is a large airport in Port Sudan, Sudan, with the ICAO code HSPN. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 71 routes through HSPN. Its most frequent links are Cairo (CAI), Jeddah (JED) and Addis Ababa (ADD).
