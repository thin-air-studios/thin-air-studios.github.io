About KAVL

Asheville Regional Airport (AVL) is a medium airport in Asheville, United States, with the ICAO code KAVL. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,002 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 240 routes through KAVL. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Orlando (SFB) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).
