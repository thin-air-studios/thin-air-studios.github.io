About RCFN

Taitung Airport (TTT) is a medium airport in Taitung City, Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCFN. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,999 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 18 routes through RCFN. Its most frequent links are Taipei (TSA) and Huxi (MZG).
