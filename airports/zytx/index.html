About ZYTX

Shenyang Taoxian International Airport (SHE) is a large airport in Shenyang, China, with the ICAO code ZYTX. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 107 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 58 airlines have 989 routes through ZYTX. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (PVG), Hangzhou (HGH) and Guangzhou (CAN).
