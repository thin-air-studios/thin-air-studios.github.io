About ZGNN

Nanning Wuxu International Airport (NNG) is a large airport in Nanning (Jiangnan), China, with the ICAO code ZGNN. It has one runway, 05/23, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 326 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 57 airlines have 811 routes through ZGNN. Its most frequent links are Hangzhou (HGH), Fuzhou (FOC) and Beijing (PEK).
