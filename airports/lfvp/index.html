About LFVP

Saint-Pierre Pointe-Blanche Airport (FSP) is a medium airport in Saint-Pierre, St. Pierre & Miquelon, with the ICAO code LFVP. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,906 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SPM has 8 routes through LFVP. Its most frequent links are Halifax (YHZ), Montréal (YUL) and St. John's (YYT).
