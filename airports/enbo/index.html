About ENBO

Bodø Airport (BOO) is a large airport in Bodø, Norway, with the ICAO code ENBO. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,167 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 207 taxiway segments, 51 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 622 routes through ENBO. Its most frequent links are Trondheim (TRD), Tromsø (TOS) and Oslo (OSL).
