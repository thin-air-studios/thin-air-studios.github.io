About MMMV

Monclova International Airport (LOV) is a medium airport in Monclova, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMMV. Its two runways are 06R/24L and 06L/24R. The longer one, 06R/24L, is 6,890 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MMMV.
