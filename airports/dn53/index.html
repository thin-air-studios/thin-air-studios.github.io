About DN53

Kaduna Old Airport (DN53) is a small airport in Nigeria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,612 ft long and 102 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DN53.
