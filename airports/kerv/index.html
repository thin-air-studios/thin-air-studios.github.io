About KERV

Kerrville / Kerr County Airport at Louis Schreiner Field (ERV) is a small airport in Kerrville, United States, with the ICAO code KERV. Its two runways are 12/30 and 03/21. The longer one, 12/30, is 6,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KERV.
