About KMWH

Grant County International Airport (MWH) is a medium airport in Moses Lake, United States, with the ICAO code KMWH. It has 5 runways, from 2,936 ft to 13,503 ft long. The longest is 14L/32R. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 251 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, RVF has 1 routes through KMWH. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Ontario (ONT).
