About LFSL

Brive Souillac airport (BVE) is a medium airport in Brive, France, with the ICAO code LFSL. It has one runway, 11/29, 6,890 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFSL.
