About KEMP

Emporia Municipal Airport (EMP) is a small airport in Emporia, United States, with the ICAO code KEMP. Its two runways are 01/19 and 06/24. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,005 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KEMP.
