About MYSM

San Salvador International Airport (ZSA) is a large airport in San Salvador, Bahamas, with the ICAO code MYSM. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 12 routes through MYSM. Its most frequent links are Montréal (YUL), Nassau (NAS) and Paris (CDG).
