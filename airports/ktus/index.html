About KTUS

Tucson International Airport (TUS) is a large airport in Tucson, United States, with the ICAO code KTUS. Its two runways are 12/30 and 04/22. The longer one, 12/30, is 10,996 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 gates, 273 taxiway segments, 28 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 265 routes through KTUS. Its most frequent links are Los Angeles (LAX), Phoenix (PHX) and Denver (DEN).
