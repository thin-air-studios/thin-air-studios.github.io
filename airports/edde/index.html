About EDDE

Erfurt-Weimar Airport (ERF) is a large airport in Erfurt, Germany, with the ICAO code EDDE. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,530 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 35 airlines have 292 routes through EDDE. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Hurghada (HRG) and Heraklion (HER).
