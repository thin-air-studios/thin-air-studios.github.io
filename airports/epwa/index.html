About EPWA

Warsaw Chopin Airport (WAW) is a large airport in Warsaw, Poland, with the ICAO code EPWA. Its two runways are 15/33 and 11/29. The longer one, 15/33, is 12,106 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 gates, 132 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 119 airlines have 5,057 routes through EPWA. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Antalya (AYT) and Luton (LTN).
