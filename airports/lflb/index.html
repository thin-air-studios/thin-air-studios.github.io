About LFLB

Chambéry Aix les Bains airport (CMF) is a medium airport in Chambéry, France, with the ICAO code LFLB. It has one runway, 18/36, 6,628 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 214 routes through LFLB. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Manchester (MAN) and London (STN).
