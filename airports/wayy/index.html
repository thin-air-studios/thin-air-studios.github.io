About WAYY

Mozes Kilangin Airport (TIM) is a medium airport in Timika, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAYY. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,841 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 13 routes through WAYY. Its most frequent links are Sentani (DJJ), Makassar (UPG) and Jakarta (CGK).
