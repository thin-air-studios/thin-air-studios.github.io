About WBSB

Brunei International Airport (BWN) is a large airport in Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei, with the ICAO code WBSB. It has one runway, 03/21, 12,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 58 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 137 routes through WBSB. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Singapore (SIN) and Dubai (DXB).
