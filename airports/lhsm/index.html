About LHSM

Hévíz–Balaton Airport (SOB) is a medium airport in Sármellék, Hungary, with the ICAO code LHSM. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,202 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 15 routes through LHSM. Its most frequent links are Dresden (DRS), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and EDDT (EDDT).
