About LTCB

Ordu–Giresun Airport (OGU) is a medium airport in Ordu, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTCB. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,848 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 181 routes through LTCB. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Düsseldorf (DUS).
