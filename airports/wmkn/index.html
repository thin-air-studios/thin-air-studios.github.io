About WMKN

Sultan Mahmud Airport (TGG) is a medium airport in Kuala Terengganu, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WMKN. It has one runway, 04/22, 11,417 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 59 routes through WMKN. Its most frequent links are Subang (SZB) and Sepang (KUL).
