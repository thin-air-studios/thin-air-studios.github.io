About HLLS

Sabha Airport (SEB) is a medium airport in Sabha, Libya, with the ICAO code HLLS. It has one runway, 13/31, 11,778 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 13 routes through HLLS. Its most frequent links are HLLT (HLLT), Tunis (TUN) and Tripoli (MJI).
