About LIMN

Cameri Air Base (LIMN) is a medium airport in Cameri (NO), Italy. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,818 ft long and 94 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIMN.
