About FGBT

Bata International Airport (BSG) is a large airport in Bata, Equatorial Guinea, with the ICAO code FGBT. It has one runway, 03/21, 10,860 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 61 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, ABS has 1 routes through FGBT. Its most frequent links are Yaoundé (NSI) and Libreville (LBV).
