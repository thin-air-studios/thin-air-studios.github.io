About KEYW

Key West International Airport (EYW) is a medium airport in Key West, United States, with the ICAO code KEYW. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,076 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 61 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 119 routes through KEYW. Its most frequent links are Tampa (TPA), Miami (MIA) and Orlando (MCO).
