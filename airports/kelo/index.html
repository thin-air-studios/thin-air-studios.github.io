About KELO

Ely Municipal Airport (LYU) is a medium airport in Ely, United States, with the ICAO code KELO. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,596 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KELO.
