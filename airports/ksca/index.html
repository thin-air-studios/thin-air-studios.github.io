About KSCA

Sidney Municipal Airport (KSCA) is a small airport in Sidney, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10/28 and 05/23. The longer one, 10/28, is 5,013 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSCA.
