About ETNT

Wittmundhafen Air Base (ETNT) is a medium airport in Wittmund, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,005 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments and 34 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETNT.
