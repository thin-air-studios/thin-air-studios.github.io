About OTBH

Al Udeid Air Base (XJD) is a medium airport in Ar Rayyan, Qatar, with the ICAO code OTBH. It has one runway, 16/34, 12,303 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 210 taxiway segments and 30 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 13 routes through OTBH. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (HHN), Ramstein-Miesenbach (RMS) and Schkeuditz (LEJ).
