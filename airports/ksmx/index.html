About KSMX

Santa Maria Public Airport Captain G Allan Hancock Field (SMX) is a medium airport in Santa Maria, United States, with the ICAO code KSMX. Its two runways are 12/30 and 02/20. The longer one, 12/30, is 8,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 118 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 24 routes through KSMX. Its most frequent links are Ontario (ONT), Las Vegas (LAS) and Burbank (BUR).
