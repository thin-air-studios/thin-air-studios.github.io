About FOGO

Oyem Airport (OYE) is a medium airport in Oyem, Gabon, with the ICAO code FOGO. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,906 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, ABS has 2 routes through FOGO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Libreville (LBV).
