About MMCZ

Cozumel International Airport (CZM) is a large airport in Cozumel, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMCZ. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 71 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 84 routes through MMCZ. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Toronto (YYZ) and Cancún (CUN).
