About FZAB

Ndolo Airport (NLO) is a medium airport in N'dolo, Congo - Kinshasa, with the ICAO code FZAB. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,526 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FZAB.
