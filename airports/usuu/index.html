About USUU

Kurgan Airport (KRO) is a medium airport in Kurgan, Russia, with the ICAO code USUU. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,530 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 12 routes through USUU. Its most frequent links are Moscow (VKO), St. Petersburg (LED) and Moscow (DME).
