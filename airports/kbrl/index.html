About KBRL

Southeast Iowa Regional Airport (BRL) is a medium airport in Burlington, United States, with the ICAO code KBRL. Its two runways are 18/36 and 12/30. The longer one, 18/36, is 6,102 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 15 routes through KBRL. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), St Louis (STL) and Quincy (UIN).
