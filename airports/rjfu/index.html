About RJFU

Nagasaki Airport (NGS) is a large airport in Nagasaki, Japan, with the ICAO code RJFU. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,840 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 46 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 180 routes through RJFU. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (ITM) and Tsushima (TSJ).
