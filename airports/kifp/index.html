About KIFP

Laughlin Bullhead International Airport (IFP) is a medium airport in Bullhead City, United States, with the ICAO code KIFP. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,501 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 55 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Sun Country has 10 routes through KIFP. Its most frequent links are Los Angeles (LAX), Flint (FNT) and Great Falls (GTF).
