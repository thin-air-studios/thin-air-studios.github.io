About YMHB

Hobart International Airport (HBA) is a large airport in Hobart (Cambridge), Australia, with the ICAO code YMHB. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,947 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 41 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 271 routes through YMHB. Its most frequent links are Melbourne (MEL), Sydney (SYD) and Brisbane (BNE).
