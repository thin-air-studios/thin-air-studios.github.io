About LZIB

M. R. Štefánik Airport (BTS) is a large airport in Bratislava, Slovakia, with the ICAO code LZIB. Its two runways are 13/31 and 04/22. The longer one, 13/31, is 10,466 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 91 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 87 airlines have 1,144 routes through LZIB. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), London (STN) and Hurghada (HRG).
