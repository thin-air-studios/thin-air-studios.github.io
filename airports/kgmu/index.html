About KGMU

Greenville Downtown Airport (GMU) is a medium airport in Greenville, United States, with the ICAO code KGMU. Its two runways are 01/19 and 10/28. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,393 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 136 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGMU.
