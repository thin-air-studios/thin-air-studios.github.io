About LFMP

Perpignan-Rivesaltes (Llabanère) Airport (PGF) is a medium airport in Perpignan/Rivesaltes, France, with the ICAO code LFMP. Its two runways are 15/33 and 13/31. The longer one, 15/33, is 8,202 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 167 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 279 routes through LFMP. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), London (STN) and Charleroi (CRL).
