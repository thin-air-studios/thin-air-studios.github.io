About KCGZ

Casa Grande Municipal Airport (CGZ) is a small airport in Casa Grande, United States, with the ICAO code KCGZ. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,200 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCGZ.
