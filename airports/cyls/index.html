About CYLS

Barrie-Lake Simcoe Regional Airport (YLK) is a medium airport in Barrie, Canada, with the ICAO code CYLS. It has one runway, 10/28, 6,001 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYLS.
