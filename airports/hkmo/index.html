About HKMO

Moi International Airport (MBA) is a large airport in Mombasa, Kenya, with the ICAO code HKMO. Its two runways are 03/21 and 15/33. The longer one, 03/21, is 10,991 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 166 routes through HKMO. Its most frequent links are Nairobi (NBO), Zanzibar (ZNZ) and Frankfurt am Main (FRA).
