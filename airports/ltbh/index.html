About LTBH

Çanakkale Airport (CKZ) is a medium airport in Çanakkale, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTBH. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,710 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 33 routes through LTBH. Its most frequent links are Ankara (ESB), Gaziantep (GZT) and Konya (KYA).
