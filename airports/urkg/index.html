About URKG

Gelendzhik Airport (GDZ) is a medium airport in Gelendzhik, Russia, with the ICAO code URKG. It has one runway, 01/19, 10,171 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 59 routes through URKG. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (SVO) and Moscow (VKO).
