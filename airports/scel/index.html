About SCEL

Comodoro Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (SCL) is a large airport in Santiago, Chile, with the ICAO code SCEL. Its two runways are 17L/35R and 17R/35L. The longer one, 17L/35R, is 12,303 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 gates, 239 taxiway segments, 28 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 80 airlines have 2,703 routes through SCEL. Its most frequent links are Calama (CJC), Puerto Montt (PMC) and São Paulo (GRU).
