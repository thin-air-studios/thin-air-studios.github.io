About EVRA

Riga International Airport (RIX) is a large airport in Riga, Latvia, with the ICAO code EVRA. It has one runway, 18/36, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 gates, 115 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 80 airlines have 1,529 routes through EVRA. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), London (STN) and Antalya (AYT).
