About KSNS

Salinas Municipal Airport (SNS) is a medium airport in Salinas, United States, with the ICAO code KSNS. Its two runways are 08/26 and 13/31. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 61 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSNS.
