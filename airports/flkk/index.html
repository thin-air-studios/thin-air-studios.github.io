About FLKK

Kenneth Kaunda International Airport (LUN) is a large airport in Lusaka, Zambia, with the ICAO code FLKK. Its two runways are 10/28 and 15/33. The longer one, 10/28, is 12,998 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 58 routes through FLKK. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Harare (HRE) and Dar es Salaam (DAR).
