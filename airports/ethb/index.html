About ETHB

Bückeburg Air Base (ETHB) is a medium airport in Bückeburg, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,010 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETHB.
