About RJTY

Yokota Air Base (OKO) is a medium airport in Fussa, Japan, with the ICAO code RJTY. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,000 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 120 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 13 routes through RJTY. Its most frequent links are Pyeongtaek (OSN), Paya Lebar (QPG) and Seattle (SEA).
