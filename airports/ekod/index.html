About EKOD

Odense Hans Christian Andersen Airport (ODE) is a large airport in Odense, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKOD. Its two runways are 06/24 and 13/31. The longer one, 06/24, is 6,053 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 18 routes through EKOD. Its most frequent links are Rhodes (RHO), Stuttgart (STR) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
