About NTAA

Fa'a'ā International Airport (PPT) is a large airport in Papeete, French Polynesia, with the ICAO code NTAA. It has one runway, 04/22, 11,360 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 145 routes through NTAA. Its most frequent links are Auckland (AKL), Los Angeles (LAX) and San Francisco (SFO).
