About KAAO

Colonel James Jabara Airport (KAAO) is a small airport in Wichita, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 6,101 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, CTL has 1 routes through KAAO.
