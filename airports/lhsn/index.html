About LHSN

Szolnok Air Base (LHSN) is a medium airport in Szolnok, Hungary. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,562 ft long and 229 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 70 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LHSN.
