About KTRI

Tri-Cities Regional TN/VA Airport (TRI) is a medium airport in Blountville, United States, with the ICAO code KTRI. Its two runways are 05/23 and 09/27. The longer one, 05/23, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 60 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 58 routes through KTRI. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Atlanta (ATL) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
