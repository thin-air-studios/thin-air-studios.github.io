About OS58

Hamah Air Base (OS58) is a small airport in Syria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,130 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OS58.
