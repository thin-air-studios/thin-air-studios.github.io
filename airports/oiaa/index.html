About OIAA

Abadan Ayatollah Jami International Airport (ABD) is a large airport in Abadan, Iran, with the ICAO code OIAA. Its two runways are 14R/32L and 14L/32R. The longer one, 14R/32L, is 10,169 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 15 routes through OIAA. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Mashhad (MHD) and Isfahan (IFN).
