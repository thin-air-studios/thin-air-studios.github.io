About VGSD

Saidpur Airport (SPD) is a medium airport in Saidpur, Bangladesh, with the ICAO code VGSD. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,000 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 39 routes through VGSD. Its most frequent links are Dhaka (DAC) and Cox's Bazar (CXB).
