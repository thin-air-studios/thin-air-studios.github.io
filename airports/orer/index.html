About ORER

Erbil International Airport (EBL) is a large airport in Arbil, Iraq, with the ICAO code ORER. It has one runway, 18/36, 15,748 ft long and 246 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 164 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 234 routes through ORER. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Baghdad (BGW) and Pendik (SAW).
