About KMKC

Charles B. Wheeler Downtown Airport (MKC) is a medium airport in Kansas City, United States, with the ICAO code KMKC. Its two runways are 01/19 and 04/22. The longer one, 01/19, is 6,827 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 67 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 6 routes through KMKC. Its most frequent links are Excelsior Springs (KGPH), Waco (ACT) and Denver (APA).
