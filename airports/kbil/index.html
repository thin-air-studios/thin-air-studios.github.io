About KBIL

Billings Logan International Airport (BIL) is a medium airport in Billings, United States, with the ICAO code KBIL. Its 3 runways are 10L/28R, 07/25 and 10R/28L. The longest, 10L/28R, is 10,518 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 75 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 151 routes through KBIL. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Louisville (SDF) and Salt Lake City (SLC).
