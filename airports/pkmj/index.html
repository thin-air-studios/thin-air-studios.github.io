About PKMJ

Marshall Islands International Airport (MAJ) is a large airport in Majuro Atoll, Marshall Islands, with the ICAO code PKMJ. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,897 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 9 routes through PKMJ. Its most frequent links are Honolulu (HNL), Kwajalein (KWA) and Hagåtña (GUM).
