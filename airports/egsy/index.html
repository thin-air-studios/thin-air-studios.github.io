About EGSY

MOD St Athan (EGSY) is a medium airport in St Athan, Vale of Glamorgan, United Kingdom. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,997 ft long and 141 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments and 9 aprons.
