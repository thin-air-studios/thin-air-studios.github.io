About HESH

Sharm El Sheikh International Airport (SSH) is a large airport in Sharm El Sheikh, Egypt, with the ICAO code HESH. Its two runways are 04L/22R and 04R/22L. The longer one, 04L/22R, is 10,108 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 79 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 150 airlines have 2,048 routes through HESH. Its most frequent links are Boryspil (KBP), London (LGW) and Cairo (CAI).
