About K3U3

Bowman Field (K3U3) is a small airport in Anaconda, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 16/34 and 04/22. The longer one, 16/34, is 6,011 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for K3U3.
