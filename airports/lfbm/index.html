About LFBM

Mont-de-Marsan (BA 118) Air Base (LFBM) is a medium airport in Mont-de-Marsan, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 11,821 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFBM.
