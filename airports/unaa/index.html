About UNAA

Abakan International Airport (ABA) is a large airport in Abakan, Russia, with the ICAO code UNAA. It has one runway, 02/20, 10,663 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 27 routes through UNAA. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (SVO) and Moscow (VKO).
