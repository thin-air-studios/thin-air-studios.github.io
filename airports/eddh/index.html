About EDDH

Hamburg Helmut Schmidt Airport (HAM) is a large airport in Hamburg, Germany, with the ICAO code EDDH. Its two runways are 15/33 and 05/23. The longer one, 15/33, is 12,028 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 gates, 161 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 151 airlines have 5,472 routes through EDDH. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Munich (MUC).
