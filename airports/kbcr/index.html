About KBCR

Tri-County Airport (KBCR) is a small airport in Bonifay, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,398 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBCR.
