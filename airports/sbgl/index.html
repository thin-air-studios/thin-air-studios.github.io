About SBGL

Rio de Janeiro Galeão – Tom Jobim International Airport (GIG) is a large airport in Rio De Janeiro, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBGL. Its two runways are 10/28 and 15/33. The longer one, 10/28, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 104 gates, 449 taxiway segments, 27 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 58 airlines have 1,303 routes through SBGL. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Salvador (SSA) and Porto Alegre (POA).
