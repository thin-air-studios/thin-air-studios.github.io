About ENAL

Ålesund Airport (AES) is a large airport in Ålesund, Norway, with the ICAO code ENAL. It has one runway, 06/24, 7,592 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 225 routes through ENAL. Its most frequent links are Bergen (BGO), Oslo (OSL) and Trondheim (TRD).
