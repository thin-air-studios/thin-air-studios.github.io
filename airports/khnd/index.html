About KHND

Henderson Executive Airport (HSH) is a small airport in Las Vegas, United States, with the ICAO code KHND. Its two runways are 17R/35L and 17L/35R. The longer one, 17R/35L, is 6,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHND.
