About MNMG

Augusto C. Sandino (Managua) International Airport (MGA) is a large airport in Managua, Nicaragua, with the ICAO code MNMG. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,012 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 119 routes through MNMG. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), San José (SJO) and Tocumen (PTY).
