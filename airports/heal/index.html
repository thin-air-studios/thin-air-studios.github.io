About HEAL

El Alamein International Airport (DBB) is a large airport in El Alamein, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEAL. It has one runway, 13/31, 11,479 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 62 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 35 routes through HEAL. Its most frequent links are Prague (PRG), Riyadh (RUH) and Cairo (CAI).
