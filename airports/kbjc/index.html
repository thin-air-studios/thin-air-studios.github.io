About KBJC

Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport (BJC) is a medium airport in Denver, United States, with the ICAO code KBJC. Its 3 runways are 12L/30R, 12R/30L and 03/21. The longest, 12L/30R, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 161 taxiway segments, 29 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JSX has 21 routes through KBJC. Its most frequent links are Dallas (DAL), Burbank (BUR) and Las Vegas (LAS).
