About KFFL

Fairfield Municipal Airport (FFL) is a small airport in Fairfield, United States, with the ICAO code KFFL. Its two runways are 18/36 and 08/26. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,503 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFFL.
