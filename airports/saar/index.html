About SAAR

Rosario Islas Malvinas International Airport (ROS) is a large airport in Rosario, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAAR. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 24 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 72 routes through SAAR. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP), São Paulo (GRU) and Rio De Janeiro (GIG).
