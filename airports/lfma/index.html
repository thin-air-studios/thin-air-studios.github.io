About LFMA

Aix Les Milles airport (LFMA) is a small airport in Aix en Provence, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 4,934 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 7 routes through LFMA. Its most frequent links are Leeds (LBA), Marignane (MRS) and Manchester (MAN).
