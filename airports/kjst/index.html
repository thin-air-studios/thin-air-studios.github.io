About KJST

John Murtha Johnstown Cambria County Airport (JST) is a medium airport in Johnstown, United States, with the ICAO code KJST. Its two runways are 15/33 and 05/23. The longer one, 15/33, is 7,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 17 routes through KJST. Its most frequent links are Dulles (IAD), Altoona (AOO) and Chicago (ORD).
