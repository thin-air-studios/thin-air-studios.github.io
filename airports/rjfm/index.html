About RJFM

Miyazaki Airport (KMI) is a large airport in Miyazaki, Japan, with the ICAO code RJFM. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,200 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 42 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 198 routes through RJFM. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (ITM) and Fukuoka (FUK).
