About KCTB

Cut Bank International Airport (CTB) is a medium airport in Cut Bank, United States, with the ICAO code KCTB. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,300 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AIP has 1 routes through KCTB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Billings (BIL).
