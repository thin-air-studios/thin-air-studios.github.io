About LTCC

Diyarbakır Airport (DIY) is a medium airport in Diyarbakır, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTCC. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 11,644 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 149 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 256 routes through LTCC. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Gaziemir (ADB).
