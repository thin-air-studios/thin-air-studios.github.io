About OIGG

Sardar-e-Jangal Airport (RAS) is a medium airport in Rasht, Iran, with the ICAO code OIGG. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,571 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through OIGG. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Bandar Abbas (BND) and Ahvaz (AWZ).
