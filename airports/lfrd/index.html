About LFRD

Dinard Pleurtuit Saint-Malo airport (DNR) is a medium airport in Dinard, France, with the ICAO code LFRD. Its two runways are 17/35 and 12/30. The longer one, 17/35, is 7,218 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 30 routes through LFRD. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Nottingham (EMA) and Hamburg (XFW).
