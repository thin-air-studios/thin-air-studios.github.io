About LTAP

Amasya Merzifon Airport (MZH) is a medium airport in Amasya, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAP. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,600 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 31 routes through LTAP. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Pendik (SAW) and Istanbul (ISL).
