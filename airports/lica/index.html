About LICA

Lamezia Terme Sant'Eufemia International Airport (SUF) is a large airport in Lamezia Terme (CZ), Italy, with the ICAO code LICA. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,898 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 36 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 58 airlines have 822 routes through LICA. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Orio al Serio (BGY) and Rome (FCO).
