About KBVY

Beverly Regional Airport (BVY) is a medium airport in Beverly / Danvers, United States, with the ICAO code KBVY. Its two runways are 16/34 and 09/27. The longer one, 16/34, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBVY.
