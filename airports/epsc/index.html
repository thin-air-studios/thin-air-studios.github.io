About EPSC

Solidarity Szczecin–Goleniów Airport (SZZ) is a large airport in Szczecin(Glewice), Poland, with the ICAO code EPSC. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,202 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 228 routes through EPSC. Its most frequent links are Warsaw (WAW), London (STN) and Sandefjord (TRF).
