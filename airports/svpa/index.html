About SVPA

Cacique Aramare Airport (PYH) is a medium airport in Puerto Ayacucho, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVPA. It has one runway, 04/22, 8,272 ft long and 140 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, VCV has 2 routes through SVPA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Maiquetía (CCS).
