About VHSK

Shek Kong Air Base (VHSK) is a medium airport in Islands, Hong Kong SAR China. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 6,180 ft long and 110 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VHSK.
