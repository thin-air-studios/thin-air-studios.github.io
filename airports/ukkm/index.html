About UKKM

Hostomel Airport (UKKM) is a medium airport in Kiev, Ukraine. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 11,483 ft long and 184 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UKKM.
