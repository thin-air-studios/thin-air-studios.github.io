About VVNB

Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) is a large airport in Hanoi (Soc Son), Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVNB. Its two runways are 11R/29L and 11L/29R. The longer one, 11R/29L, is 12,466 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 233 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 117 airlines have 2,203 routes through VVNB. Its most frequent links are Ho Chi Minh City (SGN), Da Nang (DAD) and Seoul (ICN).
