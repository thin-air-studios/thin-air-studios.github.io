About UKDE

Zaporizhzhia International Airport (OZH) is a medium airport in Zaporizhia, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKDE. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,210 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 107 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 93 routes through UKDE. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH) and Boryspil (KBP).
