About ESNJ

Jokkmokk Airfield (ESNJ) is a small airport in Sweden. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 6,562 ft long and 82 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ESNJ.
