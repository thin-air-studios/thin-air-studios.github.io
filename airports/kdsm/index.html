About KDSM

Des Moines International Airport (DSM) is a large airport in Des Moines, United States, with the ICAO code KDSM. Its two runways are 5/23 and 13/31. The longer one, 5/23, is 9,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 gates, 131 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 400 routes through KDSM. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN) and Orlando (SFB).
