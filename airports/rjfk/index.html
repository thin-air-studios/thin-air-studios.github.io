About RJFK

Kagoshima Airport (KOJ) is a large airport in Kagoshima, Japan, with the ICAO code RJFK. It has one runway, 16/34, 9,840 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 141 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 267 routes through RJFK. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Osaka (ITM) and Tokoname (NGO).
