About KM54

Lebanon Municipal Airport (KM54) is a small airport in Lebanon, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 01/19 and 04/22. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KM54.
