About EDDL

Düsseldorf Airport (DUS) is a large airport in Düsseldorf, Germany, with the ICAO code EDDL. Its two runways are 05R/23L and 05L/23R. The longer one, 05R/23L, is 9,842 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 66 gates, 181 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 177 airlines have 8,523 routes through EDDL. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Munich (MUC).
