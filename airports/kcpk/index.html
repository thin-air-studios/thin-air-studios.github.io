About KCPK

Chesapeake Regional Airport (KCPK) is a small airport in Chesapeake, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,500 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCPK.
