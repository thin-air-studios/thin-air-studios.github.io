About KDVT

Phoenix Deer Valley Airport (DVT) is a small airport in Phoenix, United States, with the ICAO code KDVT. Its two runways are 07R/25L and 07L/25R. The longer one, 07R/25L, is 8,196 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 280 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDVT.
