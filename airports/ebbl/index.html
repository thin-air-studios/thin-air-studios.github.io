About EBBL

Kleine Brogel Air Base (EBBL) is a medium airport in Kleine Brogel, Belgium. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 05L/23R and 05R/23L. The longer one, 05L/23R, is 10,158 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments and 13 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EBBL.
