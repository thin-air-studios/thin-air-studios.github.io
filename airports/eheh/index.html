About EHEH

Eindhoven Airport (EIN) is a large airport in Eindhoven, Netherlands, with the ICAO code EHEH. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 113 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 2,423 routes through EHEH. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Málaga (AGP) and Valencia (VLC).
