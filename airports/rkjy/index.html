About RKJY

Yeosu Airport (RSU) is a medium airport in Yeosu, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKJY. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,890 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 36 routes through RKJY. Its most frequent links are Seoul (GMP) and Jeju City (CJU).
