About LHTL

Tököl Airport (LHTL) is a medium airport in Tököl, Hungary. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,800 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 88 taxiway segments and 13 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LHTL.
