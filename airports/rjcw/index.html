About RJCW

Wakkanai Airport (WKJ) is a medium airport in Wakkanai, Japan, with the ICAO code RJCW. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,560 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 12 routes through RJCW. Its most frequent links are Sapporo (CTS), Tokyo (HND) and Osaka (KIX).
