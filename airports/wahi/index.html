About WAHI

Yogyakarta International Airport (YIA) is a large airport in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAHI. It has one runway, 11/29, 10,663 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 31 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 196 routes through WAHI. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Kuta (DPS) and Singapore (SIN).
