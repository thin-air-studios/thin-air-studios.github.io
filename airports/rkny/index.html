About RKNY

Yangyang International Airport (YNY) is a large airport in Gonghang-ro, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKNY. It has one runway, 15/33, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 84 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 16 routes through RKNY. Its most frequent links are Jeju City (CJU), Seoul (GMP) and Da Nang (DAD).
