About YBSG

RAAF Base Scherger (YBSG) is a small airport in Mission River, Australia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,003 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YBSG.
