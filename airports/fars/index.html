About FARS

Robertson Airport (ROD) is a medium airport in Robertson, South Africa, with the ICAO code FARS. Its two runways are 10/28 and 14/32. The longer one, 10/28, is 4,921 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FARS.
