About SAVJ

Ingeniero Jacobacci - Captain H R Bordón Airport (IGB) is a small airport in Ingeniero Jacobacci, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAVJ. It has one runway, 7/25, 6,890 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines.
