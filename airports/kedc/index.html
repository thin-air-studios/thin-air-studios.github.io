About KEDC

Austin Executive Airport (KEDC) is a medium airport in Pflugerville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 13/31 and 16/34. The longer one, 13/31, is 6,025 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JSX has 2 routes through KEDC. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Taos (TSM).
