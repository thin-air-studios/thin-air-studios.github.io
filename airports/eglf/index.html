About EGLF

Farnborough Airport (FAB) is a medium airport in Farnborough, Hampshire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGLF. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,005 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 31 routes through EGLF. Its most frequent links are Barrow-in-Furness (BWF), Warton (WRT) and Aberdeen (ABZ).
