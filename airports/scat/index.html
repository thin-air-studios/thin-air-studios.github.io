About SCAT

Desierto de Atacama Airport (CPO) is a medium airport in Copiapo, Chile, with the ICAO code SCAT. It has one runway, 17/35, 7,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 66 routes through SCAT. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), Calama (CJC) and Antofagasta (ANF).
