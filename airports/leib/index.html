About LEIB

Ibiza Airport (IBZ) is a large airport in Ibiza (Eivissa), Spain, with the ICAO code LEIB. It has one runway, 06/24, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 95 airlines have 3,882 routes through LEIB. Its most frequent links are Barcelona (BCN), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Madrid (MAD).
