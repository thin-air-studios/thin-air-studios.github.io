About LTDA

Hatay Airport (HTY) is a medium airport in Antakya, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTDA. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,830 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 115 routes through LTDA. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Istanbul (ISL).
