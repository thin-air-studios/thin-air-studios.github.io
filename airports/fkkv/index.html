About FKKV

Bamenda Airport (BPC) is a medium airport in Bamenda, Cameroon, with the ICAO code FKKV. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FKKV.
