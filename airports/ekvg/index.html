About EKVG

Vágar Airport (FAE) is a large airport in Vágar, Faroe Islands, with the ICAO code EKVG. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,905 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 140 routes through EKVG. Its most frequent links are Copenhagen (CPH), Billund (BLL) and Bergen (BGO).
