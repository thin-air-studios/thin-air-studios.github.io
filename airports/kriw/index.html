About KRIW

Central Wyoming Regional Airport (RIW) is a medium airport in Riverton, United States, with the ICAO code KRIW. Its two runways are 10/28 and 01/19. The longer one, 10/28, is 8,204 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SkyWest has 11 routes through KRIW. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Denver (DEN).
