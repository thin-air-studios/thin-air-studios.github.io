About PHNL

Daniel K. Inouye International Airport (HNL) is a large airport in Honolulu, Oahu, United States, with the ICAO code PHNL. Its 4 runways are 08L/26R, 08R/26L, 04R/22L and 04L/22R. The longest, 08L/26R, is 12,360 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 gates, 277 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 52 airlines have 1,742 routes through PHNL. Its most frequent links are Kahului (OGG), Kailua-Kona (KOA) and Lihue (LIH).
