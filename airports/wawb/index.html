About WAWB

Betoambari Airport (BUW) is a small airport in Bau Bau, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAWB. It has one runway, 04/22, 5,905 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WON has 1 routes through WAWB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Makassar (UPG).
