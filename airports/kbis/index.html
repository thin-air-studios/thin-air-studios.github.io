About KBIS

Bismarck Municipal Airport (BIS) is a medium airport in Bismarck, United States, with the ICAO code KBIS. Its two runways are 13/31 and 03/21. The longer one, 13/31, is 8,794 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 23 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 75 routes through KBIS. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Minneapolis (MSP) and Fargo (FAR).
