About FMST

Toliara Airport (TLE) is a medium airport in Toliara, Madagascar, with the ICAO code FMST. Its two runways are 04/22 and 11/29. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,562 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 12 routes through FMST. Its most frequent links are Antananarivo (TNR), Tôlanaro (FTU) and Sainte-Marie (RUN).
