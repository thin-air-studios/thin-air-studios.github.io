About WAOO

Syamsudin Noor International Airport (BDJ) is a large airport in Banjarbaru, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAOO. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 19 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 111 routes through WAOO. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Surabaya (SUB) and Yogyakarta (YIA).
