About ORBI

Baghdad International Airport / New Al Muthana Air Base (BGW) is a large airport in Baghdad, Iraq, with the ICAO code ORBI. Its two runways are 15L/33R and 15R/33L. The longer one, 15L/33R, is 13,124 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 gates, 119 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 48 airlines have 392 routes through ORBI. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Dubai (DXB) and Pendik (SAW).
