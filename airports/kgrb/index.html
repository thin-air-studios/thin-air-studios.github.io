About KGRB

Austin Straubel International Airport (GRB) is a medium airport in Green Bay, United States, with the ICAO code KGRB. Its two runways are 18/36 and 06/24. The longer one, 18/36, is 8,700 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 67 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 119 routes through KGRB. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Detroit (DTW) and Minneapolis (MSP).
