About KLWT

Lewistown Municipal Airport (LWT) is a medium airport in Lewistown, United States, with the ICAO code KLWT. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 03/21 and 13/31. The longest, 08/26, is 6,100 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLWT.
