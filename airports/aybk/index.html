About AYBK

Buka Airport (BUA) is a medium airport in Buka Island, Papua New Guinea, with the ICAO code AYBK. It has one runway, 04/22, 5,125 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for AYBK.
