About CYEY

Amos/Magny Airport (YEY) is a medium airport in Amos, Canada, with the ICAO code CYEY. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,000 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYEY.
