About KRDM

Roberts Field (RDM) is a medium airport in Redmond, United States, with the ICAO code KRDM. Its two runways are 05/23 and 11/29. The longer one, 05/23, is 7,038 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 157 routes through KRDM. Its most frequent links are Portland (PDX), San Francisco (SFO) and Seattle (SEA).
