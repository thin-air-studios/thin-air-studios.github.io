About HRZA

Kamembe Airport (KME) is a medium airport in Kamembe, Rwanda, with the ICAO code HRZA. It has one runway, 02/20, 4,921 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, RWD has 1 routes through HRZA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Kigali (KGL).
