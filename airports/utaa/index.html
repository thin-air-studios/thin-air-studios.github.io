About UTAA

Ashgabat International Airport (ASB) is a large airport in Ashgabat, Turkmenistan, with the ICAO code UTAA. Its two runways are 11L/29R and 11R/29L. The longer one, 11L/29R, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 130 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 243 routes through UTAA. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Moscow (DME) and Türkmenabat (CRZ).
