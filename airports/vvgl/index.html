About VVGL

Gia Lam Air Base (VVGL) is a medium airport in Hanoi (Long Bien), Vietnam. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,571 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VVGL.
