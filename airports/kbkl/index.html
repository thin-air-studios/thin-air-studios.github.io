About KBKL

Burke Lakefront Airport (BKL) is a medium airport in Cleveland, United States, with the ICAO code KBKL. Its two runways are 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06L/24R, is 6,604 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBKL.
