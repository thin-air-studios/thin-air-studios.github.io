About UWKD

Kazan International Airport (KZN) is a large airport in Kazan, Russia, with the ICAO code UWKD. It has one runway, 11/29, 12,303 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 152 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 59 airlines have 600 routes through UWKD. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Moscow (SVO) and Moscow (DME).
