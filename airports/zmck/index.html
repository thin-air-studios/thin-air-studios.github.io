About ZMCK

Ulaanbaatar Chinggis Khaan International Airport (UBN) is a large airport in Ulaanbaatar (Sergelen), Mongolia, with the ICAO code ZMCK. It has one runway, 11/29, 11,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 25 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 112 routes through ZMCK. Its most frequent links are Seoul (ICN), Beijing (PEK) and Busan (PUS).
