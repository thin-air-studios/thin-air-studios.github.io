About EDDN

Nuremberg Airport (NUE) is a large airport in Nuremberg, Germany, with the ICAO code EDDN. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 35 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 73 airlines have 2,020 routes through EDDN. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Paris (CDG) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
