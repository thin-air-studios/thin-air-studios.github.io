About KMDQ

Madison County Executive Airport-Tom Sharp Jr Field (KMDQ) is a small airport in Huntsville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 6,500 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 taxiway segments and 7 aprons.
