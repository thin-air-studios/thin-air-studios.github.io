About MMTP

Tapachula International Airport (TAP) is a medium airport in Tapachula, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMTP. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 31 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 55 routes through MMTP. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Monterrey (MTY) and Tijuana (TIJ).
