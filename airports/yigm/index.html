About YIGM

Ingham Airport (IGH) is a small airport in Australia, with the ICAO code YIGM. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,006 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YIGM.
