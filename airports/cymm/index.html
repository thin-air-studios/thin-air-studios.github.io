About CYMM

Fort McMurray International Airport (YMM) is a medium airport in Fort McMurray, Canada, with the ICAO code CYMM. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,503 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 103 routes through CYMM. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC), Edmonton (YEG) and Vancouver (YVR).
