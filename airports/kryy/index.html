About KRYY

Cobb County International Airport-McCollum Field (KRYY) is a medium airport in Atlanta, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,295 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments and 3 aprons.
