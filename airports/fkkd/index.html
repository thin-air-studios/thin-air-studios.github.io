About FKKD

Douala International Airport (DLA) is a large airport in Douala, Cameroon, with the ICAO code FKKD. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,350 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 118 routes through FKKD. Its most frequent links are Yaoundé (NSI), Libreville (LBV) and Zaventem (BRU).
