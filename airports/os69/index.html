About OS69

Khalkhalah Air Base (OS69) is a medium airport in Buraq, Syria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 07/25 and 15/33. The longer one, 07/25, is 9,995 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments and 30 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OS69.
