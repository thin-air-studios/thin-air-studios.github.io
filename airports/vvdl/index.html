About VVDL

Lien Khuong Airport (DLI) is a medium airport in Da Lat, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVDL. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,663 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 21 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 127 routes through VVDL. Its most frequent links are Hanoi (HAN), Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) and Vinh (VII).
