About EGPE

Inverness Airport (INV) is a medium airport in Inverness, Highland, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGPE. Its two runways are 05/23 and 11/29. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,194 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 7 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 403 routes through EGPE. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Luton (LTN) and Bristol (BRS).
