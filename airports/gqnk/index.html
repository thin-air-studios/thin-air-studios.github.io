About GQNK

Kaédi Airport (KED) is a small airport in Kaédi, Mauritania, with the ICAO code GQNK. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for GQNK.
