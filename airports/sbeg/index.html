About SBEG

Eduardo Gomes International Airport (MAO) is a large airport in Manaus, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBEG. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 gates, 12 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 332 routes through SBEG. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Campinas (VCP) and Miami (MIA).
