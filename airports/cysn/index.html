About CYSN

Niagara District Airport (YCM) is a medium airport in Niagara-on-the-Lake, Canada, with the ICAO code CYSN. Its 3 runways are 06/24, 01/19 and 11/29. The longest, 06/24, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYSN.
