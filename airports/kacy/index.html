About KACY

Atlantic City International Airport (ACY) is a medium airport in Atlantic City, United States, with the ICAO code KACY. Its two runways are 13/31 and 04/22. The longer one, 13/31, is 10,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 32 routes through KACY. Its most frequent links are Fort Lauderdale (FLL), Atlanta (ATL) and Orlando (MCO).
