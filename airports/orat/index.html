About ORAT

Al Taqaddum Air Base (TQD) is a medium airport in Al Habbaniyah, Iraq, with the ICAO code ORAT. Its two runways are 12R/30L and 12L/30R. The longer one, 12R/30L, is 13,186 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 124 taxiway segments and 49 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ORAT.
