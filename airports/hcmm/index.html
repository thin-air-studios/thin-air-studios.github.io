About HCMM

Aden Adde International Airport (MGQ) is a large airport in Mogadishu, Somalia, with the ICAO code HCMM. It has one runway, 05/23, 10,446 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 35 routes through HCMM. Its most frequent links are Djibouti City (JIB), Addis Ababa (ADD) and Nairobi (NBO).
