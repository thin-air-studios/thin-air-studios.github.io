About LFPC

Creil Air Base (CSF) is a medium airport in Creil, France, with the ICAO code LFPC. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,871 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFPC.
