About LBSF

Sofia Airport (SOF) is a large airport in Sofia, Bulgaria, with the ICAO code LBSF. It has one runway, 09/27, 11,811 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 294 taxiway segments, 56 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 120 airlines have 2,626 routes through LBSF. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), London (LGW) and Vienna (VIE).
