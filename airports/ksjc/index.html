About KSJC

Mineta San Jose International Airport (SJC) is a large airport in San Jose, United States, with the ICAO code KSJC. Its two runways are 12L/30R and 12R/30L. The longer one, 12L/30R, is 11,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 gates, 64 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 691 routes through KSJC. Its most frequent links are Los Angeles (LAX), San Diego (SAN) and Seattle (SEA).
