About EGOV

Anglesey Airport (VLY) is a medium airport in Angelsey, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGOV. Its two runways are 13/31 and 01/19. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,513 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, EZE has 4 routes through EGOV. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Cardiff (CWL).
