About LGAV

Athens Eleftherios Venizelos International Airport (ATH) is a large airport in Spata-Artemida, Greece, with the ICAO code LGAV. Its two runways are 03R/21L and 03L/21R. The longer one, 03R/21L, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 gates, 352 taxiway segments, 23 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 160 airlines have 4,614 routes through LGAV. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), London (LGW) and Tel Aviv (TLV).
