About KPSX

Palacios Municipal Airport (PSX) is a small airport in Palacios, United States, with the ICAO code KPSX. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 13/31 and 18/36. The longest, 08/26, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPSX.
