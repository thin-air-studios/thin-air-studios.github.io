About UWLL

Ulyanovsk Baratayevka Airport (ULV) is a medium airport in Ulyanovsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UWLL. It has one runway, 03/21, 12,533 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 293 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 39 routes through UWLL. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Moscow (VKO) and Moscow (DME).
