About KSAC

Sacramento Executive Airport (SAC) is a medium airport in Sacramento, United States, with the ICAO code KSAC. Its 3 runways are 02/20, 12/30 and 16/34. The longest, 02/20, is 5,503 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 135 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSAC.
