About LGTY

Tympaki Air Base (LGTY) is a small airport in Crete Island, Greece. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,900 ft long and 160 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LGTY.
