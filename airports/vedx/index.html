About VEDX

Kharagpur Airport / Kalaikunda Air Force Station (VEDX) is a small airport in Doarkhol, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 112 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VEDX.
