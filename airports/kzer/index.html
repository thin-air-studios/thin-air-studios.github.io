About KZER

Schuylkill County Joe Zerbey Airport (KZER) is a small airport in Pottsville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 11/29 and 04/22. The longer one, 11/29, is 5,101 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KZER.
