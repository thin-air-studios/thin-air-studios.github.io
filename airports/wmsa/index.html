About WMSA

Sultan Abdul Aziz Shah International Airport (SZB) is a large airport in Subang, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WMSA. It has one runway, 15/33, 12,401 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 121 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 483 routes through WMSA. Its most frequent links are Penang (PEN), Kota Baharu (KBR) and Johor Bahru (JHB).
