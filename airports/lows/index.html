About LOWS

Salzburg Airport (SZG) is a large airport in Salzburg, Austria, with the ICAO code LOWS. It has one runway, 15/33, 9,022 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 86 airlines have 1,357 routes through LOWS. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), London (STN) and Copenhagen (CPH).
