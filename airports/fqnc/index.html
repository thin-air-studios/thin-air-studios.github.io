About FQNC

Nacala International Airport (MNC) is a medium airport in Nacala, Mozambique, with the ICAO code FQNC. It has one runway, 01/19, 10,171 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FQNC.
