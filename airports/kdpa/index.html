About KDPA

Dupage Airport (DPA) is a medium airport in Chicago/West Chicago, United States, with the ICAO code KDPA. Its 4 runways are 02L/20R, 02R/20L, 10/28 and 15/33. The longest, 02L/20R, is 7,571 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 155 taxiway segments, 21 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 6 routes through KDPA. Its most frequent links are Hamilton (YHM), Akron (CAK) and Jackson (JXN).
