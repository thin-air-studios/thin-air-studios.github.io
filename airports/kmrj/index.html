About KMRJ

Iowa County Airport (KMRJ) is a small airport in Mineral Point, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 11/29 and 04/22. The longer one, 11/29, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMRJ.
