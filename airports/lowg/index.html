About LOWG

Graz Airport (GRZ) is a large airport in Feldkirchen bei Graz, Austria, with the ICAO code LOWG. Its 3 runways are 16C/34C, 16R/34L and 16L/34R. The longest, 16C/34C, is 9,842 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 98 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 37 airlines have 454 routes through LOWG. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (FRA), Munich (MUC) and Antalya (AYT).
