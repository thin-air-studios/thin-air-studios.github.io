About WIEE

Minangkabau International Airport (PDG) is a large airport in Padang (Katapiang), Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIEE. It has one runway, 15/33, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 142 routes through WIEE. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Sepang (KUL) and Batam (BTH).
