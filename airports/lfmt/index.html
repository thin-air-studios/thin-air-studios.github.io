About LFMT

Montpellier-Méditerranée Airport (MPL) is a large airport in Montpellier/Méditerranée, France, with the ICAO code LFMT. Its two runways are 12L/30R and 12R/30L. The longer one, 12L/30R, is 8,530 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 111 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 40 airlines have 937 routes through LFMT. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Nantes (NTE).
