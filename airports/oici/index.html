About OICI

Ilam Airport (IIL) is a medium airport in Ilam, Iran, with the ICAO code OICI. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,183 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 5 routes through OICI. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR) and Mashhad (MHD).
