About LECO

A Coruña Airport (LCG) is a medium airport in Culleredo, Spain, with the ICAO code LECO. It has one runway, 03/21, 7,178 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 338 routes through LECO. Its most frequent links are Madrid (MAD), Barcelona (BCN) and Seville (SVQ).
