About DAOR

Béchar Boudghene Ben Ali Lotfi Airport (CBH) is a medium airport in Béchar, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAOR. Its two runways are 06/24 and 18/36. The longer one, 06/24, is 12,245 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 207 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 16 routes through DAOR. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG), Es-Sénia (ORN) and Tindouf (TIN).
