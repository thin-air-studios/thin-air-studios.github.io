About KF70

French Valley Airport (RBK) is a small airport in Murrieta, United States, with the ICAO code KF70. It has one runway, 18/36, 6,000 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 64 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KF70.
