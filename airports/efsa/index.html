About EFSA

Savonlinna Airport (SVL) is a medium airport in Savonlinna, Finland, with the ICAO code EFSA. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,546 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 24 routes through EFSA. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and Varkaus / Joroinen (VRK).
