About KRCE

Clarence E Page Municipal Airport (KRCE) is a small airport in Oklahoma City, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 17R/35L and 17L/35R. The longer one, 17R/35L, is 6,014 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRCE.
