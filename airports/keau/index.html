About KEAU

Chippewa Valley Regional Airport (EAU) is a medium airport in Eau Claire, United States, with the ICAO code KEAU. Its two runways are 04/22 and 14/32. The longer one, 04/22, is 8,101 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 81 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 31 routes through KEAU. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Minneapolis (MSP) and Las Vegas (LAS).
