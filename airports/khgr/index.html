About KHGR

Hagerstown Regional Richard A Henson Field (HGR) is a medium airport in Hagerstown, United States, with the ICAO code KHGR. Its two runways are 09/27 and 02/20. The longer one, 09/27, is 7,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 14 routes through KHGR. Its most frequent links are Orlando (SFB), Pinellas Park (PIE) and Frankfurt am Main (FRA).
