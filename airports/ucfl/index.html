About UCFL

Issyk-Kul International Airport (IKU) is a large airport in Tamchy, Kyrgyzstan, with the ICAO code UCFL. It has one runway, 07/25, 12,467 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 21 routes through UCFL. Its most frequent links are UTTT (UTTT), Almaty (ALA) and Moscow (SVO).
