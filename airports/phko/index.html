About PHKO

Ellison Onizuka Kona International Airport at Keāhole (KOA) is a large airport in Kailua-Kona, United States, with the ICAO code PHKO. It has one runway, 17/35, 11,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 43 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 330 routes through PHKO. Its most frequent links are Honolulu (HNL), Kahului (OGG) and Los Angeles (LAX).
