About USRO

Noyabrsk Airport (NOJ) is a medium airport in Noyabrsk, Russia, with the ICAO code USRO. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,202 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 37 routes through USRO. Its most frequent links are Ufa (UFA), Sochi (AER) and Yekaterinburg (SVX).
