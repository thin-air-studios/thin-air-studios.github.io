About LRTR

Timișoara Traian Vuia International Airport (TSR) is a large airport in Timişoara, Romania, with the ICAO code LRTR. It has one runway, 11/29, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 35 airlines have 692 routes through LRTR. Its most frequent links are Otopeni (OTP), Antalya (AYT) and Munich (MUC).
