About UHWW

Vladivostok International Airport (VVO) is a large airport in Artyom, Russia, with the ICAO code UHWW. It has one runway, 07R/25L, 11,483 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 247 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 278 routes through UHWW. Its most frequent links are Irkutsk (IKT), Novosibirsk (OVB) and Moscow (SVO).
