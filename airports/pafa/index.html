About PAFA

Fairbanks International Airport (FAI) is a medium airport in Fairbanks, United States, with the ICAO code PAFA. Its 3 runways are 02L/20R, 02R/20L and 02/20. The longest, 02L/20R, is 11,800 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 124 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 94 routes through PAFA. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), Seattle (SEA) and Portland (PDX).
