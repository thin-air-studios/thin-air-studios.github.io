About LBSW

Sofia West Airfield (LBSW) is a small airport in Radomir, Bulgaria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,187 ft long and 160 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LBSW.
