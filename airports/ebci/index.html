About EBCI

Brussels South Charleroi Airport (CRL) is a large airport in Charleroi, Belgium, with the ICAO code EBCI. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,023 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 gates, 63 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 3,334 routes through EBCI. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Budapest (BUD) and Otopeni (OTP).
