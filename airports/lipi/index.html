About LIPI

Udine-Rivolto Air Base (LIPI) is a medium airport in Codroipo (UD), Italy. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,365 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments and 25 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIPI.
