About LIRA

Ciampino–G. B. Pastine International Airport (CIA) is a large airport in Rome, Italy, with the ICAO code LIRA. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,226 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 1,307 routes through LIRA. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Otopeni (OTP) and Manchester (MAN).
