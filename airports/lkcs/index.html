About LKCS

České Budějovice South Bohemian Airport (JCL) is a large airport in České Budějovice, Czechia, with the ICAO code LKCS. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,202 ft long and 262 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 66 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 8 routes through LKCS. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT) and Burgas (BOJ).
