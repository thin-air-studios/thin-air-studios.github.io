About RPVP

Puerto Princesa International Airport / PAF Antonio Bautista Air Base (PPS) is a large airport in Puerto Princesa, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPVP. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 19 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 106 routes through RPVP. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL), Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB) and Davao (DVO).
