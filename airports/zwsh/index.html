About ZWSH

Kashgar Laining International Airport (KHG) is a large airport in Kashgar, China, with the ICAO code ZWSH. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,499 ft long and 135 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 216 routes through ZWSH. Its most frequent links are Ürümqi (URC), Xi'an (XIY) and Chengdu (TFU).
