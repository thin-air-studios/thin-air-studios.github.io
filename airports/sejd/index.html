About SEJD

Jumandy Airport (TNW) is a medium airport in Ahuano, Ecuador, with the ICAO code SEJD. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SEJD.
