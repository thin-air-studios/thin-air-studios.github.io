About KGPI

Glacier Park International Airport (FCA) is a medium airport in Kalispell, United States, with the ICAO code KGPI. Its two runways are 02/20 and 12/30. The longer one, 02/20, is 9,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 81 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 76 routes through KGPI. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Great Falls (GTF) and Seattle (SEA).
