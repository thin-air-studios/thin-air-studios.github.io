About EGSC

Cambridge City Airport (CBG) is a medium airport in Cambridge, Cambridgeshire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGSC. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,444 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 20 routes through EGSC. Its most frequent links are Göteborg (GOT), Manchester (MAN) and St. Peter (JER).
