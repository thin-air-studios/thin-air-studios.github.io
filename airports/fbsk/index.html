About FBSK

Sir Seretse Khama International Airport (GBE) is a large airport in Gaborone, Botswana, with the ICAO code FBSK. It has one runway, 08/26, 13,123 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 53 routes through FBSK. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Cape Town (CPT) and Addis Ababa (ADD).
