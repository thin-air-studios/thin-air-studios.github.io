About WARR

Juanda International Airport (SUB) is a large airport in Surabaya, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WARR. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 165 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 790 routes through WARR. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Kuta (DPS) and Makassar (UPG).
