About CYLW

Kelowna International Airport (YLW) is a large airport in Kelowna, Canada, with the ICAO code CYLW. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,900 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 50 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 358 routes through CYLW. Its most frequent links are Vancouver (YVR), Calgary (YYC) and Edmonton (YEG).
