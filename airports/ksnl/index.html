About KSNL

Shawnee Regional Airport (SNL) is a small airport in Shawnee, United States, with the ICAO code KSNL. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,997 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSNL.
