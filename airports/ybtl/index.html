About YBTL

Townsville Airport / RAAF Base Townsville (TSV) is a medium airport in Townsville, Australia, with the ICAO code YBTL. Its two runways are 01/19 and 07/25. The longer one, 01/19, is 7,999 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 116 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 233 routes through YBTL. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Cairns (CNS) and Mackay (MKY).
