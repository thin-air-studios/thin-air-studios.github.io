About HESX

Sphinx International Airport (SPX) is a large airport in Al Jiza, Egypt, with the ICAO code HESX. It has one runway, 16R/34L, 11,975 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 95 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 138 routes through HESX. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Luton (LTN) and Rome (FCO).
