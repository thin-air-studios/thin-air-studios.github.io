About KABE

Lehigh Valley International Airport (ABE) is a medium airport in Allentown/Bethlehem, United States, with the ICAO code KABE. Its two runways are 06/24 and 13/31. The longer one, 06/24, is 7,599 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 89 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 241 routes through KABE. Its most frequent links are Orlando (SFB), Chicago (ORD) and Myrtle Beach (MYR).
