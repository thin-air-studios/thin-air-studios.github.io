About OAKN

Ahmad Shah Baba International Airport (KDH) is a large airport in Kandahar, Afghanistan, with the ICAO code OAKN. It has one runway, 05/23, 10,532 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 taxiway segments, 47 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 9 routes through OAKN. Its most frequent links are Kabul (KBL), Jeddah (JED) and Dubai (DXB).
