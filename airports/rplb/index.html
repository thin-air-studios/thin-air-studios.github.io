About RPLB

Subic Bay International Airport / Naval Air Station Cubi Point (SFS) is a large airport in Olongapo, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPLB. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,003 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RPLB.
