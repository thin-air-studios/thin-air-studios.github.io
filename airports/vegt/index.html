About VEGT

Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (GAU) is a large airport in Guwahati, India, with the ICAO code VEGT. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 344 routes through VEGT. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Kolkata (CCU) and Bengaluru (BLR).
