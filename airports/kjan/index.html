About KJAN

Jackson-Medgar Wiley Evers International Airport (JAN) is a medium airport in Jackson, United States, with the ICAO code KJAN. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 8,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 51 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 102 routes through KJAN. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Houston (IAH) and Charlotte (CLT).
