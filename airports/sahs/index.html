About SAHS

Rincon De Los Sauces Airport (RDS) is a medium airport in Rincon de los Sauces, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAHS. It has one runway, 10/28, 4,921 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SAHS.
