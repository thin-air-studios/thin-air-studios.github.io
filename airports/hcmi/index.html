About HCMI

Berbera Airport (BBO) is a medium airport in Berbera, Somalia, with the ICAO code HCMI. It has one runway, 05/23, 13,582 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HCMI.
