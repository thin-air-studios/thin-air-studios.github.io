About KBGD

Hutchinson County Airport (BGD) is a small airport in Borger, United States, with the ICAO code KBGD. Its two runways are 17/35 and 03/21. The longer one, 17/35, is 6,299 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBGD.
