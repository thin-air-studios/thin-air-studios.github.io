About KSVR

South Valley Regional Airport (KSVR) is a medium airport in Salt Lake City, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 16/34, 5,862 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 111 taxiway segments and 3 aprons.
