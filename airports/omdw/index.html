About OMDW

Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC) is a large airport in Dubai(Jebel Ali), United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMDW. Its two runways are 12/30 and 13/31. The longer one, 12/30, is 14,764 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 280 taxiway segments, 28 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 111 airlines have 904 routes through OMDW. Its most frequent links are Hong Kong (HKG), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and Luxembourg (LUX).
