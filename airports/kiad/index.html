About KIAD

Washington Dulles International Airport (IAD) is a large airport in Dulles, United States, with the ICAO code KIAD. Its 4 runways are 01C/19C, 01R/19L, 12/30 and 01L/19R. The longest, 01C/19C, is 11,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 130 gates, 307 taxiway segments, 22 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 96 airlines have 2,890 routes through KIAD. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Newark (EWR) and Charlotte (CLT).
