About KCLT

Charlotte Douglas International Airport (CLT) is a large airport in Charlotte, United States, with the ICAO code KCLT. It is number 35 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 53.6 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 01R/19L, 01L/19R and 18/36. The longest, 01R/19L, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 124 gates, 541 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 14 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 3,567 routes through KCLT. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Philadelphia (PHL) and Newark (EWR).
