About KILN

Wilmington Airpark (ILN) is a medium airport in Wilmington, United States, with the ICAO code KILN. Its two runways are 04L/22R and 04R/22L. The longer one, 04L/22R, is 10,701 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 101 routes through KILN. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Allentown/Bethlehem (ABE) and Ontario (ONT).
