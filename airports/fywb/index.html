About FYWB

Walvis Bay International Airport (WVB) is a large airport in Walvis Bay(Rooikop), Namibia, with the ICAO code FYWB. It has one runway, 09/27, 11,483 ft long and 196 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 47 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 14 routes through FYWB. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Cape Town (CPT) and Jamestown (HLE).
