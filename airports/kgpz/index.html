About KGPZ

Grand Rapids Itasca Co-Gordon Newstrom field (GPZ) is a small airport in Grand Rapids, United States, with the ICAO code KGPZ. Its two runways are 16/34 and 05/23. The longer one, 16/34, is 5,756 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 19 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BMJ has 3 routes through KGPZ. Its most frequent links are Minneapolis (MSP) and Hibbing (HIB).
