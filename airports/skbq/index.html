About SKBQ

Ernesto Cortissoz International Airport (BAQ) is a large airport in Barranquilla, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKBQ. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 46 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 190 routes through SKBQ. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Medellín (MDE) and Tocumen (PTY).
