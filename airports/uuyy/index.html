About UUYY

Syktyvkar Airport (SCW) is a medium airport in Syktyvkar, Russia, with the ICAO code UUYY. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,203 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 113 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 62 routes through UUYY. Its most frequent links are St. Petersburg (LED), Sochi (AER) and Moscow (SVO).
