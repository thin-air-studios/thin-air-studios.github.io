About EETN

Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport (TLL) is a large airport in Tallinn, Estonia, with the ICAO code EETN. It has one runway, 08/26, 11,417 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 89 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 85 airlines have 1,160 routes through EETN. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Antalya (AYT) and Stockholm (ARN).
