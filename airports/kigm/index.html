About KIGM

Kingman Airport (IGM) is a medium airport in Kingman, United States, with the ICAO code KIGM. Its two runways are 03/21 and 17/35. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,827 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 4 routes through KIGM. Its most frequent links are Phoenix (PHX), Savoy (CMI) and Prescott (PRC).
