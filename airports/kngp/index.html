About KNGP

Naval Air Station Corpus Christi Truax Field (NGP) is a medium airport in Corpus Christi, United States, with the ICAO code KNGP. Its 4 runways are 13R/31L, 17/35, 13L/31R and 04/22. The longest, 13R/31L, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 66 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNGP.
