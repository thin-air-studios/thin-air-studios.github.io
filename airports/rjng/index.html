About RJNG

Gifu Airport (QGU) is a medium airport in Gifu, Japan, with the ICAO code RJNG. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,860 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RJNG.
