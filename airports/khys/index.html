About KHYS

Hays Regional Airport (HYS) is a medium airport in Hays, United States, with the ICAO code KHYS. Its two runways are 16/34 and 04/22. The longer one, 16/34, is 6,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 38 routes through KHYS. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Salina (SLN) and Wichita (ICT).
