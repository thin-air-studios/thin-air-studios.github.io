About KRPD

Rice Lake Regional Airport - Carl's Field (RIE) is a small airport in Rice Lake, United States, with the ICAO code KRPD. Its two runways are 01/19 and 13/31. The longer one, 01/19, is 6,700 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BMJ has 2 routes through KRPD. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Minneapolis (MSP).
