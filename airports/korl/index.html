About KORL

Orlando Executive Airport (ORL) is a medium airport in Orlando, United States, with the ICAO code KORL. Its two runways are 07/25 and 13/31. The longer one, 07/25, is 6,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 127 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SUB has 1 routes through KORL. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Charlotte (CLT).
