About SKCL

Alfonso Bonilla Aragon International Airport (CLO) is a large airport in Cali, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKCL. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 392 routes through SKCL. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Medellín (MDE) and Cartagena (CTG).
