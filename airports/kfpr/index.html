About KFPR

Treasure Coast International Airport (FPR) is a medium airport in Fort Pierce, United States, with the ICAO code KFPR. Its two runways are 10R/28L and 14/32. The longer one, 10R/28L, is 6,492 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 107 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFPR.
