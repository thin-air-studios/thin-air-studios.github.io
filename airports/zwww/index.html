About ZWWW

Ürümqi Tianshan International Airport (URC) is a large airport in Ürümqi, China, with the ICAO code ZWWW. Its 3 runways are 07/25, 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longest, 07/25, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 400 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 1,271 routes through ZWWW. Its most frequent links are Xi'an (XIY), Kashgar (KHG) and Chengdu (TFU).
