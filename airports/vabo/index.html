About VABO

Vadodara International Airport (BDQ) is a large airport in Vadodara, India, with the ICAO code VABO. It has one runway, 04/22, 8,100 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 113 routes through VABO. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
