About EDON

Neuhardenberg Marxwalde Airfield (EDON) is a small airport in Neuhardenberg, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,874 ft long and 163 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDON.
