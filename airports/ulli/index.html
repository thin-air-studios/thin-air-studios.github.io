About ULLI

Pulkovo Airport (LED) is a large airport in St. Petersburg, Russia, with the ICAO code ULLI. Its two runways are 10R/28L and 10L/28R. The longer one, 10R/28L, is 12,402 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 gates, 412 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 141 airlines have 2,727 routes through ULLI. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Antalya (AYT) and Moscow (DME).
