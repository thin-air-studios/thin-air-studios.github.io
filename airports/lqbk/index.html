About LQBK

Banja Luka International Airport (BNX) is a large airport in Mahovljani, Bosnia & Herzegovina, with the ICAO code LQBK. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 146 routes through LQBK. Its most frequent links are Bâle / Mulhouse (BSL), Dortmund (DTM) and Belgrade (BEG).
