About NZDN

Dunedin International Airport (DUD) is a medium airport in Dunedin, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZDN. It has one runway, 03/21, 6,234 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 189 routes through NZDN. Its most frequent links are Christchurch (CHC), Wellington (WLG) and Auckland (AKL).
