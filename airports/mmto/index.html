About MMTO

Adolfo López Mateos International Airport (TLC) is a large airport in Toluca, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMTO. It has one runway, 15/33, 13,780 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 233 taxiway segments, 28 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 148 routes through MMTO. Its most frequent links are Cancún (CUN), Monterrey (MTY) and Guadalajara (GDL).
