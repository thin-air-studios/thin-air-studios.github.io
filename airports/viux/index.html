About VIUX

Udhampur Air Force Station (VIUX) is a medium airport in Udhampur, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VIUX.
