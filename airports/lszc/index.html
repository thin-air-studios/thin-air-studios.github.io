About LSZC

Buochs Airport (BXO) is a small airport in Buochs, Switzerland, with the ICAO code LSZC. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,561 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LSZC.
