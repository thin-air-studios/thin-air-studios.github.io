About KPVU

Provo Municipal Airport (PVU) is a medium airport in Provo, United States, with the ICAO code KPVU. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 8,603 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 150 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 107 routes through KPVU. Its most frequent links are Santa Ana (SNA), Mesa (AZA) and Burbank (BUR).
