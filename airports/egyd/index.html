About EGYD

RAF Cranwell (EGYD) is a medium airport in Sleaford, Lincolnshire, United Kingdom. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 4 runways are 08/26, 01/19, 06/24 and 03/21. The longest, 08/26, is 6,831 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, CWL has 5 routes through EGYD. Its most frequent links are EGCN (EGCN), Cardiff (CWL) and Darlington (MME).
