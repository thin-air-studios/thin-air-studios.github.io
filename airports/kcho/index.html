About KCHO

Charlottesville Albemarle Airport (CHO) is a medium airport in Charlottesville, United States, with the ICAO code KCHO. It has one runway, 03/21, 6,801 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 100 routes through KCHO. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Chicago (ORD) and Dulles (IAD).
