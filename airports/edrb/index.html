About EDRB

Bitburg Airport (BBJ) is a small airport in Bitburg, Germany, with the ICAO code EDRB. It has one runway, 05/23, 10,026 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDRB.
