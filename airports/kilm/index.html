About KILM

Wilmington International Airport (ILM) is a medium airport in Wilmington, United States, with the ICAO code KILM. Its two runways are 06/24 and 17/35. The longer one, 06/24, is 8,016 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 20 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 226 routes through KILM. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Philadelphia (PHL) and Orlando (MCO).
