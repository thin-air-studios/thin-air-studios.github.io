About SVBC

General José Antonio Anzoategui International Airport (BLA) is a large airport in Barcelona, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVBC. Its two runways are 15/33 and 02/20. The longer one, 15/33, is 9,842 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 17 routes through SVBC. Its most frequent links are Maiquetía (CCS), Guyana City (PZO) and Bogota (BOG).
