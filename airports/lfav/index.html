About LFAV

Valenciennes-Denain Airfield (LFAV) is a medium airport in Prouvy, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 11/29, 06/24 and 11L/29R. The longest, 11/29, is 5,604 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFAV.
