About ZLXN

Xining Caojiabao International Airport (XNN) is a large airport in Haidong (Huzhu Tu Autonomous County), China, with the ICAO code ZLXN. It has one runway, 11/29, 12,467 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 35 airlines have 557 routes through ZLXN. Its most frequent links are Nanjing (NKG), Changsha (CSX) and Chengdu (TFU).
