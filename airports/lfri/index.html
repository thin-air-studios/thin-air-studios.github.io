About LFRI

La Roche-sur-Yon Les Ajoncs Airport (EDM) is a medium airport in La Roche-sur-Yon, France, with the ICAO code LFRI. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,085 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFRI.
