About GMMD

Beni Mellal Airport (BEM) is a large airport in Oulad Yaich, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMMD. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,169 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for GMMD.
