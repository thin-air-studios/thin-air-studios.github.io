About UAIT

Hazrat Sultan International Airport (HSA) is a large airport in Turkıstan, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UAIT. It has one runway, 05/23, 10,827 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 29 routes through UAIT. Its most frequent links are Almaty (ALA), Zhengzhou (CGO) and Kuwait City (KWI).
