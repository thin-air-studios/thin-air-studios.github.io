About FZSA

Kamina Air Base (FZSA) is a medium airport in Lumwe, Congo - Kinshasa. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 13L/31R and 13R/31L. The longer one, 13L/31R, is 8,858 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FZSA.
