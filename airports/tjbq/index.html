About TJBQ

Rafael Hernández International Airport (BQN) is a medium airport in Aguadilla, Puerto Rico, with the ICAO code TJBQ. It has one runway, 08/26, 11,702 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 132 routes through TJBQ. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Orlando (MCO) and New York (JFK).
