About KAVQ

Marana Regional Airport (AVW) is a small airport in Marana, United States, with the ICAO code KAVQ. Its two runways are 12/30 and 03/21. The longer one, 12/30, is 6,901 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 197 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 1 routes through KAVQ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Frankfurt am Main (HHN).
