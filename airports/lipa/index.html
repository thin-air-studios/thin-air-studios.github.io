About LIPA

Aviano Air Base (AVB) is a medium airport in Aviano (PN), Italy, with the ICAO code LIPA. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,551 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 127 taxiway segments and 46 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through LIPA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Ar Rayyan (XJD).
