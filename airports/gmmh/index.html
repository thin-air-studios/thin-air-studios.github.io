About GMMH

Dakhla Airport (VIL) is a large airport in Dakhla, Western Sahara, with the ICAO code GMMH. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 81 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 41 routes through GMMH. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Casablanca (CMN) and Marrakesh (RAK).
