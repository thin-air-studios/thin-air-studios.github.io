About FNGI

Ngjiva Pereira Airport (VPE) is a medium airport in Ngiva, Angola, with the ICAO code FNGI. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,640 ft long and 95 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, DTA has 2 routes through FNGI. Its most frequent links are Luanda (NBJ) and Luanda (LAD).
