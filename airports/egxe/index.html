About EGXE

RAF Leeming (EGXE) is a medium airport in Northallerton, North Yorkshire, United Kingdom. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 16/34, 7,516 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, RRR has 3 routes through EGXE. Its most frequent links are Gibraltar (GIB) and Akrotiri (AKT).
