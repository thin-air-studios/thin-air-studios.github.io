About KNTD

Point Mugu Naval Air Station (Naval Base Ventura Co) (NTD) is a medium airport in Point Mugu, United States, with the ICAO code KNTD. Its two runways are 03/21 and 09/27. The longer one, 03/21, is 11,102 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNTD.
