About SBPJ

Brigadeiro Lysias Rodrigues Airport (PMW) is a medium airport in Palmas, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBPJ. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 12 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 44 routes through SBPJ. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Goiânia (GYN) and Brasília (BSB).
