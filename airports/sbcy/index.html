About SBCY

Várzea Grande–Marechal Rondon International Airport (CGB) is a large airport in Cuiabá, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBCY. It has one runway, 17/35, 7,546 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 201 routes through SBCY. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Brasília (BSB) and Campinas (VCP).
