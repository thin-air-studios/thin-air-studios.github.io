About OS57

Abu ad Duhur Air Base (OS57) is a medium airport in Abu ad Duhur, Syria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,810 ft long and 135 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OS57.
