About CYQG

Windsor International Airport (YQG) is a large airport in Windsor, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQG. Its two runways are 07/25 and 12/30. The longer one, 07/25, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 68 routes through CYQG. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Toronto (YTZ) and Calgary (YYC).
