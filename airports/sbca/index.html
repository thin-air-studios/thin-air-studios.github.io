About SBCA

Coronel Adalberto Mendes da Silva Airport (CAC) is a medium airport in Cascavel, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBCA. It has one runway, 15/33, 5,810 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 25 routes through SBCA. Its most frequent links are Campinas (VCP), Curitiba (CWB) and São Paulo (GRU).
