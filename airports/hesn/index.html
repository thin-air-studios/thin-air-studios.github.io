About HESN

Aswan International Airport (ASW) is a large airport in Aswan, Egypt, with the ICAO code HESN. It has one runway, 17/35, 11,161 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 96 taxiway segments, 36 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 68 routes through HESN. Its most frequent links are Cairo (CAI), Abu Simbel (ABS) and Madrid (MAD).
