About URSS

Sochi International Airport (AER) is a large airport in Sochi, Russia, with the ICAO code URSS. Its two runways are 06/24 and 02/20. The longer one, 06/24, is 9,498 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 311 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 62 airlines have 1,172 routes through URSS. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (SVO) and St. Petersburg (LED).
