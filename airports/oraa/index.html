About ORAA

Al Asad Air Base (IQA) is a medium airport in Hīt, Iraq, with the ICAO code ORAA. Its 3 runways are 09L/27R, 09R/27L and 08/26. The longest, 09L/27R, is 13,124 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 82 taxiway segments and 26 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 3 routes through ORAA. Its most frequent links are Grâce-Hollogne (LGG), Frankfurt am Main (HHN) and Dubai (DXB).
