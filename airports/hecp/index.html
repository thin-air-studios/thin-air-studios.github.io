About HECP

Capital International Airport (CCE) is a medium airport in New Cairo, Egypt, with the ICAO code HECP. Its 3 runways are 01L/19R, 02/20 and 01R/19L. The longest, 01L/19R, is 11,980 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 103 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HECP.
