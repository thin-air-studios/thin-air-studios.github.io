About KSVN

Hunter Army Air Field (SVN) is a medium airport in Savannah, United States, with the ICAO code KSVN. It has one runway, 10/28, 11,375 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 1 routes through KSVN. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Boston (BOS).
