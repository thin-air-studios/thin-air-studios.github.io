About KAND

Anderson Regional Airport (AND) is a medium airport in Anderson, United States, with the ICAO code KAND. Its two runways are 05/23 and 17/35. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Kalitta has 1 routes through KAND. Its most frequent links are Cincinnati / Covington (CVG) and Shenzhen (SZX).
