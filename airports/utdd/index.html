About UTDD

Dushanbe International Airport (DYU) is a large airport in Dushanbe, Tajikistan, with the ICAO code UTDD. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,170 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 58 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 179 routes through UTDD. Its most frequent links are Moscow (ZIA), St. Petersburg (LED) and Yekaterinburg (SVX).
