About MHRO

Juan Manuel Gálvez International Airport (RTB) is a large airport in Coxen Hole, Honduras, with the ICAO code MHRO. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,349 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 63 routes through MHRO. Its most frequent links are Montréal (YUL), Tegucigalpa (TGU) and Houston (IAH).
