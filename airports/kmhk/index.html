About KMHK

Manhattan Regional Airport (MHK) is a medium airport in Manhattan, United States, with the ICAO code KMHK. Its two runways are 03/21 and 13/31. The longer one, 03/21, is 7,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 18 routes through KMHK. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Houston (IAH).
