About LFRB

Brest Bretagne airport (BES) is a large airport in Brest, France, with the ICAO code LFRB. Its two runways are 07R/25L and 07L/25R. The longer one, 07R/25L, is 10,171 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 28 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 641 routes through LFRB. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS) and Paris (ORY).
