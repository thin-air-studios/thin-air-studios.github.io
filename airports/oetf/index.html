About OETF

Taif International Airport (TIF) is a large airport in Taif, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OETF. Its two runways are 07/25 and 17/35. The longer one, 07/25, is 12,254 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 287 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 88 routes through OETF. Its most frequent links are Riyadh (RUH), Cairo (CAI) and Ad Dammam (DMM).
