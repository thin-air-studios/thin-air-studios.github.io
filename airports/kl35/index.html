About KL35

Big Bear City Airport (RBF) is a small airport in Big Bear, United States, with the ICAO code KL35. It has one runway, 8/26, 5,850 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KL35.
