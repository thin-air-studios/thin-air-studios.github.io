About EGJB

Guernsey Airport (GCI) is a medium airport in Saint Peter Port, Guernsey, with the ICAO code EGJB. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,194 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 348 routes through EGJB. Its most frequent links are St. Peter (JER), Southampton (SOU) and London (LGW).
