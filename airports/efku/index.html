About EFKU

Kuopio Airport (KUO) is a large airport in Kuopio / Siilinjärvi, Finland, with the ICAO code EFKU. It has one runway, 15/33, 9,186 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 109 routes through EFKU. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Gran Canaria Island (LPA) and Souda (CHQ).
