About KFCM

Flying Cloud Airport (FCM) is a small airport in Minneapolis, United States, with the ICAO code KFCM. Its 3 runways are 10R/28L, 10L/28R and 18/36. The longest, 10R/28L, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, CTL has 2 routes through KFCM. Its most frequent links are Denver (APA), Kansas City (MKC) and Moline (MLI).
