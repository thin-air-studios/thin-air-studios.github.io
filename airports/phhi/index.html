About PHHI

Wheeler Army Airfield (HHI) is a small airport in Wahiawa, United States, with the ICAO code PHHI. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,608 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for PHHI.
