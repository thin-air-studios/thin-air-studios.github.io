About KCRP

Corpus Christi International Airport (CRP) is a medium airport in Corpus Christi, United States, with the ICAO code KCRP. Its two runways are 13/31 and 17/35. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,510 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 101 routes through KCRP. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Houston (HOU) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
