About KJBR

Jonesboro Municipal Airport (JBR) is a medium airport in Jonesboro, United States, with the ICAO code KJBR. Its two runways are 05/23 and 13/31. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,200 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, FDY has 12 routes through KJBR. Its most frequent links are Nashville (BNA) and St Louis (STL).
