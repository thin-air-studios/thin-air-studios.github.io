About YBRK

Rockhampton Airport (ROK) is a medium airport in Rockhampton, Australia, with the ICAO code YBRK. Its two runways are 15/33 and 04/22. The longer one, 15/33, is 8,622 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 158 routes through YBRK. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Mackay (MKY) and Townsville (TSV).
