About EGPF

Glasgow Airport (GLA) is a large airport in Glasgow, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGPF. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,730 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 gates, 40 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 80 airlines have 3,846 routes through EGPF. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Luton (LTN) and London (STN).
