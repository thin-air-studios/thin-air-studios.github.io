About PHOG

Kahului International Airport (OGG) is a large airport in Kahului, United States, with the ICAO code PHOG. Its two runways are 02/20 and 05/23. The longer one, 02/20, is 6,998 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 75 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 13 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 508 routes through PHOG. Its most frequent links are Honolulu (HNL), Kailua-Kona (KOA) and Los Angeles (LAX).
