About ZHXY

Xinyang Minggang Airport (XAI) is a medium airport in Xinyang, China, with the ICAO code ZHXY. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,858 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 14 routes through ZHXY. Its most frequent links are Beijing (PKX), Tianjin (TSN) and Shenzhen (SZX).
