About EPPO

Poznań-Ławica Airport (POZ) is a large airport in Poznań, Poland, with the ICAO code EPPO. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,215 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 83 taxiway segments, 22 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 52 airlines have 1,158 routes through EPPO. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Luton (LTN) and London (STN).
