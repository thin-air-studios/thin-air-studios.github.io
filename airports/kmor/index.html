About KMOR

Morristown Regional Airport (MOR) is a small airport in Morristown, United States, with the ICAO code KMOR. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,718 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMOR.
