About YBRM

Broome International Airport (BME) is a large airport in Broome, Australia, with the ICAO code YBRM. It has one runway, 10/28, 7,769 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 91 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 49 routes through YBRM. Its most frequent links are Perth (PER), Darwin (DRW) and Kununurra (KNX).
