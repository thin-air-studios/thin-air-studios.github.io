About LFPV

Vélizy-Villacoublay Air Base (VIY) is a medium airport in Vélizy-Villacoublay, Yvelines, France, with the ICAO code LFPV. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,948 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 97 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 9 routes through LFPV. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Djibouti City (JIB) and Fauville (EVX).
