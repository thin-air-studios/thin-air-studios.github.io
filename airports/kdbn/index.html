About KDBN

W H 'Bud' Barron Airport (DBN) is a small airport in Dublin, United States, with the ICAO code KDBN. Its two runways are 02/20 and 14/32. The longer one, 02/20, is 6,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDBN.
