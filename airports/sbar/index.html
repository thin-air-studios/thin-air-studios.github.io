About SBAR

Aracaju - Santa Maria Airport (AJU) is a medium airport in Aracaju, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBAR. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 64 routes through SBAR. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Recife (REC) and Salvador (SSA).
