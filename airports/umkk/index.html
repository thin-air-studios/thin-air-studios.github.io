About UMKK

Khrabrovo Airport (KGD) is a large airport in Kaliningrad, Russia, with the ICAO code UMKK. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,991 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 141 taxiway segments, 38 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 40 airlines have 354 routes through UMKK. Its most frequent links are St. Petersburg (LED), Moscow (SVO) and Moscow (DME).
