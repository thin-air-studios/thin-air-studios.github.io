About KSKX

Taos Regional Airport (TSM) is a medium airport in Taos, United States, with the ICAO code KSKX. It has one runway, 04/22, 5,504 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 19 routes through KSKX. Its most frequent links are Denver (BJC), Burbank (BUR) and Carlsbad (CLD).
