About MMSM

Felipe Ángeles International Airport (NLU) is a large airport in Mexico City, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMSM. Its 3 runways are 04C/22C, 04L/22R and 04R/22L. The longest, 04C/22C, is 14,764 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 gates, 247 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 678 routes through MMSM. Its most frequent links are Guadalajara (GDL), Monterrey (MTY) and Cancún (CUN).
