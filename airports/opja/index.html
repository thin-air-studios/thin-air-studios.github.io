About OPJA

Shahbaz Air Base (JAG) is a medium airport in Jacobabad, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPJA. Its two runways are 15L/33R and 15/33. The longer one, 15L/33R, is 10,081 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OPJA.
