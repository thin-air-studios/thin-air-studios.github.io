About KFTW

Fort Worth Meacham International Airport (FTW) is a medium airport in Fort Worth, United States, with the ICAO code KFTW. Its two runways are 16/34 and 17/35. The longer one, 16/34, is 7,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments and 15 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, GoJet has 1 routes through KFTW. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is St Louis (STL).
