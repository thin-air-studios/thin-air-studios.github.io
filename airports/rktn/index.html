About RKTN

Daegu International Airport (TAE) is a large airport in Daegu, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKTN. Its two runways are 13R/31L and 13L/31R. The longer one, 13R/31L, is 9,039 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 315 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 240 routes through RKTN. Its most frequent links are Jeju City (CJU), Taoyuan (TPE) and Da Nang (DAD).
