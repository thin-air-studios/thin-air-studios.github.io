About YPMQ

Port Macquarie Airport (PQQ) is a medium airport in Port Macquarie, Australia, with the ICAO code YPMQ. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,203 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 78 routes through YPMQ. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Brisbane (BNE) and Maroochydore (MCY).
