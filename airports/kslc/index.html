About KSLC

Salt Lake City International Airport (SLC) is a large airport in Salt Lake City, United States, with the ICAO code KSLC. Its 4 runways are 16L/34R, 16R/34L, 17/35 and 14/32. The longest, 16L/34R, is 12,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 gates, 537 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 51 airlines have 1,342 routes through KSLC. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Phoenix (PHX) and San Francisco (SFO).
