About LFPG

Charles de Gaulle International Airport (CDG) is a large airport in Paris (Roissy-en-France, Val-d'Oise), France, with the ICAO code LFPG. It is number 14 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 72.0 million passengers in 2025. It has 5 runways, from 1,444 ft to 13,829 ft long. The longest is 08L/26R. Of these, 4 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 296 gates, 788 taxiway segments, 32 aprons and 35 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 283 airlines have 20,076 routes through LFPG. Its most frequent links are Nice (NCE), Ferno (MXP) and Barcelona (BCN).
