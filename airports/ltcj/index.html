About LTCJ

Batman Airport (BAL) is a medium airport in Batman, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTCJ. It has one runway, 02/20, 10,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 76 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 95 routes through LTCJ. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Ankara (ESB).
