About KDLH

Duluth International Airport (DLH) is a medium airport in Duluth, United States, with the ICAO code KDLH. Its two runways are 09/27 and 03/21. The longer one, 09/27, is 10,591 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 61 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 46 routes through KDLH. Its most frequent links are Minneapolis (MSP), Chicago (ORD) and Fort Myers (RSW).
