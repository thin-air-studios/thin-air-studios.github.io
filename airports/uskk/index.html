About USKK

Pobedilovo Airport (KVX) is a medium airport in Kirov, Russia, with the ICAO code USKK. Its two runways are 03/21 and 06/24. The longer one, 03/21, is 7,230 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 34 routes through USKK. Its most frequent links are Simferopol (SIP), Moscow (VKO) and Sochi (AER).
