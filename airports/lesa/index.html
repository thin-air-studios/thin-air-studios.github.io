About LESA

Salamanca Airport (SLM) is a medium airport in Salamanca, Spain, with the ICAO code LESA. Its two runways are 03/21 and 08/26. The longer one, 03/21, is 8,245 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 22 routes through LESA. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Barcelona (BCN) and San Bartolomé (ACE).
