About WMKJ

Senai International Airport (JHB) is a large airport in Johor Bahru, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WMKJ. It has one runway, 16/34, 12,467 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 119 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 292 routes through WMKJ. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Subang (SZB) and Penang (PEN).
