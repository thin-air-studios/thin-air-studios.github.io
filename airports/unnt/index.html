About UNNT

Novosibirsk Tolmachevo Airport (OVB) is a large airport in Novosibirsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UNNT. Its two runways are 16/34 and 07/25. The longer one, 16/34, is 11,818 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 225 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 77 airlines have 1,513 routes through UNNT. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Luxembourg (LUX) and Irkutsk (IKT).
