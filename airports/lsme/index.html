About LSME

Emmen Air Base (EML) is a medium airport in Emmen, Switzerland, with the ICAO code LSME. It has one runway, 04/22, 8,208 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 208 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LSME.
