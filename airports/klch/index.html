About KLCH

Lake Charles Regional Airport (LCH) is a medium airport in Lake Charles, United States, with the ICAO code KLCH. Its two runways are 15/33 and 05/23. The longer one, 15/33, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 24 routes through KLCH. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
