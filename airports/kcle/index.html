About KCLE

Cleveland Hopkins International Airport (CLE) is a large airport in Cleveland, United States, with the ICAO code KCLE. Its 3 runways are 6R/24L, 6L/24R and 10/28. The longest, 6R/24L, is 9,953 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 gates, 132 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 1,276 routes through KCLE. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), New York (LGA) and Baltimore (BWI).
