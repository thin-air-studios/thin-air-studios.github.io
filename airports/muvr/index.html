About MUVR

Juan Gualberto Gomez International Airport (VRA) is a large airport in Matanzas, Cuba, with the ICAO code MUVR. It has one runway, 06/24, 11,490 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 314 routes through MUVR. Its most frequent links are Montréal (YUL), Toronto (YYZ) and Quebec (YQB).
