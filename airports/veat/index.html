About VEAT

Agartala - Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport (IXA) is a medium airport in Agartala, India, with the ICAO code VEAT. It has one runway, 18/36, 7,500 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 97 routes through VEAT. Its most frequent links are Kolkata (CCU), Guwahati (GAU) and New Delhi (DEL).
