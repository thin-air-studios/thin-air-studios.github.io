About LIED

Decimomannu Air Base (DCI) is a medium airport in Decimomannu, Italy, with the ICAO code LIED. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 9,793 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 29 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIED.
