About RJNK

Komatsu Airport / JASDF Komatsu Air Base (KMQ) is a large airport in Kanazawa, Japan, with the ICAO code RJNK. It has one runway, 06T/24T, 8,876 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 113 routes through RJNK. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Naha (OKA) and Fukuoka (FUK).
