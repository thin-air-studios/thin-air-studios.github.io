About KBXK

Buckeye Municipal Airport (BXK) is a small airport in Buckeye, United States, with the ICAO code KBXK. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,500 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBXK.
