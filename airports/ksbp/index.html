About KSBP

San Luis County Regional Airport (SBP) is a medium airport in San Luis Obispo, United States, with the ICAO code KSBP. Its two runways are 11/29 and 07/25. The longer one, 11/29, is 6,101 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 91 routes through KSBP. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Los Angeles (LAX) and Phoenix (PHX).
