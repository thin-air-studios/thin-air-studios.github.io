About LFQQ

Lille Airport (LIL) is a large airport in Lesquin, France, with the ICAO code LFQQ. Its two runways are 08/26 and 01/19. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,268 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 130 taxiway segments, 43 aprons and 14 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 55 airlines have 1,481 routes through LFQQ. Its most frequent links are Nice (NCE), Toulouse/Blagnac (TLS) and Bordeaux (BOD).
