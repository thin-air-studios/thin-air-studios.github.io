About KOVE

Oroville Municipal Airport (OVE) is a small airport in Oroville, United States, with the ICAO code KOVE. Its two runways are 02/20 and 13/31. The longer one, 02/20, is 6,020 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 80 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOVE.
