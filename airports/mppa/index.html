About MPPA

Panamá Pacífico International Airport (BLB) is a small airport in Panamá City, Panama, with the ICAO code MPPA. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,500 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MPPA.
