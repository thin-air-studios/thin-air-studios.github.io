About KSNA

John Wayne Orange County International Airport (SNA) is a large airport in Santa Ana, United States, with the ICAO code KSNA. Its two runways are 02L/20R and 02R/20L. The longer one, 02L/20R, is 5,700 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 gates, 104 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 754 routes through KSNA. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Denver (DEN) and Phoenix (PHX).
