About MMPN

Uruapan - Licenciado y General Ignacio Lopez Rayon International Airport (UPN) is a medium airport in Uruapan, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMPN. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Volaris has 12 routes through MMPN. Its most frequent links are Tijuana (TIJ) and Los Angeles (LAX).
