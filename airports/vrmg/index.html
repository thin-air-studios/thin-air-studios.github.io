About VRMG

Gan International Airport (GAN) is a large airport in Gan, Maldives, with the ICAO code VRMG. It has one runway, 10/28, 12,000 ft long and 165 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 17 routes through VRMG. Its most frequent links are Malé (MLE), Huvadhu Atoll (GKK) and Muscat/Seeb (MCT).
