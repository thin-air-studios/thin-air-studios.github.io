About KTWF

Joslin Field Magic Valley Regional Airport (TWF) is a medium airport in Twin Falls, United States, with the ICAO code KTWF. Its two runways are 08/26 and 12/30. The longer one, 08/26, is 8,704 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 151 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 18 routes through KTWF. Its most frequent links are Salt Lake City (SLC), Las Vegas (LAS) and Boise (BOI).
