About LPPR

Francisco de Sá Carneiro Airport (OPO) is a large airport in Porto, Portugal, with the ICAO code LPPR. It has one runway, 17/35, 11,417 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 54 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 88 airlines have 4,402 routes through LPPR. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Geneva (GVA) and Madrid (MAD).
