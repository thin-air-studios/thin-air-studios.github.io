About KJQF

Concord-Padgett Regional Airport (USA) is a medium airport in Concord, United States, with the ICAO code KJQF. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,402 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 69 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 97 routes through KJQF. Its most frequent links are New Haven (HVN), Rochester (ROC) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).
