About USPP

Perm International Airport (PEE) is a large airport in Perm, Russia, with the ICAO code USPP. It has one runway, 03/21, 10,520 ft long and 161 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 71 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 242 routes through USPP. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Antalya (AYT) and Sochi (AER).
