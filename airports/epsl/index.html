About EPSL

Świdnik Airfield (EPSL) is a small airport in Lublin, Poland. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,268 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EPSL.
