About KFDK

Frederick Municipal Airport (FDK) is a small airport in Frederick, United States, with the ICAO code KFDK. Its two runways are 05/23 and 12/30. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,819 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFDK.
