About GBYD

Banjul International Airport (BJL) is a large airport in Banjul (Yundum), Gambia, with the ICAO code GBYD. It has one runway, 14/32, 11,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 147 routes through GBYD. Its most frequent links are Zaventem (BRU), Amsterdam (AMS) and Dakar (DSS).
