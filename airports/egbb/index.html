About EGBB

Birmingham Airport (BHX) is a large airport in Birmingham, West Midlands, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGBB. It has one runway, 15/33, 10,013 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 gates, 134 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 112 airlines have 4,575 routes through EGBB. Its most frequent links are Dublin (DUB), Belfast (BFS) and Paris (CDG).
