About KSEA

Seattle–Tacoma International Airport (SEA) is a large airport in Seattle, United States, with the ICAO code KSEA. It is number 37 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 52.7 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 16L/34R, 16C/34C and 16R/34L. The longest, 16L/34R, is 11,901 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 103 gates, 171 taxiway segments, 36 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 74 airlines have 2,839 routes through KSEA. Its most frequent links are Los Angeles (LAX), Anchorage (ANC) and San Francisco (SFO).
