About KSUN

Friedman Memorial Airport (SUN) is a medium airport in Hailey, United States, with the ICAO code KSUN. It has one runway, 13/31, 7,550 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 49 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 30 routes through KSUN. Its most frequent links are Los Angeles (LAX), Salt Lake City (SLC) and Denver (DEN).
