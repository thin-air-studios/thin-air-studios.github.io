About LEAL

Alicante-Elche Miguel Hernández Airport (ALC) is a large airport in Alicante, Spain, with the ICAO code LEAL. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 84 gates, 118 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 92 airlines have 5,799 routes through LEAL. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Manchester (MAN) and Zaventem (BRU).
