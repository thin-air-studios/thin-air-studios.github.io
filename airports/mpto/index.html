About MPTO

Tocumen International Airport (PTY) is a large airport in Tocumen, Panama, with the ICAO code MPTO. Its two runways are 03R/21L and 03L/21R. The longer one, 03R/21L, is 10,006 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 gates, 82 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 18 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 64 airlines have 923 routes through MPTO. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Miami (MIA) and San José (SJO).
