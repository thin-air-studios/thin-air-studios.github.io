About KEKO

Elko Regional Airport (EKO) is a medium airport in Elko, United States, with the ICAO code KEKO. Its two runways are 06/24 and 12/30. The longer one, 06/24, is 7,214 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 50 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 11 routes through KEKO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Salt Lake City (SLC).
