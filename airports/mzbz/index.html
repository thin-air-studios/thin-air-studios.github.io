About MZBZ

Philip S. W. Goldson International Airport (BZE) is a large airport in Belize City, Belize, with the ICAO code MZBZ. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,678 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 25 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 56 routes through MZBZ. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Toronto (YYZ) and San Salvador (SAL).
