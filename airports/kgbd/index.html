About KGBD

Great Bend Municipal Airport (GBD) is a small airport in Great Bend, United States, with the ICAO code KGBD. Its two runways are 17/35 and 11/29. The longer one, 17/35, is 7,852 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGBD.
