About LIBA

Amendola Air Base (LIBA) is a medium airport in San Giovanni Rotondo (FG), Italy. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,727 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments and 12 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIBA.
