About KDVN

Davenport Municipal Airport (DVN) is a small airport in Davenport, United States, with the ICAO code KDVN. Its two runways are 15/33 and 03/21. The longer one, 15/33, is 5,511 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDVN.
