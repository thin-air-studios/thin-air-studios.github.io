About UZNU

Urgench International Airport (UGC) is a large airport in Urgench, Uzbekistan, with the ICAO code UZNU. It has one runway, 13/31, 11,065 ft long and 165 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UZNU.
