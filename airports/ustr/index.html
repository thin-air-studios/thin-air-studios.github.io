About USTR

Roshchino International Airport (TJM) is a large airport in Tyumen, Russia, with the ICAO code USTR. Its two runways are 03/21 and 12/30. The longer one, 03/21, is 9,852 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 154 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 278 routes through USTR. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Sochi (AER) and Antalya (AYT).
