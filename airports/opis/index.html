About OPIS

Islamabad International Airport (ISB) is a large airport in Attock, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPIS. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 12,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 108 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 262 routes through OPIS. Its most frequent links are Karachi (KHI), Dubai (DXB) and Riyadh (RUH).
