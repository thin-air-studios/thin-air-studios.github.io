About KAMA

Rick Husband Amarillo International Airport (AMA) is a medium airport in Amarillo, United States, with the ICAO code KAMA. Its two runways are 04/22 and 13/31. The longer one, 04/22, is 13,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 68 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 69 routes through KAMA. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Houston (IAH) and Denver (DEN).
