About EYSA

Šiauliai International Airport (SQQ) is a medium airport in Šiauliai, Lithuania, with the ICAO code EYSA. Its two runways are 14L/32R and 14R/32L. The longer one, 14L/32R, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, MNB has 2 routes through EYSA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Istanbul (ISL).
