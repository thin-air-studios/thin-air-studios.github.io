About LTBI

Eskişehir Air Base (ESK) is a medium airport in Eskişehir, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTBI. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,006 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 98 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LTBI.
