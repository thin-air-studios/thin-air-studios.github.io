About YCWR

Cowra Airport (CWT) is a medium airport in Australia, with the ICAO code YCWR. Its two runways are 15/33 and 03/21. The longer one, 15/33, is 5,348 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YCWR.
