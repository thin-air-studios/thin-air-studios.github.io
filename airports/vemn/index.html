About VEMN

Dibrugarh Airport (DIB) is a medium airport in Dibrugarh, India, with the ICAO code VEMN. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 46 routes through VEMN. Its most frequent links are Guwahati (GAU), New Delhi (DEL) and Siliguri (IXB).
