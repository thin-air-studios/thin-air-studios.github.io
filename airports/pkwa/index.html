About PKWA

Bucholz Army Air Field (KWA) is a medium airport in Kwajalein, Marshall Islands, with the ICAO code PKWA. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,668 ft long and 198 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 6 routes through PKWA. Its most frequent links are Majuro Atoll (MAJ), Okat (KSA) and Honolulu (HNL).
