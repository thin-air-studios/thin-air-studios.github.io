About YPKG

Kalgoorlie Boulder Airport (KGI) is a medium airport in Broadwood, Australia, with the ICAO code YPKG. Its two runways are 11/29 and 18/36. The longer one, 11/29, is 6,562 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 74 routes through YPKG. Its most frequent links are Perth (PER), Melbourne (MEL) and Sydney (SYD).
