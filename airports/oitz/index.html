About OITZ

Zanjan Airport (JWN) is a medium airport in Zanjan, Iran, with the ICAO code OITZ. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,840 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OITZ.
