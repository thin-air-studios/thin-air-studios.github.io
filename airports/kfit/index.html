About KFIT

Fitchburg Municipal Airport (KFIT) is a small airport in Fitchburg, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,001 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines.
