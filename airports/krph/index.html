About KRPH

Graham Municipal Airport (KRPH) is a small airport in Graham, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 03/21 and 18/36. The longer one, 03/21, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRPH.
