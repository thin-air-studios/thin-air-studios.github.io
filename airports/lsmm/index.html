About LSMM

Meiringen Air Base (LSMM) is a small airport in Meiringen, Switzerland. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 6,571 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LSMM.
