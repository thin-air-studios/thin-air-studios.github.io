About SYCJ

Cheddi Jagan International Airport (GEO) is a large airport in Georgetown, Guyana, with the ICAO code SYCJ. Its two runways are 06/24 and 11/29. The longer one, 06/24, is 11,023 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 97 routes through SYCJ. Its most frequent links are Port of Spain (POS), New York (JFK) and Toronto (YYZ).
