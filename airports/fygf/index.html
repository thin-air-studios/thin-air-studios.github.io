About FYGF

Grootfontein Airport (GFY) is a medium airport in Grootfontein, Namibia, with the ICAO code FYGF. Its two runways are 08/26 and 17/35. The longer one, 08/26, is 11,680 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 82 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FYGF.
