About OYAT

Ataq Airport (AXK) is a medium airport in Ataq, Yemen, with the ICAO code OYAT. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,482 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OYAT.
