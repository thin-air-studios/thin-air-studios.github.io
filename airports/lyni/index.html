About LYNI

Niš Constantine the Great Airport (INI) is a large airport in Niš, Serbia, with the ICAO code LYNI. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 10 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 218 routes through LYNI. Its most frequent links are Memmingen (FMM), Bâle / Mulhouse (BSL) and Belgrade (BEG).
