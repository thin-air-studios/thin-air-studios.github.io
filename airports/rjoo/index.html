About RJOO

Osaka Itami International Airport (ITM) is a large airport in Osaka, Japan, with the ICAO code RJOO. Its two runways are 14R/32L and 14L/32R. The longer one, 14R/32L, is 9,840 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 134 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 624 routes through RJOO. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Natori (SDJ) and Fukuoka (FUK).
