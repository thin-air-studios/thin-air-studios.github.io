About OAHR

Herat - Khwaja Abdullah Ansari International Airport (HEA) is a large airport in Guzara, Afghanistan, with the ICAO code OAHR. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,888 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 64 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through OAHR. Its most frequent links are Kabul (KBL) and Hail (HAS).
