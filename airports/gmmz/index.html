About GMMZ

Ouarzazate International Airport (OZZ) is a large airport in Ouarzazate, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMMZ. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 49 routes through GMMZ. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Marignane (MRS) and Casablanca (CMN).
