About KFLO

Florence Regional Airport (FLO) is a medium airport in Florence, United States, with the ICAO code KFLO. Its two runways are 09/27 and 01/19. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 69 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 20 routes through KFLO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Charlotte (CLT).
