About ZYHB

Harbin Taiping International Airport (HRB) is a large airport in Harbin, China, with the ICAO code ZYHB. Its two runways are 05R/23L and 05L/23R. The longer one, 05R/23L, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 gates, 299 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 58 airlines have 1,243 routes through ZYHB. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (PVG), Hangzhou (HGH) and Qingdao (TAO).
