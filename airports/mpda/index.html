About MPDA

Enrique Malek International Airport (DAV) is a medium airport in David, Panama, with the ICAO code MPDA. It has one runway, 04/22, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 19 routes through MPDA. Its most frequent links are Tocumen (PTY), Albrook (PAC) and San José (SJO).
