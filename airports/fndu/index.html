About FNDU

Dundo Airport (DUE) is a medium airport in Chitato, Angola, with the ICAO code FNDU. It has one runway, 03/21, 6,468 ft long and 80 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, DTA has 1 routes through FNDU. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Luanda (NBJ).
