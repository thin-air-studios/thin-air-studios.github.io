About YPKS

Parkes Airport (PKE) is a medium airport in Parkes, Australia, with the ICAO code YPKS. Its two runways are 04/22 and 11/29. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,525 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Rex has 35 routes through YPKS. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Bathurst (BHS) and Orange (OAG).
