About LFOH

Le Havre-Octeville Airport (LEH) is a medium airport in Le Havre, France, with the ICAO code LFOH. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,546 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 16 routes through LFOH. Its most frequent links are Monastir (MIR), Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS) and New Delhi (DEL).
