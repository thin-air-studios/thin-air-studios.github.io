About KCMX

Houghton County Memorial Airport (CMX) is a medium airport in Hancock, United States, with the ICAO code KCMX. Its two runways are 14/32 and 07/25. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 23 routes through KCMX. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Madison (MSN) and Lansing (LAN).
