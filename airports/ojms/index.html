About OJMS

Muwaffaq Salti Air Base (OJMS) is a medium airport in Al Azraq, Jordan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 13/31 and 08/26. The longer one, 13/31, is 9,777 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 96 taxiway segments and 11 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OJMS.
