About KHLN

Helena Regional Airport (HLN) is a medium airport in Helena, United States, with the ICAO code KHLN. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 05/23 and 16/34. The longest, 09/27, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 121 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 18 routes through KHLN. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Billings (BIL) and Salt Lake City (SLC).
