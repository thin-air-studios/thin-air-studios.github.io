About KCFV

Coffeyville Municipal Airport (CFV) is a small airport in Coffeyville, United States, with the ICAO code KCFV. Its two runways are 17/35 and 04/22. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,868 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCFV.
