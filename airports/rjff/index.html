About RJFF

Fukuoka Airport (FUK) is a large airport in Fukuoka, Japan, with the ICAO code RJFF. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 9,186 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 160 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 56 airlines have 995 routes through RJFF. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Narita (NRT) and Seoul (ICN).
