About LYPG

Podgorica Airport / Podgorica Golubovci Airbase (TGD) is a large airport in Podgorica, Montenegro, with the ICAO code LYPG. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 446 routes through LYPG. Its most frequent links are Belgrade (BEG), Istanbul (IST) and London (STN).
