About UG24

Tbilisi Soganlug Air Base (UG24) is a medium airport in Tbilisi, Georgia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,131 ft long and 255 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 67 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UG24.
