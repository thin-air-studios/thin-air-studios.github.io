About LFLY

Lyon Bron Airport (LYN) is a medium airport in Chassieu, Lyon, France, with the ICAO code LFLY. It has one runway, 16/34, 5,971 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, easyJet Europe has 1 routes through LFLY. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Newcastle upon Tyne (NCL).
