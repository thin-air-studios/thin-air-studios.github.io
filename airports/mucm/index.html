About MUCM

Ignacio Agramonte International Airport (CMW) is a large airport in Camaguey, Cuba, with the ICAO code MUCM. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 28 routes through MUCM. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), Toronto (YYZ) and Montréal (YUL).
