About KTVY

Bolinder Field Tooele Valley Airport (KTVY) is a small airport in Tooele, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,102 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments and 2 aprons.
