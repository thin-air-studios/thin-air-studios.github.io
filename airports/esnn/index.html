About ESNN

Sundsvall-Härnösand Airport (SDL) is a medium airport in Sundsvall/ Härnösand, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESNN. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,857 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 67 routes through ESNN. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Stockholm (BMA) and Göteborg (GOT).
