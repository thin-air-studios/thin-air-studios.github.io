About YSDU

Dubbo City Regional Airport (DBO) is a medium airport in Dubbo, Australia, with the ICAO code YSDU. Its two runways are 05/23 and 11/29. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,604 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 87 routes through YSDU. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Broken Hill (BHQ) and Cobar Airport (CAZ).
