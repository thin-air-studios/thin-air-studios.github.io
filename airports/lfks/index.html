About LFKS

Solenzara (BA 126) Air Base (SOZ) is a medium airport in Solenzara, France, with the ICAO code LFKS. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,619 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, CSH has 2 routes through LFKS. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG) and Paris (ORY).
