About VLSK

Savannakhet Airport (ZVK) is a medium airport in Laos, with the ICAO code VLSK. It has one runway, 04/22, 5,358 ft long and 125 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VLSK.
