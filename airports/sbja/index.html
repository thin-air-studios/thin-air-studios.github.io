About SBJA

Humberto Ghizzo Bortoluzzi Regional Airport (JJG) is a small airport in Jaguaruna, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBJA. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,199 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 6 routes through SBJA. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU) and Campinas (VCP).
