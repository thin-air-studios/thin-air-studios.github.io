About KTOL

Eugene F. Kranz Toledo Express Airport (TOL) is a medium airport in Toledo, United States, with the ICAO code KTOL. Its two runways are 07/25 and 16/34. The longer one, 07/25, is 10,600 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 37 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 52 routes through KTOL. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Pinellas Park (PIE) and Orlando (SFB).
