About URMO

Vladikavkaz Beslan International Airport (OGZ) is a medium airport in Beslan, Russia, with the ICAO code URMO. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 56 routes through URMO. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Antalya (AYT) and Moscow (VKO).
