About KJLN

Joplin Regional Airport (JLN) is a medium airport in Joplin, United States, with the ICAO code KJLN. Its two runways are 18/36 and 13/31. The longer one, 18/36, is 6,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 41 routes through KJLN. Its most frequent links are Decatur (DEC), Denver (DEN) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
