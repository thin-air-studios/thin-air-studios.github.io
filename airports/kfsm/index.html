About KFSM

Fort Smith Regional Airport (FSM) is a medium airport in Fort Smith, United States, with the ICAO code KFSM. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 85 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 7 routes through KFSM. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
