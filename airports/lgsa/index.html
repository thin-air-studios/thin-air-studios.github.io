About LGSA

Chania International Airport (CHQ) is a large airport in Souda, Greece, with the ICAO code LGSA. It has one runway, 11/29, 10,982 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 266 taxiway segments, 51 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 87 airlines have 1,818 routes through LGSA. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Helsinki (HEL) and London (LGW).
