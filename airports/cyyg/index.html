About CYYG

Charlottetown Airport (YYG) is a medium airport in Charlottetown, Canada, with the ICAO code CYYG. Its two runways are 03/21 and 10/28. The longer one, 03/21, is 7,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 119 routes through CYYG. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Montréal (YUL) and Ottawa (YOW).
