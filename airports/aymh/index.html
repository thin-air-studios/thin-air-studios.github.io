About AYMH

Mount Hagen Kagamuga Airport (HGU) is a medium airport in Mount Hagen, Papua New Guinea, with the ICAO code AYMH. Its two runways are 12/30 and 08/26. The longer one, 12/30, is 7,185 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for AYMH.
