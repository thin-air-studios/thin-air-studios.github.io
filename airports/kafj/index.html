About KAFJ

Washington County Airport (WSG) is a small airport in Washington, United States, with the ICAO code KAFJ. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,004 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAFJ.
