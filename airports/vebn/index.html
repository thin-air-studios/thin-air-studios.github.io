About VEBN

Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS) is a large airport in Varanasi, India, with the ICAO code VEBN. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,006 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 15 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 301 routes through VEBN. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR) and Mumbai (BOM).
