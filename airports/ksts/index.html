About KSTS

Charles M. Schulz Sonoma County Airport (STS) is a medium airport in Santa Rosa, United States, with the ICAO code KSTS. Its two runways are 14/32 and 02/20. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 72 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 147 routes through KSTS. Its most frequent links are Burbank (BUR), Los Angeles (LAX) and Portland (PDX).
