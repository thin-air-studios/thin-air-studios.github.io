About HL77

Okba Ibn Nafa Air Base (HL77) is a medium airport in Libya. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 10,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HL77.
