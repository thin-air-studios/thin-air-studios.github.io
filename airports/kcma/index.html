About KCMA

Camarillo Airport (KCMA) is a medium airport in Camarillo, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08/26 and 07/25. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,013 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCMA.
