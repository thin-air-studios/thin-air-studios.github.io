About LFMN

Nice-Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE) is a large airport in Nice, Alpes-Maritimes, France, with the ICAO code LFMN. Its two runways are 04R/22L and 04L/22R. The longer one, 04R/22L, is 9,721 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 gates, 145 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 12 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 128 airlines have 6,059 routes through LFMN. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and London (LGW).
