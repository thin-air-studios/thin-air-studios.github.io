About FABB

Brakpan Airport (FABB) is a small airport in Brakpan, South Africa. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,902 ft long and 49 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FABB.
