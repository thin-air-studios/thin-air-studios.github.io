About KSGR

Sugar Land Regional Airport (SGR) is a medium airport in Houston, United States, with the ICAO code KSGR. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,000 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSGR.
