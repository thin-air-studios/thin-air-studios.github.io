About KVAD

Moody Air Force Base (VAD) is a medium airport in Valdosta, United States, with the ICAO code KVAD. Its two runways are 19L/01R and 19R/01L. The longer one, 19L/01R, is 9,300 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments and 13 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KVAD.
