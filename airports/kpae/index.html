About KPAE

Seattle Paine Field International Airport (PAE) is a medium airport in Everett, United States, with the ICAO code KPAE. Its two runways are 16R/34L and 16L/34R. The longer one, 16R/34L, is 9,010 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 136 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 150 routes through KPAE. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Phoenix (PHX) and Los Angeles (LAX).
