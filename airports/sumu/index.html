About SUMU

Carrasco General Cesáreo L. Berisso International Airport (MVD) is a large airport in Ciudad de la Costa, Uruguay, with the ICAO code SUMU. Its two runways are 07/25 and 01/19. The longer one, 07/25, is 10,499 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 42 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 323 routes through SUMU. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Santiago (SCL) and Rio De Janeiro (GIG).
