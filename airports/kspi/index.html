About KSPI

Abraham Lincoln Capital Airport (SPI) is a medium airport in Springfield, United States, with the ICAO code KSPI. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 13/31 and 18/36. The longest, 04/22, is 8,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 58 taxiway segments, 24 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 38 routes through KSPI. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Orlando (MCO).
