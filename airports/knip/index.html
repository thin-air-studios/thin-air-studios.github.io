About KNIP

Jacksonville Naval Air Station (Towers Field) (NIP) is a medium airport in Jacksonville, United States, with the ICAO code KNIP. Its two runways are 10/28 and 14/32. The longer one, 10/28, is 9,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 105 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 7 routes through KNIP. Its most frequent links are Guantanamo Bay Naval Station (NBW), Baltimore (BWI) and Houston (IAH).
