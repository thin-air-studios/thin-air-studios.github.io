About LTAU

Kayseri Erkilet International Airport (ASR) is a large airport in Kayseri, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAU. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,841 ft long and 145 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 728 routes through LTAU. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Düsseldorf (DUS) and Istanbul (IST).
