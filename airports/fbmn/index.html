About FBMN

Maun International Airport (MUB) is a large airport in Maun, Botswana, with the ICAO code FBMN. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,562 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 79 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 14 routes through FBMN. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Cape Town (CPT) and Kasane (BBK).
