About MYNN

Lynden Pindling International Airport (NAS) is a large airport in Nassau, Bahamas, with the ICAO code MYNN. Its two runways are 14/32 and 10/28. The longer one, 14/32, is 11,353 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 333 routes through MYNN. Its most frequent links are Fort Lauderdale (FLL), Miami (MIA) and Toronto (YYZ).
