About KT82

Gillespie County Airport (KT82) is a small airport in Fredericksburg, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,002 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines.
