About EKAH

Aarhus Airport (AAR) is a large airport in Aarhus, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKAH. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 9,111 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 274 routes through EKAH. Its most frequent links are Copenhagen (CPH), London (STN) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
