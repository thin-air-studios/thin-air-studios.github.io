About UAII

Shymkent International Airport (CIT) is a large airport in Shymkent, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UAII. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,186 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 164 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 188 routes through UAII. Its most frequent links are Almaty (ALA), Astana (NQZ) and Istanbul (IST).
