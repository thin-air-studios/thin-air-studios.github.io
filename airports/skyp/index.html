About SKYP

El Alcaravan - Yopal Airport (EYP) is a medium airport in Yopal, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKYP. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,448 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 33 routes through SKYP. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG) and Mitú (MVP).
