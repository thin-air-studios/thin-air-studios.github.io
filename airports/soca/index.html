About SOCA

Cayenne – Félix Eboué Airport (CAY) is a large airport in Matoury, French Guiana, with the ICAO code SOCA. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,486 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 54 routes through SOCA. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Fort-de-France (FDF) and Paris (CDG).
