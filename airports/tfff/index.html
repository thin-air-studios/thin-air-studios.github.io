About TFFF

Martinique Aimé Césaire International Airport (FDF) is a large airport in Fort-de-France, Martinique, with the ICAO code TFFF. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 96 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 306 routes through TFFF. Its most frequent links are Pointe-à-Pitre (PTP), Paris (ORY) and Paris (CDG).
