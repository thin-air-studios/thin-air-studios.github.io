About LICD

Lampedusa Airport (LMP) is a medium airport in Lampedusa, Italy, with the ICAO code LICD. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,906 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 133 routes through LICD. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Palermo (PMO) and Orio al Serio (BGY).
