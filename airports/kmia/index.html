About KMIA

Miami International Airport (MIA) is a large airport in Miami, United States, with the ICAO code KMIA. It is number 29 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 55.3 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 09/27, 08R/26L, 12/30 and 08L/26R. The longest, 09/27, is 13,016 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 129 gates, 500 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 163 airlines have 3,846 routes through KMIA. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Santiago (SCL) and Anchorage (ANC).
