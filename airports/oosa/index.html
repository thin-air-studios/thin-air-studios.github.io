About OOSA

Salalah International Airport (SLL) is a large airport in Salalah, Oman, with the ICAO code OOSA. It has one runway, 07/25, 13,123 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 147 routes through OOSA. Its most frequent links are Muscat/Seeb (MCT), Prague (PRG) and Dubai (DXB).
