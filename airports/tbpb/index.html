About TBPB

Grantley Adams International Airport (BGI) is a large airport in Bridgetown, Barbados, with the ICAO code TBPB. It has one runway, 09/27, 11,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 50 airlines have 531 routes through TBPB. Its most frequent links are Port of Spain (POS), London (LGW) and London (LHR).
