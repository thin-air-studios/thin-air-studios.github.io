About KNCA

MCAS New River / McCutcheon Field (KNCA) is a medium airport in Jacksonville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 05/23 and 01/19. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,125 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNCA.
