About MMIT

General Antonio Cárdenas Rodríguez National Airport / Ixtepec Air Base (IZT) is a medium airport in Ixtepec, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMIT. It has one runway, 17/35, 7,640 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through MMIT. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Mexico City (NLU).
