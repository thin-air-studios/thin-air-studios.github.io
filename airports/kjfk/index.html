About KJFK

John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) is a large airport in New York, United States, with the ICAO code KJFK. It is number 23 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 62.6 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 13R/31L, 04L/22R, 13L/31R and 04R/22L. The longest, 13R/31L, is 14,511 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 137 gates, 256 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 9 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 198 airlines have 4,168 routes through KJFK. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), London (LHR) and Los Angeles (LAX).
