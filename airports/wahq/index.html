About WAHQ

Adisoemarmo International Airport (SOC) is a large airport in Surakarta, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAHQ. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 65 routes through WAHQ. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Kuta (DPS) and Sepang (KUL).
