About LTFJ

Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen International Airport (SAW) is a large airport in Pendik, Istanbul, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTFJ. It is number 46 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 48.4 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 06R/24L and 06L/24R. The longer one, 06R/24L, is 11,614 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 132 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 85 airlines have 7,774 routes through LTFJ. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Gaziemir (ADB) and Ankara (ESB).
