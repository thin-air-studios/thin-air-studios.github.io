About RPVB

Bacolod-Silay International Airport (BCD) is a large airport in Bacolod City, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPVB. It has one runway, 03/21, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 15 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 142 routes through RPVB. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL), Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB) and Mabalacat (CRK).
