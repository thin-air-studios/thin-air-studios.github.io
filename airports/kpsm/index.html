About KPSM

Portsmouth International Airport at Pease (PSM) is a medium airport in Portsmouth, United States, with the ICAO code KPSM. It has one runway, 16/34, 11,321 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 97 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 76 routes through KPSM. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (HHN), Orlando (SFB) and Punta Gorda (PGD).
