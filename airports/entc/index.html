About ENTC

Tromsø Airport (TOS) is a large airport in Tromsø, Norway, with the ICAO code ENTC. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,041 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 36 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 40 airlines have 764 routes through ENTC. Its most frequent links are Oslo (OSL), Bodø (BOO) and Hammerfest (HFT).
