About FQPB

Pemba Airport (POL) is a medium airport in Pemba, Mozambique, with the ICAO code FQPB. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,905 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 7 routes through FQPB. Its most frequent links are Maputo (MPM), Johannesburg (JNB) and Dar es Salaam (DAR).
