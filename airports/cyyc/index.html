About CYYC

Calgary International Airport (YYC) is a large airport in Calgary, Canada, with the ICAO code CYYC. Its 3 runways are 17L/35R, 17R/35L and 11/29. The longest, 17L/35R, is 14,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 gates, 437 taxiway segments, 43 aprons and 27 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 58 airlines have 2,098 routes through CYYC. Its most frequent links are Vancouver (YVR), Toronto (YYZ) and Edmonton (YEG).
