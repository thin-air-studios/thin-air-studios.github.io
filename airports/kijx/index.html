About KIJX

Jacksonville Municipal Airport (IJX) is a small airport in Jacksonville, United States, with the ICAO code KIJX. Its two runways are 13/31 and 04/22. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KIJX.
