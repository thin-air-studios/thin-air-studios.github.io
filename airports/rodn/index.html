About RODN

Kadena Air Base (DNA) is a large airport in Okinawa, Japan, with the ICAO code RODN. Its two runways are 05L/23R and 05R/23L. The longer one, 05L/23R, is 12,100 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 293 taxiway segments, 81 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 13 routes through RODN. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), Honolulu (HNL) and Pyeongtaek (OSN).
