About OIZH

Zahedan International Airport (ZAH) is a large airport in Zahedan, Iran, with the ICAO code OIZH. Its two runways are 17L/35R and 17R/35L. The longer one, 17L/35R, is 14,042 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 115 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 11 routes through OIZH. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Mashhad (MHD) and Gorgan (GBT).
