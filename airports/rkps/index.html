About RKPS

Sacheon Airport / Sacheon Air Base (HIN) is a medium airport in Sacheon, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKPS. Its two runways are 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06L/24R, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 92 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 23 routes through RKPS. Its most frequent links are Seoul (GMP) and Jeju City (CJU).
