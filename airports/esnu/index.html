About ESNU

Umeå Airport (UME) is a large airport in Umeå, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESNU. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,551 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 210 routes through ESNU. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Stockholm (BMA) and Gran Canaria Island (LPA).
