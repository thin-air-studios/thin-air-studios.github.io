About RKTU

Cheongju International Airport/Cheongju Air Base (K-59/G-513) (CJJ) is a large airport in Cheongju, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKTU. Its two runways are 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06L/24R, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 277 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 289 routes through RKTU. Its most frequent links are Jeju City (CJU), Taoyuan (TPE) and Osaka (KIX).
