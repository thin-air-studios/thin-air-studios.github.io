About MSSS

Ilopango International Airport (ILS) is a medium airport in San Salvador, El Salvador, with the ICAO code MSSS. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,349 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MSSS.
