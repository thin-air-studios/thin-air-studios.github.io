About YSWG

Wagga Wagga Airport (WGA) is a medium airport in Forest Hill, Australia, with the ICAO code YSWG. Its two runways are 05/23 and 12/30. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,807 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 83 routes through YSWG. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Melbourne (MEL) and Brisbane (BNE).
