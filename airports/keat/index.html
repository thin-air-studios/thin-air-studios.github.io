About KEAT

Pangborn Memorial Airport (EAT) is a medium airport in Wenatchee, United States, with the ICAO code KEAT. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 66 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 10 routes through KEAT. Its most frequent links are Seattle (SEA) and Spokane (GEG).
