About LFLN

Saint-Yan Airport (SYT) is a medium airport in L'Hôpital-le-Mercier, Saône-et-Loire, France, with the ICAO code LFLN. Its two runways are 15L/33R and 15R/33L. The longer one, 15L/33R, is 6,660 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFLN.
