About OPMT

Multan International Airport (MUX) is a large airport in Multan, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPMT. It has one runway, 18/36, 12,353 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 107 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 70 routes through OPMT. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Jeddah (JED) and Sharjah (SHJ).
