About PHLI

Lihue Airport (LIH) is a large airport in Lihue, Kauai, United States, with the ICAO code PHLI. Its two runways are 03/21 and 17/35. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 51 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 278 routes through PHLI. Its most frequent links are Honolulu (HNL), Kahului (OGG) and Los Angeles (LAX).
