About KIMS

Madison Regional Airport (MDN) is a small airport in Madison, United States, with the ICAO code KIMS. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,000 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 23 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KIMS.
