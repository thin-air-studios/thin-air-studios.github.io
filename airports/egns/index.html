About EGNS

Isle of Man Airport (IOM) is a large airport in Castletown, Rushen, Isle of Man, with the ICAO code EGNS. Its two runways are 08/26 and 03/21. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,923 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 64 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 356 routes through EGNS. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Liverpool (LPL) and Manchester (MAN).
