About CYFB

Iqaluit Airport (YFB) is a medium airport in Iqaluit, Canada, with the ICAO code CYFB. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,605 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 33 routes through CYFB. Its most frequent links are Ottawa (YOW), Clyde River (YCY) and Kuujjuaq (YVP).
