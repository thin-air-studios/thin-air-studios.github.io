About LGRP

Rhodes International Airport "Diagoras" (RHO) is a large airport in Rhodes, Greece, with the ICAO code LGRP. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,846 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 20 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 148 airlines have 3,536 routes through LGRP. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Amsterdam (AMS) and Manchester (MAN).
