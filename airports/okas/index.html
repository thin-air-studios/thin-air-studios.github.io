About OKAS

Ali Al Salem Air Base (OKAS) is a medium airport in Al Damaikhi, Kuwait. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 12L/30R and 12R/30L. The longer one, 12L/30R, is 9,805 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 102 taxiway segments and 41 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OKAS.
