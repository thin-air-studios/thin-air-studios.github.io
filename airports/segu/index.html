About SEGU

José Joaquín de Olmedo International Airport (GYE) is a large airport in Guayaquil, Ecuador, with the ICAO code SEGU. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,154 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 280 routes through SEGU. Its most frequent links are Quito (UIO), Bogota (BOG) and New York (JFK).
