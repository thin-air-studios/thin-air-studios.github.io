About LPMR

Monte Real Air Base (LPMR) is a medium airport in Portugal. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 36/18, 9,816 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 77 taxiway segments and 51 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LPMR.
