About KHWD

Hayward Executive Airport (HWD) is a small airport in Hayward, United States, with the ICAO code KHWD. Its two runways are 10R/28L and 10L/28R. The longer one, 10R/28L, is 5,694 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 143 taxiway segments and 9 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AMF has 3 routes through KHWD. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Burbank (BUR).
