About KNKX

Miramar Marine Corps Air Station - Mitscher Field (NKX) is a medium airport in San Diego, United States, with the ICAO code KNKX. Its 3 runways are 06L/24R, 06R/24L and 06H/24H. The longest, 06L/24R, is 12,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 471 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNKX.
