About KCSG

Columbus Airport (CSG) is a medium airport in Columbus, United States, with the ICAO code KCSG. Its two runways are 06/24 and 13/31. The longer one, 06/24, is 6,997 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 15 routes through KCSG. Its most frequent links are Atlanta (ATL), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Memphis (MEM).
