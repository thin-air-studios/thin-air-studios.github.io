About MKJS

Sangster International Airport (MBJ) is a large airport in Montego Bay, Jamaica, with the ICAO code MKJS. It has one runway, 07/25, 10,039 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 18 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 43 airlines have 447 routes through MKJS. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Montréal (YUL) and New York (JFK).
