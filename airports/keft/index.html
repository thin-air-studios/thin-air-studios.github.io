About KEFT

Monroe Municipal Airport (KEFT) is a small airport in Monroe, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 12/30 and 02/20. The longer one, 12/30, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KEFT.
