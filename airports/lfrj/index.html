About LFRJ

Landivisiau Air Base (LDV) is a medium airport in Landivisiau, France, with the ICAO code LFRJ. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 113 taxiway segments and 17 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFRJ.
