About KMOT

Minot International Airport (MOT) is a medium airport in Minot, United States, with the ICAO code KMOT. Its two runways are 13/31 and 08/26. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,700 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 44 routes through KMOT. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Fargo (FAR) and Minneapolis (MSP).
