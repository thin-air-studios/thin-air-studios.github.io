About MRLB

Daniel Oduber Quirós International Airport (LIR) is a large airport in Liberia, Costa Rica, with the ICAO code MRLB. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,022 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 192 routes through MRLB. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), San José (SJO) and Montréal (YUL).
