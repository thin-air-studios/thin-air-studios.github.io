About VOAR

INS Rajali / Arakkonam Naval Air Station (VOAR) is a small airport in Arakkonam, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 06/24, 13,460 ft long and 155 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VOAR.
