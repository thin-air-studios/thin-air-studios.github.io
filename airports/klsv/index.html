About KLSV

Nellis Air Force Base (LSV) is a medium airport in Las Vegas, United States, with the ICAO code KLSV. Its two runways are 03L/21R and 03R/21L. The longer one, 03L/21R, is 10,120 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 70 taxiway segments and 14 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, GWY has 1 routes through KLSV. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Philadelphia (PHL).
