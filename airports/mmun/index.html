About MMUN

Cancún International Airport (CUN) is a large airport in Cancún, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMUN. Its two runways are 12R/30L and 12L/30R. The longer one, 12R/30L, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 gates, 139 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 111 airlines have 2,358 routes through MMUN. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Toronto (YYZ) and Monterrey (MTY).
