About DAOI

Chlef Aboubakr Belkaid International Airport (CFK) is a large airport in Chlef, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAOI. Its two runways are 08/26 and 07/25. The longer one, 08/26, is 8,793 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Air Algerie has 4 routes through DAOI. Its most frequent links are Marignane (MRS) and Paris (CDG).
