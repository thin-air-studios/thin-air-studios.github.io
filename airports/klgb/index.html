About KLGB

Long Beach International Airport (LGB) is a large airport in Long Beach, United States, with the ICAO code KLGB. Its 3 runways are 12/30, 08L/26R and 08R/26L. The longest, 12/30, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 133 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 169 routes through KLGB. Its most frequent links are Phoenix (PHX), Denver (DEN) and Oakland (OAK).
