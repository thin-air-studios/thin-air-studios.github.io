About KLMT

Crater Lake-Klamath Regional Airport (LMT) is a medium airport in Klamath Falls, United States, with the ICAO code KLMT. Its two runways are 14/32 and 07/25. The longer one, 14/32, is 10,302 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 11 routes through KLMT. Its most frequent links are Portland (PDX), San Francisco (SFO) and Redmond (RDM).
