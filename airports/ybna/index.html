About YBNA

Ballina Byron Gateway Airport (BNK) is a medium airport in Ballina, Australia, with the ICAO code YBNA. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,234 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 6 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 56 routes through YBNA. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Melbourne (MEL) and Williamtown (NTL).
