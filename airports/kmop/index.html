About KMOP

Mount Pleasant Municipal Airport (MOP) is a small airport in Mount Pleasant, United States, with the ICAO code KMOP. Its two runways are 09/27 and 05/23. The longer one, 09/27, is 5,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMOP.
