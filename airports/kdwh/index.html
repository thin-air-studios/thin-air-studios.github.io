About KDWH

David Wayne Hooks Memorial Airport (DWH) is a small airport in Houston, United States, with the ICAO code KDWH. Its two runways are 17R/35L and 17L/35R. The longer one, 17R/35L, is 7,009 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 161 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDWH.
