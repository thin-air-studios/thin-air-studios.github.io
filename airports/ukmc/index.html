About UKMC

Jagellon Airfield (UKMC) is a small airport in Gorodok, Ukraine. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,202 ft long and 185 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments and 19 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UKMC.
