About AYWK

Wewak International Airport (WWK) is a medium airport in Wewak, Papua New Guinea, with the ICAO code AYWK. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,234 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for AYWK.
