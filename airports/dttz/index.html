About DTTZ

Tozeur Nefta International Airport (TOE) is a medium airport in Tozeur, Tunisia, with the ICAO code DTTZ. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,581 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 23 routes through DTTZ. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Monastir (MIR) and Mellita (DJE).
