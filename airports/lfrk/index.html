About LFRK

Caen Carpiquet airport (CFR) is a medium airport in Caen, France, with the ICAO code LFRK. Its two runways are 12/30 and 12L/30R. The longer one, 12/30, is 6,233 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 221 routes through LFRK. Its most frequent links are Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS), Marignane (MRS) and Nice (NCE).
