About PWAK

Wake Island Airfield (AWK) is a medium airport in Wake Island, U.S. Outlying Islands, with the ICAO code PWAK. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,843 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for PWAK.
