About EGPK

Glasgow Prestwick Airport (PIK) is a large airport in Prestwick, South Ayrshire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGPK. Its two runways are 12/30 and 02/20. The longer one, 12/30, is 9,800 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 31 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 32 airlines have 413 routes through EGPK. Its most frequent links are Alicante (ALC), Málaga (AGP) and Tenerife (TFS).
