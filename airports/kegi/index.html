About KEGI

Duke Field (EGI) is a medium airport in Crestview, United States, with the ICAO code KEGI. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,025 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KEGI.
