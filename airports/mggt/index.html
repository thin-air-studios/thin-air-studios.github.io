About MGGT

La Aurora International Airport (GUA) is a large airport in Guatemala City, Guatemala, with the ICAO code MGGT. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,800 ft long and 196 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 149 taxiway segments, 21 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 50 airlines have 358 routes through MGGT. Its most frequent links are San Salvador (SAL), Miami (MIA) and Mexico City (MEX).
