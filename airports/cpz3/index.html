About CPZ3

Trenton/Mountain View Airport (CPZ3) is a small airport in Ameliasburgh, Canada. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 06R/24L, 06L/24R and 16/34. The longest, 06R/24L, is 5,000 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CPZ3.
