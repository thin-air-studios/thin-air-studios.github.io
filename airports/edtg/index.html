About EDTG

Bremgarten Airfield (EDTG) is a small airport in Eschbach, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 05/23 and 05R/23L. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,413 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDTG.
