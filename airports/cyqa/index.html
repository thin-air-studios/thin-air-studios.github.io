About CYQA

Muskoka Airport (YQA) is a medium airport in Gravenhurst, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQA. It has one runway, 18/36, 6,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Jazz has 1 routes through CYQA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Toronto (YYZ).
