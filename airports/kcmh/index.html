About KCMH

John Glenn Columbus International Airport (CMH) is a large airport in Columbus, United States, with the ICAO code KCMH. Its two runways are 10R/28L and 10L/28R. The longer one, 10R/28L, is 10,114 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 gates, 192 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 37 airlines have 808 routes through KCMH. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), New York (LGA) and Newark (EWR).
