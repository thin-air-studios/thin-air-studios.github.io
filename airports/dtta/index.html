About DTTA

Tunis Carthage International Airport (TUN) is a large airport in Tunis, Tunisia, with the ICAO code DTTA. Its two runways are 01/19 and 11/29. The longer one, 01/19, is 10,499 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 69 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 51 airlines have 910 routes through DTTA. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Tripoli (MJI).
