About LJMB

Maribor Edvard Rusjan Airport (MBX) is a medium airport in Maribor, Slovenia, with the ICAO code LJMB. Its two runways are 14/32 and 14L/32R. The longer one, 14/32, is 8,202 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 11 routes through LJMB. Its most frequent links are Southend-on-Sea (SEN), Zgornji Brnik (LJU) and Vienna (VIE).
