About OSLK

Latakia International Airport (LTK) is a medium airport in Latakia, Syria, with the ICAO code OSLK. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,175 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 135 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 7 routes through OSLK. Its most frequent links are Sharjah (SHJ), Boryspil (KBP) and URRR (URRR).
