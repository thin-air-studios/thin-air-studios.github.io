About SCBA

Balmaceda Airport (BBA) is a medium airport in Balmaceda, Chile, with the ICAO code SCBA. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,205 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 81 routes through SCBA. Its most frequent links are Puerto Montt (PMC), Santiago (SCL) and Temuco (ZCO).
