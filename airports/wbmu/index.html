About WBMU

Mulu Airport (MZV) is a medium airport in Mulu, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WBMU. It has one runway, 03/21, 4,921 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 11 routes through WBMU. Its most frequent links are Miri (MYY), Kota Kinabalu (BKI) and Kuching (KCH).
