About KSLK

Adirondack Regional Airport (SLK) is a medium airport in Saranac Lake, United States, with the ICAO code KSLK. Its two runways are 05/23 and 09/27. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,573 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Cape Air has 37 routes through KSLK. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS) and New York (JFK).
