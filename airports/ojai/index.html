About OJAI

Queen Alia International Airport (AMM) is a large airport in Amman, Jordan, with the ICAO code OJAI. Its two runways are 08L/26R and 08R/26L. The longer one, 08L/26R, is 12,008 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 144 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 74 airlines have 1,333 routes through OJAI. Its most frequent links are Jeddah (JED), Doha (DOH) and Istanbul (IST).
