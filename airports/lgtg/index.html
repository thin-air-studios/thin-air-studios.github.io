About LGTG

Tanagra Air Base (LGTG) is a medium airport in Tanagra, Greece. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,810 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LGTG.
