About MBPV

Providenciales International Airport (PLS) is a large airport in Providenciales, Turks & Caicos Islands, with the ICAO code MBPV. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,199 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 115 routes through MBPV. Its most frequent links are New York (JFK), Montréal (YUL) and Toronto (YYZ).
