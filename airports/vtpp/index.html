About VTPP

Phitsanulok Airport (PHS) is a medium airport in Phitsanulok, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTPP. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 32 routes through VTPP. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Bangkok (DMK).
