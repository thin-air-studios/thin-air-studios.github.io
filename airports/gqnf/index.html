About GQNF

Kiffa Airport (KFA) is a small airport in Kiffa, Mauritania, with the ICAO code GQNF. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,202 ft long and 95 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for GQNF.
