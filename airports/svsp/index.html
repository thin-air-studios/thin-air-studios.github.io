About SVSP

Sub Teniente Nestor Arias Airport (SNF) is a medium airport in San Felipe, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVSP. It has one runway, 05/23, 4,921 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SVSP.
