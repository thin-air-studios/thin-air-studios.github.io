About KBMG

Monroe County Airport (BMG) is a medium airport in Bloomington, United States, with the ICAO code KBMG. Its two runways are 17/35 and 06/24. The longer one, 17/35, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBMG.
