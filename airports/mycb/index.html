About MYCB

New Bight Airport (TBI) is a medium airport in Cat Island, Bahamas, with the ICAO code MYCB. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,050 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WST has 1 routes through MYCB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Nassau (NAS).
