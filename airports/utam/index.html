About UTAM

Mary International Airport (MYP) is a medium airport in Mary, Turkmenistan, with the ICAO code UTAM. Its two runways are 17R/35L and 17L/35R. The longer one, 17R/35L, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 17 routes through UTAM. Its most frequent links are Ashgabat (ASB), Turkmenbaşy (KRW) and Daşoguz (TAZ).
