About KPIT

Pittsburgh International Airport (PIT) is a large airport in Pittsburgh, United States, with the ICAO code KPIT. Its 4 runways are 10R/28L, 10C/28C, 10L/28R and 14/32. The longest, 10R/28L, is 11,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 gates, 316 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 49 airlines have 984 routes through KPIT. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Chicago (ORD) and Newark (EWR).
