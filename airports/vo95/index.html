About VO95

Hosur Airport (VO95) is a small airport in India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,200 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VO95.
