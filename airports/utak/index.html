About UTAK

Turkmenbaşy International Airport (KRW) is a medium airport in Turkmenbaşy, Turkmenistan, with the ICAO code UTAK. Its two runways are 16R/34L and 16L/34R. The longer one, 16R/34L, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 28 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 38 routes through UTAK. Its most frequent links are Ashgabat (ASB), Schkeuditz (LEJ) and Hong Kong (HKG).
