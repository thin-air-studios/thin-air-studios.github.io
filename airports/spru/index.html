About SPRU

Capitán FAP Carlos Martínez de Pinillos International Airport (TRU) is a large airport in Trujillo, Peru, with the ICAO code SPRU. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,920 ft long and 145 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 66 routes through SPRU. Its most frequent links are Lima (LIM), Santiago (SCL) and Cusco (CUZ).
