About LFDN

Rochefort-Saint-Agnant (BA 721) Airport (RCO) is a medium airport in Rochefort/Saint-Agnant, France, with the ICAO code LFDN. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,480 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, easyJet has 1 routes through LFDN. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Copenhagen (CPH).
