About EHWO

Woensdrecht Air Base (WOE) is a medium airport in Hoogerheide, Netherlands, with the ICAO code EHWO. Its two runways are 07R/25L and 07L/25R. The longer one, 07R/25L, is 8,015 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 with no surface listed. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 34 taxiway segments and 9 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 4 routes through EHWO. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Hilversum (EHHV) and Oslo (OSL).
