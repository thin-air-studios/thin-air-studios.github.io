About KHAF

Half Moon Bay Airport (HAF) is a small airport in Half Moon Bay, United States, with the ICAO code KHAF. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHAF.
