About MMRX

General Lucio Blanco International Airport (REX) is a medium airport in Reynosa, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMRX. It has one runway, 13/31, 6,243 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 55 routes through MMRX. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Guadalajara (GDL) and Cancún (CUN).
