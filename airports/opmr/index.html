About OPMR

Masroor Air Base (OPMR) is a medium airport in Karachi, Pakistan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 09/27 and 04/22. The longer one, 09/27, is 9,022 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OPMR.
