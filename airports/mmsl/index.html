About MMSL

Cabo San Lucas International Airport (CSW) is a medium airport in Cabo San Lucas, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMSL. It has one runway, 11/29, 6,998 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JSX has 20 routes through MMSL. Its most frequent links are Dallas (DAL) and Los Angeles (LAX).
