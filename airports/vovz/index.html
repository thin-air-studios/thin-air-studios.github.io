About VOVZ

INS Dega Airport (visakhapatnam international airport) (VOVZ) is a medium airport in Visakhapatnam, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10/28 and 05/23. The longer one, 10/28, is 10,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 192 routes through VOVZ. Its most frequent links are Hyderabad (HYD), New Delhi (DEL) and Bengaluru (BLR).
