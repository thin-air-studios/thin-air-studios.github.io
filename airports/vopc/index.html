About VOPC

Pondicherry Airport (PNY) is a medium airport in Puducherry (Pondicherry), India, with the ICAO code VOPC. It has one runway, 07/25, 4,921 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 12 routes through VOPC. Its most frequent links are Bengaluru (BLR) and Hyderabad (HYD).
