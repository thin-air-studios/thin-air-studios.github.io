About DNPO

Port Harcourt International Airport (PHC) is a large airport in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, with the ICAO code DNPO. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,843 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 40 routes through DNPO. Its most frequent links are Lagos (LOS), Abuja (ABV) and Libreville (LBV).
