About KLBB

Lubbock Preston Smith International Airport (LBB) is a medium airport in Lubbock, United States, with the ICAO code KLBB. Its 3 runways are 17R/35L, 08/26 and 17L/35R. The longest, 17R/35L, is 11,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 86 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 135 routes through KLBB. Its most frequent links are Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW), Houston (IAH) and Denver (DEN).
