About EFLP

Lappeenranta Airport (LPP) is a large airport in Lappeenranta, Finland, with the ICAO code EFLP. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,202 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 41 routes through EFLP. Its most frequent links are Orio al Serio (BGY), Riga (RIX) and Copenhagen (CPH).
