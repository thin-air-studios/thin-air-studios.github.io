About KDJT

President Donald J. Trump International Airport (DJT) is a large airport in West Palm Beach, United States, with the ICAO code KDJT. Its 3 runways are 10L/28R, 14/32 and 10R/28L. The longest, 10L/28R, is 10,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 gates, 200 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 471 routes through KDJT. Its most frequent links are Newark (EWR), White Plains (HPN) and Philadelphia (PHL).
