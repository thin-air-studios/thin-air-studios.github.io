About MMIA

Licenciado Miguel de la Madrid International Airport (CLQ) is a medium airport in Colima, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMIA. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,546 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 10 routes through MMIA. Its most frequent links are Tijuana (TIJ) and Mexico City (MEX).
