About LBWN

Varna Airport (VAR) is a large airport in Varna, Bulgaria, with the ICAO code LBWN. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,258 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 30 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 111 airlines have 1,495 routes through LBWN. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Vienna (VIE) and Hamburg (HAM).
