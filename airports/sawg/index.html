About SAWG

Piloto Civil Norberto Fernández International Airport (RGL) is a large airport in Rio Gallegos, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAWG. It has one runway, 08/26, 9,022 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 19 routes through SAWG. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP), Mount Pleasant (MPN) and Punta Arenas (PUQ).
