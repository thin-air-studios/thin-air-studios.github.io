About LIRL

Latina Air Base (LIRL) is a medium airport in Latina, Italy. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,577 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIRL.
