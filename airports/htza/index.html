About HTZA

Abeid Amani Karume International Airport (ZNZ) is a large airport in Zanzibar, Tanzania, with the ICAO code HTZA. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,915 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 193 routes through HTZA. Its most frequent links are Dar es Salaam (DAR), Mombasa (MBA) and Arusha (JRO).
