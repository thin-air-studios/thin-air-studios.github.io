About LFPB

Paris-Le Bourget International Airport (LBG) is a large airport in Paris, France, with the ICAO code LFPB. Its 3 runways are 07/25, 03/21 and 09/27. The longest, 07/25, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 113 taxiway segments, 22 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 48 routes through LFPB. Its most frequent links are Riyadh (RUH), Jeddah (JED) and Mannheim (MHG).
