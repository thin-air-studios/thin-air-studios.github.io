About KSWO

Stillwater Regional Airport (SWO) is a medium airport in Stillwater, United States, with the ICAO code KSWO. Its two runways are 17/35 and 04/22. The longer one, 17/35, is 7,401 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 74 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SkyWest has 4 routes through KSWO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
