About CYLB

Lac La Biche Airport (YLB) is a small airport in Lac La Biche, Canada, with the ICAO code CYLB. It has one runway, 11/29, 5,700 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYLB.
