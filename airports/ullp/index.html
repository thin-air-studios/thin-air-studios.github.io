About ULLP

Pushkin Airport (ULLP) is a medium airport in St. Petersburg, Russia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,202 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 79 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ULLP.
