About RKNW

Wonju Airport / Hoengseong Air Base (K-38/K-46) (WJU) is a medium airport in Wonju, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKNW. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 111 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JNA has 6 routes through RKNW. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Jeju City (CJU).
