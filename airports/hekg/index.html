About HEKG

El Kharja Airport (UVL) is a small airport in Kharja Oases, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEKG. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HEKG.
