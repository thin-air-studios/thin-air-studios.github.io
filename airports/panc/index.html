About PANC

Ted Stevens Anchorage International Airport (ANC) is a large airport in Anchorage, United States, with the ICAO code PANC. Its 3 runways are 07R/25L, 15/33 and 07L/25R. The longest, 07R/25L, is 12,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 gates, 365 taxiway segments, 36 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 72 airlines have 2,160 routes through PANC. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Hong Kong (HKG) and Seoul (ICN).
