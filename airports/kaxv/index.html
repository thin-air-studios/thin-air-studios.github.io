About KAXV

Neil Armstrong Airport (AXV) is a small airport in Wapakoneta, United States, with the ICAO code KAXV. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,500 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAXV.
