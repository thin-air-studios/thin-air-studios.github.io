About HKEL

Eldoret International Airport (EDL) is a large airport in Eldoret, Kenya, with the ICAO code HKEL. It has one runway, 08/26, 11,480 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 17 routes through HKEL. Its most frequent links are Nairobi (NBO), Kisumu (KIS) and Mombasa (MBA).
