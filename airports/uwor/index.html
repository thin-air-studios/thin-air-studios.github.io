About UWOR

Orsk Airport (OSW) is a medium airport in Orsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UWOR. Its 4 runways are 07/25, 07R/25L, 16/34 and 01/19. The longest, 07/25, is 9,514 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 3 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 13 routes through UWOR. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Moscow (DME) and Sochi (AER).
