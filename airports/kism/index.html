About KISM

Kissimmee Gateway Airport (ISM) is a medium airport in Orlando, United States, with the ICAO code KISM. Its two runways are 15/33 and 06/24. The longer one, 15/33, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 172 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KISM.
