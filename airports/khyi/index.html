About KHYI

San Marcos Regional Airport (KHYI) is a medium airport in San Marcos, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 13/31 and 17/35. The longest, 08/26, is 6,330 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHYI.
