About VAAH

Sardar Vallabh Patel International Airport (AMD) is a large airport in Ahmedabad, India, with the ICAO code VAAH. It has one runway, 05/23, 11,499 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 78 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 952 routes through VAAH. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
