About CYXS

Prince George (International) Airport (YXS) is a medium airport in Prince George, Canada, with the ICAO code CYXS. Its 3 runways are 15/33, 06/24 and 01/19. The longest, 15/33, is 11,450 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 114 routes through CYXS. Its most frequent links are Vancouver (YVR), Calgary (YYC) and Edmonton (YEG).
