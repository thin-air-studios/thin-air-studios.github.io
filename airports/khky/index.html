About KHKY

Hickory Regional Airport (HKY) is a medium airport in Hickory, United States, with the ICAO code KHKY. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,401 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHKY.
