About EGHQ

Cornwall Airport Newquay (NQY) is a medium airport in Newquay, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGHQ. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,003 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 220 routes through EGHQ. Its most frequent links are Manchester (MAN), St. Mary's (ISC) and London (LGW).
