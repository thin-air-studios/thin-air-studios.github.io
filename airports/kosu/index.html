About KOSU

The Ohio State University Airport - Don Scott Field (OSU) is a medium airport in Columbus, United States, with the ICAO code KOSU. Its 3 runways are 09R/27L, 05/23 and 09L/27R. The longest, 09R/27L, is 5,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SKQ has 5 routes through KOSU. Its most frequent links are Chicago/Prospect Heights/Wheeling (PWK), Burlington (KBUY) and Charleston (CRW).
