About CYBR

Brandon Municipal Airport (YBR) is a medium airport in Brandon, Canada, with the ICAO code CYBR. Its two runways are 08/26 and 14/32. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,500 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WestJet Encore has 4 routes through CYBR. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Calgary (YYC).
