About NZCH

Christchurch International Airport (CHC) is a large airport in Christchurch, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZCH. Its 3 runways are 02/20, 11/29 and 01/19. The longest, 02/20, is 10,787 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 1,488 routes through NZCH. Its most frequent links are Wellington (WLG), Auckland (AKL) and Dunedin (DUD).
