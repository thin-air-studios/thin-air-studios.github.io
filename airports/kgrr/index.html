About KGRR

Gerald R. Ford International Airport (GRR) is a large airport in Grand Rapids, United States, with the ICAO code KGRR. Its 3 runways are 08R/26L, 17/35 and 08L/26R. The longest, 08R/26L, is 10,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 170 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 466 routes through KGRR. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Orlando (SFB) and Detroit (DTW).
