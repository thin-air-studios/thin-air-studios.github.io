About OMDM

Al Minhad Air Base (NHD) is a medium airport in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMDM. It has one runway, 09/27, 12,970 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 77 taxiway segments and 17 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 7 routes through OMDM. Its most frequent links are Carterton (BZZ), Akrotiri (AKT) and Zaventem (BRU).
