About OMAW

Abu Dhabi Northeast Airport - Suweihan Air Base (OMAW) is a small airport in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 13/31, 7,080 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 111 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OMAW.
