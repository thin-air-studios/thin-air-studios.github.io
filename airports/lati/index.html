About LATI

Tirana International Airport Mother Teresa (TIA) is a large airport in Rinas, Albania, with the ICAO code LATI. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 78 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 95 airlines have 2,151 routes through LATI. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Orio al Serio (BGY) and Ferno (MXP).
