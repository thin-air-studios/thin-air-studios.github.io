About EGHH

Bournemouth Airport (BOH) is a medium airport in Bournemouth, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGHH. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,454 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 89 taxiway segments, 23 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 574 routes through EGHH. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Geneva (GVA) and Málaga (AGP).
