About UKOH

Kherson International Airport (KHE) is a medium airport in Kherson, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKOH. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,202 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 142 taxiway segments and 14 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 19 routes through UKOH. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Boryspil (KBP) and Istanbul (ISL).
