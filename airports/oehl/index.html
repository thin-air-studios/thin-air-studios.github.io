About OEHL

Hail International Airport (HAS) is a large airport in Hail, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEHL. It has one runway, 18/36, 12,204 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 46 routes through OEHL. Its most frequent links are Riyadh (RUH), Cairo (CAI) and Ad Dammam (DMM).
