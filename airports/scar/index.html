About SCAR

Chacalluta International Airport (ARI) is a medium airport in Arica, Chile, with the ICAO code SCAR. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 36 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 72 routes through SCAR. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), La Serena-Coquimbo (LSC) and Iquique (IQQ).
