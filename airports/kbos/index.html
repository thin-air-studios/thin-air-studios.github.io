About KBOS

Boston Logan International Airport (BOS) is a large airport in Boston, United States, with the ICAO code KBOS. It has 6 runways, from 2,557 ft to 10,083 ft long. The longest is 15R/33L. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 113 gates, 255 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 94 airlines have 3,467 routes through KBOS. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Newark (EWR) and Washington (DCA).
