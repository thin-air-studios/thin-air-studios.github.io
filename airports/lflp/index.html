About LFLP

Annecy Meythet airport (NCY) is a medium airport in Annecy, France, with the ICAO code LFLP. Its two runways are 04/22 and 04R/22L. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,348 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Air France has 1 routes through LFLP. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Paris (ORY).
