About LFRN

Rennes-Saint-Jacques Airport (RNS) is a medium airport in Saint-Jacques-de-la-Lande, Ille-et-Vilaine, France, with the ICAO code LFRN. Its two runways are 10/28 and 14/32. The longer one, 10/28, is 6,890 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 116 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 509 routes through LFRN. Its most frequent links are Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS), Paris (CDG) and Nice (NCE).
