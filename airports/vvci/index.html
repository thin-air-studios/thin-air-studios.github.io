About VVCI

Cat Bi International Airport (HPH) is a large airport in Haiphong (Hai An), Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVCI. It has one runway, 07/25, 10,007 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 31 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 184 routes through VVCI. Its most frequent links are Ho Chi Minh City (SGN), Da Nang (DAD) and Nha Trang/nha Trang aiurportCam Ranh (CXR).
