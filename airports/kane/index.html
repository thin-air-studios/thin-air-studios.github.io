About KANE

Anoka County-Blaine (Janes Field) Airport (KANE) is a small airport in Minneapolis, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 09/27 and 18/36. The longer one, 09/27, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 92 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KANE.
