About KBAM

Battle Mountain Airport (BAM) is a small airport in Battle Mountain, United States, with the ICAO code KBAM. Its two runways are 04/22 and 13/31. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,300 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBAM.
