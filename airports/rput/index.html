About RPUT

Tuguegarao Airport (TUG) is a medium airport in Tuguegarao City, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPUT. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,455 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 8 routes through RPUT. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Manila (MNL).
