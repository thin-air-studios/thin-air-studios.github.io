About UKLI

Ivano-Frankivsk International Airport (IFO) is a medium airport in Ivano-Frankivsk, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKLI. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,226 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 15 routes through UKLI. Its most frequent links are Boryspil (KBP), Alicante (ALC) and Valencia (VLC).
