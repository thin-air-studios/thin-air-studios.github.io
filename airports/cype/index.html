About CYPE

Peace River Airport (YPE) is a medium airport in Peace River, Canada, with the ICAO code CYPE. It has one runway, 04/22, 5,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYPE.
