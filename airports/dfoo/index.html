About DFOO

Bobo Dioulasso Airport (BOY) is a large airport in Bobo Dioulasso, Burkina Faso, with the ICAO code DFOO. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,826 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, VBW has 4 routes through DFOO. Its most frequent links are Ouagadougou (OUA) and Abidjan (ABJ).
