About KCEU

Oconee County Regional Airport (CEU) is a small airport in Clemson, United States, with the ICAO code KCEU. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,000 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCEU.
