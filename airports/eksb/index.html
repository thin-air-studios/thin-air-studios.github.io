About EKSB

Sønderborg Airport (SGD) is a medium airport in Sønderborg, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKSB. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,895 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 49 routes through EKSB. Its most frequent links are Copenhagen (CPH), Rønne (RNN) and Paris (CDG).
