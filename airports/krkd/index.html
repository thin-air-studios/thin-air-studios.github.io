About KRKD

Knox County Regional Airport (RKD) is a medium airport in Rockland, United States, with the ICAO code KRKD. Its two runways are 13/31 and 03/21. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,412 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 23 routes through KRKD. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS), Manchester (MHT) and Waterville (WVL).
