About CYQF

Red Deer Regional Airport (YQF) is a medium airport in Springbrook, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQF. Its two runways are 17/35 and 12/30. The longer one, 17/35, is 7,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYQF.
