About DAON

Zenata – Messali El Hadj Airport (TLM) is a large airport in Zenata, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAON. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 70 routes through DAON. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS) and Algiers (ALG).
