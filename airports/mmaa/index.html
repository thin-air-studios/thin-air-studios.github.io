About MMAA

General Juan N. Álvarez International Airport (ACA) is a large airport in Acapulco, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMAA. Its two runways are 10/28 and 06/24. The longer one, 10/28, is 10,832 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 70 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 82 routes through MMAA. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Mexico City (NLU) and Tijuana (TIJ).
