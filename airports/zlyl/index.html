About ZLYL

Yulin Yuyang Airport (UYN) is a medium airport in Yulin, China, with the ICAO code ZLYL. It has one runway, 16/34, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 97 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 157 routes through ZLYL. Its most frequent links are Xi'an (XIY), Beijing (PKX) and Beijing (PEK).
