About LIRQ

Florence Airport, Peretola (FLR) is a large airport in Firenze (FI), Italy, with the ICAO code LIRQ. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,118 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 1,147 routes through LIRQ. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and Munich (MUC).
