About HEGS

Jiyanklis Air Base (HEGS) is a medium airport in Abu El Matamir, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 12/30 and 11/29. The longer one, 12/30, is 9,473 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 77 taxiway segments and 12 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HEGS.
