About EIDW

Dublin Airport (DUB) is a large airport in Dublin, Ireland, with the ICAO code EIDW. Its 3 runways are 10L/28R, 10R/28L and 16/34. The longest, 10L/28R, is 10,203 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 86 gates, 201 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 155 airlines have 8,089 routes through EIDW. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Paris (CDG) and London (LGW).
