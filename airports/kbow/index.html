About KBOW

Bartow Executive Airport (BOW) is a small airport in Bartow, United States, with the ICAO code KBOW. Its 3 runways are 05/23, 09L/27R and 09R/27L. The longest, 05/23, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 99 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBOW.
