About UGGT

Telavi Kurdgelauri Airport (UGGT) is a small airport in Telavi, Georgia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,551 ft long and 110 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UGGT.
