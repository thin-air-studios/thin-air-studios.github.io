About OATN

Tarinkot Airport (TII) is a small airport in Tarinkot, Afghanistan, with the ICAO code OATN. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,300 ft long and 90 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OATN.
