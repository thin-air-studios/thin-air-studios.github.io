About KPPO

La Porte Municipal Airport (LPO) is a small airport in La Porte, United States, with the ICAO code KPPO. Its two runways are 02/20 and 14/32. The longer one, 02/20, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPPO.
