About SBPL

Senador Nilo Coelho Airport (PNZ) is a medium airport in Petrolina, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBPL. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,055 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 47 routes through SBPL. Its most frequent links are Recife (REC), São Paulo (GRU) and Salvador (SSA).
