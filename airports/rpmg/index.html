About RPMG

Dipolog Airport (DPL) is a medium airport in Dipolog, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPMG. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,273 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 31 routes through RPMG. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL) and Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB).
