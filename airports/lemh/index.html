About LEMH

Menorca Airport (MAH) is a large airport in Mahón (Maó), Spain, with the ICAO code LEMH. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,366 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 68 airlines have 1,990 routes through LEMH. Its most frequent links are Barcelona (BCN), Madrid (MAD) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
