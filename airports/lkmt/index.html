About LKMT

Leoš Janáček Airport Ostrava (OSR) is a large airport in Mošnov, Czechia, with the ICAO code LKMT. It has one runway, 04/22, 11,484 ft long and 207 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 83 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 245 routes through LKMT. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Brno (BRQ) and Burgas (BOJ).
