About ULWC

Cherepovets Airport (CEE) is a medium airport in Cherepovets, Russia, with the ICAO code ULWC. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,202 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 47 routes through ULWC. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), St. Petersburg (LED) and Moscow (SVO).
