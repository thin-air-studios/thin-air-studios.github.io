About SCCI

President Carlos Ibáñez International Airport (PUQ) is a large airport in Punta Arenas, Chile, with the ICAO code SCCI. Its 3 runways are 07/25, 12/30 and 01/19. The longest, 07/25, is 9,154 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 56 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 137 routes through SCCI. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), Puerto Montt (PMC) and Puerto Williams (WPU).
