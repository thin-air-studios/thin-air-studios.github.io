About SPZO

Alejandro Velasco Astete International Airport (CUZ) is a large airport in Cusco, Peru, with the ICAO code SPZO. It has one runway, 10/28, 11,146 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 18 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 312 routes through SPZO. Its most frequent links are Lima (LIM), Arequipa (AQP) and Puerto Maldonado (PEM).
