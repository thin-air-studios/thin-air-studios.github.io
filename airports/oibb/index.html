About OIBB

Bushehr Airport (BUZ) is a medium airport in Bushehr, Iran, with the ICAO code OIBB. Its two runways are 13R/31L and 13L/31R. The longer one, 13R/31L, is 14,664 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 67 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 17 routes through OIBB. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Dubai (DXB) and Isfahan (IFN).
