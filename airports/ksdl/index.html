About KSDL

Scottsdale Airport (SCF) is a small airport in Scottsdale, United States, with the ICAO code KSDL. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,249 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 208 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 56 routes through KSDL. Its most frequent links are Las Vegas (LAS), Santa Ana (SNA) and Carlsbad (CLD).
