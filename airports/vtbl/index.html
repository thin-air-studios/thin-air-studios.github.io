About VTBL

Khok Kathiam Airport (KKM) is a medium airport in Lop Buri, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTBL. Its 3 runways are 16/34, 05/23 and 10/28. The longest, 16/34, is 7,252 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VTBL.
