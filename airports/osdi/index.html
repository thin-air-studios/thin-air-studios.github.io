About OSDI

Damascus International Airport (DAM) is a large airport in Damascus, Syria, with the ICAO code OSDI. It has one runway, 05R/23L, 11,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 104 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 253 routes through OSDI. Its most frequent links are Sharjah (SHJ), Kuwait City (KWI) and Dubai (DXB).
