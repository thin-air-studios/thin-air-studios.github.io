About KTPL

Draughon Miller Central Texas Regional Airport (TPL) is a medium airport in Temple, United States, with the ICAO code KTPL. Its two runways are 16/34 and 03/21. The longer one, 16/34, is 7,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTPL.
