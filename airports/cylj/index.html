About CYLJ

Meadow Lake Airport (YLJ) is a medium airport in Meadow Lake, Canada, with the ICAO code CYLJ. Its two runways are 09/27 and 17/35. The longer one, 09/27, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYLJ.
