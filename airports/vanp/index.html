About VANP

Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport (NAG) is a large airport in Nagpur, India, with the ICAO code VANP. It has one runway, 14/32, 10,500 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 225 routes through VANP. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
