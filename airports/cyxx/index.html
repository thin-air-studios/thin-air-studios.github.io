About CYXX

Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) is a medium airport in Abbotsford, Canada, with the ICAO code CYXX. Its two runways are 07/25 and 01/19. The longer one, 07/25, is 9,600 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 99 routes through CYXX. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC), Edmonton (YEG) and Toronto (YYZ).
