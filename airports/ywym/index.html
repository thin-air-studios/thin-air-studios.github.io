About YWYM

Wyndham Airport (WYN) is a small airport in Australia, with the ICAO code YWYM. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,272 ft long and 59 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YWYM.
