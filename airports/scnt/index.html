About SCNT

Lieutenant Julio Gallardo Airport (PNT) is a medium airport in Puerto Natales, Chile, with the ICAO code SCNT. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,786 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 30 routes through SCNT. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), Puerto Montt (PMC) and Punta Arenas (PUQ).
