About EGFF

Cardiff International Airport (CWL) is a large airport in Cardiff, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGFF. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,723 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 61 airlines have 613 routes through EGFF. Its most frequent links are Dublin (DUB), Alicante (ALC) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
