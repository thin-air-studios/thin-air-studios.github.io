About KDHN

Dothan Regional Airport (DHN) is a medium airport in Dothan, United States, with the ICAO code KDHN. Its two runways are 14/32 and 18/36. The longer one, 14/32, is 8,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 107 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 13 routes through KDHN. Its most frequent links are Memphis (MEM), Atlanta (ATL) and Lawrenceville (LZU).
