About ENAT

Alta Airport (ALF) is a medium airport in Alta, Norway, with the ICAO code ENAT. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,165 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 76 routes through ENAT. Its most frequent links are Tromsø (TOS), Kirkenes (KKN) and Hammerfest (HFT).
