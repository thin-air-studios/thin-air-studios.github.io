About KLND

Hunt Field (LND) is a medium airport in Lander, United States, with the ICAO code KLND. It has one runway, 04/22, 5,000 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLND.
