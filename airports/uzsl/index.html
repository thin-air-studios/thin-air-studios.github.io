About UZSL

Karshi-Khanabad Air Base (UZSL) is a small airport in Khanabad, Uzbekistan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,195 ft long and 132 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UZSL.
