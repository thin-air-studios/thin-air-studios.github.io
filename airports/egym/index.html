About EGYM

RAF Marham (KNF) is a medium airport in King's Lynn, Norfolk, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGYM. Its two runways are 05/23 and 01/19. The longer one, 05/23, is 9,131 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 76 taxiway segments and 32 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EGYM.
