About YSTW

Tamworth Airport (TMW) is a medium airport in Tamworth, Australia, with the ICAO code YSTW. Its 4 runways are 12L/30R, 12R/30L, 18/36 and 06/24. The longest, 12L/30R, is 7,218 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 59 routes through YSTW. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Brisbane (BNE) and Melbourne (MEL).
