About UGSS

Vladislav Ardzinba Sukhum International Airport (SUI) is a large airport in Sukhumi, Georgia, with the ICAO code UGSS. It has one runway, 12/30, 12,012 ft long and 172 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, NWS has 2 routes through UGSS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Samara (KUF).
