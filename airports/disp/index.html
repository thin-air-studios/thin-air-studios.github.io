About DISP

San Pedro Airport (SPY) is a medium airport in Côte d’Ivoire, with the ICAO code DISP. It has one runway, 03/21, 6,234 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, VRE has 2 routes through DISP. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Abidjan (ABJ).
