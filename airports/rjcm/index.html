About RJCM

Memanbetsu Airport (MMB) is a medium airport in Ōzora, Japan, with the ICAO code RJCM. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,202 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 50 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 55 routes through RJCM. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Sapporo (CTS) and Sapporo (OKD).
