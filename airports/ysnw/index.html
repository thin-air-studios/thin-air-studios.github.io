About YSNW

Naval Air Station Nowra - HMAS Albatross (NOA) is a medium airport in Nowra Hill, Australia, with the ICAO code YSNW. Its two runways are 08/26 and 03/21. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,870 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YSNW.
