About SCVD

Pichoy Airport (ZAL) is a medium airport in Valdivia, Chile, with the ICAO code SCVD. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,870 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 36 routes through SCVD. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), Concepcion (CCP) and Osorno (ZOS).
