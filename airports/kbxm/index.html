About KBXM

Brunswick Executive Airport (NHZ) is a medium airport in Brunswick, United States, with the ICAO code KBXM. It has one runway, 01R/19L, 8,000 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 209 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBXM.
