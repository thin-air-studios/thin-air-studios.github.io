About UUYH

Ukhta Airport (UCT) is a medium airport in Ukhta, Russia, with the ICAO code UUYH. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,691 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 93 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 17 routes through UUYH. Its most frequent links are St. Petersburg (LED), Moscow (DME) and Moscow (SVO).
