About VTBD

Don Mueang International Airport (DMK) is a large airport in Bangkok, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTBD. Its two runways are 03L/21R and 03R/21L. The longer one, 03L/21R, is 12,139 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 1,635 routes through VTBD. Its most frequent links are Chiang Mai (CNX), Phuket (HKT) and Hat Yai (HDY).
