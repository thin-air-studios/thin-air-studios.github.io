About EGAE

City of Derry Airport (LDY) is a medium airport in Derry, Derry and Strabane, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGAE. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,460 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 104 routes through EGAE. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Liverpool (LPL) and Manchester (MAN).
