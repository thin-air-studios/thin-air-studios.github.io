About K11R

Brenham Municipal Airport (K11R) is a small airport in Brenham, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 16/34, 6,003 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for K11R.
