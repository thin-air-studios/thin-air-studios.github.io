About FMMT

Toamasina Ambalamanasy Airport (TMM) is a large airport in Toamasina, Madagascar, with the ICAO code FMMT. It has one runway, 01/19, 7,218 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 24 routes through FMMT. Its most frequent links are Antananarivo (TNR), Sainte-Marie (RUN) and Sambava (SVB).
