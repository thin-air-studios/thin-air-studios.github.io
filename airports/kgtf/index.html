About KGTF

Great Falls International Airport (GTF) is a medium airport in Great Falls, United States, with the ICAO code KGTF. Its 3 runways are 03/21, 17/35 and 07/25. The longest, 03/21, is 10,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 70 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 67 routes through KGTF. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Kalispell (FCA) and Salt Lake City (SLC).
