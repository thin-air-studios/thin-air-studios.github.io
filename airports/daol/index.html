About DAOL

Oran Tafraoui Airport (TAF) is a medium airport in Tafraoui, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAOL. It has one runway, 08/26, 9,037 ft long and 140 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DAOL.
