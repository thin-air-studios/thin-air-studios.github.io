About EFIV

Ivalo Airport (IVL) is a large airport in Ivalo, Finland, with the ICAO code EFIV. Its two runways are 04/22 and 08/26. The longer one, 04/22, is 8,199 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 21 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 168 routes through EFIV. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Paris (ORY) and London (LGW).
