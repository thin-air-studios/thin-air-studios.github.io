About KPQL

Trent Lott International Airport (PGL) is a small airport in Pascagoula, United States, with the ICAO code KPQL. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,501 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPQL.
