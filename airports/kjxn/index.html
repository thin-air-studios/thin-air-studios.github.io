About KJXN

Jackson County Airport/Reynolds Field (JXN) is a medium airport in Jackson, United States, with the ICAO code KJXN. Its two runways are 07/25 and 14/32. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,351 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, CTL has 5 routes through KJXN. Its most frequent links are Chicago/West Chicago (DPA), Pontiac (PTK) and Gwinn (MQT).
