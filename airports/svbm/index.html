About SVBM

Jacinto Lara International Airport (BRM) is a large airport in Barquisimeto, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVBM. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,350 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 17 routes through SVBM. Its most frequent links are Maiquetía (CCS), Isla Margarita (PMV) and Santo Domingo (SDQ).
