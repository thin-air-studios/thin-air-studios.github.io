About GMFO

Oujda Angads Airport (OUD) is a large airport in Ahl Angad, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMFO. Its two runways are 06/24 and 13/31. The longer one, 06/24, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 38 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 440 routes through GMFO. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Charleroi (CRL) and Zaventem (BRU).
