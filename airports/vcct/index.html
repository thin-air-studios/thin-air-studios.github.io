About VCCT

China Bay Airport (TRR) is a medium airport in Trincomalee, Sri Lanka, with the ICAO code VCCT. It has one runway, 06/24, 7,850 ft long and 85 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VCCT.
