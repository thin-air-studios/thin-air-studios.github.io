About SPME

Captain Pedro Canga Rodríguez International Airport (TBP) is a medium airport in Tumbes, Peru, with the ICAO code SPME. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 15 routes through SPME. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Lima (LIM).
