About LGKA

Kastoria National Airport Aristotle (KSO) is a small airport in Argos Orestiko, Greece, with the ICAO code LGKA. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,852 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SEH has 2 routes through LGKA. Its most frequent links are Kozani (KZI) and Spata-Artemida (ATH).
