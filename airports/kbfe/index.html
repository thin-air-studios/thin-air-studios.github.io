About KBFE

Terry County Airport (KBFE) is a small airport in Brownfield, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 02/20 and 13/31. The longer one, 02/20, is 5,218 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBFE.
