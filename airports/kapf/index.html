About KAPF

Naples Municipal Airport (APF) is a medium airport in Naples, United States, with the ICAO code KAPF. Its 3 runways are 05/23, 14/32 and NE/SW. The longest, 05/23, is 6,600 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 126 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JSX has 5 routes through KAPF. Its most frequent links are White Plains (HPN) and Morristown (MMU).
