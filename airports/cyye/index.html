About CYYE

Fort Nelson Airport (YYE) is a medium airport in Fort Nelson, Canada, with the ICAO code CYYE. Its two runways are 04/22 and 08/26. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYYE.
