About ESOW

Stockholm Västerås Airport (VST) is a large airport in Stockholm / Västerås, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESOW. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,468 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 68 routes through ESOW. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Málaga (AGP) and Alicante (ALC).
