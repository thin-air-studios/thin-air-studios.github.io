About HEAZ

Almaza Air Force Base (HEAZ) is a medium airport in El Nozha, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 18/36 and 05/23. The longer one, 18/36, is 6,726 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HEAZ.
