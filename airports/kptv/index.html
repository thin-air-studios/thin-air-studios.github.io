About KPTV

Porterville Municipal Airport (PTV) is a small airport in Porterville, United States, with the ICAO code KPTV. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,960 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPTV.
