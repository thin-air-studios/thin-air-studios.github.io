About ESSA

Stockholm-Arlanda Airport (ARN) is a large airport in Stockholm, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESSA. Its 3 runways are 01L/19R, 08/26 and 01R/19L. The longest, 01L/19R, is 10,830 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 gates, 232 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 166 airlines have 6,018 routes through ESSA. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Copenhagen (CPH) and Paris (CDG).
