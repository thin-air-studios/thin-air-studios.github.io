About LGHI

Chios Island National Airport (JKH) is a medium airport in Chios Island, Greece, with the ICAO code LGHI. It has one runway, 01/19, 4,957 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 60 routes through LGHI. Its most frequent links are Spata-Artemida (ATH), Thessaloniki (SKG) and Amsterdam (AMS).
