About KCWF

Chennault International Airport (CWF) is a small airport in Lake Charles, United States, with the ICAO code KCWF. It has one runway, 15/33, 10,702 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCWF.
