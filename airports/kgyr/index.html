About KGYR

Phoenix Goodyear Airport (GYR) is a small airport in Goodyear, United States, with the ICAO code KGYR. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,500 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 78 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AIJ has 1 routes through KGYR. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Tucson (TUS).
