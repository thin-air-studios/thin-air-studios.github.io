About ESMQ

Kalmar Airport (KLR) is a medium airport in Kalmar, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESMQ. Its two runways are 16/34 and 05/23. The longer one, 16/34, is 6,726 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 80 routes through ESMQ. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (BMA), Stockholm (ARN) and Gran Canaria Island (LPA).
