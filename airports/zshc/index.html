About ZSHC

Hangzhou Xiaoshan International Airport (HGH) is a large airport in Hangzhou, China, with the ICAO code ZSHC. It is number 41 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 50.5 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 07/25 and 06/24. The longer one, 07/25, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 113 gates, 363 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 9 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 99 airlines have 2,293 routes through ZSHC. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Shenzhen (SZX) and Beijing (PEK).
