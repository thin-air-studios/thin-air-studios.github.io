About VTSB

Surat Thani Airport (URT) is a medium airport in Surat Thani, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTSB. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 25 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 77 routes through VTSB. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (DMK), Bangkok (BKK) and Chiang Mai (CNX).
