About OPFA

Faisalabad International Airport (LYP) is a large airport in Faisalabad, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPFA. Its two runways are 03R/21L and 03L/21R. The longer one, 03R/21L, is 9,324 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 82 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 24 routes through OPFA. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Karachi (KHI) and Manama (BAH).
