About LRSM

Satu Mare International Airport (SUJ) is a medium airport in Satu Mare, Romania, with the ICAO code LRSM. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,160 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 68 routes through LRSM. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Otopeni (OTP) and Beauvais (BVA).
