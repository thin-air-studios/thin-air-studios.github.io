About 8XS8

Reese Airpark (REE) is a small airport in Lubbock, United States, with the ICAO code 8XS8. Its 3 runways are 17R/35L, 08/26 and 17L/35R. The longest, 17R/35L, is 10,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for 8XS8.
