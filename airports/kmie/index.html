About KMIE

Delaware County Johnson Field (MIE) is a medium airport in Muncie, United States, with the ICAO code KMIE. Its two runways are 14/32 and 03/21. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMIE.
