About NZPM

Palmerston North Airport (PMR) is a medium airport in Palmerston North, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZPM. Its 3 runways are 07/25, 07L/25R and 07R/25L. The longest, 07/25, is 6,240 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 218 routes through NZPM. Its most frequent links are Auckland (AKL), Christchurch (CHC) and Nelson (NSN).
