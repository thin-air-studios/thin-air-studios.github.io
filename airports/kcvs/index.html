About KCVS

Cannon Air Force Base (CVS) is a medium airport in Clovis, United States, with the ICAO code KCVS. Its two runways are 04/22 and 13/31. The longer one, 04/22, is 10,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments and 15 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCVS.
