About AYNZ

Nadzab Tomodachi International Airport (LAE) is a large airport in Lae, Papua New Guinea, with the ICAO code AYNZ. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for AYNZ.
