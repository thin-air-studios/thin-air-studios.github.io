About SCDA

Diego Aracena International Airport (IQQ) is a large airport in Iquique, Chile, with the ICAO code SCDA. It has one runway, 01/19, 10,991 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 93 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 206 routes through SCDA. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), La Serena-Coquimbo (LSC) and Concepcion (CCP).
