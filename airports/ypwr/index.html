About YPWR

Woomera Airfield (UMR) is a small airport in Woomera, Australia, with the ICAO code YPWR. Its two runways are 18/36 and 12/30. The longer one, 18/36, is 7,782 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YPWR.
