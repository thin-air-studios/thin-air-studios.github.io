About SCPC

Pucón Airport (ZPC) is a small airport in Pucon, Chile, with the ICAO code SCPC. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,576 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SCPC.
