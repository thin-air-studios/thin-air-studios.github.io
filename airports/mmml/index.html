About MMML

General Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada International Airport (MXL) is a medium airport in Mexicali, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMML. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 109 routes through MMML. Its most frequent links are Guadalajara (GDL), Mexico City (MEX) and Monterrey (MTY).
