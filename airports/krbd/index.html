About KRBD

Dallas Executive Airport (RBD) is a small airport in Dallas, United States, with the ICAO code KRBD. Its two runways are 13/31 and 17/35. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,136 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRBD.
