About OIKR

Rafsanjan Airport (RJN) is a medium airport in Rafsanjan, Iran, with the ICAO code OIKR. It has one runway, 11/29, 9,814 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OIKR.
