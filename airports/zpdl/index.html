About ZPDL

Dali Fengyi Airport (DLU) is a medium airport in Dali (Xiaguan), China, with the ICAO code ZPDL. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 28 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 226 routes through ZPDL. Its most frequent links are Chengdu (TFU), Shenzhen (SZX) and Chongqing (CKG).
