About CYOW

Ottawa Macdonald-Cartier International Airport (YOW) is a large airport in Ottawa, Canada, with the ICAO code CYOW. Its 3 runways are 14/32, 07/25 and 04/22. The longest, 14/32, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 gates, 188 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 1,031 routes through CYOW. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Toronto (YTZ) and Halifax (YHZ).
