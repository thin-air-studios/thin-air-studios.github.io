About LERT

Rota Naval Station Airport (ROZ) is a medium airport in Rota, Spain, with the ICAO code LERT. It has one runway, 10/28, 12,106 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 191 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 2 routes through LERT. Its most frequent links are Norfolk (NGU) and Portsmouth (PSM).
