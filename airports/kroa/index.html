About KROA

Roanoke–Blacksburg Regional Airport (ROA) is a medium airport in Roanoke, United States, with the ICAO code KROA. Its two runways are 06/24 and 16/34. The longer one, 06/24, is 6,800 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 78 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 159 routes through KROA. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Dulles (IAD) and Chicago (ORD).
