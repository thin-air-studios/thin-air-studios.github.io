About EIKY

Kerry Airport (KIR) is a medium airport in Farranfore, Ireland, with the ICAO code EIKY. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 140 routes through EIKY. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (HHN), Luton (LTN) and Dublin (DUB).
