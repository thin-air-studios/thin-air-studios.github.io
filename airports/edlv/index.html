About EDLV

Weeze (Niederrhein) Airport (NRN) is a large airport in Weeze, Germany, with the ICAO code EDLV. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,005 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 89 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 837 routes through EDLV. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Girona (GRO) and Málaga (AGP).
