About LIPX

Verona Villafranca Valerio Catullo Airport (VRN) is a large airport in Caselle (VR), Italy, with the ICAO code LIPX. It has one runway, 04/22, 10,064 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 61 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 86 airlines have 1,589 routes through LIPX. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Catania (CTA) and Frankfurt am Main (FRA).
