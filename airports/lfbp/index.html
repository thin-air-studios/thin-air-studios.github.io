About LFBP

Pau Pyrénées Airport (PUF) is a medium airport in Pau/Pyrénées (Uzein), France, with the ICAO code LFBP. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 97 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 249 routes through LFBP. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Paris (ORY) and Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS).
