About CYGK

Kingston Norman Rogers Airport (YGK) is a medium airport in Kingston, Canada, with the ICAO code CYGK. Its two runways are 01/19 and 07/25. The longer one, 01/19, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 16 routes through CYGK. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Hamilton (YHM) and Moosonee (YMO).
