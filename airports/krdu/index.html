About KRDU

Raleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU) is a large airport in Raleigh/Durham, United States, with the ICAO code KRDU. Its 3 runways are 05L/23R, 05R/23L and 14/32. The longest, 05L/23R, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 gates, 257 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 52 airlines have 1,299 routes through KRDU. Its most frequent links are Newark (EWR), New York (LGA) and Washington (DCA).
