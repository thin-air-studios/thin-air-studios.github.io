About KDUA

Durant Regional Airport - Eaker Field (DUA) is a medium airport in Durant, United States, with the ICAO code KDUA. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,800 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 87 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDUA.
