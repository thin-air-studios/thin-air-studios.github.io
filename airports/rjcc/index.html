About RJCC

New Chitose Airport (CTS) is a large airport in Sapporo, Japan, with the ICAO code RJCC. Its two runways are 01L/19R and 01R/19L. The longer one, 01L/19R, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 gates, 183 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 57 airlines have 1,078 routes through RJCC. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Narita (NRT) and Tokoname (NGO).
