About EDRY

Speyer Airfield (EDRY) is a small airport in Speyer, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 16/34 and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16/34, is 5,501 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments, 24 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDRY.
