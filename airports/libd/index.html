About LIBD

Bari Karol Wojtyła International Airport (BRI) is a large airport in Bari, Italy, with the ICAO code LIBD. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 28 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 63 airlines have 1,938 routes through LIBD. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Orio al Serio (BGY) and Rome (FCO).
