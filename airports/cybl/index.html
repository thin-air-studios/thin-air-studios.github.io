About CYBL

Campbell River Airport (YBL) is a medium airport in Campbell River, Canada, with the ICAO code CYBL. It has one runway, 12/30, 6,499 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 7 routes through CYBL. Its most frequent links are Vancouver (YVR), Calgary (YYC) and Comox (YQQ).
