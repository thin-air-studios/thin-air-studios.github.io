About KTVI

Thomasville Regional Airport (TVI) is a small airport in Thomasville, United States, with the ICAO code KTVI. Its two runways are 04/22 and 14/32. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTVI.
