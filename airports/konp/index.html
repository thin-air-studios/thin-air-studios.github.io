About KONP

Newport Municipal Airport (ONP) is a medium airport in Newport, United States, with the ICAO code KONP. Its two runways are 16/34 and 02/20. The longer one, 16/34, is 5,398 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Empire has 2 routes through KONP. Its most frequent links are Corvallis (CVO) and Portland (PDX).
