About LFRG

Deauville Normandie airport (DOL) is a medium airport in Deauville, France, with the ICAO code LFRG. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,366 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 94 routes through LFRG. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Marrakesh (RAK) and Brest (BES).
