About LRBS

Bucharest Băneasa Aurel Vlaicu International Airport (BBU) is a large airport in Bucharest, Romania, with the ICAO code LRBS. It has one runway, 07/25, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 176 routes through LRBS. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Balice (KRK) and Orio al Serio (BGY).
