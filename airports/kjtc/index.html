About KJTC

Springerville Municipal Airport (KJTC) is a small airport in Springerville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 03/21 and 11/29. The longer one, 03/21, is 8,422 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KJTC.
