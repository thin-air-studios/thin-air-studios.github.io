About ZBAD

Beijing Daxing International Airport (PKX) is a large airport in Beijing, China, with the ICAO code ZBAD. It is number 34 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 53.6 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 11L/29R, 17L/35R, 17R/35L and 01L/19R. The longest, 11L/29R, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 gates, 905 taxiway segments, 127 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 54 airlines have 2,029 routes through ZBAD. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Shenzhen (SZX) and Kunming (KMG).
