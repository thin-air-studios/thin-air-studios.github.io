About LYTV

Tivat Airport (TIV) is a medium airport in Tivat, Montenegro, with the ICAO code LYTV. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,208 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 71 airlines have 490 routes through LYTV. Its most frequent links are Belgrade (BEG), Moscow (DME) and London (LGW).
