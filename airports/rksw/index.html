About RKSW

Suwon Airport (SWU) is a medium airport in Suwon, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKSW. Its 3 runways are 15L/33R, 15R/33L and 16/34. The longest, 15L/33R, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 207 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RKSW.
