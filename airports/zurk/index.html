About ZURK

Xigaze Peace Airport / Shigatse Air Base (RKZ) is a large airport in Xigazê (Samzhubzê), China, with the ICAO code ZURK. Its two runways are 09/27 and 07/25. The longer one, 09/27, is 16,404 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 14 routes through ZURK. Its most frequent links are Haidong (XNN), Xi'an (XIY) and Chongqing (CKG).
