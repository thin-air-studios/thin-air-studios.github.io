About KORK

North Little Rock Municipal Airport (KORK) is a small airport in North Little Rock, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 05/23 and 17/35. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KORK.
