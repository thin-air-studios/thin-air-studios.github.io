About GCRR

César Manrique-Lanzarote Airport (ACE) is a large airport in San Bartolomé, Spain, with the ICAO code GCRR. It has one runway, 03/21, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 55 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 74 airlines have 3,348 routes through GCRR. Its most frequent links are Gran Canaria Island (LPA), London (LGW) and Madrid (MAD).
