About VTSG

Krabi International Airport (KBV) is a large airport in Krabi, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTSG. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 219 routes through VTSG. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (DMK), Bangkok (BKK) and Sepang (KUL).
