About KULM

New Ulm Municipal Airport (ULM) is a small airport in New Ulm, United States, with the ICAO code KULM. Its two runways are 15/33 and 04/22. The longer one, 15/33, is 5,401 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KULM.
