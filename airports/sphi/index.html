About SPHI

Capitán FAP José A. Quiñones González International Airport (CIX) is a large airport in Chiclayo, Peru, with the ICAO code SPHI. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,266 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 48 routes through SPHI. Its most frequent links are Lima (LIM), Tocumen (PTY) and Tarapoto (TPP).
