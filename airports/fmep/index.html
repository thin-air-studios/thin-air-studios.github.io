About FMEP

Saint-Pierre Pierrefonds Airport (ZSE) is a large airport in Saint-Pierre, Réunion, with the ICAO code FMEP. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 14 routes through FMEP. Its most frequent links are Plaine Magnien (MRU), Port Mathurin (RRG) and Dzaoudzi (DZA).
