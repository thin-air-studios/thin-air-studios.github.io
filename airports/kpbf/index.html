About KPBF

Pine Bluff Regional Airport, Grider Field (PBF) is a medium airport in Pine Bluff, United States, with the ICAO code KPBF. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,998 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPBF.
