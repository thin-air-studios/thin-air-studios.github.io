About SAOR

Villa Reynolds Airport (VME) is a medium airport in Villa Mercedes, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAOR. Its two runways are 10/28 and 6/24. The longer one, 10/28, is 7,874 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SAOR.
