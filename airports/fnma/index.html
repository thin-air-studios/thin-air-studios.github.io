About FNMA

Malanje Airport (MEG) is a medium airport in Malanje, Angola, with the ICAO code FNMA. It has one runway, 13/31, 7,283 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FNMA.
