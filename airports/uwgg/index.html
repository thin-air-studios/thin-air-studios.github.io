About UWGG

Nizhny Novgorod / Strigino International Airport (GOJ) is a large airport in Nizhny Novgorod, Russia, with the ICAO code UWGG. Its 4 runways are 18L/36R, 18R/36L, 15/33 and 06/24. The longest, 18L/36R, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 145 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 249 routes through UWGG. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Moscow (SVO) and St. Petersburg (LED).
