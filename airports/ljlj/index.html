About LJLJ

Ljubljana Jože Pučnik Airport (LJU) is a large airport in Zgornji Brnik, Slovenia, with the ICAO code LJLJ. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,827 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 67 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 70 airlines have 782 routes through LJLJ. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Belgrade (BEG) and Frankfurt am Main (FRA).
