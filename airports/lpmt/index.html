About LPMT

Montijo Air Base (LPMT) is a medium airport in Montijo, Portugal. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 07/25 and 01/19. The longer one, 07/25, is 8,005 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments and 36 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LPMT.
