About UDSG

Shirak International Airport (LWN) is a large airport in Gyumri, Armenia, with the ICAO code UDSG. It has one runway, 02/20, 10,564 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 23 routes through UDSG. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (VKO) and Larnaca (LCA).
