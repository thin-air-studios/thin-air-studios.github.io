About KTMA

Henry Tift Myers Airport (TMA) is a small airport in Tifton, United States, with the ICAO code KTMA. Its two runways are 16/34 and 10/28. The longer one, 16/34, is 6,506 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTMA.
