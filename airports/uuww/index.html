About UUWW

Vnukovo International Airport (VKO) is a large airport in Moscow, Russia, with the ICAO code UUWW. Its two runways are 06/24 and 01/19. The longer one, 06/24, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 gates, 743 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 85 airlines have 2,090 routes through UUWW. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), St. Petersburg (LED) and Yerevan (EVN).
