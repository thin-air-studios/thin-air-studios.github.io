About BIKF

Keflavik International Airport (KEF) is a large airport in Reykjavík, Iceland, with the ICAO code BIKF. Its two runways are 10/28 and 01/19. The longer one, 10/28, is 10,056 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 156 taxiway segments, 45 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 96 airlines have 1,439 routes through BIKF. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Ferno (MXP) and Manchester (MAN).
