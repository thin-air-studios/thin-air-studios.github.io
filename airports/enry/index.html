About ENRY

Moss Airport, Rygge (ENRY) is a medium airport in Oslo, Norway. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,012 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 104 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 188 routes through ENRY. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Alicante (ALC) and Gran Canaria Island (LPA).
