About KLOT

Lewis University Airport (LOT) is a small airport in Chicago/Romeoville, United States, with the ICAO code KLOT. Its two runways are 02/20 and 09/27. The longer one, 02/20, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLOT.
