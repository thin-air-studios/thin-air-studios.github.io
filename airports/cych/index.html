About CYCH

Miramichi Airport (YCH) is a medium airport in Miramichi, Canada, with the ICAO code CYCH. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,006 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYCH.
