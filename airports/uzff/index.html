About UZFF

Fergana International Airport (FEG) is a medium airport in Fergana, Uzbekistan, with the ICAO code UZFF. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,383 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 61 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UZFF.
