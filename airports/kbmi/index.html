About KBMI

Central Illinois Regional Airport at Bloomington-Normal (BMI) is a medium airport in Bloomington/Normal, United States, with the ICAO code KBMI. Its two runways are 02/20 and 11/29. The longer one, 02/20, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 81 routes through KBMI. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Atlanta (ATL) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
