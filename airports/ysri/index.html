About YSRI

RAAF Base Richmond (XRH) is a medium airport in Richmond, Australia, with the ICAO code YSRI. It has one runway, 10/28, 7,001 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 107 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YSRI.
