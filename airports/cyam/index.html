About CYAM

Sault Ste Marie Airport (YAM) is a medium airport in Sault Ste Marie, Canada, with the ICAO code CYAM. Its two runways are 04/22 and 12/30. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 48 routes through CYAM. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Toronto (YTZ) and Cayo Coco (CCC).
