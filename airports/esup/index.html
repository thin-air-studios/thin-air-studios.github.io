About ESUP

Pajala Airport (PJA) is a small airport in Pajala, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESUP. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,552 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through ESUP. Its most frequent links are Luleå (LLA) and Bournemouth (BOH).
