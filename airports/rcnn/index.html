About RCNN

Tainan International Airport / Tainan Air Base (TNN) is a large airport in Tainan (Rende), Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCNN. Its two runways are 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longer one, 18L/36R, is 10,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 109 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 31 routes through RCNN. Its most frequent links are Huxi (MZG), Shang-I (KNH) and Hong Kong (HKG).
