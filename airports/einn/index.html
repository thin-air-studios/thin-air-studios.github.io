About EINN

Shannon Airport (SNN) is a large airport in Shannon, Ireland, with the ICAO code EINN. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,495 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 80 airlines have 739 routes through EINN. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Manchester (MAN) and San Bartolomé (ACE).
