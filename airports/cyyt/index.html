About CYYT

St. John's International Airport (YYT) is a large airport in St. John's, Canada, with the ICAO code CYYT. Its two runways are 10/28 and 16/34. The longer one, 10/28, is 8,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 65 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 289 routes through CYYT. Its most frequent links are Halifax (YHZ), Toronto (YYZ) and Montréal (YUL).
