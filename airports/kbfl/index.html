About KBFL

Meadows Field (BFL) is a medium airport in Bakersfield, United States, with the ICAO code KBFL. Its two runways are 12L/30R and 12R/30L. The longer one, 12L/30R, is 10,849 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 132 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 72 routes through KBFL. Its most frequent links are Phoenix (PHX), Houston (IAH) and San Francisco (SFO).
