About WAHS

Jenderal Ahmad Yani Airport (SRG) is a large airport in Semarang, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAHS. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,399 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 164 routes through WAHS. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Surabaya (SUB) and Kuta (DPS).
