About SBRF

Recife/Guararapes - Gilberto Freyre International Airport (REC) is a large airport in Recife, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBRF. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,865 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 gates, 82 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 849 routes through SBRF. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Rio De Janeiro (GIG) and São Paulo (CGH).
