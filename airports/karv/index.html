About KARV

Lakeland Airport Noble F Lee Memorial Field (ARV) is a small airport in Woodruff, United States, with the ICAO code KARV. Its two runways are 18/36 and 10/28. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,150 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KARV.
