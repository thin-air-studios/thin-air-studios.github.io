About SEPC

Patuca Airport (SEPC) is a small airport in Patuca, Ecuador. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 02/20, 5,255 ft long and 96 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SEPC.
