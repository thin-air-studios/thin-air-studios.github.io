About ZSSH

Huai'an Lianshui Airport (HIA) is a large airport in Huai'an, China, with the ICAO code ZSSH. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 98 routes through ZSSH. Its most frequent links are Chengdu (TFU), Guangzhou (CAN) and Tianjin (TSN).
