About UGSG

Gudauta Air Base (UGSG) is a medium airport in Gudauta, Georgia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 9,802 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UGSG.
