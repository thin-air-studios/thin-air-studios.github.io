About VOBG

HAL Airport (VOBG) is a medium airport in Bangalore, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,850 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 17 routes through VOBG. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), Sharjah (SHJ) and Bengaluru (BLR).
