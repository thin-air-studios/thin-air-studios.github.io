About KDAL

Dallas Love Field (DAL) is a large airport in Dallas, United States, with the ICAO code KDAL. Its two runways are 13R/31L and 13L/31R. The longer one, 13R/31L, is 8,800 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 264 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 695 routes through KDAL. Its most frequent links are Houston (HOU), Denver (DEN) and Las Vegas (LAS).
