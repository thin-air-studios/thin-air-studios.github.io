About EDSB

Karlsruhe Baden-Baden Airport (FKB) is a large airport in Rheinmünster, Germany, with the ICAO code EDSB. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,787 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 56 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 765 routes through EDSB. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Antalya (AYT) and London (STN).
