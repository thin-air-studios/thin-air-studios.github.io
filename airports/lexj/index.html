About LEXJ

Seve Ballesteros-Santander Airport (SDR) is a medium airport in Santander, Spain, with the ICAO code LEXJ. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,612 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 442 routes through LEXJ. Its most frequent links are Madrid (MAD), Barcelona (BCN) and London (STN).
