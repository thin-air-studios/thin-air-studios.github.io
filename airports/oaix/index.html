About OAIX

Bagram Airfield (OAI) is a medium airport in Bagram, Afghanistan, with the ICAO code OAIX. Its two runways are 03R/21L and 03L/21R. The longer one, 03R/21L, is 11,820 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 68 taxiway segments and 22 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 10 routes through OAIX. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Frankfurt am Main (HHN) and Ramstein-Miesenbach (RMS).
