About MMPB

Hermanos Serdán International Airport (PBC) is a large airport in Puebla, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMPB. It has one runway, 17/35, 11,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 93 routes through MMPB. Its most frequent links are Tijuana (TIJ), Cancún (CUN) and Guadalajara (GDL).
