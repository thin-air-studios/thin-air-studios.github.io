About EGTK

London Oxford Airport (OXF) is a medium airport in Kidlington, Oxfordshire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGTK. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,006 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 5 routes through EGTK. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (FRA), Bristol (BRS) and Bournemouth (BOH).
