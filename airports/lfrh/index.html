About LFRH

Lorient South Brittany (Bretagne Sud) Airport (LRT) is a medium airport in Lorient/Lann/Bihoué, France, with the ICAO code LFRH. Its two runways are 07/25 and 02/20. The longer one, 07/25, is 7,884 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 152 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 22 routes through LFRH. Its most frequent links are Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS), Paris (ORY) and Paris (CDG).
