About KBIF

Biggs Army Air Field (Fort Bliss) (BIF) is a medium airport in Fort Bliss/El Paso, United States, with the ICAO code KBIF. It has one runway, 04/22, 13,554 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 218 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 14 routes through KBIF. Its most frequent links are Portsmouth (PSM), Frankfurt am Main (HHN) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
