About RKTI

Jungwon Air Base/Chungju Airport (JWO) is a medium airport in Gimseang-ro, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKTI. Its two runways are 18R/36L and 18L/36R. The longer one, 18R/36L, is 9,330 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 227 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RKTI.
