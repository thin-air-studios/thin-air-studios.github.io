About VTSP

Phuket International Airport (HKT) is a large airport in Phuket, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTSP. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,171 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 24 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 104 airlines have 1,136 routes through VTSP. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (BKK), Bangkok (DMK) and Singapore (SIN).
