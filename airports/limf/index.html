About LIMF

Turin Airport (TRN) is a large airport in Caselle Torinese (TO), Italy, with the ICAO code LIMF. It has one runway, 18/36, 10,827 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 70 airlines have 1,742 routes through LIMF. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), London (LGW) and Catania (CTA).
