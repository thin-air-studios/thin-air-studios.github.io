About GMAA

Inezgane Airport (GMAA) is a small airport in Inezgane, Morocco. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,547 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for GMAA.
