About PAJN

Juneau International Airport (JNU) is a medium airport in Juneau, United States, with the ICAO code PAJN. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,857 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 59 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 46 routes through PAJN. Its most frequent links are Seattle (SEA), Anchorage (ANC) and Sitka (SIT).
