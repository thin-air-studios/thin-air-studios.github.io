About AYTK

Tokua Airport (RAB) is a medium airport in Kokopo, Papua New Guinea, with the ICAO code AYTK. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,643 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JAF has 1 routes through AYTK. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Zaventem (BRU).
