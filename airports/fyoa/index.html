About FYOA

Ondangwa Airport (OND) is a medium airport in Ondangwa, Namibia, with the ICAO code FYOA. Its two runways are 08/26 and 16/34. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,800 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WAA has 3 routes through FYOA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Windhoek (ERS).
