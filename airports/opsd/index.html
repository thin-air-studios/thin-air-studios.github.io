About OPSD

Skardu International Airport (KDU) is a large airport in Skardu, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPSD. Its two runways are 14L/32R and 14R/32L. The longer one, 14L/32R, is 11,995 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 7 routes through OPSD. Its most frequent links are Lahore (LHE), Karachi (KHI) and Faisalabad (LYP).
