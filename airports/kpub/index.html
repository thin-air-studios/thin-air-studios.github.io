About KPUB

Pueblo Memorial Airport (PUB) is a medium airport in Pueblo, United States, with the ICAO code KPUB. Its 3 runways are 08R/26L, 17/35 and 08L/26R. The longest, 08R/26L, is 10,498 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 97 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 17 routes through KPUB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Denver (DEN).
