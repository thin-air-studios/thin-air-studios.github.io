About OETR

Turaif Domestic Airport (TUI) is a medium airport in Turaif, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OETR. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 4 routes through OETR. Its most frequent links are Ad Dammam (DMM) and Riyadh (RUH).
