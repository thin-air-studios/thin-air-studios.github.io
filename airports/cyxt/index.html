About CYXT

Northwest Regional Airport Terrace-Kitimat (YXT) is a medium airport in Terrace, Canada, with the ICAO code CYXT. Its two runways are 15/33 and 03/21. The longer one, 15/33, is 7,497 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 96 routes through CYXT. Its most frequent links are Vancouver (YVR), Calgary (YYC) and Edmonton (YEG).
