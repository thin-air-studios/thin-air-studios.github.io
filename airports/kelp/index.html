About KELP

El Paso International Airport (ELP) is a large airport in El Paso, United States, with the ICAO code KELP. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 08R/26L and 08L/26R. The longest, 04/22, is 12,020 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 303 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 306 routes through KELP. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Phoenix (PHX) and Houston (IAH).
