About LBBG

Burgas Airport (BOJ) is a large airport in Burgas, Bulgaria, with the ICAO code LBBG. It has one runway, 04/22, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 74 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 135 airlines have 2,038 routes through LBBG. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Luton (LTN) and Katowice (KTW).
