About KCWA

Central Wisconsin Airport (CWA) is a medium airport in Mosinee, United States, with the ICAO code KCWA. Its two runways are 08/26 and 17/35. The longer one, 08/26, is 7,723 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 19 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 50 routes through KCWA. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Milwaukee (MKE) and Minneapolis (MSP).
