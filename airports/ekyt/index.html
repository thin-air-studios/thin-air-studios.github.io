About EKYT

Aalborg Airport (AAL) is a large airport in Aalborg, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKYT. Its two runways are 08L/26R and 08R/26L. The longer one, 08L/26R, is 8,707 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 229 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 40 airlines have 516 routes through EKYT. Its most frequent links are Copenhagen (CPH), Antalya (AYT) and Gran Canaria Island (LPA).
