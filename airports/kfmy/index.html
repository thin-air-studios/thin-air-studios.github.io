About KFMY

Page Field (FMY) is a medium airport in Fort Myers, United States, with the ICAO code KFMY. Its two runways are 05/23 and 13/31. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,406 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 183 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFMY.
