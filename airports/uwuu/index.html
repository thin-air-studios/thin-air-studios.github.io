About UWUU

Ufa International Airport (UFA) is a large airport in Ufa, Russia, with the ICAO code UWUU. Its two runways are 14R/32L and 14/32. The longer one, 14R/32L, is 12,339 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 156 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 489 routes through UWUU. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Moscow (SVO) and Moscow (DME).
