About VOML

Mangaluru International Airport (IXE) is a large airport in Mangaluru, India, with the ICAO code VOML. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,035 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 183 routes through VOML. Its most frequent links are Bengaluru (BLR), Mumbai (BOM) and Dubai (DXB).
