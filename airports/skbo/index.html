About SKBO

El Dorado International Airport (BOG) is a large airport in Bogota, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKBO. Its two runways are 14L/32R and 14R/32L. The longer one, 14L/32R, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 gates, 295 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 88 airlines have 2,730 routes through SKBO. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), Medellín (MDE) and Cali (CLO).
