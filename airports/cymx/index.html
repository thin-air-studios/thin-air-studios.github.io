About CYMX

Montreal Mirabel International Airport (YMX) is a medium airport in Montréal, Canada, with the ICAO code CYMX. Its two runways are 06/24 and 11/29. The longer one, 06/24, is 12,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 90 routes through CYMX. Its most frequent links are Hamilton (YHM), Toronto (YYZ) and Cincinnati / Covington (CVG).
