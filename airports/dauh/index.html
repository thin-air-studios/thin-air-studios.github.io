About DAUH

Hassi Messaoud-Oued Irara Krim Belkacem Airport (HME) is a medium airport in Hassi Messaoud, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAUH. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 43 routes through DAUH. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG), Constantine (CZL) and Es-Sénia (ORN).
