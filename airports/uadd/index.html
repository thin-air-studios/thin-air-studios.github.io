About UADD

Taraz International Airport (DMB) is a large airport in Taraz, Kazakhstan, with the ICAO code UADD. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,514 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 67 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 25 routes through UADD. Its most frequent links are Astana (NQZ), Phu Quoc Island (PQC) and Almaty (ALA).
