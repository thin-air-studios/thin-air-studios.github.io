About KARG

Walnut Ridge Regional Airport (ARG) is a small airport in Walnut Ridge, United States, with the ICAO code KARG. Its 3 runways are 04/22, 13/31 and 18/36. The longest, 04/22, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, KMA has 1 routes through KARG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is St. Petersburg (LED).
