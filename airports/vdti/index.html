About VDTI

Techo International Airport (KTI) is a large airport in Phnom Penh (Boeng Khyang), Cambodia, with the ICAO code VDTI. It has one runway, 05/23, 13,123 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 153 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 195 routes through VDTI. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (BKK), Guangzhou (CAN) and Chengdu (TFU).
