About KGSO

Piedmont Triad International Airport (GSO) is a large airport in Greensboro, United States, with the ICAO code KGSO. Its 3 runways are 05R/23L, 05L/23R and 14/32. The longest, 05R/23L, is 10,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 155 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 40 airlines have 348 routes through KGSO. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Chicago (ORD) and Dulles (IAD).
