About ZSYN

Yancheng Nanyang International Airport (YNZ) is a large airport in Yancheng (Tinghu), China, with the ICAO code ZSYN. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,186 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 90 taxiway segments and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 107 routes through ZSYN. Its most frequent links are Wuhan (WUH), Chengdu (TFU) and Shenyang (SHE).
