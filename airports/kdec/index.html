About KDEC

Decatur Airport (DEC) is a medium airport in Decatur, United States, with the ICAO code KDEC. Its 3 runways are 06/24, 12/30 and 18/36. The longest, 06/24, is 8,496 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 65 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 47 routes through KDEC. Its most frequent links are Joplin (JLN), Chicago (ORD) and Luton (LTN).
