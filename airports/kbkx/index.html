About KBKX

Brookings Regional Airport (BKX) is a small airport in Brookings, United States, with the ICAO code KBKX. Its two runways are 12/30 and 17/35. The longer one, 12/30, is 6,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBKX.
