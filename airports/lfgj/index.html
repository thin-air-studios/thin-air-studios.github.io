About LFGJ

Dole Tavaux Airport (DLE) is a medium airport in Dole, France, with the ICAO code LFGJ. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,318 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 54 routes through LFGJ. Its most frequent links are Porto (OPO), Marrakesh (RAK) and Saïss (FEZ).
