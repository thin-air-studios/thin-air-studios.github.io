About OOMA

RAFO Masirah (MSH) is a medium airport in Masirah, Oman, with the ICAO code OOMA. Its two runways are 17/35 and 07/25. The longer one, 17/35, is 10,005 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, OMS has 4 routes through OOMA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Muscat/Seeb (MCT).
