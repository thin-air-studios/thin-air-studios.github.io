About UMOO

Mogilev Airport (MVQ) is a medium airport in Mogilev, Belarus, with the ICAO code UMOO. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,422 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BRU has 5 routes through UMOO. Its most frequent links are Hurghada (HRG), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH) and Antalya (AYT).
