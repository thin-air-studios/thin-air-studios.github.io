About CYSB

Sudbury Airport (YSB) is a medium airport in Sudbury, Canada, with the ICAO code CYSB. Its two runways are 04/22 and 12/30. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,600 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 63 routes through CYSB. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Toronto (YTZ) and Ottawa (YOW).
