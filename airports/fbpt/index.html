About FBPT

Thebephatshwa Airport (FBPT) is a small airport in Botswana. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08/26 and 08L/26R. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,850 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 81 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FBPT.
