About FZQM

Kolwezi Airport (KWZ) is a medium airport in Kolwezi, Congo - Kinshasa, with the ICAO code FZQM. It has one runway, 11/29, 5,741 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FZQM.
