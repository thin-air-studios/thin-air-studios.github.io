About FYKM

Katima Mulilo Airport (MPA) is a medium airport in Mpacha, Namibia, with the ICAO code FYKM. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,520 ft long and 128 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WAA has 2 routes through FYKM. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Windhoek (ERS).
