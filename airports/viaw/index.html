About VIAW

Awantipura Air Force Station (VIAW) is a small airport in Awantipura, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,500 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VIAW.
