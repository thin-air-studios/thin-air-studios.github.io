About MHTG

Toncontín Airport (TGU) is a medium airport in Tegucigalpa, Honduras, with the ICAO code MHTG. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,112 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 38 routes through MHTG. Its most frequent links are Coxen Hole (RTB), Guatemala City (GUA) and San Pedro Sula (SAP).
