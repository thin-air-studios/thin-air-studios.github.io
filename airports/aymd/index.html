About AYMD

Madang Airport (MAG) is a medium airport in Madang, Papua New Guinea, with the ICAO code AYMD. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,174 ft long and 105 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Ryanair has 1 routes through AYMD. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Balice (KRK).
