About KFWS

Fort Worth Spinks Airport (KFWS) is a small airport in Fort Worth, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 18R/36L and 18L/36R. The longer one, 18R/36L, is 6,002 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFWS.
