About HDAM

Djibouti-Ambouli Airport (JIB) is a large airport in Djibouti City, Djibouti, with the ICAO code HDAM. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,335 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 119 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 48 routes through HDAM. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Mogadishu (MGQ) and Dubai (DXB).
