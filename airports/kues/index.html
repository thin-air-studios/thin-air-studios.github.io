About KUES

Waukesha County Airport (UES) is a small airport in Waukesha, United States, with the ICAO code KUES. Its two runways are 10/28 and 18/36. The longer one, 10/28, is 5,849 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KUES.
