About KGJT

Grand Junction Regional Airport (GJT) is a medium airport in Grand Junction, United States, with the ICAO code KGJT. Its two runways are 11/29 and 04/22. The longer one, 11/29, is 9,339 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 46 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 96 routes through KGJT. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Salt Lake City (SLC).
