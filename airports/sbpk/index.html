About SBPK

João Simões Lopes Neto International Airport (PET) is a medium airport in Pelotas, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBPK. It has one runway, 07/25, 6,496 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 18 routes through SBPK. Its most frequent links are Porto Alegre (POA), São Paulo (GRU) and Rio Grande (RIG).
