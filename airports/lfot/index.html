About LFOT

Tours Val de Loire Airport (TUF) is a medium airport in Tours, Indre-et-Loire, France, with the ICAO code LFOT. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,887 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 89 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 80 routes through LFOT. Its most frequent links are Porto (OPO), London (STN) and Marrakesh (RAK).
