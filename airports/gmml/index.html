About GMML

Laayoune Hassan I International Airport (EUN) is a large airport in El Aaiún, Western Sahara, with the ICAO code GMML. Its two runways are 02/20 and 04/22. The longer one, 02/20, is 8,861 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 27 routes through GMML. Its most frequent links are Casablanca (CMN), Gran Canaria Island (LPA) and Agadir (AGA).
