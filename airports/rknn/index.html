About RKNN

Gangneung Airport (K-18) (KAG) is a medium airport in Gangneung, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKNN. It has one runway, 08/26, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 153 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RKNN.
