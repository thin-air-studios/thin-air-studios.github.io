About YGEL

Geraldton Airport (GET) is a medium airport in Moonyoonooka, Australia, with the ICAO code YGEL. Its 3 runways are 03/21, 08/26 and 14/32. The longest, 03/21, is 7,838 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 25 routes through YGEL. Its most frequent links are Perth (PER), YBGD (YBGD) and Albany (ALH).
