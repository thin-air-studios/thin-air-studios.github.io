About FMMI

Ivato International Airport (TNR) is a large airport in Antananarivo, Madagascar, with the ICAO code FMMI. It has one runway, 11/29, 10,171 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 166 routes through FMMI. Its most frequent links are Nosy Be (NOS), Paris (CDG) and Sainte-Marie (RUN).
