About OICC

Shahid Ashrafi Esfahani Airport (KSH) is a medium airport in Kermanshah, Iran, with the ICAO code OICC. Its two runways are 11R/29L and 11L/29R. The longer one, 11R/29L, is 11,213 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 12 routes through OICC. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR) and Istanbul (ISL).
