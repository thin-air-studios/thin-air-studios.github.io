About KAPA

Centennial Airport (APA) is a medium airport in Denver, United States, with the ICAO code KAPA. Its 3 runways are 17L/35R, 17R/35L and 10/28. The longest, 17L/35R, is 10,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 213 taxiway segments, 30 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 27 routes through KAPA. Its most frequent links are Dallas (DAL), Las Vegas (LAS) and Scottsdale (SCF).
