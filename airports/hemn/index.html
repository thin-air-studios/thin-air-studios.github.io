About HEMN

El Minya Airport (EMY) is a small airport in El Minya, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEMN. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 9,842 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments and 11 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HEMN.
