About LIMZ

Cuneo International Airport (CUF) is a medium airport in Levaldigi (CN), Italy, with the ICAO code LIMZ. It has one runway, 03/21, 6,903 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 64 routes through LIMZ. Its most frequent links are Casablanca (CMN), Cagliari (CAG) and Palermo (PMO).
