About MTPP

Toussaint Louverture International Airport (PAP) is a large airport in Port-au-Prince, Haiti, with the ICAO code MTPP. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,974 ft long and 141 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 66 routes through MTPP. Its most frequent links are Pointe-à-Pitre (PTP), Miami (MIA) and Managua (MGA).
