About HADR

Aba Tenna Dejazmach Yilma International Airport (DIR) is a large airport in Dire Dawa, Ethiopia, with the ICAO code HADR. It has one runway, 15/33, 8,791 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Ethiopian has 22 routes through HADR. Its most frequent links are Addis Ababa (ADD), Djibouti City (JIB) and Jijiga (JIJ).
