About YMRY

Moruya Airport (MYA) is a medium airport in Moruya, Australia, with the ICAO code YMRY. Its two runways are 18/36 and 04/22. The longer one, 18/36, is 4,997 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Rex has 23 routes through YMRY. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD) and Merimbula (MIM).
