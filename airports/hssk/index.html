About HSSK

Khartoum International Airport (KRT) is a large airport in Khartoum, Sudan, with the ICAO code HSSK. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,751 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HSSK.
