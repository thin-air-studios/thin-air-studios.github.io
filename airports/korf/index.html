About KORF

Norfolk International Airport (ORF) is a large airport in Norfolk, United States, with the ICAO code KORF. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 9,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 479 routes through KORF. Its most frequent links are Newark (EWR), Charlotte (CLT) and Philadelphia (PHL).
