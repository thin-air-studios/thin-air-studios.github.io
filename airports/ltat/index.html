About LTAT

Malatya Erhaç Airport (MLX) is a medium airport in Malatya, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAT. It has one runway, 03/21, 10,990 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 121 routes through LTAT. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Gaziemir (ADB).
