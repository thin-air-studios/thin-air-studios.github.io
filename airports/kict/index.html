About KICT

Wichita Dwight D. Eisenhower National Airport (ICT) is a medium airport in Wichita, United States, with the ICAO code KICT. Its 3 runways are 01L/19R, 01R/19L and 14/32. The longest, 01L/19R, is 10,302 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 180 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 215 routes through KICT. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN) and Houston (IAH).
