About KSHR

Sheridan County Airport (SHR) is a medium airport in Sheridan, United States, with the ICAO code KSHR. Its two runways are 15/33 and 06/24. The longer one, 15/33, is 8,301 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SkyWest has 18 routes through KSHR. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Denver (DEN).
