About KPWT

Bremerton National Airport (PWT) is a medium airport in Bremerton, United States, with the ICAO code KPWT. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPWT.
