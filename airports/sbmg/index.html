About SBMG

Regional de Maringá - Sílvio Name Júnior Airport (MGF) is a medium airport in Maringá, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBMG. It has one runway, 10/28, 7,783 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 34 taxiway segments and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 48 routes through SBMG. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Curitiba (CWB) and Campinas (VCP).
