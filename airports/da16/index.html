About DA16

Tindouf East Airport (DA16) is a small airport in Tindouf, Algeria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 09L/27R, 09R/27L and 09/27. The longest, 09L/27R, is 12,700 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DA16.
