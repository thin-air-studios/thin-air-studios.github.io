About NFFN

Nadi International Airport (NAN) is a large airport in Nadi, Fiji, with the ICAO code NFFN. Its two runways are 02/20 and 09/27. The longer one, 02/20, is 10,739 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 234 routes through NFFN. Its most frequent links are Auckland (AKL), Sydney (SYD) and Brisbane (BNE).
