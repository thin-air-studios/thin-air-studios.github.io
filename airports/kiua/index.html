About KIUA

Canandaigua Airport (IUA) is a small airport in Ontario County IDA, United States, with the ICAO code KIUA. It has one runway, 13/31, 5,500 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KIUA.
