About WADL

Lombok International Airport (LOP) is a large airport in Mataram (Pujut, Lombok Tengah), Indonesia, with the ICAO code WADL. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,826 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 42 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 130 routes through WADL. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Kuta (DPS) and Surabaya (SUB).
