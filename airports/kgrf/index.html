About KGRF

Gray Army Air Field (GRF) is a medium airport in Joint Base Lewis McChord, United States, with the ICAO code KGRF. It has one runway, 15/33, 6,124 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGRF.
