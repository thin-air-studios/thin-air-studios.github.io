About ULAA

Talagi Airport (ARH) is a medium airport in Archangelsk, Russia, with the ICAO code ULAA. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,202 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 76 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 99 routes through ULAA. Its most frequent links are St. Petersburg (LED), Moscow (SVO) and Antalya (AYT).
