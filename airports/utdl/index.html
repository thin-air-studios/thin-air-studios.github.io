About UTDL

Khujand International Airport (LBD) is a large airport in Khujand, Tajikistan, with the ICAO code UTDL. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,433 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 93 routes through UTDL. Its most frequent links are Novosibirsk (OVB), Moscow (DME) and Moscow (ZIA).
