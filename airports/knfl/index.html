About KNFL

Fallon Naval Air Station (NFL) is a medium airport in Fallon, United States, with the ICAO code KNFL. Its 3 runways are 13R/31L, 13L/31R and 07/25. The longest, 13R/31L, is 14,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 103 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNFL.
