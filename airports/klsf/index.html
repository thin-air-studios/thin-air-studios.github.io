About KLSF

Lawson Army Air Field (LSF) is a medium airport in Fort Benning, United States, with the ICAO code KLSF. It has one runway, 15/33, 10,002 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 105 taxiway segments and 42 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Atlas has 2 routes through KLSF. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (HHN) and Baltimore (BWI).
