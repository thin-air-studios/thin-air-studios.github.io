About WMKK

Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL) is a large airport in Sepang, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WMKK. It is number 20 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 63.4 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 14L/32R, 14R/32L and 15/33. The longest, 14L/32R, is 13,530 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 134 gates, 444 taxiway segments, 42 aprons and 14 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 127 airlines have 3,512 routes through WMKK. Its most frequent links are Singapore (SIN), Kota Kinabalu (BKI) and Kuching (KCH).
