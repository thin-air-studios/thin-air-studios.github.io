About VGSY

Osmany International Airport (ZYL) is a large airport in Sylhet, Bangladesh, with the ICAO code VGSY. It has one runway, 11/29, 9,478 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 53 routes through VGSY. Its most frequent links are Dhaka (DAC), London (LHR) and Dubai (DXB).
