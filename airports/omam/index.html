About OMAM

Al Dhafra Air Base (DHF) is a medium airport in United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMAM. Its two runways are 13L/31R and 13R/31L. The longer one, 13L/31R, is 12,012 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 307 taxiway segments, 48 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OMAM.
