About YARM

Armidale Airport (ARM) is a medium airport in Armidale, Australia, with the ICAO code YARM. Its two runways are 05/23 and 09/27. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,702 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 50 routes through YARM. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD) and Brisbane (BNE).
