About LFRQ

Quimper-Cornouaille Airport (UIP) is a medium airport in Quimper/Pluguffan, France, with the ICAO code LFRQ. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,054 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 33 routes through LFRQ. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY) and London (LCY).
