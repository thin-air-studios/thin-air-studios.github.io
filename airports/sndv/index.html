About SNDV

Brigadeiro Cabral Airport (DIQ) is a small airport in Divinópolis, Brazil, with the ICAO code SNDV. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,052 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, ACN has 5 routes through SNDV. Its most frequent links are Belo Horizonte (CNF) and Campinas (VCP).
