About CYQH

Watson Lake Airport (YQH) is a medium airport in Watson Lake, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQH. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,504 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, ANT has 2 routes through CYQH. Its most frequent links are Prince George (YXS) and Whitehorse (YXY).
