About GUCY

Ahmed Sékou Touré International Airport (CKY) is a large airport in Conakry, Guinea, with the ICAO code GUCY. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,826 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 78 routes through GUCY. Its most frequent links are Dakar (DSS), Banjul (BJL) and Paris (CDG).
