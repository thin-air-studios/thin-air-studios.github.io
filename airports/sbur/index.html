About SBUR

Mário de Almeida Franco Airport (UBA) is a medium airport in Uberaba, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBUR. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,771 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 15 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 19 routes through SBUR. Its most frequent links are Belo Horizonte (CNF), Araxá (AAX) and Belo Horizonte (PLU).
