About OIAJ

Omidiyeh Airport (OMI) is a small airport in Omidiyeh, Iran, with the ICAO code OIAJ. Its two runways are 12R/30L and 12L/30R. The longer one, 12R/30L, is 13,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, GWI has 1 routes through OIAJ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Köln (CGN).
