About KDDC

Dodge City Regional Airport (DDC) is a medium airport in Dodge City, United States, with the ICAO code KDDC. Its two runways are 14/32 and 02/20. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,899 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SkyWest has 10 routes through KDDC. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Denver (DEN).
