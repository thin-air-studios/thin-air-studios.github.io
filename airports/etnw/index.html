About ETNW

Wunstorf Air Base (ETNW) is a medium airport in Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 02/20 and 08R/26L. The longest, 08/26, is 8,199 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETNW.
