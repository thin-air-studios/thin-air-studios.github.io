About EPMO

Warsaw Modlin Airport (WMI) is a large airport in Nowy Dwór Mazowiecki, Poland, with the ICAO code EPMO. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,202 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 24 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 1,031 routes through EPMO. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Dublin (DUB) and Orio al Serio (BGY).
