About KTKX

Kennett Memorial Airport (KNT) is a small airport in Kennett, United States, with the ICAO code KTKX. Its two runways are 02/20 and 18/36. The longer one, 02/20, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KTKX.
