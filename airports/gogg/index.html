About GOGG

Ziguinchor Airport (ZIG) is a medium airport in Ziguinchor, Senegal, with the ICAO code GOGG. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,069 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SZN has 4 routes through GOGG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Dakar (DSS).
