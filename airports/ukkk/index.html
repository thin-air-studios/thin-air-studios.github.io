About UKKK

Igor Sikorsky Kyiv International Airport (Zhuliany) (IEV) is a medium airport in Kyiv, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKKK. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,579 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 106 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 313 routes through UKKK. Its most frequent links are Sharm El Sheikh (SSH), Burgas (BOJ) and Antalya (AYT).
