About KUTA

Tunica Municipal Airport (UTM) is a small airport in Tunica, United States, with the ICAO code KUTA. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,500 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Republic has 1 routes through KUTA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Islip (ISP).
