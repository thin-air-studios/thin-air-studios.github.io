About KEYF

Curtis L Brown Jr Field (KEYF) is a small airport in Elizabethtown, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 5,006 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KEYF.
