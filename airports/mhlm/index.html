About MHLM

Ramón Villeda Morales International Airport (SAP) is a large airport in San Pedro Sula, Honduras, with the ICAO code MHLM. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,203 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 111 routes through MHLM. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), San Salvador (SAL) and Guatemala City (GUA).
