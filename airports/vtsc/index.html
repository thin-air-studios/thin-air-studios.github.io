About VTSC

Narathiwat Airport (NAW) is a medium airport in Thailand, with the ICAO code VTSC. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 7 routes through VTSC. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (DMK) and Bangkok (BKK).
