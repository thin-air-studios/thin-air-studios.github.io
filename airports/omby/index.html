About OMBY

Sir Bani Yas Airport (XSB) is a medium airport in Sir Bani Yas, United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMBY. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,272 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OMBY.
