About SULS

Capitan Corbeta CA Curbelo International Airport (PDP) is a medium airport in Punta del Este, Uruguay, with the ICAO code SULS. Its two runways are 08/26 and 01/19. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,998 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 43 routes through SULS. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP), São Paulo (GRU) and Campinas (VCP).
