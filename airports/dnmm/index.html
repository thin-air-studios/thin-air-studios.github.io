About DNMM

Murtala Muhammed International Airport (LOS) is a large airport in Lagos, Nigeria, with the ICAO code DNMM. Its two runways are 18R/36L and 18L/36R. The longer one, 18R/36L, is 12,794 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 129 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 12 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 59 airlines have 417 routes through DNMM. Its most frequent links are Abuja (ABV), Doha (DOH) and Accra (ACC).
