About UKON

Mykolaiv International Airport [CLOSED] (NLV) is a medium airport in Mykolaiv, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKON. Its two runways are 05/23 and 05L/23R. The longer one, 05/23, is 8,438 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 138 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines.
