About LPMA

Cristiano Ronaldo International Airport (FNC) is a large airport in Funchal, Portugal, with the ICAO code LPMA. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,110 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 78 airlines have 1,710 routes through LPMA. Its most frequent links are Lisbon (LIS), Porto (OPO) and London (LGW).
