About RCTP

Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport (TPE) is a large airport in Taoyuan, Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCTP. It is number 48 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 47.8 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 05L/23R and 05R/23L. The longer one, 05L/23R, is 12,008 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 gates, 231 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 103 airlines have 2,459 routes through RCTP. Its most frequent links are Hong Kong (HKG), Narita (NRT) and Anchorage (ANC).
