About EKSP

Skrydstrup Air Base (SKS) is a medium airport in Vojens, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKSP. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 9,863 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 274 taxiway segments, 70 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AZG has 2 routes through EKSP. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Baku (GYD).
