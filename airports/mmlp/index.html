About MMLP

Manuel Márquez de León International Airport (LAP) is a medium airport in La Paz, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMLP. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 71 routes through MMLP. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Guadalajara (GDL) and Tijuana (TIJ).
