About KEWB

New Bedford Regional Airport (EWB) is a medium airport in New Bedford, United States, with the ICAO code KEWB. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Cape Air has 3 routes through KEWB. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS) and Nantucket (ACK).
