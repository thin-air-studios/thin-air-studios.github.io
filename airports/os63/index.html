About OS63

Marj Ruhayyil Air Base (OS63) is a small airport in Marj Ruhayyil, Syria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06L/24R, is 9,820 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OS63.
