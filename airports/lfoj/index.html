About LFOJ

Orléans-Bricy (BA 123) Air Base (LFOJ) is a medium airport in Boulay-les-Barres, Loiret, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,887 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 243 taxiway segments and 40 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFOJ.
