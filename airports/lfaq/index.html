About LFAQ

Albert Bray airport (BYF) is a small airport in Albert, France, with the ICAO code LFAQ. Its two runways are 08/26 and 08R/26L. The longer one, 08/26, is 7,218 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 21 routes through LFAQ. Its most frequent links are Saint-Nazaire/Montoir (SNR), Augsburg (AGB) and Toulouse/Blagnac (TLS).
