About K1G4

Grand Canyon West Airport (GCW) is a small airport in Peach Springs, United States, with the ICAO code K1G4. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,000 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 31 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for K1G4.
