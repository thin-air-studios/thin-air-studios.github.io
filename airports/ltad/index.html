About LTAD

Etimesgut Air Base (ANK) is a medium airport in Ankara, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAD. It has one runway, 11L/29R, 9,045 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SXD has 1 routes through LTAD. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Düsseldorf (DUS).
