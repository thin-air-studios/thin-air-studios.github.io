About KITH

Ithaca Tompkins Regional Airport (ITH) is a medium airport in Ithaca, United States, with the ICAO code KITH. Its two runways are 14/32 and 15/33. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,977 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 12 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 37 routes through KITH. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Dulles (IAD) and Detroit (DTW).
