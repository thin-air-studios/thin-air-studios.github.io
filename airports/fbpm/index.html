About FBPM

Phillip Gaonwe Matante International Airport (FRW) is a large airport in Francistown, Botswana, with the ICAO code FBPM. Its two runways are 13/31 and 11/29. The longer one, 13/31, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FBPM.
