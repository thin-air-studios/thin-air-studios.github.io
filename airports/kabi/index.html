About KABI

Abilene Regional Airport (ABI) is a medium airport in Abilene, United States, with the ICAO code KABI. Its 3 runways are 17R/35L, 17L/35R and 04/22. The longest, 17R/35L, is 7,208 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 36 routes through KABI. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Lubbock (LBB).
