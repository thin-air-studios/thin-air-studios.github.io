About UHMA

Ugolny Yuri Ryktheu Airport (DYR) is a medium airport in Anadyr, Russia, with the ICAO code UHMA. It has one runway, 02/20, 11,483 ft long and 196 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 14 routes through UHMA. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (VKO) and Khabarovsk (KHV).
