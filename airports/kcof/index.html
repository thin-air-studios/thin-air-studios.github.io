About KCOF

Patrick Space Force Base (COF) is a medium airport in Cocoa Beach, United States, with the ICAO code KCOF. Its two runways are 03/21 and 11/29. The longer one, 03/21, is 9,008 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through KCOF. Its most frequent links are Osbourn (ANU), Schkeuditz (LEJ) and Wilmington (ILN).
