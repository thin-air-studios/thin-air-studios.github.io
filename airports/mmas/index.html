About MMAS

Aguascalientes International Airport (AGU) is a large airport in Aguascalientes, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMAS. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 60 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 101 routes through MMAS. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Houston (IAH) and Tijuana (TIJ).
