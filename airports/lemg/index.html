About LEMG

Málaga-Costa del Sol Airport (AGP) is a large airport in Málaga, Spain, with the ICAO code LEMG. Its two runways are 13/31 and 12/30. The longer one, 13/31, is 10,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 284 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 112 airlines have 7,374 routes through LEMG. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Amsterdam (AMS) and Manchester (MAN).
