About EDHI

Hamburg-Finkenwerder Airport (XFW) is a medium airport in Hamburg, Germany, with the ICAO code EDHI. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,629 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 305 routes through EDHI. Its most frequent links are Toulouse/Blagnac (TLS), Saint-Nazaire/Montoir (SNR) and Broughton (CEG).
