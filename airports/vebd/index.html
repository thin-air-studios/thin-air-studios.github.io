About VEBD

Bagdogra Airport (IXB) is a large airport in Siliguri, India, with the ICAO code VEBD. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,035 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 203 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 236 routes through VEBD. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Kolkata (CCU) and Bengaluru (BLR).
