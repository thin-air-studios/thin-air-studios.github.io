About KDEW

Deer Park Airport (KDEW) is a small airport in Deer Park, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 16/34 and 04/22. The longer one, 16/34, is 6,101 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDEW.
