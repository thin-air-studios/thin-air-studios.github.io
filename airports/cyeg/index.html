About CYEG

Edmonton International Airport (YEG) is a large airport in Edmonton, Canada, with the ICAO code CYEG. Its two runways are 02/20 and 12/30. The longer one, 02/20, is 11,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 52 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 1,001 routes through CYEG. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC), Vancouver (YVR) and Toronto (YYZ).
