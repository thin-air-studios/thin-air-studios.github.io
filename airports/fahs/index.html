About FAHS

Eastgate Airport / Air Force Base Hoedspruit (HDS) is a medium airport in Hoedspruit, South Africa, with the ICAO code FAHS. Its two runways are 18/36 and 09/27. The longer one, 18/36, is 13,094 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 271 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 24 routes through FAHS. Its most frequent links are Cape Town (CPT) and Johannesburg (JNB).
