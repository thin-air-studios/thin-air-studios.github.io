About LUKK

Chişinău International Airport (RMO) is a large airport in Chişinău, Moldova, with the ICAO code LUKK. Its two runways are 08/26 and 09/27. The longer one, 08/26, is 11,778 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 180 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 51 airlines have 1,069 routes through LUKK. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Luton (LTN) and Tel Aviv (TLV).
