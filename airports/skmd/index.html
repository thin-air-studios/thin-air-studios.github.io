About SKMD

Enrique Olaya Herrera Airport (EOH) is a medium airport in Medellín, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKMD. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,202 ft long and 125 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 177 routes through SKMD. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Quibdó (UIB) and Santiago de Tolú (TLU).
