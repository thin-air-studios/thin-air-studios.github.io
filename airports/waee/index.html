About WAEE

Sultan Babullah Airport (TTE) is a medium airport in Ternate, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAEE. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,875 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 27 routes through WAEE. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Makassar (UPG) and Surabaya (SUB).
