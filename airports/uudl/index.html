About UUDL

Golden Ring Yaroslavl International Airport (IAR) is a large airport in Tunoshna, Russia, with the ICAO code UUDL. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,870 ft long and 135 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 15 routes through UUDL. Its most frequent links are St. Petersburg (LED), Krasnodar (KRR) and Mineralnyye Vody (MRV).
