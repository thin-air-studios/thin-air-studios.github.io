About FOOL

Libreville Leon M'ba International Airport (LBV) is a large airport in Libreville, Gabon, with the ICAO code FOOL. It has one runway, 16/34, 9,844 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 91 routes through FOOL. Its most frequent links are Douala (DLA), Cotonou (COO) and Paris (CDG).
