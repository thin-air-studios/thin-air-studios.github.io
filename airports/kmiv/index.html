About KMIV

Millville Municipal Airport (MIV) is a medium airport in Millville, United States, with the ICAO code KMIV. Its two runways are 10/28 and 14/32. The longer one, 10/28, is 6,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 40 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMIV.
