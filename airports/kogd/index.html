About KOGD

Ogden Hinckley Airport (OGD) is a medium airport in Ogden, United States, with the ICAO code KOGD. Its two runways are 03/21 and 17/35. The longer one, 03/21, is 8,107 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 92 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Breeze has 3 routes through KOGD. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Santa Ana (SNA).
