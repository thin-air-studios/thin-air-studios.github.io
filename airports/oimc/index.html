About OIMC

Sarakhs Airport (CKT) is a medium airport in Sarakhs, Iran, with the ICAO code OIMC. It has one runway, 14/32, 13,076 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OIMC.
