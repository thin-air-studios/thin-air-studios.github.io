About DTTB

Bizerte Sidi Ahmed Air Base (DTTB) is a medium airport in Borj Challouf, Tunisia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08/26 and 11/29. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,845 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DTTB.
