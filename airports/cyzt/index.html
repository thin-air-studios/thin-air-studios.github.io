About CYZT

Port Hardy Airport (YZT) is a medium airport in Port Hardy, Canada, with the ICAO code CYZT. Its 3 runways are 11/29, 08/26 and 16/34. The longest, 11/29, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYZT.
