About OIKK

Ayatollah Hashemi Rafsanjani International Airport (KER) is a large airport in Kerman, Iran, with the ICAO code OIKK. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 12,635 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 69 taxiway segments, 22 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 22 routes through OIKK. Its most frequent links are Tehran (THR), Dubai (DXB) and Mashhad (MHD).
