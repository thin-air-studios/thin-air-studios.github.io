About EEKE

Kuressaare Airport (URE) is a medium airport in Kuressaare, Estonia, with the ICAO code EEKE. Its two runways are 17/35 and 05/23. The longer one, 17/35, is 6,561 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 14 routes through EEKE. Its most frequent links are Tallinn (TLL), Helsinki (HEL) and Jyväskylän Maalaiskunta (JYV).
