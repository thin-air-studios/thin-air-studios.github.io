About LFBT

Tarbes-Lourdes-Pyrénées Airport (LDE) is a medium airport in Tarbes/Lourdes/Pyrénées, France, with the ICAO code LFBT. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 37 airlines have 296 routes through LFBT. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), London (STN) and Dublin (DUB).
