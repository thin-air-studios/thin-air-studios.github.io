About KIKK

Greater Kankakee Airport (IKK) is a medium airport in Kankakee, United States, with the ICAO code KIKK. Its two runways are 04/22 and 16/34. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,981 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KIKK.
