About KMHR

Sacramento Mather Airport (MHR) is a medium airport in Sacramento, United States, with the ICAO code KMHR. Its two runways are 04R/22L and 04L/22R. The longer one, 04R/22L, is 11,301 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 62 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 87 routes through KMHR. Its most frequent links are Louisville (SDF), Ontario (ONT) and San Jose (SJC).
