About EGFD

Llanbedr Airfield (EGFD) is a small airport in Llanbedr, United Kingdom. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 17/35, 05/23 and 15/33. The longest, 17/35, is 7,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EGFD.
