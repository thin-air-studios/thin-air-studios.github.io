About SPLO

General Jorge Fernandez Maldon Airport (ILQ) is a medium airport in Ilo, Peru, with the ICAO code SPLO. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through SPLO. Its most frequent links are Lima (LIM) and Arequipa (AQP).
