About YBMK

Mackay Airport (MKY) is a medium airport in Mackay, Australia, with the ICAO code YBMK. It has one runway, 14/32, 6,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 43 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 140 routes through YBMK. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Townsville (TSV) and Melbourne (MEL).
