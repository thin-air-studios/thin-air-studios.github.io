About KNGT

Naval Outlying Landing Field Goliad (KNGT) is a small airport in Goliad, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 11/29 and 17/35. The longer one, 11/29, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNGT.
