About KDTL

Detroit Lakes Airport (DTL) is a small airport in Detroit Lakes, United States, with the ICAO code KDTL. Its two runways are 14/32 and 17/35. The longer one, 14/32, is 5,201 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDTL.
