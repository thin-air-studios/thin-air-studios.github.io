About MMGL

Guadalajara International Airport (GDL) is a large airport in Guadalajara, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMGL. Its two runways are 11R/29L and 11L/29R. The longer one, 11R/29L, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 199 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 45 airlines have 1,463 routes through MMGL. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Tijuana (TIJ) and Los Angeles (LAX).
