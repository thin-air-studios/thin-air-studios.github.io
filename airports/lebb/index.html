About LEBB

Bilbao Airport (BIO) is a large airport in Bilbao, Spain, with the ICAO code LEBB. Its two runways are 12/30 and 10/28. The longer one, 12/30, is 8,530 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 42 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 67 airlines have 2,062 routes through LEBB. Its most frequent links are Barcelona (BCN), Madrid (MAD) and Seville (SVQ).
