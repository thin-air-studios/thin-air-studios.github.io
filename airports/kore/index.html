About KORE

Orange Municipal Airport (KORE) is a small airport in Orange, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 01/19 and 14/32. The longer one, 01/19, is 5,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KORE.
