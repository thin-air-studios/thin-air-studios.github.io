About NZAA

Auckland International Airport (AKL) is a large airport in Auckland, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZAA. It has one runway, 05R/23L, 11,926 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 gates, 144 taxiway segments, 63 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 59 airlines have 2,722 routes through NZAA. Its most frequent links are Christchurch (CHC), Sydney (SYD) and Wellington (WLG).
