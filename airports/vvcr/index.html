About VVCR

Cam Ranh International Airport / Cam Ranh Air Base (CXR) is a large airport in Nha Trang/nha Trang aiurportCam Ranh, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVCR. Its two runways are 02L/20R and 02R/20L. The longer one, 02L/20R, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 40 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 46 airlines have 569 routes through VVCR. Its most frequent links are Hanoi (HAN), Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) and Seoul (ICN).
