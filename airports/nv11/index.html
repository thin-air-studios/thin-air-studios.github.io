About NV11

Yucca Airstrip (UCC) is a small airport in Mercury, United States, with the ICAO code NV11. Its two runways are 14/32 and 1/19. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for NV11.
