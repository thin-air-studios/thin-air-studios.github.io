About RCAY

Gangshan Air Force Base (RCAY) is a medium airport in Kaohsiung (Gangshan), Taiwan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 18R/36L and 18L/36R. The longer one, 18R/36L, is 8,019 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RCAY.
