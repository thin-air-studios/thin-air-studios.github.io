About ZUXC

Xichang Qingshan Airport (XIC) is a medium airport in Liangshan (Xichang), China, with the ICAO code ZUXC. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,811 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 70 routes through ZUXC. Its most frequent links are Chengdu (CTU), Ningbo (NGB) and Chongqing (CKG).
