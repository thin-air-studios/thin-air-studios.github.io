About EGSS

London Stansted Airport (STN) is a large airport in London, Essex, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGSS. It has one runway, 04/22, 10,003 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 gates, 142 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 145 airlines have 10,502 routes through EGSS. Its most frequent links are Ingliston (EDI), Belfast (BFS) and Dublin (DUB).
