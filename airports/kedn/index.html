About KEDN

Enterprise Municipal Airport (ETS) is a small airport in Enterprise, United States, with the ICAO code KEDN. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,080 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KEDN.
