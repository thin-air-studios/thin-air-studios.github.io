About VVDN

Da Nang International Airport (DAD) is a large airport in Da Nang, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVDN. Its two runways are 17L/35R and 17R/35L. The longer one, 17L/35R, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 102 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 48 airlines have 872 routes through VVDN. Its most frequent links are Ho Chi Minh City (SGN), Hanoi (HAN) and Seoul (ICN).
