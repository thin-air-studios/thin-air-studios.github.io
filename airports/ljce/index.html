About LJCE

Cerklje ob Krki Air Base (LJCE) is a medium airport in Cerklje ob Krki, Slovenia. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 101 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LJCE.
