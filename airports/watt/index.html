About WATT

El Tari Airport (KOE) is a medium airport in Kupang, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WATT. Its two runways are 07/25 and 12/30. The longer one, 07/25, is 8,202 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 40 routes through WATT. Its most frequent links are Surabaya (SUB), Jakarta (CGK) and Kuta (DPS).
