About YWSL

West Sale Airport (SXE) is a medium airport in Sale, Australia, with the ICAO code YWSL. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 14/32 and 05/23. The longest, 09/27, is 5,010 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YWSL.
