About KCVO

Corvallis Municipal Airport (CVO) is a medium airport in Corvallis, United States, with the ICAO code KCVO. Its two runways are 17/35 and 10/28. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,900 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through KCVO. Its most frequent links are Portland (PDX), Crescent City (CEC) and Newport (ONP).
