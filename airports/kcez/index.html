About KCEZ

Cortez Municipal Airport (CEZ) is a medium airport in Cortez, United States, with the ICAO code KCEZ. It has one runway, 03/21, 7,205 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, LYM has 10 routes through KCEZ. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN) and Phoenix (PHX).
