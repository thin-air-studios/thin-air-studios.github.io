About FCBB

Maya-Maya International Airport (BZV) is a large airport in Brazzaville, Congo - Brazzaville, with the ICAO code FCBB. It has one runway, 05/23, 10,827 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 37 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 51 routes through FCBB. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Kinshasa (FIH) and Pointe Noire (PNR).
