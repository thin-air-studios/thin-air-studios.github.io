About FAGR

Graaff Reinet Airport (FAGR) is a small airport in Graaff-Reinet, South Africa. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 02/20 and 15/33. The longer one, 02/20, is 4,921 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FAGR.
