About EIKN

Ireland West Airport Knock (NOC) is a large airport in Charlestown, Ireland, with the ICAO code EIKN. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,546 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 324 routes through EIKN. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Luton (LTN) and Liverpool (LPL).
