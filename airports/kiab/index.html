About KIAB

McConnell Air Force Base (IAB) is a medium airport in Wichita, United States, with the ICAO code KIAB. Its two runways are 01R/19L and 01L/19R. The longer one, 01R/19L, is 12,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 134 taxiway segments and 13 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 8 routes through KIAB. Its most frequent links are Everett (PAE), Charleston (CHS) and New York (JFK).
