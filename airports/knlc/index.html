About KNLC

Lemoore Naval Air Station (Reeves Field) Airport (NLC) is a medium airport in Lemoore, United States, with the ICAO code KNLC. Its two runways are 14L/32R and 14R/32L. The longer one, 14L/32R, is 13,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 196 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNLC.
