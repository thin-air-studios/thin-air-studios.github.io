About UIUU

Baikal International Airport (UUD) is a large airport in Ulan Ude, Russia, with the ICAO code UIUU. It has one runway, 08/26, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 93 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 93 routes through UIUU. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Novosibirsk (OVB) and Krasnoyarsk (KJA).
