About ENTO

Sandefjord Airport, Torp (TRF) is a large airport in Sandefjord(Torp), Norway, with the ICAO code ENTO. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,216 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 65 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 776 routes through ENTO. Its most frequent links are Bergen (BGO), Trondheim (TRD) and Stavanger (SVG).
