About DRRN

Diori Hamani International Airport (NIM) is a large airport in Niamey, Niger, with the ICAO code DRRN. Its two runways are 09L/27R and 09R/27L. The longer one, 09L/27R, is 10,499 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 48 routes through DRRN. Its most frequent links are Ouagadougou (OUA), Paris (CDG) and Istanbul (IST).
