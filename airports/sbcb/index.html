About SBCB

Cabo Frio Airport (CFB) is a small airport in Cabo Frio, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBCB. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,366 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 24 routes through SBCB. Its most frequent links are Belo Horizonte (CNF), Belo Horizonte (PLU) and Manaus (MAO).
