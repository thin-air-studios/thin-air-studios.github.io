About MNCE

Costa Esmeralda Airport (ECI) is a small airport in Tola, Nicaragua, with the ICAO code MNCE. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,003 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MNCE.
