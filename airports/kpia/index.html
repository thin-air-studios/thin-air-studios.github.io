About KPIA

General Wayne A. Downing Peoria International Airport (PIA) is a medium airport in Peoria, United States, with the ICAO code KPIA. Its two runways are 13/31 and 04/22. The longer one, 13/31, is 10,104 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 83 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 142 routes through KPIA. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Mesa (AZA) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
