About KMPO

Pocono Mountains Municipal Airport (MPO) is a small airport in Mount Pocono, United States, with the ICAO code KMPO. Its two runways are 13/31 and 05/23. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMPO.
