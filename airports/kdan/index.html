About KDAN

Danville Regional Airport (DAN) is a medium airport in Danville, United States, with the ICAO code KDAN. Its two runways are 02/20 and 13/31. The longer one, 02/20, is 5,900 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDAN.
