About MMVA

Carlos Rovirosa Pérez International Airport (VSA) is a large airport in Villahermosa, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMVA. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 193 routes through MMVA. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Cancún (CUN) and Monterrey (MTY).
