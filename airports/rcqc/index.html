About RCQC

Penghu Magong Airport (MZG) is a large airport in Huxi, Taiwan, with the ICAO code RCQC. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 279 routes through RCQC. Its most frequent links are Taipei (TSA), Kaohsiung (KHH) and Taichung (RMQ).
