About KULS

Ulysses Airport (KULS) is a small airport in Ulysses, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 17/35 and 12/30. The longer one, 17/35, is 6,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KULS.
