About KHON

Huron Regional Airport (HON) is a medium airport in Huron, United States, with the ICAO code KHON. Its two runways are 12/30 and 18/36. The longer one, 12/30, is 7,201 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, CCQ has 1 routes through KHON. Its most frequent links are Sioux Falls (FSD) and Pierre (PIR).
