About SBFL

Hercílio Luz International Airport (FLN) is a large airport in Florianópolis, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBFL. Its two runways are 14/32 and 03/21. The longer one, 14/32, is 7,874 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 35 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 24 airlines have 465 routes through SBFL. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), São Paulo (CGH) and Santiago (SCL).
