About PAGA

Edward G. Pitka Sr Airport (GAL) is a medium airport in Galena, United States, with the ICAO code PAGA. Its two runways are 08/26 and 06/24. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for PAGA.
