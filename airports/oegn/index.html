About OEGN

Jizan Regional Airport / King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz Airport (GIZ) is a medium airport in Jizan, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEGN. It has one runway, 15/33, 10,006 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 98 routes through OEGN. Its most frequent links are Jeddah (JED), Riyadh (RUH) and Ad Dammam (DMM).
