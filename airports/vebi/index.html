About VEBI

Shillong Airport (SHL) is a medium airport in Shillong, India, with the ICAO code VEBI. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 16 routes through VEBI. Its most frequent links are Kolkata (CCU), Aizawl (VEAZ) and Imphal (IMF).
