About EKEB

Esbjerg Airport (EBJ) is a medium airport in Esbjerg, Denmark, with the ICAO code EKEB. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,527 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 34 routes through EKEB. Its most frequent links are Stavanger (SVG), Aberdeen (ABZ) and Grimsby (HUY).
