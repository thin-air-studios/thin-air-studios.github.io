About LERJ

Logroño-Agoncillo Airport (RJL) is a medium airport in Logroño, Spain, with the ICAO code LERJ. It has one runway, 11/29, 7,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 17 routes through LERJ. Its most frequent links are Madrid (MAD), Gran Canaria Island (LPA) and Barcelona (BCN).
