About KABR

Aberdeen Regional Airport (ABR) is a medium airport in Aberdeen, United States, with the ICAO code KABR. Its two runways are 13/31 and 17/35. The longer one, 13/31, is 6,901 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 20 routes through KABR. Its most frequent links are Minneapolis (MSP), Sioux Falls (FSD) and Mobridge (MBG).
