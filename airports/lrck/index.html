About LRCK

Mihail Kogălniceanu International Airport (CND) is a large airport in Constanța, Romania, with the ICAO code LRCK. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 120 routes through LRCK. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Istanbul (IST) and Strasbourg (SXB).
