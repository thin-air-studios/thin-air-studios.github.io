About KMTN

Martin State Airport (MTN) is a medium airport in Baltimore, United States, with the ICAO code KMTN. It has one runway, 15/33, 6,999 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMTN.
