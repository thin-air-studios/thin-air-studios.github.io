About YBMA

Mount Isa Airport (ISA) is a medium airport in Mount Isa, Australia, with the ICAO code YBMA. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,399 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 gates, 8 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 39 routes through YBMA. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Townsville (TSV) and Cairns (CNS).
