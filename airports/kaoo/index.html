About KAOO

Altoona Blair County Airport (AOO) is a medium airport in Altoona, United States, with the ICAO code KAOO. Its two runways are 03/21 and 12/30. The longer one, 03/21, is 5,465 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 18 routes through KAOO. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Johnstown (JST) and Philadelphia (PHL).
