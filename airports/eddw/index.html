About EDDW

Bremen Airport (BRE) is a large airport in Bremen, Germany, with the ICAO code EDDW. Its two runways are 09/27 and 05/23. The longer one, 09/27, is 8,642 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 56 airlines have 1,067 routes through EDDW. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Munich (MUC).
