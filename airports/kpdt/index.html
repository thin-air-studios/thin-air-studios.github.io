About KPDT

Eastern Oregon Regional Airport at Pendleton (PDT) is a medium airport in Pendleton, United States, with the ICAO code KPDT. Its two runways are 08/26 and 11/29. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,301 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Boutique has 7 routes through KPDT. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Portland (PDX).
