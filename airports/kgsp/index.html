About KGSP

Greenville-Spartanburg International Airport (GSP) is a medium airport in Greenville/Greer/Spartanburg, United States, with the ICAO code KGSP. It has one runway, 04/22, 11,001 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 44 airlines have 395 routes through KGSP. Its most frequent links are Dulles (IAD), Charlotte (CLT) and Chicago (ORD).
