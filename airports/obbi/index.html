About OBBI

Bahrain International Airport (BAH) is a large airport in Manama, Bahrain, with the ICAO code OBBI. Its two runways are 12L/30R and 12R/30L. The longer one, 12L/30R, is 12,979 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 132 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 73 airlines have 979 routes through OBBI. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Schkeuditz (LEJ) and Doha (DOH).
