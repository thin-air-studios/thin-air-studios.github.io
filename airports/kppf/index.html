About KPPF

Tri-City Airport (PPF) is a small airport in Parsons, United States, with the ICAO code KPPF. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,001 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPPF.
