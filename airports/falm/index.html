About FALM

Makhado Air Force Base Airport (FALM) is a small airport in Louis Trichardt Southwest, South Africa. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10/28 and 10L/28R. The longer one, 10/28, is 13,189 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 508 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FALM.
