About DAAG

Houari Boumediene Airport (ALG) is a large airport in Algiers, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAAG. Its two runways are 05/23 and 09/27. The longer one, 05/23, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 156 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 1,059 routes through DAAG. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Es-Sénia (ORN).
