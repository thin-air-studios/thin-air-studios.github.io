About RPLI

Laoag International Airport (LAO) is a large airport in Laoag City, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPLI. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,120 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 11 routes through RPLI. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Manila (MNL).
