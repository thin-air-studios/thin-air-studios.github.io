About SVVA

Arturo Michelena International Airport (VLN) is a large airport in Valencia, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVVA. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,842 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 49 routes through SVVA. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Tocumen (PTY) and Maiquetía (CCS).
