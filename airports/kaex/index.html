About KAEX

Alexandria International Airport (AEX) is a medium airport in Alexandria, United States, with the ICAO code KAEX. Its two runways are 14/32 and 18/36. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,352 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 11 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 49 routes through KAEX. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Atlanta (ATL).
