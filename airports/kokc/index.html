About KOKC

OKC Will Rogers World Airport (OKC) is a large airport in Oklahoma City, United States, with the ICAO code KOKC. Its 4 runways are 17L/35R, 17R/35L, 13/31 and 18/36. The longest, 17L/35R, is 9,802 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 197 taxiway segments, 25 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 381 routes through KOKC. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Houston (IAH) and Chicago (ORD).
