About MMBT

Bahías de Huatulco International Airport (HUX) is a large airport in Huatulco, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMBT. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 148 routes through MMBT. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Toronto (YYZ) and Vancouver (YVR).
