About EDGS

Siegerland Airport (SGE) is a medium airport in Burbach, Germany, with the ICAO code EDGS. It has 5 runways, from 820 ft to 5,315 ft long. The longest is 13/31. Of these, 1 hard and 4 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDGS.
