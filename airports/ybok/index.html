About YBOK

Oakey Army Aviation Centre (OKY) is a medium airport in Australia, with the ICAO code YBOK. It has 5 runways, from 1,000 ft to 5,410 ft long. The longest is 14/32. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YBOK.
