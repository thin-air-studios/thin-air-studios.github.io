About UMMS

Minsk National Airport (MSQ) is a large airport in Minsk, Belarus, with the ICAO code UMMS. Its two runways are 13L/31R and 13R/31L. The longer one, 13L/31R, is 12,139 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 274 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 517 routes through UMMS. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Antalya (AYT) and Hurghada (HRG).
