About ESGL

Lidköping-Hovby Airport (LDK) is a small airport in Lidköping, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESGL. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,529 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 23 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ESGL.
