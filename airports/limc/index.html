About LIMC

Milan Malpensa International Airport (MXP) is a large airport in Ferno (VA), Italy, with the ICAO code LIMC. Its two runways are 17L/35R and 17R/35L. The longer one, 17L/35R, is 12,861 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 gates, 157 taxiway segments, 23 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 227 airlines have 8,993 routes through LIMC. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Barcelona (BCN) and London (LGW).
