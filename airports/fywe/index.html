About FYWE

Eros Airport (ERS) is a medium airport in Windhoek, Namibia, with the ICAO code FYWE. Its two runways are 01/19 and 09/27. The longer one, 01/19, is 7,381 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 65 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WAA has 16 routes through FYWE. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Ondangwa (OND) and Oranjemund (OMD).
