About LFOZ

Orléans–Saint-Denis-de-l'Hôtel Airport (ORE) is a small airport in Saint-Denis-de-l'Hôtel, Loiret, France, with the ICAO code LFOZ. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,250 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFOZ.
