About FBSP

Selebi Phikwe Airport (PKW) is a medium airport in Selebi Phikwe, Botswana, with the ICAO code FBSP. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,840 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FBSP.
