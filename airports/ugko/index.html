About UGKO

David the Builder Kutaisi International Airport (KUT) is a large airport in Kopitnari, Georgia, with the ICAO code UGKO. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,202 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 23 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 420 routes through UGKO. Its most frequent links are Warsaw (WAW), Rome (FCO) and Katowice (KTW).
