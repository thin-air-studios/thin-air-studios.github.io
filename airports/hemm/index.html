About HEMM

Mersa Matruh International Airport (MUH) is a large airport in Marsa Matruh, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEMM. Its two runways are 06/24 and 15/33. The longer one, 06/24, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 102 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 29 routes through HEMM. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Cairo (CAI) and Prague (PRG).
