About KALN

St Louis Regional Airport (ALN) is a medium airport in Alton/St Louis, United States, with the ICAO code KALN. Its two runways are 11/29 and 17/35. The longer one, 11/29, is 8,099 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KALN.
