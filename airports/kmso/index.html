About KMSO

Missoula Montana Airport (MSO) is a medium airport in Missoula, United States, with the ICAO code KMSO. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,501 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 98 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 110 routes through KMSO. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Salt Lake City (SLC) and Billings (BIL).
