About WIMM

Kualanamu International Airport (KNO) is a large airport in Beringin, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIMM. It has one runway, 05/23, 12,303 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 105 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 393 routes through WIMM. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Sepang (KUL) and Batam (BTH).
