About KVGT

North Las Vegas Airport (VGT) is a medium airport in Las Vegas, United States, with the ICAO code KVGT. Its 3 runways are 07/25, 12R/30L and 12L/30R. The longest, 07/25, is 5,005 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 228 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KVGT.
