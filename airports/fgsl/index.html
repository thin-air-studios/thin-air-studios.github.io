About FGSL

Malabo International Airport (SSG) is a large airport in Malabo, Equatorial Guinea, with the ICAO code FGSL. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,647 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 19 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 45 routes through FGSL. Its most frequent links are Madrid (MAD), Paris (CDG) and Douala (DLA).
