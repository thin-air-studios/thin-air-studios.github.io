About VECA

Chabua Air Force Station (VECA) is a small airport in Chabua, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,003 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VECA.
