About MMZH

Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo International Airport (ZIH) is a large airport in Ixtapa, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMZH. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,222 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 23 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 103 routes through MMZH. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Mexico City (NLU) and Tijuana (TIJ).
