About CYQY

Sydney / J.A. Douglas McCurdy Airport (YQY) is a medium airport in Sydney, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQY. Its two runways are 06/24 and 18/36. The longer one, 06/24, is 7,070 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 49 routes through CYQY. Its most frequent links are Halifax (YHZ), Toronto (YYZ) and Montréal (YUL).
