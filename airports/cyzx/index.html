About CYZX

CFB Greenwood (YZX) is a medium airport in Greenwood, Canada, with the ICAO code CYZX. Its two runways are 08/26 and 12/30. The longer one, 08/26, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments and 9 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYZX.
