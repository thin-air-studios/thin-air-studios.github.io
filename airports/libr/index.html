About LIBR

Brindisi Airport (BDS) is a large airport in Brindisi, Italy, with the ICAO code LIBR. Its two runways are 13/31 and 05/23. The longer one, 13/31, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 43 airlines have 915 routes through LIBR. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Orio al Serio (BGY) and Geneva (GVA).
