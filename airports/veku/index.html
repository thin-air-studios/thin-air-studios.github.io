About VEKU

Silchar Airport (IXS) is a medium airport in Silchar, India, with the ICAO code VEKU. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,993 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 22 routes through VEKU. Its most frequent links are Kolkata (CCU), Guwahati (GAU) and Imphal (IMF).
