About MMVR

General Heriberto Jara International Airport (VER) is a large airport in Veracruz, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMVR. It has one runway, 19/01, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 gates, 44 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 159 routes through MMVR. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Cancún (CUN) and Guadalajara (GDL).
