About ZJHK

Haikou Meilan International Airport (HAK) is a large airport in Haikou (Meilan), China, with the ICAO code ZJHK. Its two runways are 09/27 and 10/28. The longer one, 09/27, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 gates, 258 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 69 airlines have 1,472 routes through ZJHK. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Shenzhen (SZX) and Changsha (CSX).
