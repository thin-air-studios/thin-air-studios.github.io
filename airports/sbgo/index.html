About SBGO

Santa Genoveva International Airport (GYN) is a large airport in Goiânia, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBGO. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,500 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 67 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 218 routes through SBGO. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), São Paulo (CGH) and Recife (REC).
