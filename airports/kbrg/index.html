About KBRG

Belen Regional Airport (KBRG) is a small airport in Belen, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 03/21 and 13/31. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,601 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBRG.
