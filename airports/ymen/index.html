About YMEN

Melbourne Essendon Airport (MEB) is a medium airport in Essendon Fields, Australia, with the ICAO code YMEN. Its two runways are 08/26 and 17/35. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,302 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 96 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 11 routes through YMEN. Its most frequent links are Dubbo (DBO), Albion Park Rail (WOL) and Yulara (AYQ).
