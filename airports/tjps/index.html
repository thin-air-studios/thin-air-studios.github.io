About TJPS

Mercedita International Airport (PSE) is a medium airport in Ponce, Puerto Rico, with the ICAO code TJPS. It has one runway, 12/30, 6,904 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 23 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 11 routes through TJPS. Its most frequent links are Orlando (MCO) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL).
