About YWYY

Wynyard Airport (BWT) is a medium airport in Burnie, Australia, with the ICAO code YWYY. Its two runways are 09/27 and 05/23. The longer one, 09/27, is 5,413 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 14 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 32 routes through YWYY. Its most frequent links are Melbourne (MEL) and King Island Airport (KNS).
