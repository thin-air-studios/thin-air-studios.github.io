About URMT

Stavropol Shpakovskoye Airport (STW) is a medium airport in Stavropol, Russia, with the ICAO code URMT. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,530 ft long and 157 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 62 routes through URMT. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Moscow (DME) and Moscow (VKO).
