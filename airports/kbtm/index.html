About KBTM

Bert Mooney Airport (BTM) is a medium airport in Butte, United States, with the ICAO code KBTM. Its two runways are 15/33 and 12/30. The longer one, 15/33, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 12 routes through KBTM. Its most frequent links are Salt Lake City (SLC), Billings (BIL) and Denver (DEN).
