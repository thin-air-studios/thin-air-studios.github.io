About SBCT

Curitiba-Afonso Pena International Airport (CWB) is a large airport in Curitiba, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBCT. Its two runways are 15/33 and 11/29. The longer one, 15/33, is 7,277 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 gates, 69 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 553 routes through SBCT. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (CGH), São Paulo (GRU) and Campinas (VCP).
