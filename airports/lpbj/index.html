About LPBJ

Beja Airport / Airbase (BYJ) is a medium airport in Beja, Portugal, with the ICAO code LPBJ. Its two runways are 01L/19R and 01R/19L. The longer one, 01L/19R, is 11,319 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LPBJ.
