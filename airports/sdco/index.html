About SDCO

Sorocaba Airport (SOD) is a small airport in Sorocaba, Brazil, with the ICAO code SDCO. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,348 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 66 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SDCO.
