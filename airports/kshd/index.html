About KSHD

Shenandoah Valley Regional Airport (SHD) is a medium airport in Weyers Cave, United States, with the ICAO code KSHD. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,002 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 31 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 17 routes through KSHD. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Dulles (IAD) and Chicago (ORD).
