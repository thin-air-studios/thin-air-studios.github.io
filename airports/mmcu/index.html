About MMCU

General Roberto Fierro Villalobos International Airport (CUU) is a large airport in Chihuahua, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMCU. Its 3 runways are 18L/36R, 18R/36L and 05/23. The longest, 18L/36R, is 8,530 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 72 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 114 routes through MMCU. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Guadalajara (GDL) and Monterrey (MTY).
