About OEDR

King Abdulaziz Air Base (DHA) is a large airport in Dhahran, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEDR. Its two runways are 16R/34L and 16L/34R. The longer one, 16R/34L, is 12,008 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 122 taxiway segments and 11 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OEDR.
