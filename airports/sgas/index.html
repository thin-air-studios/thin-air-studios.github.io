About SGAS

Silvio Pettirossi International Airport (ASU) is a large airport in Asunción, Paraguay, with the ICAO code SGAS. It has one runway, 02/20, 10,997 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 taxiway segments, 21 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 144 routes through SGAS. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Buenos Aires (EZE) and Buenos Aires (AEP).
