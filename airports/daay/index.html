About DAAY

Mecheria Airport (MZW) is a medium airport in Mecheria, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAAY. Its two runways are 06/24 and 17/35. The longer one, 06/24, is 11,780 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 236 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DAAY.
