About LTAJ

Gaziantep Oğuzeli International Airport (GZT) is a large airport in Gaziantep, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAJ. It has one runway, 10L/28R, 9,842 ft long and 157 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 435 routes through LTAJ. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Düsseldorf (DUS).
