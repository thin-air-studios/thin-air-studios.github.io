About YCCY

Cloncurry Airport (CNJ) is a medium airport in Cloncurry, Australia, with the ICAO code YCCY. Its two runways are 12/30 and 06/24. The longer one, 12/30, is 6,562 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 10 routes through YCCY. Its most frequent links are Townsville (TSV), Brisbane (BNE) and Cairns (CNS).
