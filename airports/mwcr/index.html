About MWCR

Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM) is a large airport in George Town, Cayman Islands, with the ICAO code MWCR. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,867 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 151 routes through MWCR. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), Kingston (KIN) and West End (CYB).
