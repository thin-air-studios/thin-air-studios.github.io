About LFLX

Châteauroux Déols airport (CHR) is a medium airport in Châteauroux, France, with the ICAO code LFLX. It has one runway, 03/21, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 28 routes through LFLX. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Paris (CDG) and Paris (ORY).
