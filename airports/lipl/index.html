About LIPL

Ghedi Air Base (LIPL) is a medium airport in Ghedi (BS), Italy. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,813 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 80 taxiway segments and 52 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LIPL.
