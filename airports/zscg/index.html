About ZSCG

Changzhou Benniu International Airport (CZX) is a medium airport in Changzhou, China, with the ICAO code ZSCG. It has one runway, 11/29, 11,155 ft long, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 245 routes through ZSCG. Its most frequent links are Harbin (HRB), Shenyang (SHE) and Kunming (KMG).
