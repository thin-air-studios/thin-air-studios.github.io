About KRNT

Renton Municipal Airport (RNT) is a small airport in Renton, United States, with the ICAO code KRNT. It has one runway, 16/34, 5,382 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRNT.
