About CYYJ

Victoria International Airport (YYJ) is a large airport in Victoria, Canada, with the ICAO code CYYJ. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 03/21 and 14/32. The longest, 09/27, is 7,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 87 taxiway segments, 25 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 318 routes through CYYJ. Its most frequent links are Vancouver (YVR), Calgary (YYC) and Seattle (SEA).
