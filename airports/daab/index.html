About DAAB

Blida Airport (DAAB) is a medium airport in Blida, Algeria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,833 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for DAAB.
