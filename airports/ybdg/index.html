About YBDG

Bendigo Airport (BXG) is a small airport in Australia, with the ICAO code YBDG. Its two runways are 17/35 and 05/23. The longer one, 17/35, is 5,249 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 9 routes through YBDG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Sydney (SYD).
