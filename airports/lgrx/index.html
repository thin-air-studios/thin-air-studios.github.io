About LGRX

Patras Araxos Agamemnon Airport (GPA) is a medium airport in Patras, Greece, with the ICAO code LGRX. It has one runway, 18R/36L, 10,997 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 125 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 107 routes through LGRX. Its most frequent links are Vienna (VIE), Moscow (DME) and Frankfurt am Main (FRA).
