About RCQS

Chihhang Air Base (RCQS) is a medium airport in Taitung City, Taiwan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 04/22, 11,055 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RCQS.
