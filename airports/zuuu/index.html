About ZUUU

Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (CTU) is a large airport in Chengdu (Shuangliu), China, with the ICAO code ZUUU. Its two runways are 02L/20R and 02R/20L. The longer one, 02L/20R, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 312 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 69 airlines have 1,566 routes through ZUUU. Its most frequent links are Beijing (PEK), Shenzhen (SZX) and Guangzhou (CAN).
