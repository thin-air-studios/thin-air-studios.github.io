About EPSN

Swidwin Military Air Base (EPSN) is a medium airport in Poland. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,200 ft long and 190 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 137 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EPSN.
