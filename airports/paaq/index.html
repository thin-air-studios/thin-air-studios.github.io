About PAAQ

Warren "Bud" Woods Palmer Municipal Airport (PAQ) is a medium airport in Palmer, United States, with the ICAO code PAAQ. Its 3 runways are 16/34, 09/27 and 16S/34S. The longest, 16/34, is 6,006 ft. Of these, 2 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for PAAQ.
