About WATE

H. Hasan Aroeboesman (Ende) Airport (ENE) is a small airport in Ende, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WATE. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,440 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WON has 4 routes through WATE. Its most frequent links are Radamata (TMC), Labuan Bajo (LBJ) and Kupang (KOE).
