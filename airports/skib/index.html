About SKIB

Perales Airport (IBE) is a medium airport in Ibagué, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKIB. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,905 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 27 routes through SKIB. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG), Medellín (EOH) and Cali (CLO).
