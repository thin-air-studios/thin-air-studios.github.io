About YKRY

Kingaroy Airport (KGY) is a medium airport in Australia, with the ICAO code YKRY. Its two runways are 16/34 and 05/23. The longer one, 16/34, is 5,249 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YKRY.
