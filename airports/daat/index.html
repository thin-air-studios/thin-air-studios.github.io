About DAAT

Aguenar – Hadj Bey Akhamok Airport (TMR) is a large airport in Tamanrasset, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAAT. Its two runways are 02/20 and 08/26. The longer one, 02/20, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 102 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 33 routes through DAAT. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG), Es-Sénia (ORN) and Djanet (DJG).
