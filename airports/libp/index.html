About LIBP

Abruzzo Airport (PSR) is a large airport in Pescara, Italy, with the ICAO code LIBP. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,933 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 12 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 316 routes through LIBP. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Orio al Serio (BGY) and Charleroi (CRL).
