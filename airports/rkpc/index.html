About RKPC

Jeju International Airport (CJU) is a large airport in Jeju City, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKPC. Its two runways are 07/25 and 13/31. The longer one, 07/25, is 10,433 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 gates, 82 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 35 airlines have 1,267 routes through RKPC. Its most frequent links are Seoul (GMP), Cheongju (CJJ) and Busan (PUS).
