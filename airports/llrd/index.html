About LLRD

Ramat David Air Base (LLRD) is a medium airport in Haifa, Israel. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 11/29 and 15/33. The longest, 09/27, is 8,550 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LLRD.
