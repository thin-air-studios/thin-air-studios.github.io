About KRVS

Tulsa Riverside Airport (RVS) is a medium airport in Tulsa, United States, with the ICAO code KRVS. Its 3 runways are 01L/19R, 01R/19L and 13/31. The longest, 01L/19R, is 5,101 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 159 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRVS.
