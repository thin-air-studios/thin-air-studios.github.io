About ZSCN

Nanchang Changbei International Airport (KHN) is a large airport in Nanchang, China, with the ICAO code ZSCN. It has one runway, 03/21, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 gates, 113 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 43 airlines have 543 routes through ZSCN. Its most frequent links are Kunming (KMG), Haikou (HAK) and Beijing (PKX).
