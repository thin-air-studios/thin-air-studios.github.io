About HHAS

Asmara International Airport (ASM) is a medium airport in Asmara, Eritrea, with the ICAO code HHAS. Its two runways are 07/25 and 12/30. The longer one, 07/25, is 9,842 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 25 routes through HHAS. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Addis Ababa (ADD) and Istanbul (IST).
