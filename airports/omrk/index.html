About OMRK

Ras Al Khaimah International Airport (RKT) is a large airport in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMRK. It has one runway, 16/34, 12,336 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 49 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 190 routes through OMRK. Its most frequent links are Mumbai (BOM), Kochi (COK) and New Delhi (DEL).
