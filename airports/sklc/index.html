About SKLC

Antonio Roldán Betancur Airport (APO) is a medium airport in Carepa, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKLC. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,153 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 18 routes through SKLC. Its most frequent links are Medellín (EOH) and Bogota (BOG).
