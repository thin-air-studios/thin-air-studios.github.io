About LRCV

Craiova International Airport (CRA) is a large airport in Craiova, Romania, with the ICAO code LRCV. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,203 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 318 routes through LRCV. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Orio al Serio (BGY) and Antalya (AYT).
