About KLYH

Lynchburg Regional Airport - Preston Glenn Field (LYH) is a medium airport in Lynchburg, United States, with the ICAO code KLYH. Its two runways are 04/22 and 17/35. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,100 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 35 routes through KLYH. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Dulles (IAD) and Chicago (ORD).
