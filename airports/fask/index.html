About FASK

Swartkop Air Force Base (FASK) is a medium airport in Pretoria, South Africa. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 06/24 and 02/20. The longer one, 06/24, is 6,589 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 147 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FASK.
