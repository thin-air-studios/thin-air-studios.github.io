About KMSP

Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport / Wold–Chamberlain Field (MSP) is a large airport in Minneapolis, United States, with the ICAO code KMSP. Its 4 runways are 04/22, 12R/30L, 12L/30R and 17/35. The longest, 04/22, is 11,006 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 121 gates, 428 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 14 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 60 airlines have 2,318 routes through KMSP. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Chicago (ORD) and Milwaukee (MKE).
