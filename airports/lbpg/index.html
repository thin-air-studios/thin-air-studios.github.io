About LBPG

Graf Ignatievo Air Base (LBPG) is a medium airport in Graf Ignatievo, Bulgaria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 08/26, 9,815 ft long and 270 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 89 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LBPG.
