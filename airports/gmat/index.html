About GMAT

Tan Tan Airport (TTA) is a medium airport in Tan Tan, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMAT. It has one runway, 03/21, 6,562 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Royal Air Maroc has 2 routes through GMAT. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Casablanca (CMN).
