About SBSV

Deputado Luiz Eduardo Magalhães International Airport (SSA) is a large airport in Salvador, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBSV. Its two runways are 10/28 and 17/35. The longer one, 10/28, is 9,859 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 gates, 110 taxiway segments, 24 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 667 routes through SBSV. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), São Paulo (CGH) and Rio De Janeiro (GIG).
