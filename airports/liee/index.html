About LIEE

Cagliari Elmas Airport (CAG) is a large airport in Cagliari, Italy, with the ICAO code LIEE. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,196 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 73 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 54 airlines have 1,468 routes through LIEE. Its most frequent links are Ferno (MXP), Rome (FCO) and Orio al Serio (BGY).
