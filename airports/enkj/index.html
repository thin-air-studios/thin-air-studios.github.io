About ENKJ

Lillestrøm Airfield, Kjeller (ENKJ) is a small airport in Kjeller, Norway. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,321 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ENKJ.
