About WMKB

RMAF Butterworth Air Base (BWH) is a medium airport in Butterworth, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WMKB. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for WMKB.
