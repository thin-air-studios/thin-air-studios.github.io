About EGCC

Manchester Airport (MAN) is a large airport in Manchester, Greater Manchester, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGCC. Its two runways are 05R/23L and 05L/23R. The longer one, 05R/23L, is 10,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 78 gates, 151 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 14 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 156 airlines have 10,318 routes through EGCC. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Tenerife (TFS) and Alicante (ALC).
