About OPRN

PAF Base Nur Khan (Benazir Bhutto International Airport) (OPRN) is a medium airport in Rawalpindi, Pakistan. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,785 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 59 routes through OPRN. Its most frequent links are Karachi (KHI), Riyadh (RUH) and Manchester (MAN).
