About KRND

Randolph Air Force Base (RND) is a medium airport in Universal City, United States, with the ICAO code KRND. Its two runways are 15R/33L and 15L/33R. The longer one, 15R/33L, is 8,352 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRND.
