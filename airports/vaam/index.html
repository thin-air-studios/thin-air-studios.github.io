About VAAM

Amravati Airport (AVR) is a medium airport in Amravati, India, with the ICAO code VAAM. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,070 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, LLR has 2 routes through VAAM. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Mumbai (BOM).
