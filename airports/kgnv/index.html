About KGNV

Gainesville Regional Airport (GNV) is a medium airport in Gainesville, United States, with the ICAO code KGNV. Its two runways are 11/29 and 07/25. The longer one, 11/29, is 7,504 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 44 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 52 routes through KGNV. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Atlanta (ATL) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
