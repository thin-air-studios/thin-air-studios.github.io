About GMTT

Tangier Ibn Battuta Airport (TNG) is a large airport in Tangier, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMTT. It has one runway, 10/28, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 18 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 37 airlines have 841 routes through GMTT. Its most frequent links are Zaventem (BRU), Madrid (MAD) and Paris (ORY).
