About KHZY

Northeast Ohio Regional Airport (JFN) is a medium airport in Ashtabula, United States, with the ICAO code KHZY. It has one runway, 09/27, 5,900 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHZY.
