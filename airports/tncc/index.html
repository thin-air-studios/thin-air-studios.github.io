About TNCC

Hato International Airport (CUR) is a large airport in Willemstad, Curaçao, with the ICAO code TNCC. It has one runway, 11/29, 11,188 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 82 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 46 airlines have 255 routes through TNCC. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Kralendijk (BON) and Oranjestad (AUA).
