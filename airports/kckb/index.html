About KCKB

North Central West Virginia Airport (CKB) is a medium airport in Bridgeport, United States, with the ICAO code KCKB. It has one runway, 03/21, 7,800 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 27 routes through KCKB. Its most frequent links are Orlando (SFB), Dulles (IAD) and Chicago (ORD).
