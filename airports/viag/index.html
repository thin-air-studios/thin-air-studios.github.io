About VIAG

Agra Airport / Agra Air Force Station (AGR) is a medium airport in Agra, India, with the ICAO code VIAG. Its two runways are 05/23 and 12/30. The longer one, 05/23, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, IndiGo has 20 routes through VIAG. Its most frequent links are Ahmedabad (AMD), Bengaluru (BLR) and Hyderabad (HYD).
