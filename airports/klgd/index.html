About KLGD

La Grande/Union County Airport (LGD) is a small airport in La Grande, United States, with the ICAO code KLGD. Its two runways are 12/30 and 17/35. The longer one, 12/30, is 6,261 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AMF has 1 routes through KLGD. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Portland (PDX).
