About SBBE

Val de Cans/Júlio Cezar Ribeiro International Airport (BEL) is a large airport in Belém, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBBE. Its two runways are 07/25 and 03/21. The longer one, 07/25, is 9,186 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 63 taxiway segments, 28 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 278 routes through SBBE. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (GRU), Brasília (BSB) and Fortaleza (FOR).
