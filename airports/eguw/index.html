About EGUW

Wattisham Airfield (EGUW) is a medium airport in Ipswich, Suffolk, United Kingdom. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,953 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 1 aprons.
