About USHH

Khanty Mansiysk Airport (HMA) is a medium airport in Khanty-Mansiysk, Russia, with the ICAO code USHH. It has one runway, 06/24, 9,180 ft long and 141 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 37 routes through USHH. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (VKO) and Ufa (UFA).
