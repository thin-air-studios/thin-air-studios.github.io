About KHUM

Houma Terrebonne Airport (HUM) is a small airport in Houma, United States, with the ICAO code KHUM. Its two runways are 18/36 and 12/30. The longer one, 18/36, is 6,509 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHUM.
