About KBHM

Birmingham-Shuttlesworth International Airport (BHM) is a large airport in Birmingham, United States, with the ICAO code KBHM. Its two runways are 06/24 and 18/36. The longer one, 06/24, is 12,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 222 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 240 routes through KBHM. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Houston (IAH) and Chicago (ORD).
