About VELR

Lilabari North Lakhimpur Airport (IXI) is a medium airport in Lilabari, India, with the ICAO code VELR. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,500 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, LLR has 2 routes through VELR. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Kolkata (CCU).
