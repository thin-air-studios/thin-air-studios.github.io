About KIPT

Williamsport Regional Airport (IPT) is a medium airport in Williamsport, United States, with the ICAO code KIPT. Its two runways are 09/27 and 12/30. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,825 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 13 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 16 routes through KIPT. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Dulles (IAD) and Harrisburg (MDT).
