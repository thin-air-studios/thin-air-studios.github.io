About NSFA

Faleolo International Airport (APW) is a large airport in Apia, Samoa, with the ICAO code NSFA. It has one runway, 08/26, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 76 routes through NSFA. Its most frequent links are Auckland (AKL), Nadi (NAN) and Los Angeles (LAX).
