About DIAP

Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport (ABJ) is a large airport in Abidjan, Côte d’Ivoire, with the ICAO code DIAP. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,843 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 117 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 227 routes through DIAP. Its most frequent links are Accra (ACC), Zaventem (BRU) and Ouagadougou (OUA).
