About ZBHH

Hohhot Baita International Airport (HET) is a large airport in Hohhot, China, with the ICAO code ZBHH. It has one runway, 08/26, 11,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 607 routes through ZBHH. Its most frequent links are Tongliao (TGO), Chongqing (CKG) and Hailar (HLD).
