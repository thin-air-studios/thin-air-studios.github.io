About KMMI

McMinn County Airport (MMI) is a small airport in Athens, United States, with the ICAO code KMMI. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,450 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMMI.
