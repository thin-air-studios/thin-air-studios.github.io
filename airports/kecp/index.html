About KECP

Northwest Florida Beaches International Airport (ECP) is a medium airport in Panama City Beach, United States, with the ICAO code KECP. It has one runway, 16/34, 10,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 117 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 74 routes through KECP. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Atlanta (ATL) and Nashville (BNA).
