About KPSP

Palm Springs International Airport (PSP) is a large airport in Palm Springs, United States, with the ICAO code KPSP. Its two runways are 13R/31L and 13L/31R. The longer one, 13R/31L, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 gates, 155 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 28 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 248 routes through KPSP. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Denver (DEN) and Los Angeles (LAX).
