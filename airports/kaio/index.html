About KAIO

Atlantic Municipal Airport (AIO) is a small airport in Atlantic, United States, with the ICAO code KAIO. Its two runways are 02/20 and 12/30. The longer one, 02/20, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KAIO.
