About EPOK

Oksywie Air Base / Gdynia-Kosakowo Airfield (EPOK) is a small airport in Kosakowo, Poland. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 13/31 and 08/26. The longer one, 13/31, is 8,190 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 70 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EPOK.
