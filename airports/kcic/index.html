About KCIC

Chico Regional Airport (CIC) is a small airport in Chico, United States, with the ICAO code KCIC. Its two runways are 13L/31R and 13R/31L. The longer one, 13L/31R, is 6,724 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 102 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through KCIC. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Sacramento (SMF) and Arcata/Eureka (ACV).
