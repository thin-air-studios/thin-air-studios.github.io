About EGSH

Norwich Airport (NWI) is a medium airport in Norwich, Norfolk, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGSH. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,043 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 221 routes through EGSH. Its most frequent links are Aberdeen (ABZ), Amsterdam (AMS) and Tenerife (TFS).
