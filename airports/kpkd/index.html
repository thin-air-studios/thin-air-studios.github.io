About KPKD

Park Rapids Municipal Airport Konshok Field (PKD) is a small airport in Park Rapids, United States, with the ICAO code KPKD. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,497 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPKD.
