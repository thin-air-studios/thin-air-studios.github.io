About SLVR

Viru Viru International Airport (VVI) is a large airport in Santa Cruz, Bolivia, with the ICAO code SLVR. It has one runway, 16/34, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 162 routes through SLVR. Its most frequent links are La Paz / El Alto (LPB), Cochabamba (CBB) and São Paulo (GRU).
