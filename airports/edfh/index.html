About EDFH

Frankfurt-Hahn Airport (HHN) is a large airport in Frankfurt am Main (Lautzenhausen), Germany, with the ICAO code EDFH. It has one runway, 03/21, 12,467 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 36 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 67 airlines have 1,444 routes through EDFH. Its most frequent links are Baku (GYD), London (STN) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
