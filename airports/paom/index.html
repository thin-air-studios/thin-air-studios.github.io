About PAOM

Nome Airport (OME) is a medium airport in Nome, United States, with the ICAO code PAOM. Its two runways are 03/21 and 10/28. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,176 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 7 routes through PAOM. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC) and Kotzebue (OTZ).
