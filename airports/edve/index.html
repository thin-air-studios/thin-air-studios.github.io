About EDVE

Braunschweig-Wolfsburg Airport (BWE) is a medium airport in Braunschweig, Germany, with the ICAO code EDVE. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,546 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 58 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 32 routes through EDVE. Its most frequent links are Moscow (VKO), Prague (PRG) and Barcelona (BCN).
