About EGNJ

Humberside Airport (HUY) is a medium airport in Grimsby, Lincolnshire, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGNJ. Its two runways are 02/20 and 08/26. The longer one, 02/20, is 7,218 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 95 routes through EGNJ. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Aberdeen (ABZ) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
