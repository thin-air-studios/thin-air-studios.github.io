About KLHW

MidCoast Regional Airport at Wright Army Airfield (LIY) is a small airport in Hinesville, United States, with the ICAO code KLHW. Its 4 runways are 06L/24R, 15L/33R, 06R/24L and 15R/33L. The longest, 06L/24R, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLHW.
