About LFBI

Poitiers-Biard Airport (PIS) is a medium airport in Poitiers/Biard, France, with the ICAO code LFBI. It has one runway, 03/21, 7,710 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 68 routes through LFBI. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Ingliston (EDI) and Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS).
