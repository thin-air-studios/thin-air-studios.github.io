About GATB

Tombouktou Airport (TOM) is a large airport in Timbuktu, Mali, with the ICAO code GATB. It has one runway, 07/25, 6,923 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for GATB.
