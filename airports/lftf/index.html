About LFTF

Cuers-Pierrefeu Airfield (LFTF) is a small airport in Pierrefeu-du-Var, Var, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 11/29, 6,471 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 19 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFTF.
