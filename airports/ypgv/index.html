About YPGV

Gove Airport (GOV) is a medium airport in Nhulunbuy, Australia, with the ICAO code YPGV. It has one runway, 13/31, 7,244 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 10 routes through YPGV. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Darwin (DRW) and Cairns (CNS).
