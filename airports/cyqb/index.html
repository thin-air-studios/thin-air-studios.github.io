About CYQB

Quebec Jean Lesage International Airport (YQB) is a large airport in Quebec, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQB. Its two runways are 06/24 and 11/29. The longer one, 06/24, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 82 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 43 airlines have 541 routes through CYQB. Its most frequent links are Montréal (YUL), Toronto (YYZ) and Ottawa (YOW).
