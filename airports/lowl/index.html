About LOWL

Linz-Hörsching Airport (LNZ) is a large airport in Linz, Austria, with the ICAO code LOWL. Its two runways are 08/26 and 08E/26E. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,843 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 45 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 44 airlines have 326 routes through LOWL. Its most frequent links are Frankfurt am Main (FRA), Antalya (AYT) and Rhodes (RHO).
