About CYBG

Saguenay-Bagotville Airport (YBG) is a medium airport in Saguenay, Canada, with the ICAO code CYBG. Its two runways are 11/29 and 18/36. The longer one, 11/29, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 46 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 33 routes through CYBG. Its most frequent links are Montréal (YUL), Quebec (YQB) and La Grande-4 (YAH).
