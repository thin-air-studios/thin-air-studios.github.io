About KGGG

East Texas Regional Airport (GGG) is a medium airport in Longview, United States, with the ICAO code KGGG. Its two runways are 13/31 and 18/36. The longer one, 13/31, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 5 routes through KGGG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
