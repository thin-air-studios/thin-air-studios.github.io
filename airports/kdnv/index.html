About KDNV

Vermilion Regional Airport (DNV) is a small airport in Danville, United States, with the ICAO code KDNV. Its two runways are 03/21 and 16/34. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,006 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SNC has 2 routes through KDNV. Its most frequent links are Decatur (DEC) and Louisville (SDF).
