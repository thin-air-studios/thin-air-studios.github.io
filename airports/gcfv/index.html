About GCFV

Fuerteventura Airport (FUE) is a large airport in El Matorral, Spain, with the ICAO code GCFV. It has one runway, 01/19, 11,220 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 80 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 87 airlines have 2,854 routes through GCFV. Its most frequent links are Gran Canaria Island (LPA), Düsseldorf (DUS) and Madrid (MAD).
