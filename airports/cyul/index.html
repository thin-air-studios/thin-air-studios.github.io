About CYUL

Montreal / Pierre Elliott Trudeau International Airport (YUL) is a large airport in Montréal, Canada, with the ICAO code CYUL. Its two runways are 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06L/24R, is 11,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 62 gates, 378 taxiway segments, 30 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 89 airlines have 3,147 routes through CYUL. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Quebec (YQB) and Chicago (ORD).
