About KMRH

Michael J. Smith Field (KMRH) is a small airport in Beaufort, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 03/21 and 14/32. The longest, 08/26, is 5,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMRH.
