About KPOY

Powell Municipal Airport (POY) is a small airport in Powell, United States, with the ICAO code KPOY. Its 3 runways are 13/31, 03/21 and 16/34. The longest, 13/31, is 6,200 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPOY.
