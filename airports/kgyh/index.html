About KGYH

Donaldson Field Airport (GDC) is a small airport in Greenville, United States, with the ICAO code KGYH. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AIP has 1 routes through KGYH. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Moselle (PIB).
