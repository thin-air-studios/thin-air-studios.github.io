About HAAM

Arba Minch Airport (AMH) is a medium airport in Arba Minch, Ethiopia, with the ICAO code HAAM. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,170 ft long and 153 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Ethiopian has 6 routes through HAAM. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Addis Ababa (ADD).
