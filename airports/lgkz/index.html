About LGKZ

Kozani National Airport Filippos (KZI) is a medium airport in Kozani, Greece, with the ICAO code LGKZ. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,978 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SEH has 4 routes through LGKZ. Its most frequent links are Spata-Artemida (ATH) and Argos Orestiko (KSO).
