About NVVV

Bauerfield International Airport (VLI) is a large airport in Port Vila, Vanuatu, with the ICAO code NVVV. Its two runways are 11/29 and 11R/29L. The longer one, 11/29, is 8,530 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 92 routes through NVVV. Its most frequent links are Luganville (SON), Brisbane (BNE) and Nouméa (NOU).
