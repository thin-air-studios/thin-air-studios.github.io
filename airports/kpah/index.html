About KPAH

Barkley Regional Airport (PAH) is a medium airport in Paducah, United States, with the ICAO code KPAH. Its two runways are 04/22 and 14/32. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,499 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 18 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 24 routes through KPAH. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Charlotte (CLT) and Houston (IAH).
