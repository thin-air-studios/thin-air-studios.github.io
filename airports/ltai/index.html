About LTAI

Antalya International Airport (AYT) is a large airport in Antalya, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAI. Its 3 runways are 18C/36C, 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longest, 18C/36C, is 11,155 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 gates, 177 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 189 airlines have 15,479 routes through LTAI. Its most frequent links are Düsseldorf (DUS), Amsterdam (AMS) and Köln (CGN).
