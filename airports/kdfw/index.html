About KDFW

Dallas Fort Worth International Airport (DFW) is a large airport in Dallas-Fort Worth, United States, with the ICAO code KDFW. It is number 4 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 85.7 million passengers in 2025. It has 7 runways, from 8,500 ft to 13,401 ft long. The longest is 18L/36R. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 168 gates, 387 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 12 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 91 airlines have 3,907 routes through KDFW. Its most frequent links are Louisville (SDF), Chicago (ORD) and Denver (DEN).
