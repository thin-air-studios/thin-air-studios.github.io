About LGKL

Kalamata Airport (KLX) is a medium airport in Kalamata, Greece, with the ICAO code LGKL. Its two runways are 17L/35R and 17R/35L. The longer one, 17L/35R, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 214 routes through LGKL. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Vienna (VIE) and Amsterdam (AMS).
