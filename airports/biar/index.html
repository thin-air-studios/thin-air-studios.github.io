About BIAR

Akureyri International Airport (AEY) is a large airport in Akureyri, Iceland, with the ICAO code BIAR. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 77 routes through BIAR. Its most frequent links are Reykjavík (RKV), London (LGW) and Glasgow (GLA).
