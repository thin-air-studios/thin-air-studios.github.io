About WBKS

Sandakan Airport (SDK) is a medium airport in Sandakan, Malaysia, with the ICAO code WBKS. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 63 routes through WBKS. Its most frequent links are Sepang (KUL), Kota Kinabalu (BKI) and Lahad Datu (LDU).
