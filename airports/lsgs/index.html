About LSGS

Sion Airport (SIR) is a medium airport in Sion, Switzerland, with the ICAO code LSGS. Its two runways are 07/25 and 07G/25G. The longer one, 07/25, is 6,562 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 179 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 9 routes through LSGS. Its most frequent links are Palma de Mallorca (PMI), Calvi (CLY) and London (LHR).
