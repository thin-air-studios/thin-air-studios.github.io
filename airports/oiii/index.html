About OIII

Mehrabad International Airport (THR) is a large airport in Tehran, Iran, with the ICAO code OIII. Its two runways are 11R/29L and 11L/29R. The longer one, 11R/29L, is 13,258 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 261 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 312 routes through OIII. Its most frequent links are Kish Island (KIH), Mashhad (MHD) and Bandar Abbas (BND).
