About FVRG

Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport (HRE) is a large airport in Harare, Zimbabwe, with the ICAO code FVRG. It has one runway, 05/23, 15,502 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 96 routes through FVRG. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Lusaka (LUN) and Nairobi (NBO).
