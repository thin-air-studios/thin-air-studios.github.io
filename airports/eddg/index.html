About EDDG

Münster Osnabrück Airport (FMO) is a large airport in Greven, Germany, with the ICAO code EDDG. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,119 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 70 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 45 airlines have 563 routes through EDDG. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Munich (MUC).
