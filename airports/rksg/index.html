About RKSG

Camp Humphreys (A-511) Desiderio Army Airfield (RKSG) is a medium airport in Pyeongtaek, South Korea. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,217 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 115 taxiway segments and 9 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RKSG.
