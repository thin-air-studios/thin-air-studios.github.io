About UHPP

Yelizovo Airport (PKC) is a large airport in Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, Russia, with the ICAO code UHPP. It has one runway, 16R/34L, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 156 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 62 routes through UHPP. Its most frequent links are Artyom (VVO), Moscow (SVO) and Novosibirsk (OVB).
