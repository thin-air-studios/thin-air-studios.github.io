About KOLU

Columbus Municipal Airport (OLU) is a medium airport in Columbus, United States, with the ICAO code KOLU. Its two runways are 14/32 and 02/20. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,801 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOLU.
