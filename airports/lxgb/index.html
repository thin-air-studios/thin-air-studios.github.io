About LXGB

Gibraltar Airport (GIB) is a large airport in Gibraltar, Gibraltar, with the ICAO code LXGB. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 18 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 185 routes through LXGB. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Manchester (MAN) and Bristol (BRS).
