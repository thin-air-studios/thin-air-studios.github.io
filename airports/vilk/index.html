About VILK

Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport (LKO) is a large airport in Lucknow, India, with the ICAO code VILK. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,996 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 460 routes through VILK. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
