About OIFM

Isfahan Shahid Beheshti International Airport (IFN) is a large airport in Isfahan, Iran, with the ICAO code OIFM. Its two runways are 07L/25R and 07R/25L. The longer one, 07L/25R, is 14,425 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 182 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 62 routes through OIFM. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Tehran (THR) and Dubai (DXB).
