About KAVP

Wilkes-Barre/Scranton International Airport (AVP) is a medium airport in Wilkes-Barre/Scranton, United States, with the ICAO code KAVP. Its two runways are 04/22 and 10/28. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 41 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 85 routes through KAVP. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Philadelphia (PHL) and Charlotte (CLT).
