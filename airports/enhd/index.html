About ENHD

Haugesund Airport, Karmøy (HAU) is a medium airport in Karmøy, Norway, with the ICAO code ENHD. It has one runway, 13/31, 6,957 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 216 routes through ENHD. Its most frequent links are Bergen (BGO), Oslo (OSL) and Gdańsk (GDN).
