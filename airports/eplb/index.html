About EPLB

Lublin Airport (LUZ) is a large airport in Lublin, Poland, with the ICAO code EPLB. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,268 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 30 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 230 routes through EPLB. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Dublin (DUB) and Sandefjord (TRF).
