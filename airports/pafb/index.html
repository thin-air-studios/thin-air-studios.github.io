About PAFB

Ladd Army Airfield (FBK) is a medium airport in Fairbanks, United States, with the ICAO code PAFB. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,575 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for PAFB.
