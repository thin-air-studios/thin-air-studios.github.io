About LFBD

Bordeaux–Mérignac Airport (BOD) is a large airport in Bordeaux, France, with the ICAO code LFBD. Its two runways are 05/23 and 11/29. The longer one, 05/23, is 10,171 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 gates, 171 taxiway segments, 34 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 86 airlines have 3,208 routes through LFBD. Its most frequent links are Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS), Paris (CDG) and Marignane (MRS).
