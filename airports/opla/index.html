About OPLA

Allama Iqbal International Airport (LHE) is a large airport in Lahore, Pakistan, with the ICAO code OPLA. Its two runways are 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longer one, 18L/36R, is 11,024 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 139 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 350 routes through OPLA. Its most frequent links are Karachi (KHI), Dubai (DXB) and Jeddah (JED).
