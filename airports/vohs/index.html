About VOHS

Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) is a large airport in Hyderabad, India, with the ICAO code VOHS. Its two runways are 09R/27L and 09L/27R. The longer one, 09R/27L, is 13,976 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 gates, 169 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 50 airlines have 2,176 routes through VOHS. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR) and Mumbai (BOM).
