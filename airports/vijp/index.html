About VIJP

Jaipur International Airport (JAI) is a large airport in Jaipur, India, with the ICAO code VIJP. Its two runways are 08/26 and 15/33. The longer one, 08/26, is 9,177 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 48 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 388 routes through VIJP. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
