About OAJL

Jalalabad Airport (JAA) is a medium airport in Jalalabad, Afghanistan, with the ICAO code OAJL. It has one runway, 13/31, 7,262 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments and 11 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AVJ has 2 routes through OAJL. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Bishkek (BSZ).
