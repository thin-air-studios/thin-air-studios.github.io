About CYSJ

Saint John Airport (YSJ) is a medium airport in Saint John, Canada, with the ICAO code CYSJ. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 7,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 89 routes through CYSJ. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Montréal (YUL) and Halifax (YHZ).
