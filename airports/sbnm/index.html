About SBNM

Santo Ângelo Airport (GEL) is a medium airport in Santo Ângelo, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBNM. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,331 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 10 routes through SBNM. Its most frequent links are Porto Alegre (POA), Campinas (VCP) and Santa Maria (RIA).
