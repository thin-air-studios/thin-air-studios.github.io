About HE37

Bilbeis Air Base (HE37) is a medium airport in Bilbeis, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has 5 runways, from 5,100 ft to 11,480 ft long. The longest is 04/22. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 75 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE37.
