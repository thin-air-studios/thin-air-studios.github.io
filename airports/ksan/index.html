About KSAN

San Diego International Airport (SAN) is a large airport in San Diego, United States, with the ICAO code KSAN. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,401 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 gates, 138 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 45 airlines have 1,692 routes through KSAN. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Denver (DEN) and Los Angeles (LAX).
