About CYKF

Region of Waterloo International Airport (YKF) is a medium airport in Breslau, Canada, with the ICAO code CYKF. Its two runways are 08/26 and 14/32. The longer one, 08/26, is 7,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 78 routes through CYKF. Its most frequent links are Calgary (YYC), Halifax (YHZ) and Cancún (CUN).
