About LTCK

Muş Airport (MSR) is a medium airport in Muş, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTCK. It has one runway, 11/29, 11,649 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 106 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 89 routes through LTCK. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Pendik (SAW) and Yenişehir (YEI).
