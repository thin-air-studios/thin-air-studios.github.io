About ETHS

Faßberg Air Base (ETHS) is a medium airport in Faßberg, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 09/27, 09L/27R and 06/24. The longest, 09/27, is 8,005 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETHS.
