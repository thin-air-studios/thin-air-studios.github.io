About OIAD

Dezful Airport (DEF) is a medium airport in Dezful, Iran, with the ICAO code OIAD. Its two runways are 14R/32L and 14L/32R. The longer one, 14R/32L, is 12,641 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, IRA has 3 routes through OIAD. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Tehran (THR).
