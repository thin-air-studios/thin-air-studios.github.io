About LEZG

Zaragoza Airport (ZAZ) is a large airport in Zaragoza, Spain, with the ICAO code LEZG. Its two runways are 12R/30L and 12L/30R. The longer one, 12R/30L, is 12,198 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 104 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 48 airlines have 393 routes through LEZG. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Otopeni (OTP).
