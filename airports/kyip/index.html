About KYIP

Willow Run Airport (YIP) is a medium airport in Detroit, United States, with the ICAO code KYIP. Its two runways are 05/23 and 09/27. The longer one, 05/23, is 7,543 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 4 routes through KYIP. Its most frequent links are Milwaukee (MKE), Laredo (LRD) and Oakland (OAK).
