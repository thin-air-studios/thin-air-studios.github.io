About KHST

Homestead Air Reserve Base (HST) is a medium airport in Homestead, United States, with the ICAO code KHST. It has one runway, 05/23, 11,201 ft long and 300 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHST.
