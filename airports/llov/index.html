About LLOV

Ovda Airport (VDA) is a small airport in Eilat, Israel, with the ICAO code LLOV. Its two runways are 02R/20L and 02L/20R. The longer one, 02R/20L, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 47 routes through LLOV. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Moscow (DME) and Helsinki (HEL).
