About EBFS

Florennes Air Base (EBFS) is a medium airport in Florennes, Belgium. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08L/26R and 08R/26L. The longer one, 08L/26R, is 11,105 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 178 taxiway segments and 55 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EBFS.
