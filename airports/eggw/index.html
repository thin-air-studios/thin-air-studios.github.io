About EGGW

London Luton Airport (LTN) is a large airport in Luton, Luton, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGGW. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,093 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 gates, 32 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 93 airlines have 8,268 routes through EGGW. Its most frequent links are Geneva (GVA), Amsterdam (AMS) and Belfast (BFS).
