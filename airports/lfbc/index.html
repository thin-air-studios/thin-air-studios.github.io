About LFBC

Cazaux (BA 120) Air Base (LFBC) is a medium airport in Cazaux (La Teste-de-Buch), France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 06/24, 7,900 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 103 taxiway segments and 15 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFBC.
