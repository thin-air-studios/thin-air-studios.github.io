About UZSB

Bukhara International Airport (BHK) is a large airport in Bukhara, Uzbekistan, with the ICAO code UZSB. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,843 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 48 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UZSB.
