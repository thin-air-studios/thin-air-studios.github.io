About HUEN

Entebbe International Airport (EBB) is a large airport in Entebbe, Uganda, with the ICAO code HUEN. Its two runways are 17/35 and 12/30. The longer one, 17/35, is 12,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 106 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 172 routes through HUEN. Its most frequent links are Kigali (KGL), Nairobi (NBO) and Zaventem (BRU).
