About EPWR

Copernicus Wrocław Airport (WRO) is a large airport in Wrocław, Poland, with the ICAO code EPWR. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,212 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 57 airlines have 1,592 routes through EPWR. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), London (STN) and Luton (LTN).
