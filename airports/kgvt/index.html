About KGVT

Majors Airport (GVT) is a small airport in Greenville, United States, with the ICAO code KGVT. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,030 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGVT.
