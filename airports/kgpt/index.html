About KGPT

Gulfport Biloxi International Airport (GPT) is a medium airport in Gulfport, United States, with the ICAO code KGPT. Its two runways are 14/32 and 18/36. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 65 routes through KGPT. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Charlotte (CLT) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
