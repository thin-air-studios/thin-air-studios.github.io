About KORD

Chicago O'Hare International Airport (ORD) is a large airport in Chicago, United States, with the ICAO code KORD. It is number 6 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 84.9 million passengers in 2025. It has 8 runways, from 7,500 ft to 13,000 ft long. The longest is 10L/28R. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 226 gates, 971 taxiway segments, 38 aprons and 19 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 134 airlines have 8,495 routes through KORD. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), Toronto (YYZ) and Newark (EWR).
