About SBDO

Dourados Airport (DOU) is a small airport in Dourados, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBDO. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,398 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SBDO.
