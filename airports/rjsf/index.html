About RJSF

Fukushima Airport (FKS) is a medium airport in Sukagawa, Japan, with the ICAO code RJSF. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,202 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 41 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 40 routes through RJSF. Its most frequent links are Osaka (ITM), Sapporo (CTS) and Taoyuan (TPE).
