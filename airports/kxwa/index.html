About KXWA

Williston Basin International Airport (XWA) is a medium airport in Williston, United States, with the ICAO code KXWA. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,503 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 104 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 27 routes through KXWA. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Fargo (FAR) and Minneapolis (MSP).
