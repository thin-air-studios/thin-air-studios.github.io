About KMMV

McMinnville Municipal Airport (KMMV) is a medium airport in McMinnville, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 04/22 and 17/35. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,420 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMMV.
