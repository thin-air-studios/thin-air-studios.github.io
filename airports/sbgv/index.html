About SBGV

Coronel Altino Machado Airport (GVR) is a medium airport in Governador Valadares, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBGV. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,581 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 19 routes through SBGV. Its most frequent links are Belo Horizonte (PLU), Ipatinga (IPN) and Belo Horizonte (CNF).
