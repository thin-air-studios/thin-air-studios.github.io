About KRNV

Cleveland Municipal Airport (KRNV) is a small airport in Cleveland, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,005 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRNV.
