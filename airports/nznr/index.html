About NZNR

Hawke's Bay Airport (NPE) is a medium airport in Napier, New Zealand, with the ICAO code NZNR. Its two runways are 16/34 and 07/25. The longer one, 16/34, is 5,741 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 170 routes through NZNR. Its most frequent links are Auckland (AKL), Christchurch (CHC) and Wellington (WLG).
