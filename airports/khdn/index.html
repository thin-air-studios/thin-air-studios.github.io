About KHDN

Yampa Valley Airport (HDN) is a medium airport in Hayden, United States, with the ICAO code KHDN. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 15 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 30 routes through KHDN. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Newark (EWR) and Houston (IAH).
