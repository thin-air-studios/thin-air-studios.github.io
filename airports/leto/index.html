About LETO

Madrid–Torrejón Airport / Torrejón Air Base (TOJ) is a medium airport in Madrid, Spain, with the ICAO code LETO. It has one runway, 04/22, 15,807 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, UPA has 1 routes through LETO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Segrate (LIN).
