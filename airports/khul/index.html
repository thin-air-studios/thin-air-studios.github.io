About KHUL

Houlton International Airport (HUL) is a medium airport in Houlton, United States, with the ICAO code KHUL. Its two runways are 05/23 and 01/19. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,015 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHUL.
