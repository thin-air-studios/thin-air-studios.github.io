About OKKK

Kuwait International Airport (KWI) is a large airport in Kuwait City, Kuwait, with the ICAO code OKKK. Its 3 runways are 16/34, 15L/33R and 15R/33L. The longest, 16/34, is 15,026 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 240 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 83 airlines have 1,359 routes through OKKK. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Doha (DOH) and Jeddah (JED).
