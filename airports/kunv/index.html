About KUNV

State College Regional Airport (SCE) is a medium airport in State College, United States, with the ICAO code KUNV. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,701 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 65 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 63 routes through KUNV. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Chicago (ORD) and Dulles (IAD).
