About UZST

Termez Airport (TMJ) is a medium airport in Termez, Uzbekistan, with the ICAO code UZST. It has one runway, 07/25, 9,843 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UZST.
