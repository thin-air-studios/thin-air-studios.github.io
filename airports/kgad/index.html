About KGAD

Northeast Alabama Regional Airport (GAD) is a small airport in Gadsden, United States, with the ICAO code KGAD. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,802 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 10 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KGAD.
