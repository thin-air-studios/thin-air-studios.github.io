About KSEF

Sebring Regional Airport (SEF) is a small airport in Sebring, United States, with the ICAO code KSEF. Its two runways are 18/36 and 14/32. The longer one, 18/36, is 5,190 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSEF.
