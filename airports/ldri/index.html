About LDRI

Rijeka Airport (RJK) is a large airport in Rijeka(Omišalj), Croatia, with the ICAO code LDRI. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,164 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 144 routes through LDRI. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Köln (CGN) and Stuttgart (STR).
