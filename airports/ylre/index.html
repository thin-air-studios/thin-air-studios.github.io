About YLRE

Longreach Airport (LRE) is a medium airport in Longreach, Australia, with the ICAO code YLRE. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,352 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 19 routes through YLRE. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Barcaldine (BCI) and Blackall (BKQ).
