About LIBF

Foggia Gino Lisa Airport (FOG) is a medium airport in Foggia (FG), Italy, with the ICAO code LIBF. It has one runway, 15/33, 5,692 ft long and 154 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 26 routes through LIBF. Its most frequent links are Caselle Torinese (TRN), Segrate (LIN) and Orio al Serio (BGY).
