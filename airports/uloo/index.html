About ULOO

Princess Olga Pskov International Airport (PKV) is a medium airport in Pskov, Russia, with the ICAO code ULOO. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,281 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 78 taxiway segments, 32 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 19 routes through ULOO. Its most frequent links are Moscow (VKO), Kaliningrad (KGD) and Mineralnyye Vody (MRV).
