About KMSY

Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY) is a large airport in New Orleans, United States, with the ICAO code KMSY. Its two runways are 11/29 and 02/20. The longer one, 11/29, is 10,104 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 685 routes through KMSY. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Newark (EWR) and Orlando (MCO).
