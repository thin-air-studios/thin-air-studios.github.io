About VIJR

Jaisalmer Airport (JSA) is a medium airport in India, with the ICAO code VIJR. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 235 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 15 routes through VIJR. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR) and Mumbai (BOM).
