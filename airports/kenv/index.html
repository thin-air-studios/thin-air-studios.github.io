About KENV

Wendover Airport (ENV) is a medium airport in Wendover, United States, with the ICAO code KENV. Its two runways are 08/26 and 12/30. The longer one, 08/26, is 10,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KENV.
