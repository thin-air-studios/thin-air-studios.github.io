About VEGK

Gorakhpur Airport (GOP) is a medium airport in Gorakhpur, India, with the ICAO code VEGK. It has one runway, 11/29, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 74 routes through VEGK. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Bengaluru (BLR) and Mumbai (BOM).
