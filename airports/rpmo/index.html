About RPMO

Labo Airport (OZC) is a medium airport in Ozamiz, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPMO. It has one runway, 03/21, 5,720 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 26 routes through RPMO. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL) and Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB).
