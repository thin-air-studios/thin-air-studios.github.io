About LTFE

Milas Bodrum International Airport (BJV) is a large airport in Bodrum, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTFE. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 115 airlines have 1,693 routes through LTFE. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Zaventem (BRU) and Amsterdam (AMS).
