About OJMF

King Hussein Air College (OMF) is a small airport in Mafraq, Jordan, with the ICAO code OJMF. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,819 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OJMF.
