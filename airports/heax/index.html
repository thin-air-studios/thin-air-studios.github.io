About HEAX

Alexandria International Airport (HBE) is a large airport in Alexandria, Egypt, with the ICAO code HEAX. Its two runways are 14L/32R and 14R/32L. The longer one, 14L/32R, is 11,156 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 169 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 13 routes through HEAX. Its most frequent links are Cairo (CAI), Jeddah (JED) and Manama (BAH).
