About GQPA

Atar International Airport (ATR) is a medium airport in Atar, Mauritania, with the ICAO code GQPA. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,855 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 7 routes through GQPA. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Greven (FMO) and Zurich (ZRH).
