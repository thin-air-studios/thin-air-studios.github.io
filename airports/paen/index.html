About PAEN

Kenai Municipal Airport (ENA) is a medium airport in Kenai, United States, with the ICAO code PAEN. Its two runways are 02L/20R and 02R/20L. The longer one, 02L/20R, is 7,855 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 50 routes through PAEN. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC) and Rome (CIA).
