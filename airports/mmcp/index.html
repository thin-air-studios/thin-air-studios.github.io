About MMCP

Ingeniero Alberto Acuña Ongay International Airport (CPE) is a medium airport in Campeche, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMCP. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 25 routes through MMCP. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX) and Mexico City (NLU).
