About KBWI

Baltimore/Washington International Thurgood Marshall Airport (BWI) is a large airport in Baltimore, United States, with the ICAO code KBWI. Its 3 runways are 10/28, 15R/33L and 15L/33R. The longest, 10/28, is 10,503 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 77 gates, 197 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 58 airlines have 1,925 routes through KBWI. Its most frequent links are Atlanta (ATL), Boston (BOS) and Orlando (MCO).
