About KJAC

Jackson Hole Airport (JAC) is a medium airport in Jackson, United States, with the ICAO code KJAC. It has one runway, 01/19, 6,300 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 65 routes through KJAC. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), San Francisco (SFO) and Salt Lake City (SLC).
