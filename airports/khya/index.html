About KHYA

Cape Cod Gateway Airport (HYA) is a medium airport in Hyannis, United States, with the ICAO code KHYA. Its two runways are 06/24 and 15/33. The longer one, 06/24, is 5,425 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 28 routes through KHYA. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS), Nantucket (ACK) and New York (JFK).
