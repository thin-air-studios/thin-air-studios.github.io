About OMDB

Dubai International Airport (DXB) is a large airport in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMDB. It is number 2 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 95.2 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 12R/30L and 12L/30R. The longer one, 12R/30L, is 14,590 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 111 gates, 375 taxiway segments, 219 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 181 airlines have 4,417 routes through OMDB. Its most frequent links are Jeddah (JED), Riyadh (RUH) and Kuwait City (KWI).
