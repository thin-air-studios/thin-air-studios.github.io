About FKKN

N'Gaoundéré Airport (NGE) is a medium airport in N'Gaoundéré, Cameroon, with the ICAO code FKKN. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FKKN.
