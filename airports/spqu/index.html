About SPQU

Rodríguez Ballón International Airport (AQP) is a large airport in Arequipa, Peru, with the ICAO code SPQU. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,777 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 14 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 163 routes through SPQU. Its most frequent links are Lima (LIM), Cusco (CUZ) and Santiago (SCL).
