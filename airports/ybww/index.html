About YBWW

Toowoomba Wellcamp Airport (WTB) is a large airport in Toowoomba, Australia, with the ICAO code YBWW. It has one runway, 12/30, 9,416 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 33 routes through YBWW. Its most frequent links are Melbourne (MEL), Brisbane (BNE) and Townsville (TSV).
