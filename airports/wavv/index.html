About WAVV

Wamena Airport (WMX) is a medium airport in Wamena, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAVV. It has one runway, 15/33, 7,135 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for WAVV.
