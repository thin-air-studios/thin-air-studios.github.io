About RJAW

Ioto (Iwo Jima) Airbase (IWO) is a medium airport in Ogasawara, Japan, with the ICAO code RJAW. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,700 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RJAW.
