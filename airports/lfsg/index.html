About LFSG

Épinal Mirecourt Airport (EPL) is a medium airport in Épinal, France, with the ICAO code LFSG. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 92 taxiway segments, 38 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, EDW has 2 routes through LFSG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Zurich (ZRH).
