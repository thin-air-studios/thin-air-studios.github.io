About LFPO

Paris-Orly Airport (ORY) is a large airport in Paris (Orly, Val-de-Marne), France, with the ICAO code LFPO. Its 3 runways are 06/24, 07/25 and 02/20. The longest, 06/24, is 11,975 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 102 gates, 168 taxiway segments, 29 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 73 airlines have 13,087 routes through LFPO. Its most frequent links are Nice (NCE), Toulouse/Blagnac (TLS) and Marignane (MRS).
