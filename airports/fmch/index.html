About FMCH

Prince Said Ibrahim International Airport (HAH) is a large airport in Moroni, Comoros, with the ICAO code FMCH. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,514 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 3 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 24 routes through FMCH. Its most frequent links are Dzaoudzi (DZA), Antananarivo (TNR) and Nairobi (NBO).
