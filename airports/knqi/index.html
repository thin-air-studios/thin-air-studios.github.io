About KNQI

Kingsville Naval Air Station (NQI) is a medium airport in Kingsville, United States, with the ICAO code KNQI. Its 4 runways are 13L/31R, 13R/31L, 17L/35R and 17R/35L. The longest, 13L/31R, is 8,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNQI.
