About EBBE

Beauvechain Air Base (EBBE) is a medium airport in Beauvechain, Belgium. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 04L/22R and 04R/22L. The longer one, 04L/22R, is 10,085 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments and 40 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EBBE.
