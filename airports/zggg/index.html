About ZGGG

Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN) is a large airport in Guangzhou (Huadu), China, with the ICAO code ZGGG. It is number 9 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 83.6 million passengers in 2025. It has 5 runways, from 11,155 ft to 12,467 ft long. The longest is 02L/20R. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 267 gates, 968 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 130 airlines have 3,594 routes through ZGGG. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (SHA), Hangzhou (HGH) and Chongqing (CKG).
