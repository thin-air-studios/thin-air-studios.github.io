About EGHI

Southampton Airport (SOU) is a medium airport in Southampton, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGHI. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,191 ft long and 119 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 23 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 430 routes through EGHI. Its most frequent links are Belfast (BHD), Saint Peter Port (GCI) and St. Peter (JER).
