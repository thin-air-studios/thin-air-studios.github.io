About KHUA

Redstone Army Air Field (HUA) is a medium airport in Redstone Arsnl Huntsville, United States, with the ICAO code KHUA. It has one runway, 17/35, 7,300 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHUA.
