About EYPP

Panevėžys Air Base (PNV) is a medium airport in Panevėžys, Lithuania, with the ICAO code EYPP. It has one runway, 13/31, 6,562 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EYPP.
