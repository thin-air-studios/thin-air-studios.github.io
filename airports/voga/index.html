About VOGA

Manohar International Airport (GOX) is a large airport in Mopa, India, with the ICAO code VOGA. It has one runway, 10/28, 11,483 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 30 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 355 routes through VOGA. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
