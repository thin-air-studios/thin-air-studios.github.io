About LTBJ

Adnan Menderes International Airport (ADB) is a large airport in Gaziemir, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTBJ. Its two runways are 16L/34R and 16R/34L. The longer one, 16L/34R, is 10,630 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 71 airlines have 2,888 routes through LTBJ. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Istanbul (IST) and Düsseldorf (DUS).
