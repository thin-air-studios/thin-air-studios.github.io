About ESMS

Malmö Sturup Airport (MMX) is a large airport in Malmö, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESMS. Its two runways are 17/35 and 11/29. The longer one, 17/35, is 9,186 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 40 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 59 airlines have 752 routes through ESMS. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Stockholm (BMA) and Gran Canaria Island (LPA).
