About PAED

Elmendorf Air Force Base (EDF) is a medium airport in Anchorage, United States, with the ICAO code PAED. Its two runways are 06/24 and 17/35. The longer one, 06/24, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 731 taxiway segments, 32 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 6 routes through PAED. Its most frequent links are Fairfield (SUU), Okinawa (DNA) and Columbus (LCK).
