About EDTY

Adolf Würth Airport (EDTY) is a medium airport in Schwäbisch Hall, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10/28 and 10L/28R. The longer one, 10/28, is 5,053 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDTY.
