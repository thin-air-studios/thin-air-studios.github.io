About KNQX

Naval Air Station Key West/Boca Chica Field (NQX) is a medium airport in Key West, United States, with the ICAO code KNQX. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 04/22 and 14/32. The longest, 08/26, is 10,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 336 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNQX.
