About KLEX

Blue Grass Airport (LEX) is a medium airport in Lexington, United States, with the ICAO code KLEX. Its two runways are 04/22 and 09/27. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 70 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 204 routes through KLEX. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Atlanta (ATL) and Charlotte (CLT).
