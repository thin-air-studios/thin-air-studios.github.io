About SAME

Governor Francisco Gabrielli International Airport (MDZ) is a large airport in Mendoza, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAME. It has one runway, 18/36, 9,301 ft long and 177 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 180 routes through SAME. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP), Santiago (SCL) and San Carlos de Bariloche (BRC).
