About FAGC

Grand Central Airport (GCJ) is a medium airport in Midrand, South Africa, with the ICAO code FAGC. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,004 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 94 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FAGC.
