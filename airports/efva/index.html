About EFVA

Vaasa Airport (VAA) is a large airport in Vaasa, Finland, with the ICAO code EFVA. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,727 ft long and 157 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 17 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 139 routes through EFVA. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Stockholm (ARN) and Copenhagen (CPH).
