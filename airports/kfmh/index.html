About KFMH

Cape Cod Coast Guard Air Station (FMH) is a small airport in Falmouth, United States, with the ICAO code KFMH. Its two runways are 14/32 and 05/23. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,501 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 102 taxiway segments and 16 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFMH.
