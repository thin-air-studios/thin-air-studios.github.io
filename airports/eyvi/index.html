About EYVI

Vilnius International Airport (VNO) is a large airport in Vilnius, Lithuania, with the ICAO code EYVI. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,251 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 145 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 77 airlines have 1,343 routes through EYVI. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Antalya (AYT) and Barcelona (BCN).
