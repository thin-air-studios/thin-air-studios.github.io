About LIME

Il Caravaggio International Airport (BGY) is a large airport in Orio al Serio (BG), Italy, with the ICAO code LIME. Its two runways are 10/28 and 12/30. The longer one, 10/28, is 9,429 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 45 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 99 airlines have 4,144 routes through LIME. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Otopeni (OTP) and Rinas (TIA).
