About CYQM

Greater Moncton Roméo LeBlanc International Airport (YQM) is a medium airport in Moncton, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQM. Its two runways are 06/24 and 11/29. The longer one, 06/24, is 10,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 64 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 181 routes through CYQM. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Montréal (YUL) and Ottawa (YOW).
