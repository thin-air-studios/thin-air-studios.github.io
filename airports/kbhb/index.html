About KBHB

Hancock County-Bar Harbor Airport (BHB) is a medium airport in Bar Harbor, United States, with the ICAO code KBHB. Its two runways are 04/22 and 17/35. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,200 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Cape Air has 27 routes through KBHB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Boston (BOS).
