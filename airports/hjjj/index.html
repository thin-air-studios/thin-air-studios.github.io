About HJJJ

Juba International Airport (JUB) is a large airport in Juba, South Sudan, with the ICAO code HJJJ. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,171 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HJJJ.
