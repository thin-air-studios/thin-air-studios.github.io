About OS62

Jirah Air Base (OS62) is a small airport in Al Mahdum, Syria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,180 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OS62.
