About SARP

Libertador Gral D Jose De San Martin Airport (PSS) is a medium airport in Posadas, Argentina, with the ICAO code SARP. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,388 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 47 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 26 routes through SARP. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP) and Puerto Iguazu (IGR).
