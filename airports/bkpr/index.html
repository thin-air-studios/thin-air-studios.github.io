About BKPR

Priština Adem Jashari International Airport (PRN) is a large airport in Prishtina, Kosovo, with the ICAO code BKPR. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,974 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 43 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 54 airlines have 1,033 routes through BKPR. Its most frequent links are Bâle / Mulhouse (BSL), Geneva (GVA) and Zurich (ZRH).
