About LGTS

Thessaloniki Macedonia International Airport (SKG) is a large airport in Thessaloniki, Greece, with the ICAO code LGTS. Its two runways are 10/28 and 16/34. The longer one, 10/28, is 11,286 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 194 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 102 airlines have 1,985 routes through LGTS. Its most frequent links are Spata-Artemida (ATH), London (LGW) and Berlin (BER).
