About SARL

Paso De Los Libres Airport (AOL) is a medium airport in Paso de los Libres, Argentina, with the ICAO code SARL. It has one runway, 18/36, 7,415 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SARL.
