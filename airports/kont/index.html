About KONT

Ontario International Airport (ONT) is a large airport in Ontario, United States, with the ICAO code KONT. Its two runways are 08L/26R and 08R/26L. The longer one, 08L/26R, is 12,197 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 gates, 125 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 43 airlines have 1,064 routes through KONT. Its most frequent links are Honolulu (HNL), Louisville (SDF) and Denver (DEN).
