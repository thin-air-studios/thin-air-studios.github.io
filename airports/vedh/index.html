About VEDH

Darbhanga Airport (DBR) is a medium airport in Darbhanga, India, with the ICAO code VEDH. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VEDH.
