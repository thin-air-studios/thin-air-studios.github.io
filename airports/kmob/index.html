About KMOB

Mobile Regional Airport (MOB) is a medium airport in Mobile, United States, with the ICAO code KMOB. Its two runways are 15/33 and 18/36. The longer one, 15/33, is 8,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 102 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 70 routes through KMOB. Its most frequent links are Houston (IAH), Charlotte (CLT) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
