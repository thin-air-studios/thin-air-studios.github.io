About LGSM

Samos Airport (SMI) is a medium airport in Samos Island, Greece, with the ICAO code LGSM. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,706 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 243 routes through LGSM. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Spata-Artemida (ATH) and Stockholm (ARN).
