About KLVS

Las Vegas Municipal Airport (LVS) is a medium airport in Las Vegas, United States, with the ICAO code KLVS. Its two runways are 14/32 and 02/20. The longer one, 14/32, is 8,199 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLVS.
