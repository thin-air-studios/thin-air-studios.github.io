About YBUD

Bundaberg Airport (BDB) is a medium airport in Bundaberg, Australia, with the ICAO code YBUD. Its two runways are 14/32 and 07/25. The longer one, 14/32, is 6,562 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 61 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 33 routes through YBUD. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE) and Melbourne (MEL).
