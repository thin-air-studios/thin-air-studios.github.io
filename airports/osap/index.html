About OSAP

Aleppo International Airport (ALP) is a large airport in Aleppo, Syria, with the ICAO code OSAP. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,547 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 53 routes through OSAP. Its most frequent links are Sharjah (SHJ), Damascus (DAM) and Amman (AMM).
