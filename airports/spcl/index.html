About SPCL

Cap FAP David Abenzur Rengifo International Airport (PCL) is a large airport in Pucallpa, Peru, with the ICAO code SPCL. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 31 routes through SPCL. Its most frequent links are Lima (LIM) and Iquitos (IQT).
