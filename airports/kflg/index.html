About KFLG

Flagstaff Pulliam Airport (FLG) is a medium airport in Flagstaff, United States, with the ICAO code KFLG. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,800 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 64 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 24 routes through KFLG. Its most frequent links are Phoenix (PHX), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Denver (DEN).
