About VAOZ

Nashik International Airport (ISK) is a large airport in Nashik, India, with the ICAO code VAOZ. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, IndiGo has 1 routes through VAOZ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Hyderabad (HYD).
