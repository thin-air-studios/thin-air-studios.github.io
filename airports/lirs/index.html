About LIRS

Grosseto Corrado Baccarini Air Base / Grosseto Airport (GRS) is a medium airport in Grosseto (GR), Italy, with the ICAO code LIRS. Its two runways are 03/21 and 03R/21L. The longer one, 03/21, is 9,823 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through LIRS. Its most frequent links are Munich (MUC) and Warsaw (WAW).
