About EDNY

Bodensee Airport Friedrichshafen (FDH) is a large airport in Friedrichshafen, Germany, with the ICAO code EDNY. Its two runways are 06/24 and 06L/24R. The longer one, 06/24, is 7,729 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 52 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 337 routes through EDNY. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and London (LGW).
