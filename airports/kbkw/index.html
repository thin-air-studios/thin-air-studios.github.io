About KBKW

Raleigh County Memorial Airport (BKW) is a medium airport in Beaver, United States, with the ICAO code KBKW. Its two runways are 01/19 and 10/28. The longer one, 01/19, is 6,750 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 19 routes through KBKW. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Parkersburg (PKB) and Dulles (IAD).
