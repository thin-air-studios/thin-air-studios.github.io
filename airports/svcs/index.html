About SVCS

Caracas Óscar Machado Zuloaga International Airport (SVCS) is a medium airport in Charallave, Venezuela. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 6,562 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 93 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SVCS.
