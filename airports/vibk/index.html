About VIBK

Nal Airport (BKB) is a medium airport in Bikaner, India, with the ICAO code VIBK. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,960 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 146 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 6 routes through VIBK. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL) and Jaipur (JAI).
