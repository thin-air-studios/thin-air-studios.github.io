About LTAW

Tokat Airport (TJK) is a medium airport in Tokat, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAW. Its two runways are 06/24 and 04/22. The longer one, 06/24, is 8,858 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 21 routes through LTAW. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Pendik (SAW) and Gaziemir (ADB).
