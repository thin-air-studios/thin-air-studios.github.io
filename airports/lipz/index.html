About LIPZ

Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE) is a large airport in Venezia (VE), Italy, with the ICAO code LIPZ. Its two runways are 04R/22L and 04L/22R. The longer one, 04R/22L, is 10,827 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 92 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 25 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 123 airlines have 3,576 routes through LIPZ. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), London (LGW) and Paris (ORY).
