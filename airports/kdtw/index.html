About KDTW

Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport (DTW) is a large airport in Detroit, United States, with the ICAO code KDTW. It has 6 runways, from 8,500 ft to 12,003 ft long. The longest is 04R/22L. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 133 gates, 442 taxiway segments, 26 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 59 airlines have 1,960 routes through KDTW. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Newark (EWR) and Dulles (IAD).
