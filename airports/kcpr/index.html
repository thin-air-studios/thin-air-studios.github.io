About KCPR

Casper-Natrona County International Airport (CPR) is a medium airport in Casper, United States, with the ICAO code KCPR. Its two runways are 03/21 and 17/35. The longer one, 03/21, is 10,165 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 58 routes through KCPR. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Salt Lake City (SLC) and Scottsbluff (BFF).
