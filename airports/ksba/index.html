About KSBA

Santa Barbara Municipal Airport (SBA) is a medium airport in Santa Barbara, United States, with the ICAO code KSBA. Its 3 runways are 07/25, 15L/33R and 15R/33L. The longest, 07/25, is 6,037 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 181 routes through KSBA. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Los Angeles (LAX) and Phoenix (PHX).
