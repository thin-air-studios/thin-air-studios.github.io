About KMMU

Morristown Municipal Airport (MMU) is a medium airport in Morristown, United States, with the ICAO code KMMU. Its two runways are 05/23 and 13/31. The longer one, 05/23, is 5,998 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 12 routes through KMMU. Its most frequent links are Naples (APF), Boca Raton (BCT) and West Palm Beach (DJT).
