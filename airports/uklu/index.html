About UKLU

Uzhhorod International Airport (UDJ) is a medium airport in Uzhhorod, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKLU. It has one runway, 10/28, 6,686 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WRC has 2 routes through UKLU. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Boryspil (KBP).
