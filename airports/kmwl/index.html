About KMWL

Mineral Wells Regional Airport (MWL) is a medium airport in Mineral Wells, United States, with the ICAO code KMWL. Its two runways are 13/31 and 17/35. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,996 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMWL.
