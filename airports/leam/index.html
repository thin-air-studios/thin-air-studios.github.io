About LEAM

Almería Airport (LEI) is a medium airport in Almería, Spain, with the ICAO code LEAM. It has one runway, 07/25, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 31 airlines have 435 routes through LEAM. Its most frequent links are Madrid (MAD), London (LGW) and Barcelona (BCN).
