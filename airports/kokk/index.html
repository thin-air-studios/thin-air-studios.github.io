About KOKK

Kokomo Municipal Airport (OKK) is a small airport in Kokomo, United States, with the ICAO code KOKK. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 78 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KOKK.
