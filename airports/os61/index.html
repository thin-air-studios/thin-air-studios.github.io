About OS61

Dumayr Air Base (OS61) is a small airport in Syria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06L/24R, is 10,335 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 143 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OS61.
