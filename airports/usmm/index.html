About USMM

Nadym Airport (NYM) is a medium airport in Nadym, Russia, with the ICAO code USMM. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,360 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 88 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 30 routes through USMM. Its most frequent links are Yekaterinburg (SVX), Ufa (UFA) and Moscow (DME).
