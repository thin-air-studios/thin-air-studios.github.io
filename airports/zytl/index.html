About ZYTL

Dalian Zhoushuizi International Airport (DLC) is a large airport in Dalian (Ganjingzi), China, with the ICAO code ZYTL. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,827 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 28 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 47 airlines have 920 routes through ZYTL. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (PVG), Beijing (PEK) and Hangzhou (HGH).
