About GMMX

Marrakesh Menara Airport (RAK) is a large airport in Marrakesh, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMMX. It has one runway, 10/28, 10,170 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 gates, 107 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 72 airlines have 2,808 routes through GMMX. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), London (LGW) and Paris (CDG).
