About MDST

Cibao International Airport (STI) is a large airport in Santiago, Dominican Republic, with the ICAO code MDST. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,595 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 25 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 81 routes through MDST. Its most frequent links are New York (JFK), Newark (EWR) and Boston (BOS).
