About KAZO

Kalamazoo/Battle Creek International Airport (AZO) is a medium airport in Kalamazoo, United States, with the ICAO code KAZO. Its 3 runways are 17/35, 05/23 and 09/27. The longest, 17/35, is 6,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 66 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 51 routes through KAZO. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Detroit (DTW) and Washington (DCA).
