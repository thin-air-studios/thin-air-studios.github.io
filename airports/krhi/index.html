About KRHI

Rhinelander Oneida County Airport (RHI) is a medium airport in Rhinelander, United States, with the ICAO code KRHI. Its two runways are 09/27 and 15/33. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,800 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 39 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 18 routes through KRHI. Its most frequent links are Minneapolis (MSP), Milwaukee (MKE) and Madison (MSN).
