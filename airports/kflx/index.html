About KFLX

Fallon Municipal Airport (FLX) is a small airport in Fallon, United States, with the ICAO code KFLX. Its two runways are 03/21 and 13/31. The longer one, 03/21, is 5,705 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KFLX.
