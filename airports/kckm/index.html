About KCKM

Fletcher Field (CKM) is a small airport in Clarksdale, United States, with the ICAO code KCKM. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,404 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCKM.
