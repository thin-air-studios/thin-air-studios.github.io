About ETNH

Hohn Air Base (ETNH) is a medium airport in Hohn, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,005 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 68 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETNH.
