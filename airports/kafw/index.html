About KAFW

Perot Field/Fort Worth Alliance Airport (AFW) is a medium airport in Fort Worth, United States, with the ICAO code KAFW. Its two runways are 16R/34L and 16L/34R. The longer one, 16R/34L, is 11,125 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 72 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 146 routes through KAFW. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), Chicago (ORD) and Tulsa (TUL).
