About LOWI

Innsbruck Airport (INN) is a large airport in Innsbruck, Austria, with the ICAO code LOWI. Its two runways are 08/26 and 08G/28G. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,562 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 873 routes through LOWI. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Vienna (VIE) and Bristol (BRS).
