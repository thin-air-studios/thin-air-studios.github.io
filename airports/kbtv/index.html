About KBTV

Patrick Leahy Burlington International Airport (BTV) is a medium airport in Burlington, United States, with the ICAO code KBTV. Its two runways are 15/33 and 01/19. The longer one, 15/33, is 8,319 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 77 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 256 routes through KBTV. Its most frequent links are Dulles (IAD), Chicago (ORD) and Philadelphia (PHL).
