About WART

Trunojoyo Airport (SUP) is a small airport in Sumenep, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WART. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,249 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WON has 2 routes through WART. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Surabaya (SUB).
