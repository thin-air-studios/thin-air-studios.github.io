About OMSJ

Sharjah International Airport (SHJ) is a large airport in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, with the ICAO code OMSJ. It has one runway, 12/30, 13,320 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 172 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 95 airlines have 1,209 routes through OMSJ. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Doha (DOH) and Singapore (SIN).
