About FSIA

Seychelles International Airport (SEZ) is a large airport in Victoria, Seychelles, with the ICAO code FSIA. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,800 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 100 routes through FSIA. Its most frequent links are Plaine Magnien (MRU), Abu Dhabi (AUH) and Frankfurt am Main (FRA).
