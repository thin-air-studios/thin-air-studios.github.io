About KSUT

Cape Fear Regional Jetport / Howie Franklin Field Airport (KSUT) is a small airport in Southport, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 5,505 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments and 1 aprons.
