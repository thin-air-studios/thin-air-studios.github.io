About KBAF

Westfield-Barnes Regional Airport (BAF) is a medium airport in Westfield, United States, with the ICAO code KBAF. Its two runways are 02/20 and 15/33. The longer one, 02/20, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 112 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KBAF.
