About KASE

Aspen-Pitkin County Airport (Sardy Field) (ASE) is a medium airport in Aspen, United States, with the ICAO code KASE. It has one runway, 15/33, 8,006 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 68 routes through KASE. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Chicago (ORD) and Los Angeles (LAX).
