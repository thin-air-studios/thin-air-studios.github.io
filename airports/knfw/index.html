About KNFW

NAS Fort Worth JRB / Carswell Field (FWH) is a medium airport in Fort Worth, United States, with the ICAO code KNFW. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,999 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNFW.
