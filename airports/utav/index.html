About UTAV

Türkmenabat International Airport (CRZ) is a large airport in Türkmenabat, Turkmenistan, with the ICAO code UTAV. It has one runway, 13/31, 12,467 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 34 routes through UTAV. Its most frequent links are Ashgabat (ASB), Istanbul (IST) and Hong Kong (HKG).
