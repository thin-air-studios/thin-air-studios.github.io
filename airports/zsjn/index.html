About ZSJN

Jinan Yaoqiang International Airport (TNA) is a large airport in Jinan (Licheng), China, with the ICAO code ZSJN. It has one runway, 01/19, 11,812 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 gates, 174 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 55 airlines have 765 routes through ZSJN. Its most frequent links are Shenzhen (SZX), Chengdu (TFU) and Chongqing (CKG).
