About KIDI

Indiana County–Jimmy Stewart Airport (IDI) is a small airport in Indiana, United States, with the ICAO code KIDI. It has one runway, 11/29, 5,502 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KIDI.
