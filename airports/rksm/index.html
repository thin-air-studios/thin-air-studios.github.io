About RKSM

Seoul Air Base (K-16) (SSN) is a medium airport in Seongnam, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKSM. Its two runways are 02/20 and 01/19. The longer one, 02/20, is 9,700 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 166 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through RKSM. Its most frequent links are Nowy Dwór Mazowiecki (WMI) and Shanghai (PVG).
