About LQTZ

Tuzla International Airport (TZL) is a large airport in Dubrave Gornje, Bosnia & Herzegovina, with the ICAO code LQTZ. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,152 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 241 routes through LQTZ. Its most frequent links are Bâle / Mulhouse (BSL), Memmingen (FMM) and Frankfurt am Main (HHN).
