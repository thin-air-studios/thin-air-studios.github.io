About VGHS

Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) is a large airport in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with the ICAO code VGHS. It has one runway, 14/32, 11,500 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 148 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 84 airlines have 880 routes through VGHS. Its most frequent links are Chattogram (CGP), Sylhet (ZYL) and Cox's Bazar (CXB).
