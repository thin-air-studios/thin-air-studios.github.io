About ESTA

Ängelholm-Helsingborg Airport (AGH) is a medium airport in Ängelholm, Sweden, with the ICAO code ESTA. It has one runway, 14/32, 6,381 ft long and 131 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 86 routes through ESTA. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (BMA), Stockholm (ARN) and Visby (VBY).
