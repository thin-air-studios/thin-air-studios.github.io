About DAUB

Biskra - Mohamed Khider Airport (BSK) is a large airport in Biskra, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAUB. It has one runway, 13/31, 9,469 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 50 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 21 routes through DAUB. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Algiers (ALG) and Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS).
