About OYAA

Aden International Airport (ADE) is a large airport in Aden, Yemen, with the ICAO code OYAA. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,171 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 40 routes through OYAA. Its most frequent links are Cairo (CAI), Jeddah (JED) and Amman (AMM).
