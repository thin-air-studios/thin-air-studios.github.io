About CYSG

Saint-Georges Airport (CYSG) is a medium airport in Saint-Georges, Canada. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 06/24, 5,108 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYSG.
