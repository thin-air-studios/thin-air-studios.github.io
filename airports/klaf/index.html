About KLAF

Purdue University Airport (LAF) is a medium airport in West Lafayette, United States, with the ICAO code KLAF. Its two runways are 10/28 and 05/23. The longer one, 10/28, is 6,600 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 19 routes through KLAF. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Chicago (ORD).
