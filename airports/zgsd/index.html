About ZGSD

Zhuhai Jinwan Airport (ZUH) is a large airport in Zhuhai (Jinwan), China, with the ICAO code ZGSD. It has one runway, 05/23, 13,517 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 gates, 80 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 41 airlines have 690 routes through ZGSD. Its most frequent links are Haikou (HAK), Nanjing (NKG) and Hangzhou (HGH).
