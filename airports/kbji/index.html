About KBJI

Bemidji Regional Airport (BJI) is a medium airport in Bemidji, United States, with the ICAO code KBJI. Its two runways are 13/31 and 07/25. The longer one, 13/31, is 7,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 13 routes through KBJI. Its most frequent links are Minneapolis (MSP) and Fargo (FAR).
