About VTST

Trang Airport (TST) is a medium airport in Trang, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTST. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,890 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 21 routes through VTST. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Bangkok (DMK).
