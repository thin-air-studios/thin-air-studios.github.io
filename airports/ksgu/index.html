About KSGU

St George Regional Airport (SGU) is a medium airport in St George, United States, with the ICAO code KSGU. It has one runway, 1/19, 9,300 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 73 routes through KSGU. Its most frequent links are Salt Lake City (SLC), Denver (DEN) and Phoenix (PHX).
