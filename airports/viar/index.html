About VIAR

Sri Guru Ram Das Ji International Airport (ATQ) is a large airport in Amritsar, India, with the ICAO code VIAR. It has one runway, 16/34, 12,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 105 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 263 routes through VIAR. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Sepang (KUL).
