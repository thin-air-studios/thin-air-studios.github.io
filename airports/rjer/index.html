About RJER

Rishiri Airport (RIS) is a medium airport in Rishiri, Japan, with the ICAO code RJER. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,906 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 11 routes through RJER. Its most frequent links are Sapporo (OKD) and Sapporo (CTS).
