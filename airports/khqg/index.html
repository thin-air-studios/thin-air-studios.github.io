About KHQG

Hugoton Municipal Airport (KHQG) is a small airport in Hugoton, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 02/20 and 13/31. The longer one, 02/20, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHQG.
