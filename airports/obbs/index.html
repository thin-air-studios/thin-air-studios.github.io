About OBBS

Sheik Isa Air Base (OBBS) is a medium airport in Sitrah, Bahrain. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 12,467 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 12 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OBBS.
