About LHPR

Győr-Pér Airport (LHPR) is a medium airport in Győr, Hungary. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 11/29 and 12L/30R. The longer one, 11/29, is 6,660 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through LHPR. Its most frequent links are Schkeuditz (LEJ) and Göteborg (GOT).
