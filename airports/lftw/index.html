About LFTW

Nîmes-Arles-Camargue Airport (FNI) is a medium airport in Nîmes/Garons, France, with the ICAO code LFTW. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,005 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 205 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 73 routes through LFTW. Its most frequent links are Charleroi (CRL), Luton (LTN) and Saïss (FEZ).
