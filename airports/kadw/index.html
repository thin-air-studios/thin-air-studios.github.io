About KADW

Joint Base Andrews (ADW) is a medium airport in Camp Springs, United States, with the ICAO code KADW. Its two runways are 01L/19R and 01R/19L. The longer one, 01L/19R, is 11,301 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 182 taxiway segments, 45 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 20 routes through KADW. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Atlanta (ATL) and Otopeni (OTP).
