About HLTQ

Tobruk International Airport (TOB) is a small airport in Adam, Libya, with the ICAO code HLTQ. Its 3 runways are 02/20, 15/33 and 09/27. The longest, 02/20, is 9,895 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HLTQ.
