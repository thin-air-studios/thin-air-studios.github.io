About LICZ

Sigonella Navy Air Base (LICZ) is a medium airport in Sigonella (CT), Italy. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10R/28L and 10L/28R. The longer one, 10R/28L, is 8,077 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 65 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, UPS has 2 routes through LICZ. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Manama (BAH).
