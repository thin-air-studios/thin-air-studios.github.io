About WIGG

Fatmawati Soekarno Airport (BKS) is a medium airport in Bengkulu, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIGG. It has one runway, 13/31, 7,345 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 28 routes through WIGG. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Batam (BTH) and Palembang (PLM).
