About YCFS

Coffs Harbour Airport (CFS) is a medium airport in Coffs Harbour, Australia, with the ICAO code YCFS. Its two runways are 03/21 and 10/28. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,824 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 97 routes through YCFS. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Melbourne (MEL) and Brisbane (BNE).
