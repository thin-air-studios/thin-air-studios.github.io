About KLAS

Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) is a large airport in Las Vegas, United States, with the ICAO code KLAS. It is number 30 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 55.0 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 08L/26R, 08R/26L, 01L/19R and 01R/19L. The longest, 08L/26R, is 14,835 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 110 gates, 376 taxiway segments, 25 aprons and 12 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 67 airlines have 2,951 routes through KLAS. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Los Angeles (LAX) and Denver (DEN).
