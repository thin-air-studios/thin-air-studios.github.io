About LFCK

Castres Mazamet Airport (DCM) is a medium airport in Castres, France, with the ICAO code LFCK. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,988 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 27 routes through LFCK. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Ajaccio (AJA) and Aurillac (AUR).
