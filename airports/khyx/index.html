About KHYX

Saginaw County H.W. Browne Airport (KHYX) is a small airport in Saginaw, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10/28 and 05/23. The longer one, 10/28, is 5,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHYX.
