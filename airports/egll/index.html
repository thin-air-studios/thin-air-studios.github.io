About EGLL

London Heathrow Airport (LHR) is a large airport in London, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGLL. It is number 7 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 84.5 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 09L/27R and 09R/27L. The longer one, 09L/27R, is 12,799 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 148 gates, 417 taxiway segments, 39 aprons and 14 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 186 airlines have 7,942 routes through EGLL. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Madrid (MAD) and Doha (DOH).
