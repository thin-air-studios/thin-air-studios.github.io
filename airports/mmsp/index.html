About MMSP

Ponciano Arriaga International Airport (SLP) is a medium airport in San Luis Potosí, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMSP. Its two runways are 14/32 and 04/22. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,867 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 56 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 134 routes through MMSP. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Monterrey (MTY) and Mexico City (NLU).
