About KLVK

Livermore Municipal Airport (LVK) is a small airport in Livermore, United States, with the ICAO code KLVK. Its two runways are 07L/25R and 07R/25L. The longer one, 07L/25R, is 5,253 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLVK.
