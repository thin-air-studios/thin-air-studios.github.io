About KGDV

Dawson Community Airport (GDV) is a medium airport in Glendive, United States, with the ICAO code KGDV. Its two runways are 12/30 and 02/20. The longer one, 12/30, is 5,704 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AIP has 1 routes through KGDV. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Billings (BIL).
