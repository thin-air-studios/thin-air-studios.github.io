About UKDD

Dnipro International Airport (DNK) is a medium airport in Dnipro, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKDD. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,320 ft long and 144 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 170 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 65 routes through UKDD. Its most frequent links are Boryspil (KBP), Burgas (BOJ) and Sharm El Sheikh (SSH).
