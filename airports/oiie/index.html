About OIIE

Imam Khomeini International Airport (IKA) is a large airport in Tehran, Iran, with the ICAO code OIIE. Its two runways are 11L/29R and 11R/29L. The longer one, 11L/29R, is 13,772 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 234 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 63 airlines have 453 routes through OIIE. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Dubai (DXB) and Pendik (SAW).
