About UKBB

Boryspil International Airport (KBP) is a large airport in Boryspil, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKBB. Its two runways are 18L/36R and 18R/36L. The longer one, 18L/36R, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 gates, 510 taxiway segments, 29 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 78 airlines have 1,417 routes through UKBB. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH) and Hurghada (HRG).
