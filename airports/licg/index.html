About LICG

Pantelleria Airport (PNL) is a medium airport in Pantelleria (TP), Italy, with the ICAO code LICG. Its two runways are 07/25 and 02/20. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,495 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 59 routes through LICG. Its most frequent links are Palermo (PMO), Trapani (TPS) and Orio al Serio (BGY).
