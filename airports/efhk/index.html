About EFHK

Helsinki Vantaa Airport (HEL) is a large airport in Helsinki (Vantaa), Finland, with the ICAO code EFHK. Its 3 runways are 04R/22L, 04L/22R and 15/33. The longest, 04R/22L, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 70 gates, 279 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 137 airlines have 5,501 routes through EFHK. Its most frequent links are Stockholm (ARN), Copenhagen (CPH) and Oulu / Oulunsalo (OUL).
