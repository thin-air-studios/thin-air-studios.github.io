About CYWG

Winnipeg / James Armstrong Richardson International Airport (YWG) is a large airport in Winnipeg, Canada, with the ICAO code CYWG. Its two runways are 18/36 and 13/31. The longer one, 18/36, is 11,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 120 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 602 routes through CYWG. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Calgary (YYC) and Vancouver (YVR).
