About KLIT

Bill & Hillary Clinton National Airport/Adams Field (LIT) is a medium airport in Little Rock, United States, with the ICAO code KLIT. Its 3 runways are 04L/22R, 04R/22L and 18/36. The longest, 04L/22R, is 8,273 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 69 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 214 routes through KLIT. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Houston (IAH) and Denver (DEN).
