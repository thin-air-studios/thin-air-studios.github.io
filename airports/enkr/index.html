About ENKR

Kirkenes Airport, Høybuktmoen (KKN) is a medium airport in Kirkenes, Norway, with the ICAO code ENKR. It has one runway, 05/23, 6,939 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 28 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 84 routes through ENKR. Its most frequent links are Tromsø (TOS), Vadsø (VDS) and Alta (ALF).
