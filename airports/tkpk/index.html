About TKPK

Robert L. Bradshaw International Airport (SKB) is a large airport in Basseterre, St. Kitts & Nevis, with the ICAO code TKPK. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,602 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 42 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 65 routes through TKPK. Its most frequent links are Sint Maarten (SXM), Bridgetown (BGI) and Osbourn (ANU).
