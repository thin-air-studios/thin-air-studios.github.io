About KLBE

Arnold Palmer Regional Airport (LBE) is a medium airport in Latrobe, United States, with the ICAO code KLBE. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,222 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 35 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KLBE.
