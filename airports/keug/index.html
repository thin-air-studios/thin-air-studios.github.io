About KEUG

Eugene Airport (EUG) is a medium airport in Eugene, United States, with the ICAO code KEUG. Its two runways are 16R/34L and 16L/34R. The longer one, 16R/34L, is 8,009 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 159 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 15 airlines have 201 routes through KEUG. Its most frequent links are Seattle (SEA), Portland (PDX) and Denver (DEN).
