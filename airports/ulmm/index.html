About ULMM

Emperor Nicholas II Murmansk Airport (MMK) is a large airport in Murmansk, Russia, with the ICAO code ULMM. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,202 ft long and 138 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 66 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 110 routes through ULMM. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), St. Petersburg (LED) and Moscow (DME).
