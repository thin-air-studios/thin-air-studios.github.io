About EBOS

Ostend-Bruges International Airport (OST) is a large airport in Oostende, Belgium, with the ICAO code EBOS. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 403 routes through EBOS. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Tenerife (TFS) and Alicante (ALC).
