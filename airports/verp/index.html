About VERP

Swami Vivekananda Airport (RPR) is a medium airport in Raipur, India, with the ICAO code VERP. It has one runway, 06/24, 6,414 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 22 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 167 routes through VERP. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Hyderabad (HYD) and Mumbai (BOM).
