About LFRC

Cherbourg Manche airport (CER) is a medium airport in Cherbourg, France, with the ICAO code LFRC. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,005 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JOL has 1 routes through LFRC. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Kristiansand (KRS).
