About FTTJ

N'Djamena International Airport (NDJ) is a large airport in N'Djamena, Chad, with the ICAO code FTTJ. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 39 routes through FTTJ. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Addis Ababa (ADD) and Abuja (ABV).
