About LERS

Reus Airport (REU) is a large airport in Reus, Spain, with the ICAO code LERS. It has one runway, 07/25, 8,054 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 gates, 21 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 555 routes through LERS. Its most frequent links are Manchester (MAN), Dublin (DUB) and London (STN).
