About CYXL

Sioux Lookout Airport (YXL) is a medium airport in Sioux Lookout, Canada, with the ICAO code CYXL. It has one runway, 16/34, 5,300 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 13 routes through CYXL. Its most frequent links are Thunder Bay (YQT) and Winnipeg (YWG).
