About KALO

Waterloo Regional Airport (ALO) is a medium airport in Waterloo, United States, with the ICAO code KALO. Its two runways are 12/30 and 18/36. The longer one, 12/30, is 8,399 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 16 routes through KALO. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD) and Dulles (IAD).
