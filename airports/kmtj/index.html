About KMTJ

Montrose Regional Airport (MTJ) is a medium airport in Montrose, United States, with the ICAO code KMTJ. Its two runways are 17/35 and 13/31. The longer one, 17/35, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 103 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 49 routes through KMTJ. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Chicago (ORD) and Santa Ana (SNA).
