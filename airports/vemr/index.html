About VEMR

Dimapur Airport (DMU) is a medium airport in Dimapur, India, with the ICAO code VEMR. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,513 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 21 routes through VEMR. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Guwahati (GAU) and Kolkata (CCU).
