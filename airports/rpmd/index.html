About RPMD

Francisco Bangoy International Airport (DVO) is a large airport in Davao, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPMD. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 57 taxiway segments, 13 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 405 routes through RPMD. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL), Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB) and RPVT (RPVT).
