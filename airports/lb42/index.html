About LB42

Kamenets Air Base (LB42) is a small airport in Kamenets, Bulgaria. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,200 ft long and 262 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LB42.
