About LYBE

Belgrade Nikola Tesla Airport (BEG) is a large airport in Belgrade, Serbia, with the ICAO code LYBE. Its two runways are 12R/30L and 12L/30R. The longer one, 12R/30L, is 11,483 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 87 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 86 airlines have 2,764 routes through LYBE. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Zurich (ZRH) and Tivat (TIV).
