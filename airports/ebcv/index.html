About EBCV

Chièvres Air Base (EBCV) is a medium airport in Chièvres, Belgium. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,197 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments and 44 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EBCV.
