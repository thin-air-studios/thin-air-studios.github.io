About LZPP

Piešťany Airport (PZY) is a medium airport in Piešťany, Slovakia, with the ICAO code LZPP. It has one runway, 01/19, 6,562 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 16 taxiway segments, 33 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 14 routes through LZPP. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Tel Aviv (TLV) and Hurghada (HRG).
