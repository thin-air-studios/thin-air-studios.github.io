About SBMT

Campo de Marte Airport (RTE) is a medium airport in São Paulo, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBMT. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,249 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 124 taxiway segments and 40 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SBMT.
