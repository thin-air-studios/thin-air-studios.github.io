About LEBG

Burgos Airport (RGS) is a medium airport in Burgos, Spain, with the ICAO code LEBG. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,890 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 8 routes through LEBG. Its most frequent links are Barcelona (BCN) and Ibiza (IBZ).
