About KNRS

Naval Outlying Field Imperial Beach (Ream Field) (NRS) is a small airport in Imperial Beach, United States, with the ICAO code KNRS. Its two runways are 09/27 and 08/26. The longer one, 09/27, is 4,998 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KNRS.
