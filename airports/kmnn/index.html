About KMNN

Marion Municipal Airport (MNN) is a small airport in Marion, United States, with the ICAO code KMNN. Its two runways are 07/25 and 13/31. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMNN.
