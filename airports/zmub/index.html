About ZMUB

Buyant-Ukhaa International Airport (ULN) is a large airport in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, with the ICAO code ZMUB. Its two runways are 14/32 and 15/33. The longer one, 14/32, is 10,170 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 75 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 40 routes through ZMUB. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Narita (NRT) and Paris (CDG).
