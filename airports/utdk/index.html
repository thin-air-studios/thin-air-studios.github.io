About UTDK

Kulob International Airport (TJU) is a large airport in Kulob, Tajikistan, with the ICAO code UTDK. It has one runway, 01/19, 9,843 ft long and 137 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 28 routes through UTDK. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Novosibirsk (OVB) and Yekaterinburg (SVX).
