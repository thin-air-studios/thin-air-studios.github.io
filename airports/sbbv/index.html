About SBBV

Atlas Brasil Cantanhede International Airport (BVB) is a large airport in Boa Vista, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBBV. It has one runway, 8/26, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 48 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 22 routes through SBBV. Its most frequent links are Manaus (MAO), Brasília (BSB) and São Paulo (GRU).
