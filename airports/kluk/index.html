About KLUK

Cincinnati Municipal Airport Lunken Field (LUK) is a medium airport in Cincinnati, United States, with the ICAO code KLUK. Its 3 runways are 03R/21L, 07/25 and 03L/21R. The longest, 03R/21L, is 6,101 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 55 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, LBQ has 3 routes through KLUK. Its most frequent links are Pittsburgh (AGC), Columbus (CMH) and Chicago/West Chicago (DPA).
