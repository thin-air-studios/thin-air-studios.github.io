About HABD

Bahir Dar Airport (BJR) is a medium airport in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, with the ICAO code HABD. It has one runway, 04/22, 9,842 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Ethiopian has 25 routes through HABD. Its most frequent links are Addis Ababa (ADD) and HADC (HADC).
