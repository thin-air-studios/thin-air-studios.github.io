About LFSX

Luxeuil-Saint-Sauveur (BA 116) Air Base (LFSX) is a medium airport in Saint-Sauveur, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 11/29 and 04/22. The longer one, 11/29, is 7,982 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 143 taxiway segments and 27 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFSX.
