About KVEL

Vernal Regional Airport (VEL) is a medium airport in Vernal, United States, with the ICAO code KVEL. It has one runway, 17/35, 7,000 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through KVEL. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Phoenix (PHX) and Price (PUC).
