About KFHU

Sierra Vista Municipal Airport / Libby Army Air Field (FHU) is a medium airport in Fort Huachuca / Sierra Vista, United States, with the ICAO code KFHU. Its 3 runways are 08/26, 12/30 and 03/21. The longest, 08/26, is 12,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 taxiway segments, 16 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 3 routes through KFHU. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Phoenix (PHX).
