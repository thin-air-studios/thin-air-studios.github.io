About HLMS

Misrata International Airport (MRA) is a small airport in Misrata, Libya, with the ICAO code HLMS. It has one runway, 15/33, 11,155 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 66 routes through HLMS. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Benina (BEN) and Cairo (CAI).
