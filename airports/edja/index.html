About EDJA

Memmingen Allgau Airport (FMM) is a large airport in Memmingen, Germany, with the ICAO code EDJA. It has one runway, 06/24, 8,629 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 22 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 21 airlines have 952 routes through EDJA. Its most frequent links are Sofia (SOF), London (STN) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI).
