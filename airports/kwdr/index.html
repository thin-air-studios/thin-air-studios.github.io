About KWDR

Barrow County Airport (WDR) is a small airport in Winder, United States, with the ICAO code KWDR. Its two runways are 13/31 and 05/23. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,202 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KWDR.
