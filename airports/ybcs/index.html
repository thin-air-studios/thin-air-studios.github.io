About YBCS

Cairns International Airport (CNS) is a large airport in Cairns, Australia, with the ICAO code YBCS. It has one runway, 15/33, 10,489 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 156 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 30 airlines have 447 routes through YBCS. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Sydney (SYD) and Townsville (TSV).
