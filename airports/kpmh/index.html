About KPMH

Greater Portsmouth Regional Airport (PMH) is a small airport in Portsmouth, United States, with the ICAO code KPMH. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,001 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KPMH.
