About KPQI

Presque Isle International Airport (PQI) is a medium airport in Presque Isle, United States, with the ICAO code KPQI. Its two runways are 01/19 and 10/28. The longer one, 01/19, is 7,441 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 12 routes through KPQI. Its most frequent links are Portland (PWM), Boston (BOS) and Manchester (MHT).
