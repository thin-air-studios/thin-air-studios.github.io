About EGTE

Exeter International Airport (EXT) is a medium airport in Exeter, Devon, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGTE. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 36 airlines have 279 routes through EGTE. Its most frequent links are St. Peter (JER), St. Mary's (ISC) and Paphos (PFO).
