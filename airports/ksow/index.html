About KSOW

Show Low Regional Airport (SOW) is a medium airport in Show Low, United States, with the ICAO code KSOW. Its two runways are 07/25 and 04/22. The longer one, 07/25, is 7,202 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 15 routes through KSOW. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Phoenix (PHX).
