About OJAM

Marka International (Amman Civil) Airport (ADJ) is a large airport in Amman, Jordan, with the ICAO code OJAM. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,745 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 38 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 38 routes through OJAM. Its most frequent links are Toronto (YYZ), Kuwait City (KWI) and Montréal (YUL).
