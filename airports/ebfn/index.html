About EBFN

Koksijde Air Base (EBFN) is a medium airport in Koksijde, Belgium. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 11/29 and 02/20. The longer one, 11/29, is 8,786 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EBFN.
