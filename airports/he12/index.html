About HE12

Inshas Air Base (HE12) is a small airport in Inshas, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 09/27 and 04/22. The longer one, 09/27, is 8,646 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE12.
