About PTRO

Roman Tmetuchl International Airport (ROR) is a large airport in Babelthuap Island, Palau, with the ICAO code PTRO. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,200 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 53 routes through PTRO. Its most frequent links are Hong Kong (HKG), Nossa Senhora do Carmo (MFM) and Hagåtña (GUM).
