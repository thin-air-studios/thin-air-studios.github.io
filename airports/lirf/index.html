About LIRF

Rome–Fiumicino Leonardo da Vinci International Airport (FCO) is a large airport in Rome, Italy, with the ICAO code LIRF. It is number 40 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 50.9 million passengers in 2025. Its 3 runways are 16L/34R, 16R/34L and 07/25. The longest, 16L/34R, is 12,801 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 65 gates, 231 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 14 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 210 airlines have 8,073 routes through LIRF. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Madrid (MAD) and Paris (CDG).
