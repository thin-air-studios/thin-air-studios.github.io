About KCAE

Columbia Metropolitan Airport (CAE) is a medium airport in Columbia, United States, with the ICAO code KCAE. Its two runways are 11/29 and 05/23. The longer one, 11/29, is 8,601 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 52 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 242 routes through KCAE. Its most frequent links are Philadelphia (PHL), Charlotte (CLT) and Chicago (ORD).
