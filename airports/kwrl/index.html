About KWRL

Worland Municipal Airport (WRL) is a medium airport in Worland, United States, with the ICAO code KWRL. Its 3 runways are 16/34, 10/28 and 04/22. The longest, 16/34, is 7,000 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KWRL.
