About SADP

El Palomar Airport (EPA) is a medium airport in El Palomar, Argentina, with the ICAO code SADP. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,923 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 22 routes through SADP. Its most frequent links are Mendoza (MDZ), Corrientes (CNQ) and Asunción (ASU).
