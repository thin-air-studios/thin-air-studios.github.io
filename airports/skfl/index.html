About SKFL

Gustavo Artunduaga Paredes Airport (FLA) is a medium airport in Florencia, Colombia, with the ICAO code SKFL. It has one runway, 12/30, 4,921 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 16 routes through SKFL. Its most frequent links are Bogota (BOG) and Cali (CLO).
