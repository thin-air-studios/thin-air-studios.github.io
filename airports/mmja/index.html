About MMJA

El Lencero Airport (JAL) is a medium airport in Emiliano Zapata, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMJA. It has one runway, 08/26, 5,577 ft long and 82 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MMJA.
