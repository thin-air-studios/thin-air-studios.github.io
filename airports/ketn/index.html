About KETN

Eastland Municipal Airport (ETN) is a small airport in Eastland, United States, with the ICAO code KETN. It has one runway, 17/35, 5,000 ft long and 60 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KETN.
