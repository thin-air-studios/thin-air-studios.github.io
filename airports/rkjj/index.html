About RKJJ

Gwangju Airport (KWJ) is a medium airport in Gwangju, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKJJ. Its two runways are 04L/22R and 04R/22L. The longer one, 04L/22R, is 9,300 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 197 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 82 routes through RKJJ. Its most frequent links are Jeju City (CJU) and Seoul (GMP).
