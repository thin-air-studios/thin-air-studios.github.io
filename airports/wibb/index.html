About WIBB

Sultan Syarif Kasim II International Airport / Roesmin Nurjadin AFB (PKU) is a medium airport in Pekanbaru, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIBB. It has one runway, 18/36, 7,360 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 52 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 159 routes through WIBB. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Sepang (KUL) and Batam (BTH).
