About KRPJ

Rochelle Municipal Airport - Koritz Field (KRPJ) is a small airport in Rochelle, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,001 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments and 3 aprons.
