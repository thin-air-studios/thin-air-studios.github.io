About SBFI

Cataratas International Airport (IGU) is a large airport in Foz do Iguaçu, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBFI. It has one runway, 15/33, 8,875 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 156 routes through SBFI. Its most frequent links are Rio De Janeiro (GIG), São Paulo (GRU) and Curitiba (CWB).
