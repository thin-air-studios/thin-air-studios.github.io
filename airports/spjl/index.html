About SPJL

Inca Manco Capac International Airport (JUL) is a large airport in Juliaca, Peru, with the ICAO code SPJL. It has one runway, 12/30, 13,780 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 29 routes through SPJL. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Lima (LIM).
