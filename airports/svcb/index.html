About SVCB

General Tomas de Heres Airport (CBL) is a medium airport in Ciudad Bolivar, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVCB. It has one runway, 07/25, 5,527 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, RUC has 4 routes through SVCB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Maiquetía (CCS).
