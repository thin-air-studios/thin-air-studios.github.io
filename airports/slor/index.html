About SLOR

Juan Mendoza International Airport (ORU) is a large airport in Oruro, Bolivia, with the ICAO code SLOR. Its two runways are 18/36 and 09/27. The longer one, 18/36, is 8,075 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SLOR.
