About KLAX

Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is a large airport in Los Angeles, United States, with the ICAO code KLAX. It is number 13 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 73.7 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 7L/25R, 7R/25L, 6R/24L and 6L/24R. The longest, 7L/25R, is 12,894 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 148 gates, 382 taxiway segments, 24 aprons and 25 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 167 airlines have 5,144 routes through KLAX. Its most frequent links are San Francisco (SFO), Seattle (SEA) and New York (JFK).
