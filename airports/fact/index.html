About FACT

Cape Town International Airport (CPT) is a large airport in Cape Town, South Africa, with the ICAO code FACT. Its two runways are 01/19 and 16/34. The longer one, 01/19, is 10,502 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 151 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 37 airlines have 1,007 routes through FACT. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Durban (DUR) and Johannesburg (HLA).
