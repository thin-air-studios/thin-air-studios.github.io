About CYXY

Whitehorse / Erik Nielsen International Airport (YXY) is a medium airport in Whitehorse, Canada, with the ICAO code CYXY. Its two runways are 14R/32L and 14L/32R. The longer one, 14R/32L, is 9,497 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 105 routes through CYXY. Its most frequent links are Vancouver (YVR), Calgary (YYC) and Edmonton (YEG).
