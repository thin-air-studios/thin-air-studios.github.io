About MMLM

Valle del Fuerte International Airport (LMM) is a medium airport in Los Mochis, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMLM. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 42 routes through MMLM. Its most frequent links are Guadalajara (GDL), Mexico City (MEX) and Tijuana (TIJ).
