About MUCF

Jaime Gonzalez Airport (CFG) is a medium airport in Cienfuegos, Cuba, with the ICAO code MUCF. It has one runway, 02/20, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 18 routes through MUCF. Its most frequent links are Miami (MIA), Toronto (YYZ) and Montréal (YUL).
