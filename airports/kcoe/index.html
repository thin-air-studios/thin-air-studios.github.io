About KCOE

Coeur D'Alene Airport - Pappy Boyington Field (COE) is a medium airport in Coeur d'Alene, United States, with the ICAO code KCOE. Its two runways are 06/24 and 02/20. The longer one, 06/24, is 7,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 89 taxiway segments and 12 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCOE.
