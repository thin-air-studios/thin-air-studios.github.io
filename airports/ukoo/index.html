About UKOO

Odesa International Airport (ODS) is a large airport in Odesa, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKOO. Its two runways are 16/34 and 07/25. The longer one, 16/34, is 9,186 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 191 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 33 airlines have 196 routes through UKOO. Its most frequent links are Sharm El Sheikh (SSH), Antalya (AYT) and Boryspil (KBP).
