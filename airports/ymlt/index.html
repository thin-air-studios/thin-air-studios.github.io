About YMLT

Launceston Airport (LST) is a medium airport in Launceston (Western Junction), Australia, with the ICAO code YMLT. Its 3 runways are 14R/32L, 14L/32R and 18/36. The longest, 14R/32L, is 6,499 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 2 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 174 routes through YMLT. Its most frequent links are Melbourne (MEL), Sydney (SYD) and Brisbane (BNE).
