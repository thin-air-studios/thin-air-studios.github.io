About WILL

Radin Inten II International Airport (TKG) is a medium airport in Bandar Lampung, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WILL. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,088 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 67 routes through WILL. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Batam (BTH) and Yogyakarta (YIA).
