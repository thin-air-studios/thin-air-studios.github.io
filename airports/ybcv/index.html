About YBCV

Charleville Airport (CTL) is a medium airport in Charleville, Australia, with the ICAO code YBCV. Its two runways are 12/30 and 18/36. The longer one, 12/30, is 5,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 6 routes through YBCV. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Roma (RMA) and Toowoomba (WTB).
