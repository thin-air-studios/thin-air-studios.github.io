About RJNO

Oki Global Geopark Airport (OKI) is a medium airport in Okinoshima, Japan, with the ICAO code RJNO. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,531 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JAL has 2 routes through RJNO. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Osaka (ITM).
