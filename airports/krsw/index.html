About KRSW

Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW) is a large airport in Fort Myers, United States, with the ICAO code KRSW. It has one runway, 06/24, 12,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 28 gates, 134 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 32 airlines have 825 routes through KRSW. Its most frequent links are Newark (EWR), Boston (BOS) and Cleveland (CLE).
