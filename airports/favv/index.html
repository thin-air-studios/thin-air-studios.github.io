About FAVV

Vereeniging Airport (FAVV) is a medium airport in Vereeniging, South Africa. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 03/21 and 15/33. The longer one, 03/21, is 5,266 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FAVV.
