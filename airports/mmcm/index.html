About MMCM

Chetumal International Airport (CTM) is a medium airport in Chetumal, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMCM. It has one runway, 10/28, 7,244 ft long and 151 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 46 routes through MMCM. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Mexico City (NLU) and Cancún (CUN).
