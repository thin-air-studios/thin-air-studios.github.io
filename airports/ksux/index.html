About KSUX

Sioux Gateway Airport / Brigadier General Bud Day Field (SUX) is a medium airport in Sioux City, United States, with the ICAO code KSUX. Its two runways are 13/31 and 17/35. The longer one, 13/31, is 9,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 23 routes through KSUX. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN) and Chicago (ORD).
