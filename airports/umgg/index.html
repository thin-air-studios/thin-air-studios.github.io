About UMGG

Gomel Airport (GME) is a medium airport in Gomel, Belarus, with the ICAO code UMGG. It has one runway, 10/28, 8,428 ft long and 141 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BRU has 17 routes through UMGG. Its most frequent links are Hurghada (HRG), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH) and Antalya (AYT).
