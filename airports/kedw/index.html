About KEDW

Edwards Air Force Base (EDW) is a medium airport in Edwards, United States, with the ICAO code KEDW. Its 3 runways are 05R/23L, 05L/23R and 07/25. The longest, 05R/23L, is 16,798 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 151 taxiway segments and 20 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KEDW.
