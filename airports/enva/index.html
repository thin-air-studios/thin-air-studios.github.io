About ENVA

Trondheim Airport, Værnes (TRD) is a large airport in Trondheim, Norway, with the ICAO code ENVA. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,052 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 gates, 70 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 34 airlines have 1,119 routes through ENVA. Its most frequent links are Oslo (OSL), Bergen (BGO) and Bodø (BOO).
