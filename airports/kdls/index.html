About KDLS

Columbia Gorge Regional Airport (DLS) is a medium airport in Dallesport / The Dalles, United States, with the ICAO code KDLS. Its two runways are 13/31 and 07/25. The longer one, 13/31, is 5,097 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDLS.
