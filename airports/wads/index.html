About WADS

Sultan Muhammad Kaharuddin III Airport (SWQ) is a small airport in Sumbawa Besar, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WADS. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,906 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 34 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WON has 2 routes through WADS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Kuta (DPS).
