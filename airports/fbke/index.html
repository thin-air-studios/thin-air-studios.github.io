About FBKE

Kasane International Airport (BBK) is a large airport in Kasane, Botswana, with the ICAO code FBKE. It has one runway, 08/26, 6,562 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 5 routes through FBKE. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB) and Maun (MUB).
