About KFSD

Sioux Falls Regional Airport (FSD) is a medium airport in Sioux Falls, United States, with the ICAO code KFSD. Its 3 runways are 03/21, 15/33 and 09/27. The longest, 03/21, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 164 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 26 airlines have 202 routes through KFSD. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN) and Minneapolis (MSP).
