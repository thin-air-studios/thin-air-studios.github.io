About RKTP

Seosan Air Base (HMY) is a small airport in Seosan, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKTP. Its two runways are 03L/21R and 03R/21L. The longer one, 03L/21R, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 357 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for RKTP.
