About UBBG

Ganja International Airport (GNJ) is a large airport in Ganja, Azerbaijan, with the ICAO code UBBG. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,827 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 35 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 25 routes through UBBG. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Moscow (VKO) and Pendik (SAW).
