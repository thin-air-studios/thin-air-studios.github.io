About WAGI

Iskandar Airport (PKN) is a small airport in Pangkalanbun, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAGI. It has one runway, 13/31, 5,415 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 21 routes through WAGI. Its most frequent links are Surabaya (SUB), Semarang (SRG) and Jakarta (CGK).
