About SVLF

La Fria Airport (LFR) is a medium airport in Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVLF. It has one runway, 01/19, 6,643 ft long and 190 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, VCV has 2 routes through SVLF. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Isla Margarita (PMV).
