About YWLM

Newcastle Airport (NTL) is a large airport in Williamtown, Australia, with the ICAO code YWLM. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,033 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 80 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 12 airlines have 161 routes through YWLM. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Melbourne (MEL) and Sydney (SYD).
