About SVCP

General Francisco Bermúdez Airport (CUP) is a medium airport in Carúpano, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVCP. It has one runway, 17/35, 6,611 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SVCP.
