About LGIR

Heraklion International Nikos Kazantzakis Airport (HER) is a large airport in Heraklion, Greece, with the ICAO code LGIR. Its two runways are 09/27 and 12/30. The longer one, 09/27, is 8,904 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 134 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 145 airlines have 3,944 routes through LGIR. Its most frequent links are Amsterdam (AMS), Düsseldorf (DUS) and London (LGW).
