About RJBB

Kansai International Airport (KIX) is a large airport in Osaka, Japan, with the ICAO code RJBB. Its two runways are 06L/24R and 06R/24L. The longer one, 06L/24R, is 13,123 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 gates, 370 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 114 airlines have 1,830 routes through RJBB. Its most frequent links are Seoul (ICN), Shanghai (PVG) and Hong Kong (HKG).
