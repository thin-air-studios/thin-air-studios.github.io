About GMTN

Sania Ramel Airport (TTU) is a large airport in Tétouan, Morocco, with the ICAO code GMTN. It has one runway, 07/25, 10,784 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 108 routes through GMTN. Its most frequent links are Málaga (AGP), Madrid (MAD) and Zaventem (BRU).
