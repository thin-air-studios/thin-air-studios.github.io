About ENEV

Harstad/Narvik Airport (EVE) is a large airport in Evenes, Norway, with the ICAO code ENEV. It has one runway, 17/35, 9,236 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 90 taxiway segments, 57 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 194 routes through ENEV. Its most frequent links are Oslo (OSL), Bodø (BOO) and Andenes (ANX).
