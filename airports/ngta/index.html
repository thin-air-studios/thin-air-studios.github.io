About NGTA

Bonriki International Airport (TRW) is a large airport in South Tarawa, Kiribati, with the ICAO code NGTA. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,598 ft long and 135 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Fiji has 2 routes through NGTA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Nadi (NAN).
