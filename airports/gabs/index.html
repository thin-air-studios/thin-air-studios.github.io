About GABS

Modibo Keita International Airport (BKO) is a large airport in Bamako, Mali, with the ICAO code GABS. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,498 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 107 routes through GABS. Its most frequent links are Paris (CDG), Dakar (DSS) and Abidjan (ABJ).
