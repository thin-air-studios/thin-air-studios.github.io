About KVBG

Vandenberg Space Force Base (VBG) is a medium airport in Lompoc, United States, with the ICAO code KVBG. It has one runway, 12/30, 15,000 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KVBG.
