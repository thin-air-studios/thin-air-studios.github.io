About GCLP

Gran Canaria Airport (LPA) is a large airport in Gran Canaria Island, Spain, with the ICAO code GCLP. Its two runways are 03L/21R and 03R/21L. The longer one, 03L/21R, is 10,171 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 30 gates, 80 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 111 airlines have 6,042 routes through GCLP. Its most frequent links are Madrid (MAD), Amsterdam (AMS) and San Bartolomé (ACE).
