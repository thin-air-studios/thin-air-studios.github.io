About CYGM

Gimli Industrial Park Airport (YGM) is a medium airport in Gimli, Canada, with the ICAO code CYGM. It has one runway, 15/33, 6,800 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CYGM.
