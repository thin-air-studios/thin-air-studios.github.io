About KPSC

Tri Cities Airport (PSC) is a medium airport in Pasco, United States, with the ICAO code KPSC. Its 3 runways are 03L/21R, 12/30 and 03R/21L. The longest, 03L/21R, is 7,707 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 84 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 100 routes through KPSC. Its most frequent links are Seattle (SEA), Denver (DEN) and Mesa (AZA).
