About LIBG

Taranto-Grottaglie Marcello Arlotta Airport (TAR) is a medium airport in Grottaglie, Italy, with the ICAO code LIBG. It has one runway, 17/35, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 13 routes through LIBG. Its most frequent links are Charleston (CHS), Prestwick (PIK) and New York (JFK).
