About LGKV

Kavala Alexander the Great International Airport (KVA) is a large airport in Kavala, Greece, with the ICAO code LGKV. It has one runway, 05/23, 9,844 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 184 routes through LGKV. Its most frequent links are Köln (CGN), London (LGW) and Stuttgart (STR).
