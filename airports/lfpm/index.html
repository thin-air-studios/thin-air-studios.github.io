About LFPM

Melun-Villaroche Airfield (LFPM) is a medium airport in Moissy-Cramayel, Seine-et-Marne, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 10/28 and 01/19. The longer one, 10/28, is 6,480 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 59 taxiway segments and 8 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFPM.
