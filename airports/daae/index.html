About DAAE

Soummam–Abane Ramdane Airport (BJA) is a large airport in Béjaïa, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAAE. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,874 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 85 routes through DAAE. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Colombier-Saugnieu (LYS) and Paris (CDG).
