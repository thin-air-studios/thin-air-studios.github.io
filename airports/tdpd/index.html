About TDPD

Douglas-Charles Airport (DOM) is a medium airport in Marigot, Dominica, with the ICAO code TDPD. It has one runway, 09/27, 6,352 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 62 routes through TDPD. Its most frequent links are Bridgetown (BGI), Osbourn (ANU) and Port of Spain (POS).
