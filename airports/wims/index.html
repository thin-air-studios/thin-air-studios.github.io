About WIMS

Dr. Ferdinand Lumban Tobing Airport (FLZ) is a medium airport in Sibolga (Pinangsori), Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIMS. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,655 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, Garuda has 1 routes through WIMS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Jakarta (CGK).
