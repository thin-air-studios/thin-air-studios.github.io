About KDRO

Durango La Plata County Airport (DRO) is a medium airport in Durango, United States, with the ICAO code KDRO. It has one runway, 03/21, 9,201 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 69 routes through KDRO. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Phoenix (PHX).
