About RJCB

Tokachi-Obihiro Airport (OBO) is a medium airport in Obihiro, Japan, with the ICAO code RJCB. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,202 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 36 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 38 routes through RJCB. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Tokoname (NGO) and Seoul (ICN).
