About KCGI

Cape Girardeau Regional Airport (CGI) is a medium airport in Cape Girardeau, United States, with the ICAO code KCGI. Its two runways are 10/28 and 02/20. The longer one, 10/28, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 13 routes through KCGI. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Nashville (BNA) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
