About SCIE

Carriel Sur International Airport (CCP) is a large airport in Concepcion, Chile, with the ICAO code SCIE. Its two runways are 02/20 and 02R/20L. The longer one, 02/20, is 8,530 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 gates, 65 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 283 routes through SCIE. Its most frequent links are Santiago (SCL), Antofagasta (ANF) and Calama (CJC).
