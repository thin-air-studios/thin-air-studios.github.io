About LFOA

Avord (BA 702) Air Base (LFOA) is a medium airport in Avord, France. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 06/24, 11,493 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 80 taxiway segments and 14 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LFOA.
