About MMHO

General Ignacio L. Pesqueira International Airport (HMO) is a large airport in Hermosillo, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMHO. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,546 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 gates, 200 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 167 routes through MMHO. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Guadalajara (GDL) and Tijuana (TIJ).
