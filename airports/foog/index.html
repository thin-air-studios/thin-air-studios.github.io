About FOOG

Port Gentil International Airport (POG) is a large airport in Port Gentil, Gabon, with the ICAO code FOOG. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,234 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, ABS has 4 routes through FOOG. Its most frequent links are Libreville (LBV) and Gamba (GAX).
