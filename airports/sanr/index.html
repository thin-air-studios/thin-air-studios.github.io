About SANR

Termas de Río Hondo international Airport (RHD) is a medium airport in Termas de Río Hondo, Argentina, with the ICAO code SANR. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,232 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SANR.
