About KSFQ

Suffolk Executive Airport (KSFQ) is a small airport in Suffolk, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 04/22 and 07/25. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,007 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KSFQ.
