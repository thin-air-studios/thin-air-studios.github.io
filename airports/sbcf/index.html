About SBCF

Tancredo Neves International Airport (CNF) is a large airport in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBCF. It has one runway, 16/34, 11,811 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 71 gates, 73 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 993 routes through SBCF. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (CGH), São Paulo (GRU) and Rio De Janeiro (GIG).
