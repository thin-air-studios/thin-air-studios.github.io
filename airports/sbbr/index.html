About SBBR

Presidente Juscelino Kubitschek International Airport (BSB) is a large airport in Brasília, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBBR. Its two runways are 11R/29L and 11L/29R. The longer one, 11R/29L, is 10,827 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 89 gates, 356 taxiway segments, 33 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 948 routes through SBBR. Its most frequent links are São Paulo (CGH), São Paulo (GRU) and Rio de Janeiro (SDU).
