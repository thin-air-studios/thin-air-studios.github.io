About YMML

Melbourne Airport (MEL) is a large airport in Melbourne, Australia, with the ICAO code YMML. Its two runways are 16/34 and 09/27. The longer one, 16/34, is 11,998 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 91 gates, 171 taxiway segments, 25 aprons and 11 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 81 airlines have 2,822 routes through YMML. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Brisbane (BNE) and Adelaide (ADL).
