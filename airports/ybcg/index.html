About YBCG

Gold Coast Airport (OOL) is a large airport in Gold Coast, Australia, with the ICAO code YBCG. Its two runways are 14/32 and 17/35. The longer one, 14/32, is 8,176 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 gates, 47 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 18 airlines have 473 routes through YBCG. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Melbourne (MEL) and Auckland (AKL).
