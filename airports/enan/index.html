About ENAN

Andøya Airport, Andenes (ANX) is a medium airport in Andenes, Norway, with the ICAO code ENAN. Its two runways are 14/32 and 03/21. The longer one, 14/32, is 8,097 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 186 taxiway segments, 64 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 29 routes through ENAN. Its most frequent links are Evenes (EVE), Tromsø (TOS) and Bodø (BOO).
