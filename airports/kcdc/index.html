About KCDC

Cedar City Regional Airport (CDC) is a medium airport in Cedar City, United States, with the ICAO code KCDC. Its two runways are 02/20 and 08/26. The longer one, 02/20, is 8,653 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 73 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 14 routes through KCDC. Its most frequent links are Salt Lake City (SLC), Las Vegas (LAS) and St George (SGU).
