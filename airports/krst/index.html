About KRST

Rochester International Airport (RST) is a medium airport in Rochester, United States, with the ICAO code KRST. Its two runways are 13/31 and 03/21. The longer one, 13/31, is 9,034 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 111 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 49 routes through KRST. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Minneapolis (MSP) and Memphis (MEM).
