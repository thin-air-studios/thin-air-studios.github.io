About VTSS

Hat Yai International Airport (HDY) is a large airport in Hat Yai, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTSS. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,007 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 26 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 167 routes through VTSS. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (DMK), Bangkok (BKK) and Singapore (SIN).
