About KBZN

Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport (BZN) is a medium airport in Bozeman, United States, with the ICAO code KBZN. Its 4 runways are 12/30, 11/29, 11G/29G and 03/21. The longest, 12/30, is 8,994 ft. Of these, 3 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 gates, 152 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 145 routes through KBZN. Its most frequent links are Denver (DEN), Seattle (SEA) and Chicago (ORD).
