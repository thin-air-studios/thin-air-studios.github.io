About LIPK

Forlì-Luigi Ridolfi International Airport (FRL) is a medium airport in Forlì (FC), Italy, with the ICAO code LIPK. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,907 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 20 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 85 routes through LIPK. Its most frequent links are Catania (CTA), Palermo (PMO) and Cagliari (CAG).
