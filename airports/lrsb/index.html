About LRSB

Sibiu International Airport (SBZ) is a large airport in Sibiu, Romania, with the ICAO code LRSB. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,629 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 22 airlines have 260 routes through LRSB. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Antalya (AYT) and Dortmund (DTM).
