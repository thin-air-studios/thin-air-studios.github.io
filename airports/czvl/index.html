About CZVL

Edmonton / Villeneuve Airport (CZVL) is a small airport in Edmonton, Canada. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08/26 and 16/34. The longer one, 08/26, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments, 17 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CZVL.
