About EDAV

Eberswalde-Finow Airfield (EDAV) is a small airport in Eberswalde, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 10/28, 5,046 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDAV.
