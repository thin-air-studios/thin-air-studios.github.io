About KVCV

Southern California Logistics Airport (VCV) is a small airport in Victorville, United States, with the ICAO code KVCV. Its two runways are 17/35 and 03/21. The longer one, 17/35, is 13,052 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments and 10 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 17 routes through KVCV. Its most frequent links are Baltimore (BWI), Fort Cavazos (GRK) and Phoenix (PHX).
