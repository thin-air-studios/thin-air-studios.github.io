About EFTU

Turku Airport (TKU) is a large airport in Turku, Finland, with the ICAO code EFTU. It has one runway, 08/26, 8,202 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 33 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 212 routes through EFTU. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Gdańsk (GDN) and Stockholm (ARN).
