About LROD

Oradea International Airport (OMR) is a large airport in Oradea, Romania, with the ICAO code LROD. It has one runway, 01/19, 8,267 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 26 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 20 airlines have 146 routes through LROD. Its most frequent links are Otopeni (OTP), Antalya (AYT) and Hurghada (HRG).
