About DAUZ

Zarzaitine - In Aménas Airport (IAM) is a medium airport in In Aménas, Algeria, with the ICAO code DAUZ. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 9,843 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 12 routes through DAUZ. Its most frequent links are Algiers (ALG), Es-Sénia (ORN) and Djanet (DJG).
