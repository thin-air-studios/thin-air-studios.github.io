About FAPI

Pietersburg Municipal Airport (FAPI) is a medium airport in Polokwane, South Africa. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 08/26, 7,218 ft long and 82 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for FAPI.
