About KFAY

Fayetteville Regional Airport - Grannis Field (FAY) is a medium airport in Fayetteville, United States, with the ICAO code KFAY. Its two runways are 04/22 and 10/28. The longer one, 04/22, is 7,709 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 43 routes through KFAY. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Atlanta (ATL) and Dulles (IAD).
