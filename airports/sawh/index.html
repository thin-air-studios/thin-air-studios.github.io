About SAWH

Ushuaia - Malvinas Argentinas International Airport (USH) is a medium airport in Ushuaia, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAWH. It has one runway, 7/25, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 28 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 100 routes through SAWH. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP), Buenos Aires (EZE) and El Calafate (FTE).
