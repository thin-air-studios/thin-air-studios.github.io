About KCNO

Chino Airport (CNO) is a small airport in Chino, United States, with the ICAO code KCNO. Its 3 runways are 08R/26L, 03/21 and 08L/26R. The longest, 08R/26L, is 7,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCNO.
