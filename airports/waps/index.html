About WAPS

Mathilda Batlayeri Airport (SXK) is a small airport in Saumlaki-Yamdena Island, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WAPS. It has one runway, 11/29, 6,562 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for WAPS.
