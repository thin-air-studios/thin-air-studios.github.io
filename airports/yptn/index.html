About YPTN

Tindal Airport (KTR) is a medium airport in Australia, with the ICAO code YPTN. It has one runway, 14/32, 9,003 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 53 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for YPTN.
