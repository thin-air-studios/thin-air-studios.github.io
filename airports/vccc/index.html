About VCCC

Colombo Ratmalana International Airport (RML) is a large airport in Colombo, Sri Lanka, with the ICAO code VCCC. It has one runway, 04/22, 6,013 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VCCC.
