About KHMN

Holloman Air Force Base (HMN) is a medium airport in Alamogordo, United States, with the ICAO code KHMN. Its 3 runways are 07/25, 16/34 and 04/22. The longest, 07/25, is 12,922 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 43 taxiway segments and 16 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHMN.
