About VTUD

Udon Thani International Airport (UTH) is a large airport in Udon Thani, Thailand, with the ICAO code VTUD. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 98 routes through VTUD. Its most frequent links are Bangkok (DMK), Bangkok (BKK) and Hat Yai (HDY).
