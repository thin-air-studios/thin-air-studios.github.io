About SBSR

Prof. Eribelto Manoel Reino State Airport (SJP) is a medium airport in São José do Rio Preto, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBSR. It has one runway, 7/25, 5,381 ft long and 115 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 62 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 65 routes through SBSR. Its most frequent links are Campinas (VCP), São Paulo (CGH) and São Paulo (GRU).
