About ESTL

Ljungbyhed Airfield (ESTL) is a medium airport in Ljungbyhed, Sweden. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 11R/29L and 11L/29R. The longer one, 11R/29L, is 7,449 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ESTL.
