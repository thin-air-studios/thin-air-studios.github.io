About SAWC

El Calafate - Commander Armando Tola International Airport (FTE) is a medium airport in El Calafate, Argentina, with the ICAO code SAWC. It has one runway, 7/25, 8,366 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 65 routes through SAWC. Its most frequent links are Buenos Aires (AEP), Ushuaia (USH) and Buenos Aires (EZE).
