About KCCO

Newnan Coweta County Airport (KCCO) is a small airport in Newnan, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 15/33, 5,500 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KCCO.
