About EFOU

Oulu Airport (OUL) is a large airport in Oulu / Oulunsalo, Finland, with the ICAO code EFOU. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,205 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 27 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 280 routes through EFOU. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Gran Canaria Island (LPA) and Antalya (AYT).
