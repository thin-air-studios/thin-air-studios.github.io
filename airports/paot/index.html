About PAOT

Ralph Wien Memorial Airport (OTZ) is a medium airport in Kotzebue, United States, with the ICAO code PAOT. Its two runways are 09/27 and 17/35. The longer one, 09/27, is 6,300 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 40 routes through PAOT. Its most frequent links are Anchorage (ANC), Selawik (WLK) and Kiana (IAN).
