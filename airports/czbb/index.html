About CZBB

Boundary Bay Airport (YDT) is a medium airport in Delta, Canada, with the ICAO code CZBB. Its two runways are 07/25 and 13/31. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,606 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for CZBB.
