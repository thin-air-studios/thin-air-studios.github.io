About K0V7

Kayenta Airport (MVM) is a small airport in Kayenta, United States, with the ICAO code K0V7. It has one runway, 05/23, 7,140 ft long and 75 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for K0V7.
