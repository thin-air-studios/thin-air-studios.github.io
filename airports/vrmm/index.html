About VRMM

Velana International Airport (MLE) is a large airport in Malé, Maldives, with the ICAO code VRMM. It has one runway, 18/36, 11,155 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 gates, 75 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 10 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 68 airlines have 397 routes through VRMM. Its most frequent links are Colombo (CMB), Doha (DOH) and Dubai (DXB).
