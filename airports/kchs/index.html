About KCHS

Charleston International Airport (CHS) is a large airport in Charleston, United States, with the ICAO code KCHS. Its two runways are 15/33 and 03/21. The longer one, 15/33, is 9,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 75 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 40 airlines have 541 routes through KCHS. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Washington (DCA) and Newark (EWR).
