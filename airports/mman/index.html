About MMAN

Del Norte International Airport (NTR) is a medium airport in Monterrey, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMAN. Its two runways are 02/20 and 11/29. The longer one, 02/20, is 6,598 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 144 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for MMAN.
