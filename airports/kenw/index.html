About KENW

Kenosha Regional Airport (ENW) is a medium airport in Kenosha, United States, with the ICAO code KENW. Its 3 runways are 07L/25R, 15/33 and 07R/25L. The longest, 07L/25R, is 6,600 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 32 taxiway segments and 6 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KENW.
