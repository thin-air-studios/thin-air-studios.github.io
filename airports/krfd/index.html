About KRFD

Chicago Rockford International Airport (RFD) is a medium airport in Chicago/Rockford, United States, with the ICAO code KRFD. Its two runways are 07/25 and 01/19. The longer one, 07/25, is 10,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 42 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 474 routes through KRFD. Its most frequent links are Louisville (SDF), Ontario (ONT) and Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
