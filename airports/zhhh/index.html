About ZHHH

Wuhan Tianhe International Airport (WUH) is a large airport in Wuhan (Huangpi), China, with the ICAO code ZHHH. Its 3 runways are 05L/23R, 04/22 and 05R/23L. The longest, 05L/23R, is 11,811 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 91 gates, 442 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 72 airlines have 1,265 routes through ZHHH. Its most frequent links are Guangzhou (CAN), Chengdu (TFU) and Haikou (HAK).
