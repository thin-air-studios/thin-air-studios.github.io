About HSKA

Kassala Airport (KSL) is a medium airport in Kassala, Sudan, with the ICAO code HSKA. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BDR has 1 routes through HSKA. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Port Sudan (PZU).
