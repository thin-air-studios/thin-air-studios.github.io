About ORKK

Kirkuk International Airport (KIK) is a large airport in Kirkuk, Iraq, with the ICAO code ORKK. Its two runways are 13/31 and 14/32. The longer one, 13/31, is 9,809 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 53 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 13 routes through ORKK. Its most frequent links are Istanbul (IST), Ankara (ESB) and Beirut (BEY).
