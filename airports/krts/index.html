About KRTS

Reno-Stead Airport (KRTS) is a small airport in Reno, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 14/32 and 08/26. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 105 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KRTS.
