About MMMX

Mexico City Benito Juárez International Airport (MEX) is a large airport in Mexico City, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMMX. Its two runways are 05L/23R and 05R/23L. The longer one, 05L/23R, is 12,966 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 73 gates, 381 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 79 airlines have 3,412 routes through MMMX. Its most frequent links are Cancún (CUN), Guadalajara (GDL) and Monterrey (MTY).
