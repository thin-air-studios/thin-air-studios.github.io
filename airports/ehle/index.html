About EHLE

Lelystad Airport (LEY) is a medium airport in Lelystad, Netherlands, with the ICAO code EHLE. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 110 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 4 routes through EHLE. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT) and Amsterdam (AMS).
