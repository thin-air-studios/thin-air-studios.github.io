About KDMA

Davis Monthan Air Force Base (DMA) is a medium airport in Tucson, United States, with the ICAO code KDMA. It has one runway, 12/30, 13,643 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 189 taxiway segments and 32 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through KDMA. Its most frequent links are KOFF (KOFF) and Chicago (ORD).
