About KTXK

Texarkana Regional Airport (Webb Field) (TXK) is a medium airport in Texarkana, United States, with the ICAO code KTXK. Its two runways are 04/22 and 13/31. The longer one, 04/22, is 6,602 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 47 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 6 routes through KTXK. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW).
