About CYQL

Lethbridge County Airport (YQL) is a medium airport in Lethbridge, Canada, with the ICAO code CYQL. Its two runways are 06/24 and 13/31. The longer one, 06/24, is 6,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, WestJet Encore has 6 routes through CYQL. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Calgary (YYC).
