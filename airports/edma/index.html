About EDMA

Augsburg Airport (AGB) is a medium airport in Augsburg, Germany, with the ICAO code EDMA. Its two runways are 07/25 and 07R/25L. The longer one, 07/25, is 5,230 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 103 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, SUS has 15 routes through EDMA. Its most frequent links are Albert (BYF), Marignane (MRS) and Boos (URO).
