About KTVC

Cherry Capital Airport (TVC) is a medium airport in Traverse City, United States, with the ICAO code KTVC. Its two runways are 10/28 and 18/36. The longer one, 10/28, is 7,016 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 96 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 19 airlines have 120 routes through KTVC. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Detroit (DTW) and Minneapolis (MSP).
