About GMAD

Al Massira Airport (AGA) is a large airport in Agadir (Temsia), Morocco, with the ICAO code GMAD. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 47 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 57 airlines have 1,053 routes through GMAD. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Manchester (MAN) and London (LGW).
