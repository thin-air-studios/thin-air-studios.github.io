About KMFR

Rogue Valley International-Medford Airport (MFR) is a medium airport in Medford, United States, with the ICAO code KMFR. It has one runway, 14/32, 8,800 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 157 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 117 routes through KMFR. Its most frequent links are Portland (PDX), Seattle (SEA) and San Francisco (SFO).
