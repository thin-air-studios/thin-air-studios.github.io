About MYIG

Inagua Airport (IGA) is a medium airport in Matthew Town, Bahamas, with the ICAO code MYIG. Its two runways are 10/28 and 09/27. The longer one, 10/28, is 7,020 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, BHS has 3 routes through MYIG. Its most frequent links are Abraham Bay Settlement (MYG) and Nassau (NAS).
