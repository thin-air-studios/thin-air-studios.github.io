About VDSV

Sihanouk International Airport (KOS) is a large airport in Preah Sihanouk, Cambodia, with the ICAO code VDSV. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,202 ft long and 112 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 43 routes through VDSV. Its most frequent links are Phnom Penh (PNH), Guangzhou (CAN) and Sepang (KUL).
