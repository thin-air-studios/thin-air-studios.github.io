About RKSO

Osan Air Base (OSN) is a medium airport in Pyeongtaek, South Korea, with the ICAO code RKSO. Its two runways are 09L/27R and 09R/27L. The longer one, 09L/27R, is 9,004 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 134 taxiway segments and 12 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 10 routes through RKSO. Its most frequent links are Fussa (OKO), Honolulu (HNL) and Okinawa (DNA).
