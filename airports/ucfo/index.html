About UCFO

Osh International Airport (OSS) is a large airport in Osh, Kyrgyzstan, with the ICAO code UCFO. It has one runway, 12/30, 10,538 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 49 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 191 routes through UCFO. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Bishkek (BSZ) and Krasnoyarsk (KJA).
