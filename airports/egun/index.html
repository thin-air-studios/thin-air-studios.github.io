About EGUN

RAF Mildenhall (MHZ) is a medium airport in Bury Saint Edmunds, Suffolk, United Kingdom, with the ICAO code EGUN. It has one runway, 10/28, 9,213 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 159 taxiway segments, 34 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 5 routes through EGUN. Its most frequent links are Ramstein-Miesenbach (RMS), Wrightstown (WRI) and Dover (DOV).
