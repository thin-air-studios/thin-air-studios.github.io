About KE06

Lea County-Zip Franklin Memorial Airport (KE06) is a small airport in Lovington, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 03/21 and 12/30. The longer one, 03/21, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KE06.
