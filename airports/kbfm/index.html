About KBFM

Mobile Downtown Airport (BFM) is a medium airport in Mobile, United States, with the ICAO code KBFM. Its two runways are 14/32 and 18/36. The longer one, 14/32, is 9,618 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 64 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 15 routes through KBFM. Its most frequent links are Orlando (MCO), Memphis (MEM) and Albany (ABY).
