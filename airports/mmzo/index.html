About MMZO

Playa de Oro International Airport (ZLO) is a medium airport in Manzanillo, Mexico, with the ICAO code MMZO. It has one runway, 10/28, 7,218 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 27 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 29 routes through MMZO. Its most frequent links are Mexico City (MEX), Houston (IAH) and Los Angeles (LAX).
