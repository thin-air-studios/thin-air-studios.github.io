About WIKK

Depati Amir Airport (PGK) is a medium airport in Pangkal Pinang, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIKK. It has one runway, 16/34, 7,382 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 4 gates, 28 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 7 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for WIKK.
