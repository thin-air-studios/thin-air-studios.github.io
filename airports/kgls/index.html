About KGLS

Scholes International At Galveston Airport (GLS) is a medium airport in Galveston, United States, with the ICAO code KGLS. Its two runways are 18/36 and 14/32. The longer one, 18/36, is 6,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, TUI has 1 routes through KGLS. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Zakynthos (ZTH).
