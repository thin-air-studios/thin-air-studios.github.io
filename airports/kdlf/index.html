About KDLF

Laughlin Air Force Base (DLF) is a medium airport in Del Rio, United States, with the ICAO code KDLF. Its 3 runways are 13C/31C, 13L/31R and 13R/31L. The longest, 13C/31C, is 8,852 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 21 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KDLF.
