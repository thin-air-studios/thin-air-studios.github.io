About OYSN

Sanaa International Airport (SAH) is a large airport in Sanaa, Yemen, with the ICAO code OYSN. It has one runway, 18/36, 10,669 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 74 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 38 routes through OYSN. Its most frequent links are Cairo (CAI), Dubai (DXB) and Paris (CDG).
