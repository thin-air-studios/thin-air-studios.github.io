About KLEW

Auburn Lewiston Municipal Airport (LEW) is a small airport in Auburn/Lewiston, United States, with the ICAO code KLEW. Its two runways are 04/22 and 17/35. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,001 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 13 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, AMF has 2 routes through KLEW. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Manchester (MHT).
