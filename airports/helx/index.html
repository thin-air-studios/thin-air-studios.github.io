About HELX

Luxor International Airport (LXR) is a large airport in Luxor, Egypt, with the ICAO code HELX. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 87 taxiway segments, 11 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 224 routes through HELX. Its most frequent links are Cairo (CAI), Paris (CDG) and Hurghada (HRG).
