About KHBG

Hattiesburg Bobby L Chain Municipal Airport (HBG) is a medium airport in Hattiesburg, United States, with the ICAO code KHBG. It has one runway, 13/31, 6,094 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHBG.
