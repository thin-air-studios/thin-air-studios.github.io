About EFJY

Jyväskylä Airport (JYV) is a medium airport in Jyväskylän Maalaiskunta, Finland, with the ICAO code EFJY. It has one runway, 12/30, 8,533 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 74 routes through EFJY. Its most frequent links are Helsinki (HEL), Kajaani (KAJ) and Gran Canaria Island (LPA).
