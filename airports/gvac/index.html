About GVAC

Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID) is a large airport in Espargos, Cape Verde, with the ICAO code GVAC. It has one runway, 01/19, 10,760 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 9 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 48 airlines have 438 routes through GVAC. Its most frequent links are Rabil (BVC), Amsterdam (AMS) and Zaventem (BRU).
