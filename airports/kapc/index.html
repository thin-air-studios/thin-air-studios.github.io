About KAPC

Napa County Airport (APC) is a small airport in Napa, United States, with the ICAO code KAPC. Its 3 runways are 01L/19R, 6/24 and 01R/19L. The longest, 01L/19R, is 5,930 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 57 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, JSX has 10 routes through KAPC. Its most frequent links are Burbank (BUR), Santa Ana (SNA) and Santa Monica (SMO).
