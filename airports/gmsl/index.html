About GMSL

Sidi Slimane Airport (GMSL) is a small airport in Sidi Slimane, Morocco. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 07/25, 11,302 ft long and 140 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 89 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for GMSL.
