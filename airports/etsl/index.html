About ETSL

Lechfeld Air Base (ETSL) is a medium airport in Graben, Germany. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 03/21, 8,012 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments and 5 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ETSL.
