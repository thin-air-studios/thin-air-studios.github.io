About KOAK

Oakland San Francisco Bay Airport (OAK) is a large airport in Oakland, United States, with the ICAO code KOAK. Its 4 runways are 12/30, 10R/28L, 10L/28R and 15/33. The longest, 12/30, is 10,520 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 gates, 139 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 35 airlines have 838 routes through KOAK. Its most frequent links are Phoenix (PHX), Memphis (MEM) and Las Vegas (LAS).
