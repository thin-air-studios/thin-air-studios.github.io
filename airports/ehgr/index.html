About EHGR

Gilze Rijen Air Base (GLZ) is a medium airport in Rijen, Netherlands, with the ICAO code EHGR. Its two runways are 10/28 and 02/20. The longer one, 10/28, is 9,081 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EHGR.
