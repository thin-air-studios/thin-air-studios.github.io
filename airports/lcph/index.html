About LCPH

Paphos International Airport (PFO) is a large airport in Paphos, Cyprus, with the ICAO code LCPH. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,858 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 92 taxiway segments, 14 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 76 airlines have 1,587 routes through LCPH. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Manchester (MAN) and Bristol (BRS).
