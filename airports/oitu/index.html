About OITU

Maku National Airport (IMQ) is a medium airport in Showt, Iran, with the ICAO code OITU. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,827 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for OITU.
