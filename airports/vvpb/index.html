About VVPB

Phu Bai International Airport (HUI) is a large airport in Huế, Vietnam, with the ICAO code VVPB. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,775 ft long and 130 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 9 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 129 routes through VVPB. Its most frequent links are Ho Chi Minh City (SGN), Hanoi (HAN) and Da Lat (DLI).
