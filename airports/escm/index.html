About ESCM

Ärna Air Base (ESCM) is a small airport in Uppsala, Sweden. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 08/26 and 03/21. The longer one, 08/26, is 6,594 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 33 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for ESCM.
