About KX60

Williston Municipal Airport (KX60) is a small airport in Williston, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 05/23 and 14/32. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,669 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 44 taxiway segments and 1 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KX60.
