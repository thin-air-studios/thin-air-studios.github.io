About FAEL

King Phalo Airport (ELS) is a large airport in East London, South Africa, with the ICAO code FAEL. Its two runways are 11/29 and 06/24. The longer one, 11/29, is 6,362 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 78 routes through FAEL. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Cape Town (CPT) and Durban (DUR).
