About CYDL

Dease Lake Airport (YDL) is a small airport in Dease Lake, Canada, with the ICAO code CYDL. It has one runway, 02/20, 6,000 ft long and 90 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through CYDL. Its most frequent links are Kelowna (YLW) and Prince George (YXS).
