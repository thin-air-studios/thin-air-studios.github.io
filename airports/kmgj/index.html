About KMGJ

Orange County Airport (MGJ) is a small airport in Montgomery, United States, with the ICAO code KMGJ. Its two runways are 04/22 and 08/26. The longer one, 04/22, is 5,006 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 46 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KMGJ.
