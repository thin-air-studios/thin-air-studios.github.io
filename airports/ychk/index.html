About YCHK

Christmas Creek Airport (CKW) is a small airport in Christmas Creek Mine, Australia, with the ICAO code YCHK. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 4 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 11 routes through YCHK. Its most frequent links are Perth (PER) and Busselton (BQB).
