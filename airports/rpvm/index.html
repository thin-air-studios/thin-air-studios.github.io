About RPVM

Mactan Cebu International Airport (CEB) is a large airport in Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City, Philippines, with the ICAO code RPVM. Its two runways are 04R/22L and 04L/22R. The longer one, 04R/22L, is 10,860 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 gates, 102 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 48 airlines have 1,228 routes through RPVM. Its most frequent links are Manila (MNL), Davao (DVO) and Seoul (ICN).
