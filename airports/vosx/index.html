About VOSX

Coimbatore Air Force Station (VOSX) is a small airport in Sulur, India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,255 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 56 taxiway segments and 23 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VOSX.
