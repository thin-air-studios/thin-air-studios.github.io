About KDAB

Daytona Beach International Airport (DAB) is a medium airport in Daytona Beach, United States, with the ICAO code KDAB. Its 3 runways are 07L/25R, 16/34 and 07R/25L. The longest, 07L/25R, is 10,500 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 205 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 45 routes through KDAB. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Atlanta (ATL) and Hartford (BDL).
