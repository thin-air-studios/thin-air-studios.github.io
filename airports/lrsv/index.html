About LRSV

Suceava Ștefan cel Mare International Airport (SCV) is a large airport in Suceava, Romania, with the ICAO code LRSV. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,070 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 23 airlines have 269 routes through LRSV. Its most frequent links are Luton (LTN), Antalya (AYT) and Dortmund (DTM).
