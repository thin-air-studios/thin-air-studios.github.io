About ZSWZ

Wenzhou Longwan International Airport (WNZ) is a large airport in Wenzhou (Longwan), China, with the ICAO code ZSWZ. It has one runway, 03/21, 10,499 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 29 gates, 65 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 46 airlines have 830 routes through ZSWZ. Its most frequent links are Guiyang (KWE), Guangzhou (CAN) and Chongqing (CKG).
