About YBHM

Hamilton Island Airport (HTI) is a medium airport in Hamilton Island, Australia, with the ICAO code YBHM. It has one runway, 14/32, 5,591 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 10 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 49 routes through YBHM. Its most frequent links are Brisbane (BNE), Melbourne (MEL) and Sydney (SYD).
