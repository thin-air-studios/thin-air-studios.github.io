About ZPPP

Kunming Changshui International Airport (KMG) is a large airport in Kunming, China, with the ICAO code ZPPP. It is number 44 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 49.7 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 04/22 and 03/21. The longer one, 04/22, is 14,764 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 103 gates, 527 taxiway segments, 15 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 64 airlines have 2,077 routes through ZPPP. Its most frequent links are Dehong (LUM), Nanjing (NKG) and Zhengzhou (CGO).
