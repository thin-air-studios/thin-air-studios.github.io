About ULPB

Petrozavodsk Airport (PES) is a medium airport in Petrozavodsk, Russia, with the ICAO code ULPB. It has one runway, 02/20, 8,202 ft long and 157 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 64 taxiway segments, 10 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 5 airlines have 21 routes through ULPB. Its most frequent links are Moscow (DME), Mineralnyye Vody (MRV) and Moscow (SVO).
