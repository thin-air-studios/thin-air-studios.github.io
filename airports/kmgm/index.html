About KMGM

Montgomery Regional (Dannelly Field) Airport (MGM) is a medium airport in Montgomery, United States, with the ICAO code KMGM. Its two runways are 10/28 and 03/21. The longer one, 10/28, is 9,020 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 61 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 39 routes through KMGM. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Atlanta (ATL) and Houston (IAH).
