About SVJC

Josefa Camejo International Airport (LSP) is a medium airport in Paraguaná, Venezuela, with the ICAO code SVJC. It has one runway, 09/27, 9,186 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 6 routes through SVJC. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Maiquetía (CCS).
