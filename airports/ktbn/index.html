About KTBN

Waynesville-St. Robert Regional Airport-Forney Field (TBN) is a medium airport in Fort Leonard Wood, United States, with the ICAO code KTBN. It has one runway, 15/33, 6,037 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 37 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, VTE has 8 routes through KTBN. Its most frequent links are Nashville (BNA), Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) and Chicago (ORD).
