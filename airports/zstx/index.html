About ZSTX

Huangshan Tunxi International Airport (TXN) is a large airport in Huangshan, China, with the ICAO code ZSTX. It has one runway, 13/31, 8,530 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 17 airlines have 54 routes through ZSTX. Its most frequent links are Seoul (ICN), Hong Kong (HKG) and Busan (PUS).
