About ETSI

Ingolstadt Manching Airport (IGS) is a medium airport in Manching, Germany, with the ICAO code ETSI. Its two runways are 06R/24L and 06L/24R. The longer one, 06R/24L, is 9,646 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 86 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 8 routes through ETSI. Its most frequent links are Hamburg (XFW), Hannover (HAJ) and Bremen (BRE).
