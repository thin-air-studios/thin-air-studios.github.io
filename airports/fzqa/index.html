About FZQA

Lubumbashi International Airport (FBM) is a large airport in Lubumbashi, Congo - Kinshasa, with the ICAO code FZQA. It has one runway, 07/25, 10,623 ft long and 164 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 39 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 19 routes through FZQA. Its most frequent links are Johannesburg (JNB), Kinshasa (FIH) and Ndola (NLA).
