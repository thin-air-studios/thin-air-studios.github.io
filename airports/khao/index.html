About KHAO

Butler Co Regional Airport - Hogan Field (HAO) is a small airport in Hamilton, United States, with the ICAO code KHAO. It has one runway, 12/30, 5,500 ft long and 100 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 41 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KHAO.
