About KMKL

McKellar-Sipes Regional Airport (MKL) is a medium airport in Jackson, United States, with the ICAO code KMKL. Its two runways are 02/20 and 11/29. The longer one, 02/20, is 6,005 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 4 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 11 routes through KMKL. Its most frequent links are Atlanta (ATL), Chicago (ORD) and St Louis (STL).
