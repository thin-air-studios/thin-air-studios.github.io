About VYHL

Hommalinn Airport (HOX) is a small airport in Hommalinn, Myanmar (Burma), with the ICAO code VYHL. It has one runway, 16/34, 11,970 ft long and 200 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, UBA has 3 routes through VYHL. Its most frequent links are Mandalay (MDL) and Monywar (NYW).
