About UZTP

Tashkent-Khumo International Airport (TVT) is a medium airport in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, with the ICAO code UZTP. It has one runway, 08/26, 10,630 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 123 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UZTP.
