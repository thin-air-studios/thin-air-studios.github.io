About KX07

Lake Wales Municipal Airport (KX07) is a small airport in Lake Wales, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its two runways are 06/24 and 17/35. The longer one, 06/24, is 5,400 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for KX07.
