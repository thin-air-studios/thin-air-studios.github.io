About WALS

Aji Pangeran Tumenggung Pranoto International Airport (AAP) is a medium airport in Samarinda, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WALS. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,382 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 8 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 34 routes through WALS. Its most frequent links are Surabaya (SUB), Jakarta (CGK) and Yogyakarta (YIA).
