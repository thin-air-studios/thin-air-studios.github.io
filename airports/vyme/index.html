About VYME

Myeik Airport (MGZ) is a medium airport in Mkeik, Myanmar (Burma), with the ICAO code VYME. It has one runway, 18/36, 8,795 ft long and 180 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 20 routes through VYME. Its most frequent links are Yangon (RGN), Bokpyinn (VBP) and Dawei (TVY).
