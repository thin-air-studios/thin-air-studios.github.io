About HECA

Cairo International Airport (CAI) is a large airport in Cairo, Egypt, with the ICAO code HECA. Its 3 runways are 05R/23L, 05C/23C and 05L/23R. The longest, 05R/23L, is 13,124 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 31 gates, 343 taxiway segments, 19 aprons and 13 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 117 airlines have 2,061 routes through HECA. Its most frequent links are Jeddah (JED), Riyadh (RUH) and Medina (MED).
