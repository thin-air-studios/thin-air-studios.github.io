About HE39

As Salihiyah Airport (HE39) is a small airport in El Husseiniya, Egypt. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 02L/20R, 02C/20C and 02R/20L. The longest, 02L/20R, is 9,857 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 51 taxiway segments and 2 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for HE39.
