About YMAV

Melbourne Avalon International Airport (AVV) is a large airport in Geelong/Melbourne, Australia, with the ICAO code YMAV. It has one runway, 18/36, 10,000 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 9 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 10 airlines have 78 routes through YMAV. Its most frequent links are Sydney (SYD), Gold Coast (OOL) and Brisbane (BNE).
