About KTUL

Tulsa International Airport (TUL) is a large airport in Tulsa, United States, with the ICAO code KTUL. Its 3 runways are 18L/36R, 08/26 and 18R/36L. The longest, 18L/36R, is 10,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 gates, 174 taxiway segments, 20 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 35 airlines have 292 routes through KTUL. Its most frequent links are Chicago (ORD), Houston (IAH) and Denver (DEN).
