About WIPP

Sultan Mahmud Badaruddin II Airport (PLM) is a medium airport in Palembang, Indonesia, with the ICAO code WIPP. It has one runway, 11/29, 8,202 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 63 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 14 airlines have 127 routes through WIPP. Its most frequent links are Jakarta (CGK), Jakarta (HLP) and Sepang (KUL).
