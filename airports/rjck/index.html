About RJCK

Kushiro Airport (KUH) is a medium airport in Kushiro, Japan, with the ICAO code RJCK. It has one runway, 17/35, 8,202 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 gates, 47 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 49 routes through RJCK. Its most frequent links are Tokyo (HND), Sapporo (OKD) and Sapporo (CTS).
