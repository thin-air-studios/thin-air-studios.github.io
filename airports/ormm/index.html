About ORMM

Basra International Airport (BSR) is a large airport in Basra, Iraq, with the ICAO code ORMM. It has one runway, 14/32, 13,124 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 54 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 13 airlines have 74 routes through ORMM. Its most frequent links are Dubai (DXB), Doha (DOH) and Istanbul (IST).
