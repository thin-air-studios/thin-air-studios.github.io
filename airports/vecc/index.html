About VECC

Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport (CCU) is a large airport in Kolkata, India, with the ICAO code VECC. Its two runways are 01R/19L and 01L/19R. The longer one, 01R/19L, is 11,919 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 gates, 71 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 42 airlines have 1,662 routes through VECC. Its most frequent links are New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM) and Bengaluru (BLR).
