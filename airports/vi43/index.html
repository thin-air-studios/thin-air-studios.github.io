About VI43

Suratgarh New Airport (VI43) is a small airport in India. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,990 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 17 taxiway segments and 9 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for VI43.
