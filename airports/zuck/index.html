About ZUCK

Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport (CKG) is a large airport in Chongqing, China, with the ICAO code ZUCK. It is number 43 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 50.1 million passengers in 2025. Its 4 runways are 03L/21R, 02R/20L, 03R/21L and 02L/20R. The longest, 03L/21R, is 12,467 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 61 gates, 870 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 6 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 82 airlines have 2,509 routes through ZUCK. Its most frequent links are Shenzhen (SZX), Guangzhou (CAN) and Shanghai (PVG).
