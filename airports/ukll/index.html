About UKLL

Lviv International Airport (LWO) is a large airport in Lviv, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKLL. It has one runway, 13/31, 10,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 134 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 27 airlines have 273 routes through UKLL. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH) and Boryspil (KBP).
