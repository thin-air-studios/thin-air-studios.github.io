About YBAS

Alice Springs Airport (ASP) is a medium airport in Alice Springs, Australia, with the ICAO code YBAS. Its two runways are 12/30 and 17/35. The longer one, 12/30, is 7,999 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 8 gates, 51 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 9 airlines have 70 routes through YBAS. Its most frequent links are Adelaide (ADL), Darwin (DRW) and Melbourne (MEL).
