About RPLL

Ninoy Aquino International Airport (MNL) is a large airport in Manila (Pasay), Philippines, with the ICAO code RPLL. It is number 38 on the ACI World list of the busiest airports, with 52.0 million passengers in 2025. Its two runways are 06/24 and 13/31. The longer one, 06/24, is 12,261 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 69 gates, 270 taxiway segments, 39 aprons and 5 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 93 airlines have 2,529 routes through RPLL. Its most frequent links are Cebu City/Lapu-Lapu City (CEB), Davao (DVO) and Cagayan de Oro (RPML).
