About KPHL

Philadelphia International Airport (PHL) is a large airport in Philadelphia, United States, with the ICAO code KPHL. Its 4 runways are 09R/27L, 09L/27R, 17/35 and 08/26. The longest, 09R/27L, is 12,000 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 127 gates, 365 taxiway segments, 18 aprons and 25 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 62 airlines have 3,753 routes through KPHL. Its most frequent links are Boston (BOS), Louisville (SDF) and Orlando (MCO).
