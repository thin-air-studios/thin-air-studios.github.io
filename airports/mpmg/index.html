About MPMG

Marcos A. Gelabert International Airport (PAC) is a medium airport in Albrook, Panama, with the ICAO code MPMG. It has one runway, 01/19, 5,906 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 22 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 28 routes through MPMG. Its most frequent links are Isla Colón (BOC), David (DAV) and Chitré (CTD).
