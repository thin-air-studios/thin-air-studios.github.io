About KCPS

St Louis Downtown Airport (CPS) is a small airport in Cahokia/St Louis, United States, with the ICAO code KCPS. Its 3 runways are 12R/30L, 12L/30R and 05/23. The longest, 12R/30L, is 7,002 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 197 taxiway segments and 17 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 2 routes through KCPS. Its most frequent links are Nashville (BNA) and Madison (MSN).
