About ZGKL

Guilin Liangjiang International Airport (KWL) is a large airport in Guilin (Lingui), China, with the ICAO code ZGKL. It has one runway, 01/19, 10,499 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 23 gates, 96 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 39 airlines have 372 routes through ZGKL. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (PVG), Haikou (HAK) and Nanjing (NKG).
