About LFKF

Figari Sud-Corse Airport (FSC) is a large airport in Figari, France, with the ICAO code LFKF. It has one runway, 05/23, 8,136 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 600 routes through LFKF. Its most frequent links are Paris (ORY), Paris (CDG) and Nantes (NTE).
