About KHTS

Tri-State Airport / Milton J. Ferguson Field (HTS) is a medium airport in Huntington, United States, with the ICAO code KHTS. It has one runway, 12/30, 7,017 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 11 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 30 routes through KHTS. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Pinellas Park (PIE) and Orlando (SFB).
