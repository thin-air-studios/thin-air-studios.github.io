About OEMA

Prince Mohammad Bin Abdulaziz Airport (MED) is a large airport in Medina, Saudi Arabia, with the ICAO code OEMA. Its two runways are 17/35 and 18/36. The longer one, 17/35, is 14,222 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 101 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 3 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 51 airlines have 588 routes through OEMA. Its most frequent links are Cairo (CAI), Riyadh (RUH) and Dubai (DXB).
