About SBSJ

Professor Urbano Ernesto Stumpf Airport (SJK) is a medium airport in São José Dos Campos, Brazil, with the ICAO code SBSJ. It has one runway, 16/34, 8,780 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 2 gates, 29 taxiway segments, 24 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 15 routes through SBSJ. Its most frequent links are Rio de Janeiro (SDU), Rio De Janeiro (GIG) and Campinas (VCP).
