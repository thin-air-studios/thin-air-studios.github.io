About LGKF

Kefallinia Airport (EFL) is a medium airport in Kefallinia Island, Greece, with the ICAO code LGKF. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,992 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 14 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 45 airlines have 506 routes through LGKF. Its most frequent links are London (LGW), Manchester (MAN) and London (STN).
