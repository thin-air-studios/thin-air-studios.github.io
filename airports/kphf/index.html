About KPHF

Newport News Williamsburg International Airport (PHF) is a medium airport in Newport News, United States, with the ICAO code KPHF. Its two runways are 07/25 and 02/20. The longer one, 07/25, is 8,003 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 51 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 8 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 77 routes through KPHF. Its most frequent links are Charlotte (CLT), Philadelphia (PHL) and Atlanta (ATL).
