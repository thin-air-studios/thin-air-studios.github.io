About UIII

Irkutsk International Airport (IKT) is a large airport in Irkutsk, Russia, with the ICAO code UIII. It has one runway, 12/30, 11,696 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 gates, 171 taxiway segments, 68 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 38 airlines have 512 routes through UIII. Its most frequent links are Novosibirsk (OVB), Moscow (DME) and Artyom (VVO).
