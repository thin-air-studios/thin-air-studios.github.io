About 83TX

Texas A And M Flight Test Station Airport (83TX) is a small airport in Bryan, United States. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 17R/35L, 7,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 6 taxiway segments and 5 aprons.
