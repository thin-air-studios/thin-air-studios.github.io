About UKCM

Mariupol International Airport (MPW) is a medium airport in Mariupol, Ukraine, with the ICAO code UKCM. Its two runways are 02/20 and 11/29. The longer one, 02/20, is 8,431 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 76 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for UKCM.
