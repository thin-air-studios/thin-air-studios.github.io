About EDVK

Kassel Airport (KSF) is a large airport in Calden, Germany, with the ICAO code EDVK. It has one runway, 09/27, 8,202 ft long and 147 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 24 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 11 airlines have 67 routes through EDVK. Its most frequent links are Antalya (AYT), Palma de Mallorca (PMI) and Heraklion (HER).
