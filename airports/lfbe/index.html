About LFBE

Bergerac Dordogne-Périgord airport (EGC) is a medium airport in Bergerac, France, with the ICAO code LFBE. It has one runway, 09/27, 7,234 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 14 gates, 16 taxiway segments, 5 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 7 airlines have 124 routes through LFBE. Its most frequent links are London (STN), Rotterdam (RTM) and Nottingham (EMA).
