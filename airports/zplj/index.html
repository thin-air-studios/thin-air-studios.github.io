About ZPLJ

Lijiang Sanyi International Airport (LJG) is a large airport in Lijiang, China, with the ICAO code ZPLJ. It has one runway, 02/20, 9,843 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 15 gates, 65 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 28 airlines have 453 routes through ZPLJ. Its most frequent links are Chengdu (TFU), Guangzhou (CAN) and Changsha (CSX).
