About KTTN

Trenton Mercer Airport (TTN) is a medium airport in Ewing Township, United States, with the ICAO code KTTN. Its two runways are 06/24 and 16/34. The longer one, 06/24, is 6,006 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 gates, 34 taxiway segments, 8 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 3 airlines have 110 routes through KTTN. Its most frequent links are Orlando (MCO), Tampa (TPA) and Atlanta (ATL).
