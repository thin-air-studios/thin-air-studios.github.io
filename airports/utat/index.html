About UTAT

Dashoguz International Airport (TAZ) is a large airport in Daşoguz, Turkmenistan, with the ICAO code UTAT. It has one runway, 08/26, 12,467 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 19 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 23 routes through UTAT. Its most frequent links are Ashgabat (ASB), Turkmenbaşy (KRW) and Mary (MYP).
