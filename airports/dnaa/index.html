About DNAA

Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport (ABV) is a large airport in Abuja, Nigeria, with the ICAO code DNAA. It has one runway, 04/22, 11,842 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 67 taxiway segments, 6 aprons and 4 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 29 airlines have 198 routes through DNAA. Its most frequent links are Lagos (LOS), Port Harcourt (PHC) and Benin (BNI).
