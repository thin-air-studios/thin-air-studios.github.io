About EDAC

Leipzig–Altenburg Airport (AOC) is a medium airport in Nobitz, Germany, with the ICAO code EDAC. It has one runway, 04/22, 7,988 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 25 taxiway segments, 1 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for EDAC.
