About SADO

Campo de Mayo Military Airport (SADO) is a small airport in Campo de Mayo, Argentina. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 18/36, 5,905 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 5 taxiway segments and 3 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SADO.
