About KGSB

Seymour Johnson Air Force Base (GSB) is a medium airport in Goldsboro, United States, with the ICAO code KGSB. It has one runway, 08/26, 11,760 ft long and 300 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 12 taxiway segments and 17 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, GWY has 1 routes through KGSB. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Philadelphia (PHL).
