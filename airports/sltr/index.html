About SLTR

Teniente Av. Jorge Henrich Arauz Airport (TDD) is a medium airport in Trinidad, Bolivia, with the ICAO code SLTR. It has one runway, 14/32, 7,874 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 2 airlines have 8 routes through SLTR. Its most frequent links are Cochabamba (CBB) and La Paz / El Alto (LPB).
