About LOWK

Klagenfurt Airport (KLU) is a large airport in Klagenfurt am Wörthersee, Austria, with the ICAO code LOWK. Its two runways are 10L/28R and 10R/28L. The longer one, 10L/28R, is 8,924 ft. Of these, 1 hard and 1 soft. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 3 gates, 23 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 16 airlines have 102 routes through LOWK. Its most frequent links are Vienna (VIE), Köln (CGN) and London (STN).
