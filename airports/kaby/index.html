About KABY

Southwest Georgia Regional Airport (ABY) is a medium airport in Albany, United States, with the ICAO code KABY. Its two runways are 05/23 and 17/35. The longer one, 05/23, is 6,601 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 60 taxiway segments, 3 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 4 airlines have 20 routes through KABY. Its most frequent links are Atlanta (ATL), Louisville (SDF) and Jacksonville (JAX).
