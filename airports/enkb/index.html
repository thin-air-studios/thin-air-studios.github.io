About ENKB

Kristiansund Airport, Kvernberget (KSU) is a medium airport in Kvernberget, Norway, with the ICAO code ENKB. It has one runway, 07/25, 7,841 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 10 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 6 airlines have 79 routes through ENKB. Its most frequent links are Bergen (BGO), Oslo (OSL) and Ålesund (AES).
