About SAFV

Venado Tuerto Airport (SAFV) is a small airport in Venado Tuerto, Argentina. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. It has one runway, 3/21, 4,921 ft long and 98 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 1 taxiway segments, 7 aprons and 1 terminal outlines. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for SAFV.
