About USCC

Kurchatov Chelyabinsk International Airport (CEK) is a large airport in Chelyabinsk, Russia, with the ICAO code USCC. It has one runway, 09/27, 10,499 ft long and 197 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 131 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 25 airlines have 234 routes through USCC. Its most frequent links are Moscow (SVO), Antalya (AYT) and Moscow (DME).
