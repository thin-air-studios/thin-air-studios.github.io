About LLHB

Hatzerim Air Base (LLHB) is a small airport in Beersheba, Israel. Our airport list gives it no IATA code. Its 3 runways are 10R/28L, 10L/28R and 15/33. The longest, 10R/28L, is 9,657 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 76 taxiway segments and 7 aprons. The route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026 has no scheduled routes for LLHB.
