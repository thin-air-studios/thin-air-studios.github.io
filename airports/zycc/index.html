About ZYCC

Changchun Longjia International Airport (CGQ) is a large airport in Changchun, China, with the ICAO code ZYCC. It has one runway, 06/24, 10,500 ft long and 148 ft wide, with a hard surface. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 7 gates, 76 taxiway segments, 2 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 44 airlines have 805 routes through ZYCC. Its most frequent links are Shanghai (PVG), Hangzhou (HGH) and Nanjing (NKG).
