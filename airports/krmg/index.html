About KRMG

Richard B Russell Airport (RMG) is a medium airport in Rome, United States, with the ICAO code KRMG. Its two runways are 01/19 and 07/25. The longer one, 01/19, is 7,010 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 18 taxiway segments and 4 aprons. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, OPT has 1 routes through KRMG. Its only linked airport in the snapshot is Waterville (WVL).
