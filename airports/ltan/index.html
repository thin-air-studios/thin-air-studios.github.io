About LTAN

Konya Airport (KYA) is a large airport in Konya, Türkiye, with the ICAO code LTAN. Its two runways are 01L/19R and 01R/19L. The longer one, 01L/19R, is 10,990 ft. All of them have hard surfaces. The OpenStreetMap layout shows 81 taxiway segments, 12 aprons and 2 terminal outlines. In the route snapshot of 14 Oct 2026, 8 airlines have 276 routes through LTAN. Its most frequent links are Pendik (SAW), Copenhagen (CPH) and Istanbul (IST).
